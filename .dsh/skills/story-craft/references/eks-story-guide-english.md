## Instructions for Use

1. **File Naming**: `ch-{{Chapter Number}}-{{Chapter Title Abbreviation}}.md`
2. **ID Generation**: Character numbers should remain unique and can be customized based on logic
   such as race, batch, and appearance order. The first two digits can represent the base or batch,
   and the last three digits are the serial number.
3. **Commentary Core**: The **"Their Final Stories"** section is the soul of the template,
   aiming to transform every insignificant Kiké Soldier death into a life epic worth seeing. Please
   invest emotion and imagination to fill it.
4. **Adhere to Settings**: All descriptions must strictly comply with the rules in the 《Empire
   Forever》 setting collection (setting.md), including physiological mechanisms, ranking systems,
   equipment characteristics, etc.
5. **Maintain Style**: The writing style should remain冷酷,压抑, with细腻的感官刻画,契合 the dark
   aesthetics of the worldview.
