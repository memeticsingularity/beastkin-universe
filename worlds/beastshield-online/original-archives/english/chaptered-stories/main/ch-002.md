# Chapter 2

　　"Players who clear the game will split a ten-million RMB reward." I kept turning this sentence over in my mind the whole way.

　　That means the players are in a competitive relationship. If we meet on a narrow path, it's very likely I'll be ambushed and killed by another player.

　　But the game's clear conditions are still unclear. If the conditions are very difficult and impossible to clear solo, then the players will have to cooperate.

　　This left me extremely conflicted. But whether it's competition or cooperation, the right to choose shouldn't be left to other players. So what I need to focus on right now is not exposing my player identity.

　　After coming down the mountain, I finally found a small village. The village was very small, with only about a dozen or so farming households. Due to its remote location, there were no shops, no inns, not even many pedestrians. All I could see were a few farmers tending their backyards.

　　I cautiously walked into the village, looking around shiftily at the others. All the beastmen were busy with their own affairs, behaving completely normally. There were no extra indicators like names or levels floating above their heads. Just by observing, there was no way to tell who was a player.

　　Since I couldn't tell who others were, other players couldn't tell who I was either. I simply dropped my guard and focused my attention on the game itself.

　　Speaking of which, this game doesn't even have a main quest to guide me. I have no idea what I'm supposed to do. Am I just supposed to wander around freely and trigger things by exploration?

　　So I aimlessly strolled around the village. Before long, I spotted a frail white wolf beastman under a mulberry tree not far away.

　　This wolf beastman looked very young, dressed in a white robe of very fine material, but it had already been worn to tatters. His snow-white fur was covered in filth, looking slovenly and unkempt. He was skin and bones, utterly haggard.

　　I approached him cautiously and asked, "Bro, what happened to you?"

　　The white wolf saw me talking to him and grabbed at me like a drowning man grabbing a lifeline. "I haven't eaten anything for a day. I'm so hungry. Please, do me a favor and get me some food?"

　　Hmm? Don't people usually say they haven't eaten for three days? Well, that doesn't matter. I finally triggered a quest!

　　"Wait a moment, I'll go find you something to eat."

　　"Wait... preferably something with meat." The white wolf added.

　　This guy is pretty picky.

　　But where am I supposed to find food? Although I have three rubles on me, this village doesn't even have a shop!

　　Maybe I should just go pick some vegetables from the fields. I just don't know if those farmers will treat me like a thief.

　　Speaking of which, in other games, heroes can smash pots in villagers' houses. Me picking some vegetables from the fields should be fine, right?

　　So I mustered my courage and came to a radish field. Next to it, an ox beastman farmer was plowing the soil. I slowly squatted down while observing the ox beastman's reaction. He just kept plowing and didn't pay me much mind. Then I reached out and grabbed the radish leaf stalk, making a motion to pull it out.

　　At that moment, the ox beastman instantly stopped farming. He hoisted his pitchfork onto his shoulder, his eyes glaring death at my hand. His facial muscles twitched, and his right hand clenched with a cracking sound. It was clearly a warning. If I didn't know any better and continued to pull out this radish, the ox beastman would likely pull out my prostate!

　　"Hahahaha, these radishes are growing so well, so great!" I awkwardly let go, gave the ox beastman a thumbs-up, and retreated with an apologetic smile.

　　Stealing definitely won't work. Looks like I'll have to buy.

　　I knocked on a wooden door of a household. The door opened a crack, and an eye peered out from the gap, looking at me vigilantly.

　　"Hello, I'd like to buy some food." I forced a slight smile.

　　"I only have agricultural products here, no cooked food." The beastman in the door crack said.

　　"That works too... I'd like to buy a few radishes. Oh, and if you have it, some pork too."

　　The moment I finished speaking, the wooden door was violently pushed open!

　　"You little punk are looking for trouble, aren't you!" A white-skinned pig beastman rushed out, waving his fist and roaring at me.

　　Although this world also has ordinary livestock like pigs, sheep, and cattle, out of taboo, beastmen generally don't eat meat from the same species as themselves. Asking a beastman for meat of the same species is naturally a very rude thing to do.

　　"Sorry, sorry! I didn't know you were a pig!" I was so scared I fell on my butt, immediately pulling out two rubles from my bag and holding them up in front of me.

　　That sounded like an insult, but it made perfect sense to the pig beastman.

　　The pig beastman snorted, snatched the two rubles and went inside, then threw out a few radishes and a piece of blue meat, roaring: "Only nan meat. Take it or leave it." Then he slammed the door shut.

　　Nan meat? What kind of meat is this? It looks poisonous.

　　Whatever, it's not for me anyway.

　　I brought the radishes and nan meat to the white wolf. The white wolf said weakly: "I can't eat it raw either. Please help me cook it."

　　This NPC is really troublesome.

　　Then, under the white wolf's guidance, I built a fire nearby. I used my axe to chop the radishes and nan meat into pieces, skewered them alternately with meat and vegetables, and started grilling.

　　Although this nan meat looked bizarre in color, the smell when it was grilled was absolutely divine!

　　"By the way, what species does this nan meat come from?" I asked, handing the grilled skewer to the white wolf.

　　The starving white wolf took the grilled meat and devoured it ravenously. Only after finishing the entire skewer did his mouth have a moment of free time to speak: "Nan meat isn't the meat of any particular species. It's a method of preparation. Raw meat is soaked in heather flower juice to marinate it, achieving a flavor where the fishy taste cancels out the fishy taste. This blue color is the effect of the heather flower juice dye."

　　"So, no matter what kind of meat it is, as long as it's marinated in heather flower juice, it's collectively called nan meat?" I asked, handing him another grilled skewer.

　　"Exactly!" The white wolf continued to devour it ravenously.

　　Out of curiosity, I also took a bite to taste it. This game's VR headset didn't just provide sight and sound; it also stimulated my taste nerves with electric currents, allowing me to taste the food in the game.

　　A burst of fatty, meaty flavor melted in my mouth. I never expected that with just a simple grill, this nan meat could be so delicious! My appetite was instantly opened up, and I immediately polished off two large skewers of nan meat.

　　After eating, I felt a warmth spread through my body. My limbs seemed to have become more powerful. It looks like diet also has a certain effect on strengthening character attributes.

　　After eating and drinking my fill, I put the remaining ingredients into my backpack. At this moment, the system suddenly popped up a notification:

　　【Congratulations, you have comprehended Cooking Mastery Level 1】

　　So that's how it is. In this game, you don't learn skills by leveling up and分配ing points, but by practicing and comprehending them yourself. This design is quite interesting.

　　"Thanks!" The white wolf let out a loud burp, satisfied, and leaned against the tree trunk to take a short nap.

　　"You're welcome." The quest was complete. I sat cross-legged nearby, looking at him expectantly, waiting to claim the quest reward.

　　"Hmm?"

　　"Hmm?"

　　"Is there anything else, sir?" The white wolf asked, puzzled.

　　"Where's my reward?" I continued to look at him with a smile.

　　After running around so much for this, and even paying two rubles out of my own pocket, there's no way there's no reward, right! The skill I just comprehended was the result of my own practice. That definitely doesn't count as a reward!

　　"You helped me..." The white wolf pointed at me.

　　"Mm-hmm!"

　　"I said thank you..." Then he pointed at himself.

　　"Mm-hmm!"

　　"Doesn't that make us even?" Finally, he spread his hands with a look of course.

　　"Mm-hmm," I smiled sweetly and drew the iron axe from behind my back. "Was that your final words?"

　　"W-wait!" The white wolf instantly sobered up and said urgently: "I don't have a single coin on me, there's nothing I can... Oh right! D-do you want to join the army? I have connections at the recruitment office. I can introduce you and get you enlisted as a formal soldier!"

　　Hmph, that's more like it. Although there's no material reward, at least I got a follow-up quest.

　　I put away the axe and went with him to the village recruitment office.

　　Along the way, chatting idly, he introduced himself as Bai Bai, a beastman who had fled from Wolfwind Valley.

　　I wanted to ask a few more questions to understand the world setting, but the village was too small. Before we could chat much, we arrived at the recruitment office.

　　Calling it a recruitment office was generous; it was actually just a temporary open-air workspace. Several fierce beastmen in armor were gathered in a cluster, chatting casually.

　　The white wolf beastman walked over shiftily and whispered a few words to one of the officers, then pointed at me. That officer nodded and then handed the white wolf a bag of rubles.

　　Strange, why did the officer give him money when he went to bribe the officer?

　　Then several officers walked up to me. The officer who gave the money said to me: "Thank you for your willingness to devote yourself to the village. You are now setting out to讨伐 bandits. From now on, you are a member of the volunteer soldiers."

　　After speaking, several officers pulled me toward the outside of the village.

　　Wait a minute! Volunteer soldier? Shouldn't it be formally enlisted soldier!

　　"Officer, did you make some kind of mistake?" I was being dragged further and further from the village. In my panic, I turned back and waved at the white wolf, shouting: "Come over here and explain things clearly to them, Bai Bai!"

　　The white wolf also waved at me with a smile and said:

　　"Bye-bye!"
