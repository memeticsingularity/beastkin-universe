## 一、简介 / Introduction

**一句话钩子**：一只黑豹，一夜清剿——废弃工厂里的兽人小队以最屈辱的方式逐一点名，唯一的活口被锁在铁柜中，困在永不停歇的折磨循环里。

深夜，一座废弃工厂外，黑豹特工潜入了一支驻守兽人小队的驻地。从水塔哨兵开始，他以沉默而高效的手法逐一解决外围守卫，最终进入工厂内部。被俘的牛副队长被锁在铁柜中，被迫目睹所有队员——包括他崇拜的虎队长——被当着他的面逐一处决。而他自己的命运，比死亡更加残酷。

---

**One-line Hook**: A black panther, one night, one clearing — an orc squad eliminated one by one in the most humiliating way, leaving a single survivor trapped in an endless cycle of torment.

At midnight, a black panther agent infiltrates a remote factory guarded by an orc squad. Starting from the water tower sentry, he eliminates the outer guards silently and efficiently before entering the factory itself. The captured bull deputy captain is locked in an iron closet, forced to watch every single one of his men — including the captain he worships — executed before his eyes. His own fate is crueler than death.
