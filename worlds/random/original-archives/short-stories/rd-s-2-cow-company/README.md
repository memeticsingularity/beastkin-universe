## 一、简介 / Introduction

**一句话钩子**：经济窘迫的牛兽人第一次上门提供鲜奶榨汁服务，遇到一个温柔体贴的客户，最后意外收获好评和小费。

奶牛公司的二号员工因为经济困难，第一次正式上门为客户提供鲜奶榨汁服务。他紧张、羞愧、假装睡觉，但客户却温柔耐心，全程手动操作，事后还给了五星好评和一笔不菲的小费。

---

**One-line Hook**: A cash-strapped bull orc's first home visit for fresh milk extraction service ends with an unexpectedly kind customer, a five-star review, and a generous tip.

Cow Company's Employee No. 2, driven by financial hardship, makes his first formal house call for fresh milk service. Nervous, ashamed, and pretending to sleep, he is met by a gentle, patient customer who handles everything manually and leaves a five-star review plus a generous tip.
