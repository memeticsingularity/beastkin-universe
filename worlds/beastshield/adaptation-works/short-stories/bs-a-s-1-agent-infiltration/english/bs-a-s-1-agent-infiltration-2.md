> *The Infiltrator's Flame*  
> Young agent **Lei Yanhuan** of the **Special Agent Bureau** is ordered to infiltrate a remote **Beastshield** warehouse. For this red tiger beastman who has mastered the **Scorching Flow Hidden Force**, this is not merely a mission, but a trial of power, restraint, and growth.

---

**Scene-1 Waiting Beneath the Moon**

Night hung over this desolate wilderness like a sheet of ink-soaked cloth, heavy and suffocating.

Lei Yanhuan crouched in the bushes outside the warehouse, his crimson fur gleaming dully in the moonlight. He was lean and well-built, with smooth, powerful muscle lines—a typical tiger combat physique. At the moment he wore only a pair of black tactical shorts, his upper body bare, letting the night wind brush across the full muscles of his chest and abdomen.

His mission was to eliminate the guards at this remote warehouse. Intelligence indicated five to six G-rank grunts were stationed here, but exactly who they were, what they looked like, what their specialties were—all of that was unknown. The Special Agent Bureau's mission briefings had always been this concise: location, target count, notes. The rest was up to you to encounter.

The Commander-in-Chief had specifically warned him: "Don't be careless. Remote outposts often have traps of complacency that make you underestimate them."

Lei Yanhuan licked his lips, revealing two sharp tiger fangs. His eyes glowed faintly in the darkness—a sign that the Scorching Flow Hidden Force was circulating within his body. He raised his right hand, palm upward, and channeled his inner strength—a trace of barely perceptible red light flowed beneath his skin, as if molten lava were surging through his veins.

"Control," he whispered to himself. "Today's task is cleanup, not slaughter."

He could already sense the auras of the beastmen inside the warehouse—the smell of sweat, tobacco, and that particular odor unique to low-level employees, a mixture of fear and numbness. At least five of them, all grunts, wearing olive-green uniforms.

Lei Yanhuan took a deep breath. The air carried the chill of late autumn, with the bitter scent of decaying leaves. He stood up, his muscles sliding slowly beneath his skin, like a predator about to hunt.

**Scene-2 The Gamble Outside the Gate**

Outside the warehouse gate, a **wild boar beastman** was leaning against the wall smoking.

His name was **Dazhuang**, thirty-one years old, having guarded this gate for three years. His colleagues had given him the nickname "Doorplate"—partly because he always stood at the door, and partly because his thick layer of fat really was like a doorplate.

"Doorplate, lend me a light." Another **brown bear beastman** came staggering over, a crumpled cigarette between his fingers.

Dazhuang glanced at him and tossed over his lighter: "Fatty Xiong, didn't you say you were quitting?"

"Quit my ass," Xiong Houshi—that was the brown bear's real name, but everyone called him Fatty Xiong—lit up and took a deep drag. "It's my night watch tonight. If I don't smoke to stay awake, I'll die of sleepiness."

"When have you ever not been sleepy on night watch? Last time you snored so loud the whole warehouse heard it. The captain nearly kicked you out of bed."

"Fuck, don't bring that up," Fatty Xiong scratched his big belly, the olive-green tight uniform stretched taut and bulging. "That yellow tiger's a piece of shit. He can't sleep himself and won't let anyone else sleep either."

Dazhuang smiled, revealing two cloudy tusks. He liked Fatty Xiong. The guy was lazy and gluttonous, but a decent sort. At least better than his night-shift partner—that **dog beastman** named **Laifu**, who was jumpy all day long, startled by every rustle of wind and grass.

"Speaking of Laifu, where's that kid?" Dazhuang asked.

"Upstairs, playing cards with Brother Hu and Greymane." Fatty Xiong exhaled a smoke ring. "That coward's lost a week's worth of cigarette money and still won't quit."

"Brother Hu cheating the fool again?"

"Of course. With Brother Hu's sleight of hand, could Laifu's brain catch it?" Fatty Xiong chuckled twice, the fat on his chest quivering. "Just wait. Tonight Laifu will lose next month's allowance too."

Dazhuang shook his head. He'd seen too many young people like Laifu—just joined Beastshield, thinking they could earn a living, only to get eaten down to the bone.

"Fatty Xiong," Dazhuang suddenly lowered his voice, "tell me... what exactly are we guarding here?"

"Huh?" Fatty Xiong looked blank.

"This warehouse is full of scrap metal. Is it worth five of us taking turns guarding it?"

Fatty Xiong thought about it, scratching his head: "The higher-ups told us to guard it, so we guard it. Why ask so many questions? Anyway, we get paid, we have a place to sleep. Pretty good."

Dazhuang said nothing. He thought of his parents back home, of his promise to send money back, of that crumpled family photo. He hadn't sent money for three months—lost it gambling, spent it on cigarettes, drank it away treating Fatty Xiong.

He wanted to say he'd definitely go back to see his parents next year, but the words died in his throat.

Next year? Who knew if he'd still be in this godforsaken place next year.

Just then, Dazhuang smelled something.

Not the smell of smoke, not the smell of sweat, but something... hot. Like magma, like burning metal, carrying a dangerous, trembling masculine hormonal scent.

"Fatty Xiong," Dazhuang's voice suddenly tightened, "do you smell..."

He didn't finish his sentence.

A crimson claw had pressed against the back of his neck.

**Scene-3 First Blood**

"Uncles," a young voice rang in the darkness, carrying a teasing smile, "still smoking so late, aren't you afraid of black lungs?"

Dazhuang went rigid. He wanted to shout, but that claw's temperature was astonishingly high, scorching his throat tight. He could feel the body heat of the beastman behind him—like a red-hot branding iron, the burning sensation could be felt even through the air.

Fatty Xiong was stunned. His cigarette fell to the ground, tracing a faint spark in the darkness. He wanted to run, but his legs felt like they were filled with lead and wouldn't move.

"Who... who are you..." Dazhuang squeezed out a few words through his teeth.

"Me?" The voice laughed. "Just a passing intern."

Before the words had faded, Dazhuang felt a scorching heat pour into his body from the back of his neck. It wasn't ordinary pain, but something... like magma, surging from his spine all the way down, straight to his groin.

"Agh...!" He let out a scream, his legs involuntarily clamping together.

The red tiger beastman behind him—Lei Yanhuan—withdrew his claw, watching the wild boar beastman slowly turn around. Dazhuang's face was already flushed red, his pig snout exhaling rough breaths, and the thick, short pig whip between his legs was erecting at a visible speed, pitching an embarrassing tent in his olive-green shorts.

"What... what is this..." Dazhuang looked at his own lower body in horror. He could feel his essence gauge skyrocketing—30%... 50%... 70%...

Not pleasure, but fear, a physiological reaction forcibly driven by some incomprehensible power.

"Scorching Flow Hidden Force," Lei Yanhuan said calmly. "Inner strength transformed into scorching heat, rushing straight to the essence gate. What you feel is magma flowing through your veins."

"Please... please..." Dazhuang knelt down, the pig whip between his legs already hard and aching, the horse-eye opening secreting transparent fluid that ran down the shaft, "I... I'm just a gatekeeper... My name is Dazhuang... I have parents..."

"Dazhuang?" Lei Yanhuan repeated the name, his eyes holding neither pity nor cruelty, only a near-cold calmness. "But today, your uniform is on the list."

He stepped forward, pressing his palm against Dazhuang's chest. Beneath that thick layer of fat beat a heart; he could feel the wild boar beastman's violent heartbeat, fear making that heart nearly burst through his ribcage.

Scorching Flow Hidden Force, Second Level.

Heat poured from his palm, penetrating flesh and skin, reaching straight to the internal organs. Dazhuang let out a shrill howl, his body convulsing as if electrocuted. His essence gauge instantly broke through 100%, and that hard pig whip twitched uncontrollably, a thick stream of semen shooting out from the horse-eye opening, drawing an arc in the air and splattering onto the dead leaves on the ground.

First spurt... second spurt... third spurt...

He thought of Fatty Xiong, of their conversation just now, of that cigarette fallen to the ground. He wanted to turn back and look at his friend, but his neck no longer obeyed him.

His body finally couldn't support itself anymore, collapsing forward, his face buried in the stain of his own ejaculate mixed with dirt. His thick limbs twitched a few times, then went completely still.

"Dazhuang...?" He trembled as he called out, but knew there would be no response.

**Scene-4 The Coward's Choice**

Lei Yanhuan turned toward Fatty Xiong.

That brown bear beastman had already collapsed sitting on the ground, his massive body curled into a ball, his olive-green uniform soaked with urine, emitting a pungent stench. His tears and snot covered his face, his mouth endlessly muttering: "Don't kill me... don't kill me... I didn't do anything..."

"What's your name?" Lei Yanhuan asked.

"Xiong... Xiong Houshi..." the brown bear trembled as he answered, "Everyone calls me Fatty Xiong... I... I'm just getting by... I have elderly parents and young children..."

"Fatty Xiong," Lei Yanhuan squatted down, meeting him at eye level, "you and Dazhuang were chatting quite happily just now."

"Dazhuang... Dazhuang is my friend..." Fatty Xiong choked, "You killed him... you killed him..."

"I've killed many people," Lei Yanhuan said, his tone as flat as if he were stating the weather, "Today you're the fifth, or sixth. I can't remember clearly."

He stood up, looking down at this tear-covered brown bear. Moonlight spilled through the warehouse's broken window, shining on Fatty Xiong's protruding belly, forming an absurd picture.

"Tell me," Lei Yanhuan said, "how many people are upstairs?"

Fatty Xiong hesitated. His loyalty swayed before life and death—betraying his colleagues was treason, but not speaking might mean dying right now.

"Three... three..." He ultimately chose to save his life, "Brother Hu... Greymane... and Laifu... They're playing cards on the second floor..."

"Brother Hu? Greymane? Laifu?" Lei Yanhuan tilted his head, "Those are some friendly names."

"Brother Hu is the captain... Greymane is a wolf beastman... Laifu is a dog beastman..." Fatty Xiong spilled everything like beans from a bamboo tube, "They... they're all good fighters... Brother Hu is especially formidable... You... you can't beat him..."

"Is that so?" Lei Yanhuan smiled, that smile appearing exceptionally cold in the moonlight, "Then let me see for myself."

He raised his hand, palm facing Fatty Xiong's chest.

"Wait!" Fatty Xiong suddenly shouted, "I... I told you everything... Can you spare me... I promise I won't say anything... I'll run away tonight... back to my hometown..."

Lei Yanhuan looked at him, a flash of something passing through those red eyes.

"Fatty Xiong," he said, "do you know why I told you I've killed many people?"

Fatty Xiong shook his head, tears flying everywhere.

"Because dead men tell no tales," Lei Yanhuan said, "And besides, I hate cowards."

Scorching Flow Hidden Force, maximum power.

Fatty Xiong didn't even have time to scream. His essence gauge instantly skyrocketed to 150%. His bear whip jumped frantically inside his tight pants, and then—the ejaculation began.

One spurt after another, like some cruel counting. Five spurts... eight spurts... eleven spurts... fourteen spurts...

Fatty Xiong's body convulsed violently, his massive frame writhing on the ground, his olive-green uniform soaked with semen. His tears were still flowing, but the corners of his mouth uncontrollably curled upward—that was the bizarre smile left by the overlap of death and climax.

Seventeen spurts... eighteen spurts...

Finally, the convulsions stopped.

Fatty Xiong's body lay softly on the ground, head tilted to one side, eyes unfocused. His bear whip still twitched slightly, the horse-eye opening hanging with a final drop of semen, gleaming with a lascivious luster in the moonlight.

Dead.

Lei Yanhuan stood up, feeling the residual warmth in his palm. This bear died quickly, without pain—somewhat, this was mercy. But Lei Yanhuan knew he chose maximum power not out of mercy, but out of impatience.

He didn't like cowards. Didn't like people who betrayed their friends. Even if that friend had just died before his eyes.

"Brother Hu... Greymane... Laifu..." Lei Yanhuan murmured these three names, then turned and walked toward the warehouse interior.

**Scene-5 The Card Game Upstairs**

On the second floor of the warehouse, three beastmen sat around a worn wooden table.

**Brother Hu**—a **yellow tiger beastman**, powerfully built, his olive-green uniform stretched tight by his solid muscles. He was the captain of this small squad, though G-rank grunts hardly counted as any kind of "leader," but at this remote outpost, he was the local emperor.

**Greymane**—a **gray wolf beastman**, lean but muscular, with a sinister gaze. He was Brother Hu's follower, also the outpost's "strategist"—though the so-called strategist just came up with shady tricks to cheat newbies out of their money.

**Laifu**—a **yellow dog beastman**, the youngest, having joined Beastshield less than half a year ago. At this moment his face was flushed, tears in his eyes, because—

"Laifu," Brother Hu leisurely shuffled the cards, the joints on his claws cracking, "you've lost again. How much do you owe me now?"

"Th... thirty lu coins..." Laifu's voice trembled.

"That doesn't sound right," Greymane chimed in from the side, the corner of his mouth carrying a cold smile, "That round just now was twenty, plus the previous fifty, and last week's..."

"Se... seventy..." Laifu corrected, tears nearly falling. That was two months of his allowance.

"Good that you know," Brother Hu slammed the cards on the table, "Come, one more round. If you win this one, all debts are cleared."

"Re... really?" Laifu's eyes lit up.

"When has Brother Hu's word ever not counted?" Brother Hu grinned, revealing two sharp tiger fangs.

Greymane glanced at Brother Hu, and the two exchanged a knowing look. Laifu didn't notice this detail. He was too young, too eager, too desperate to win back his losses.

The card game began.

Laifu's hands were shaking. He held the cards, looking at the patterns, his mind blank. He knew he was being cheated—had known since the first time he lost money—but he couldn't stop. Just like Brother Hu said, one more win and he could break even, one more win and he could clear his debts.

"Call or fold?" Brother Hu asked.

"Call..." Laifu pushed out his last chips.

Brother Hu smiled. He slowly flipped over his cards—

Just then, a scream came from downstairs.

All three froze simultaneously.

"What was that?" Laifu was the first to react; he had been in a state of high tension all along.

"Dazhuang?" Greymane frowned, "Is that dead pig up to his tricks again..."

Another scream, this one more shrill, more brief.

Brother Hu stood up, the muscles beneath his olive-green uniform tensing. He wasn't stupid. Having guarded this remote outpost for three years, he knew which sounds meant "messing around" and which meant "trouble."

"Something's wrong," he said, his voice low, "Greymane, go check."

Greymane nodded, silently walking toward the stairway. He moved like a true wolf—light, agile, always ready to pounce or flee.

Laifu sat in place, his legs shaking. He wanted to follow, but his body wouldn't obey. He wanted to run, but didn't know where to run.

"Laifu," Brother Hu suddenly said, "you owe me seventy lu coins, and you must pay it back today."

"Wh... what?" Laifu was stunned, "Brother Hu, now's not the time to talk about this..."

"I don't care what time it is," Brother Hu turned around, those yellow tiger eyes staring at Laifu, "Money, or your life. Your choice."

Laifu opened his mouth, but before he could speak, he heard a dull thud from downstairs—like something heavy falling to the ground.

Then came footsteps.

Slow, steady, step by step up the stairs.

**Scene-6 Brother Hu's Dignity**

Greymane stood at the stairway entrance, back against the wall, breathing rapidly.

He saw.

He saw Fatty Xiong's corpse collapsed in a corner on the first floor, his uniform soaked with semen, his face still carrying that bizarre smile. He saw Dazhuang lying outside the main gate, his body already stiff. He saw a crimson figure emerging from the shadows, eyes glowing red, like a demon crawling out of hell.

Greymane wanted to run. His instincts told him: Run, this isn't something you can handle.

But Brother Hu's order was "go check."

He couldn't run. At least not in front of Brother Hu. In this small group, Brother Hu was the boss, the protector, the rule-maker. Betraying Brother Hu was worse than death.

At least that's what he thought.

The footsteps drew closer. That red tiger was coming upstairs.

Greymane took a deep breath, drawing the baton from his waist. His claws gripped it tightly, knuckles white.

"Who!" he shouted, his voice sharper than expected, "Halt! This is Beastshield Company territory!"

The footsteps stopped.

A crimson figure appeared from the stairway corner. It was a young tiger beastman, but unlike any tiger beastman he had ever seen—red fur, red eyes, his entire body emitting a scorching aura, as if he had just crawled out of a fire pit.

"Beastshield territory?" The red tiger tilted his head, a playful smile hanging at the corner of his mouth, "Then who am I?"

"You... you're a bounty hunter?" Greymane took a step back, "We... we're all poor here... don't... don't have much money..."

"Bounty hunter?" The red tiger laughed, "I'm not that low-class."

He walked up the stairs step by step, each step like treading on Greymane's heartbeat.

"I'm from the Special Agent Bureau," the red tiger said, "Here today to clean up this outpost. You're Greymane? Wolf beastman?"

Greymane's pupils contracted. The other knew his name—what did that mean? It meant this wasn't a random attack, but a targeted elimination operation.

"How do you know..."

"The fatty downstairs told me," the red tiger had already reached the stairway landing, only three steps from Greymane, "He also told me there's someone called Brother Hu upstairs, especially good at fighting."

Greymane gripped the baton tighter. He wanted to rush forward, wanted to smash this red tiger's head with one strike.

But his body wouldn't obey.

"You... don't come closer..." Greymane's voice was shaking, "Brother Hu... Brother Hu won't let you go..."

"Is that so?" The red tiger smiled, "Then let him come."

Before the words faded, the red tiger's figure suddenly vanished.

Greymane only saw a blur before his eyes, a scorching aura rushing toward his face. He wanted to swing the baton, but his wrist was already caught by a burning hot claw.

"Greymane," the red tiger pressed against his ear, his voice as light as if whispering sweet nothings, "You know what? I hate accomplices like you the most. You clearly know you're doing bad things, yet you still find a boss to take the blame."

"I... I'm just getting by..." Greymane begged.

"Getting by," the red tiger repeated, "Everyone says they're just getting by. Then who takes responsibility for those newbies you've bullied?"

He raised his other hand, palm facing Greymane's abdomen.

"Laifu owes you seventy lu coins," the red tiger said, "Today I'll settle his debt in full."

Scorching Flow Hidden Force.

Greymane let out a scream, but the sound quickly turned into a whimper. He felt that scorching heat pour into his body from his abdomen, rushing straight to his groin. Essence gauge skyrocketing—40%... 70%... 100%...

His wolf whip erected inside his shorts, then uncontrollably began ejaculating.

One spurt... three spurts... five spurts...

Seven spurts... nine spurts...

His body went soft, like a cloth doll with its bones removed.

Dead.

The red tiger—Lei Yanhuan—stepped over his corpse, walking toward the depths of the second floor.

**Scene-7 The Final Choice**

Brother Hu stood in the center of the room, body tense, like a fully drawn bow.

He saw Greymane's fate. That cunning gray wolf, his capable assistant, now lying at the stairway entrance, his body still twitching slightly, his crotch a mess.

He also saw that crimson figure. That young tiger beastman claiming to be from the "Special Agent Bureau."

"You're Brother Hu?" Lei Yanhuan walked into the room, his gaze sweeping over the card game on the table, "Yellow tiger, G-rank grunt, local emperor of this outpost for three years. Bullies newbies, extorts money, occasionally sells some intel to outside thugs."

Brother Hu didn't deny it. His claws clenched into fists, muscles bulging beneath his uniform.

"You've done your homework," he said, his voice low, "But do you know who I am?"

"Aren't you just a tiger wearing green skin?" Lei Yanhuan laughed, "Like me, we're both tiger beastmen. But you chose this path, I chose that one."

"Cut the bullshit with me," Brother Hu suddenly exploded, "I've lived thirty-five years and never feared anyone!"

He lunged forward.

Brother Hu could indeed fight. At this remote outpost, his ability to call himself king wasn't due to connections, but real fists. His speed was fast, his strength great, his claws as sharp as a real tiger's.

But Lei Yanhuan was faster.

With the Scorching Flow Hidden Force circulating within his body, Lei Yanhuan's senses were pushed to their limit. He could see every trajectory of Brother Hu's movements, could predict every angle of attack.

He sidestepped Brother Hu's lunge, striking a palm against the other's waist.

"Agh!" Brother Hu let out a muffled groan, his body staggering. He felt a scorching heat pour into his waist from the side, but quickly suppressed it with his willpower.

"Interesting," Lei Yanhuan nodded in approval, "Being able to withstand the first strike shows your foundation is solid."

"Less nonsense!" Brother Hu roared as he lunged again.

This time, Lei Yanhuan didn't dodge.

Essence gauge skyrocketing—50%... 80%... 100%... 120%...

Dead.

Lei Yanhuan looked at this corpse, silent for a while.

**Scene-8 Laifu's Release**

This was the last one today. Five Beastshield grunts, all eliminated.

Mission completed.

But why was there no sense of accomplishment?

**Scene-9 The Silence After Battle**

The warehouse fell silent.

Five corpses lay scattered across the ground, the air thick with the heavy fishy stench of semen, mixed with sweat, urine, and the body odor of fear. Olive-green uniforms were soaked with various fluids, clinging tightly to the fat-over-muscle bodies of the dead.

Lei Yanhuan stood among the corpses, the red glow on his body gradually fading. He looked down at his hands—those two claws that had just taken five lives, now returned to normal temperature.

He walked toward that worn wooden table, looking at the scattered card game. Brother Hu's cards—three kings, indeed a good hand. Laifu's cards—a pair of threes, destined to lose.

This was an unfair game, unfair from the very beginning.

Lei Yanhuan reached out, sweeping all the cards on the table to the ground.

"Control..." he said softly, "Still need practice."

He remembered the Commander-in-Chief's words: "The Scorching Flow Hidden Force is a double-edged sword. It can let you kill in one strike, but it can also make you addicted to it, degenerating into a beast of slaughter. A true strong one understands the measure of controlling power."

Today, he had killed five Beastshield grunts.

Dazhuang—that wild boar who just wanted to get by, who wanted to go home and see his parents.

Fatty Xiong—that lazy but loyal brown bear, who in the end betrayed his friend.

Greymane—that cunning accomplice, who until death relied on his boss.

Brother Hu—that villain with dignity, who fought until the final moment.

Laifu—that young person who took the wrong path, tricked, exploited, and died without understanding why.

Five grunts, five lives, five ways to die.

Lei Yanhuan sighed, pulling out his communicator from his pocket.

"Mission complete. Five G-ranks, confirmed kills. Outpost cleanup finished."

He paused, then added: "I... still need more control training."

A light laugh came from the other end of the communicator: "Very good, Yanhuan. You've already improved greatly since your graduation internship. Come back. There's a new mission tomorrow."

Lei Yanhuan turned off the communicator, taking one last look at this chaotic warehouse.

Moonlight spilled through the broken window, shining on the five corpses. Their white socks gleamed faintly in the darkness, the numbers on the soles blurred, like some forgotten code.

But Lei Yanhuan remembered their names.

Dazhuang. Fatty Xiong. Greymane. Brother Hu. Laifu.

They weren't just numbers. They were people. People who took the wrong path, people who did bad things, but ultimately—people.

Lei Yanhuan turned and left, his crimson figure vanishing into the night.

The warehouse returned to silence, only the mixture of semen and urine slowly seeping into the ground through the cracks in the floor.

### Story Reflections

This story shows another side of the Beastshield worldview—not the cold hunting of bounty hunters, but the "justice enforcement" of Special Agent Bureau members.

But the word "justice" appears so ambiguous in this story. The five Beastshield grunts Lei Yanhuan killed—what's the difference from the Zhuya killed by bounty hunters in "The Night Watch"? They're all bottom-level employees, all small people who put on green skin just to earn a mouthful of food.

The difference is, bounty hunters do it for money, while Lei Yanhuan does it for "justice." But death is the same—semen ejaculated to the last, life flowing away, numbers forgotten.

But this time, I tried to make these five "numbers" into "people."

Dazhuang was a homesick person, with simple friendship and vague expectations for the future. Fatty Xiong was a coward, but his cowardice came from fear rather than nature. Greymane was an accomplice, cunning but weak. Brother Hu was a villain, but with dignity. Laifu was a victim, tricked, exploited, and died without understanding why.

They all had their own stories, their own choices, their own endings.

Lei Yanhuan's struggle is quite interesting. He possesses power (Scorching Flow Hidden Force), but he's still learning control. He felt some respect from Brother Hu—though the other was an enemy, though the other was a villain. This complex emotion is something that simple "justice enforcement" cannot explain.

He remembered their names. This is what makes him different from those cold bounty hunters. He killed them, but he acknowledged they were people.

In this world of only male beastmen, power is the only language. But how you use power determines whether you are a person, or a beast.

Lei Yanhuan is still growing. He realized his own problem; this is the first step. But whether he can truly control the beast inside his heart depends on future cultivation.

As for those five dead Beastshield grunts... their stories are just five footnotes among countless tragedies in this world.

Dazhuang, Fatty Xiong, Greymane, Brother Hu, Laifu. Five names, five lives, five unfulfilled dreams.

And in some archive room, their numbers have already been replaced by new numbers. Beastshield's giant trash can never runs out of stock.
