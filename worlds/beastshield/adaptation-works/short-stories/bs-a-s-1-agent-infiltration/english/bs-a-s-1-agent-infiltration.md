> *The Infiltrator's Flame*
> Young agent **Lei Yanhuan** of the **Special Agent Bureau** is ordered to infiltrate a remote **Beastshield** warehouse. For this red tiger beastman who has mastered the **Scorching Flow Hidden Force**, this is not merely a mission, but a trial of power, restraint, and growth.

**Scene-1 Waiting Beneath the Moon**

Night hung over this desolate wilderness like a sheet of ink-soaked cloth, heavy and suffocating.

Lei Yanhuan crouched in the bushes outside the warehouse, his crimson fur gleaming dully in the moonlight. He was lean and well-built, with smooth, powerful muscle lines—a typical tiger combat physique. At the moment he wore only a pair of black tactical shorts, his upper body bare, letting the night wind brush across the full muscles of his chest and abdomen.

His mission was to eliminate the guards at this remote warehouse—five G-rank grunts. For someone who had already passed his graduation internship, this should have been a simple cleanup task.

But the Commander-in-Chief had specifically warned him: "Don't be careless. Remote outposts often have traps of complacency that make you underestimate them."

Lei Yanhuan licked his lips, revealing two sharp tiger fangs. His eyes glowed faintly in the darkness—a sign that the Scorching Flow Hidden Force was circulating within his body. He raised his right hand, palm upward, and channeled his inner strength—a trace of barely perceptible red light flowed beneath his skin, as if molten lava were surging through his veins.

"Control," he whispered to himself. "Today's task is cleanup, not slaughter."

He could already sense the auras of the beastmen inside the warehouse—the smell of sweat, tobacco, and that particular odor unique to low-level employees, a mixture of fear and numbness. Five of them, all grunts, wearing olive-green uniforms.

Lei Yanhuan took a deep breath. The air carried the chill of late autumn, with the bitter scent of decaying leaves. He stood up, his muscles sliding slowly beneath his skin, like a predator about to hunt.

**Scene-2 First Blood**

Outside the warehouse gate, a **wild boar beastman** was leaning against the wall smoking.

His name was **G-533**, his number printed on the sole of the white sock on his left foot, already blurred by sweat. He was a typical fat-over-muscle wild boar—beneath a thick layer of fat lay muscles built up by years of labor, his chest muscles slightly swollen due to hermaphroditism, pushing out a full curve beneath the olive-green tight uniform. His belly was not small either, round and protruding against the abdominal area of the uniform.

G-533 yawned, his pig snout exhaling a turbid breath. This place was remote; it had been two months since any bounty hunter had visited. He was beginning to think those rumors about the "Special Agent Bureau" were nothing more than scare stories.

"Maybe I can get a good night's sleep tonight," he muttered to himself, tossing the cigarette butt on the ground and extinguishing it with his thick foot clad in white long socks.

Just then, he smelled something.

Not the smell of smoke, not the smell of sweat, but something... hot. Like magma, like burning metal, carrying a dangerous, trembling masculine hormonal scent.

Before he could turn around, a crimson claw had pressed against the back of his neck.

"Uncle," a young voice rang in his ear, carrying a teasing smile, "still smoking so late, aren't you afraid of black lungs?"

G-533 went rigid. He wanted to shout, but that claw's temperature was astonishingly high, scorching his throat tight. He could feel the body heat of the beastman behind him—like a red-hot branding iron, the burning sensation could be felt even through the air.

"Who... who are you..." G-533 squeezed out a few words through his teeth.

"Me?" The voice laughed. "Just a passing intern."

Before the words had faded, G-533 felt a scorching heat pour into his body from the back of his neck. It wasn't ordinary pain, but something... like magma, surging from his spine all the way down, straight to his groin.

"Agh...!" He let out a scream, his legs involuntarily clamping together.

**Scene-3 The Terror of Scorching Flow**

G-533's face was already flushed red, his pig snout exhaling rough breaths, and the thick, short pig whip between his legs was erecting at a visible speed, pitching an embarrassing tent in his olive-green shorts.

"What... what is this..." G-533 looked at his own lower body in horror. He could feel his essence gauge skyrocketing—30%... 50%... 70%...

Not pleasure, but fear, a physiological reaction forcibly driven by some incomprehensible power.

"Scorching Flow Hidden Force," Lei Yanhuan said calmly. "Inner strength transformed into scorching heat, rushing straight to the essence gate. What you feel is magma flowing through your veins."

He raised his hand, palm facing G-533's chest. In the moonlight, one could see his palm glowing with a faint red light, like red-hot charcoal.

"Please... please..." G-533 knelt down, the pig whip between his legs already hard and aching, the horse-eye opening secreting transparent fluid that ran down the shaft, "I... I'm just a gatekeeper..."

"I know," Lei Yanhuan said, his eyes holding neither pity nor cruelty, only a near-cold calmness. "But today, your number is on the list."

He stepped forward, pressing his palm against G-533's chest. Beneath that thick layer of fat beat a heart; he could feel the wild boar beastman's violent heartbeat, fear making that heart nearly burst through his ribcage.

Scorching Flow Hidden Force, Second Level.

**Scene-4 Awakening Inside the Warehouse**

On the bed in the inner room of the warehouse, a **brown bear beastman** suddenly opened his eyes.

His name was **G-534**, the second guard on duty tonight. He should have been sleeping, but that scream just now—though weakened by the night wind—had still reached his ears.

"G-533?" He sat up, the thick chest muscles beneath his olive-green uniform rising and falling with his breath. He was fat and heavy, with more fat than muscle, but his strength was not to be underestimated.

No response.

G-534 rolled off the bed, his feet in white long socks stepping onto the concrete floor. He could smell a strange odor drifting in from outside—the fishy, musky smell of semen, and something... hot, like scorched metal.

He grabbed the baton leaning beside the bed and tiptoed toward the door.

Beneath the door crack, a pool of liquid was slowly seeping in. In the dim light, the liquid had a milky luster, with a viscous texture.

G-534's heartbeat began to accelerate.

He pushed the door open—

A crimson fist came straight at him.

**Scene-5 Desperate Struggle**

Lei Yanhuan's fist hit G-534 square in the abdomen.

That was no ordinary punch. The Scorching Flow Hidden Force surged in at the moment of contact, like a ball of fire exploding in the brown bear's abdominal cavity. G-534 let out a muffled groan, his massive body bending like a shrimp, the baton slipping from his hand and falling to the ground with a "clank."

"Hello there, Uncle," Lei Yanhuan's voice still carried that teasing smile. "Did I wake you?"

G-534 wanted to answer, but the scorching inner strength was already rampaging through his body. He felt his essence gauge skyrocketing—20%... 40%... 60%...

"You... you're from the Special Agent Bureau..." G-534 squeezed out a few words through his teeth, his legs beginning to tremble. He could feel his bear whip erecting, that forcibly triggered physiological reaction filling him with both fear and shame.

"Smart," Lei Yanhuan nodded approvingly, then threw another punch.

This punch hit G-534's waist. The Scorching Flow Hidden Force penetrated the fat layer, striking directly at his kidneys. G-534 let out a shrill scream, his massive body crashing into the door frame with a "thud," his olive-green uniform instantly soaked with sweat.

Essence gauge: 80%... 90%... 100%...

"No... don't..." G-534 knelt down, both hands covering his crotch, but unable to stop that torrent. His bear whip was jumping frantically inside the tight pants, as if trying to break through the fabric.

"Scared?" Lei Yanhuan squatted down, meeting him at eye level. Those red-glowing eyes held a calmness beyond his years. "You should be."

He reached out, pressing his palm against G-534's chest. Beneath the thick fat layer was a violently beating heart.

"Let me tell you a secret," Lei Yanhuan whispered. "I could have finished you all with one punch each today. But the Commander said to learn control. So—"

He paused, his palm exerting slight pressure.

"I'll let you die a little slower."

**Scene-6 Slow Withering**

G-534 felt that scorching heat was no longer a chaotic flame, but had become a thin thread, wandering inside his body.

It slid from his chest to his abdomen, from his abdomen to his groin, then circled at the root of his bear whip.

"Ah... ah..." G-534's breathing grew rapid, his thick bear claws scratching the ground, leaving white marks. He could feel his essence gauge being precisely maintained at the 100% critical point—not an instant explosion, but forcibly maintained at the edge of ejaculation.

"This is the exquisite subtlety of the Scorching Flow Hidden Force," Lei Yanhuan explained, as if giving a lecture. "I can make you die from shooting your load in one second, or I can keep you in this state for ten minutes."

Tears welled up in G-534's eyes. That feeling of being maintained at the edge of climax was more terrifying than direct death. His body was desperately craving release, but that crimson claw firmly controlled his essence gate.

"Please... please give me a quick death..." he begged.

Lei Yanhuan tilted his head, as if thinking.

"A quick death?" He repeated the word. "You people from Beastshield, are you even worthy of talking about a quick death?"

He stood up, looking down at this tear-streaked brown bear. Moonlight poured in through the broken warehouse window, illuminating G-534's protruding belly and erect crotch, forming an absurd tableau.

"But you're right," Lei Yanhuan sighed. "There are still three more today. Can't waste too much time on you."

He raised his foot, the sole of his boot pressing against G-534's erect bear whip.

"Goodbye, Uncle."

The boot sole pressed down hard.

G-534 let out a heart-rending scream, his essence gauge instantly breaking through the critical point. The bear whip twitched frantically beneath the boot sole, semen shooting out spurt after spurt, soaking the olive-green shorts, running down his thighs, drenching the white long socks.

First spurt... third spurt... fifth spurt... seventh spurt...

Lei Yanhuan counted, feeling the rhythmic pulsation through the boot sole. The brown bear's body spasmed beneath his foot, thick limbs flailing wildly, as if dancing some absurd dance.

Ninth spurt... eleventh spurt... thirteenth spurt...

Finally, the twitching stopped.

G-534's body went limp on the ground, his head tilted to one side, his eyes unfocused. His bear whip was still twitching slightly, the horse-eye opening holding one last drop of semen, gleaming with a lascivious luster in the moonlight.

Dead.

Number G-534, brown bear beastman, died after shooting thirteen spurts of semen.

**Scene-7 The Remaining Three**

On the second floor of the warehouse, three beastmen were awakened by the screams.

A **wolf beastman**, a **dog beastman**, a **tiger beastman**—all G-rank grunts, wearing the same olive-green uniforms, the same white long socks.

"What's going on?" The tiger beastman grabbed the baton beside his bed, his voice trembling slightly.

"Down... down there... something..." The dog beastman leaned over the railing, looking down—

He only saw darkness, and in that darkness, a pair of red-glowing eyes.

"Found you," a young voice came from below. "Uncles."

The next second, a crimson figure leaped from the shadows, like a ball of burning flame.

**Scene-8 The Dance of Flames**

Lei Yanhuan didn't give them time to react.

The Scorching Flow Hidden Force operated at full power within his body. His eyes had completely turned red, his crimson fur blazing like flames in the moonlight. First punch, hitting the tiger beastman's chest; second punch, hitting the wolf beastman's waist; third punch—

The dog beastman tried to run, but Lei Yanhuan was too fast.

That crimson claw grabbed the back of his neck, inner strength pouring in, instantly destroying his ability to resist.

"Don't... don't kill me..." The dog beastman knelt down, urine flowing from his shorts, leaving dark stains on the olive-green fabric.

Lei Yanhuan looked at him, something flashing in those red eyes.

"Are you afraid?" he asked.

"Afraid... afraid..." The dog beastman trembled and nodded.

"Good," Lei Yanhuan said. "Remember this fear. In your next life, don't wear this green skin."

He raised his hand, pressing his palm against the dog beastman's forehead.

Scorching Flow Hidden Force, maximum power.

The dog beastman didn't even have time to scream, his essence gauge instantly skyrocketing to 150%. His dog whip jumped frantically inside the tight pants, and then—the ejaculation began.

Spurt after spurt, like some cruel counting. Five... eight... eleven... fourteen...

Lei Yanhuan counted, feeling the life of the dog beastman beneath his palm draining away. This feeling was wonderful—he held the other's life and death in his hands, not through violence, but through an almost artistic way, guiding the other toward climax and death.

This was the essence of the Scorching Flow Hidden Force: using heat as the medium, using essence as the guide, letting the enemy perish in ultimate pleasure.

Seventeenth spurt... eighteenth spurt...

The dog beastman's body finally went soft, like a boneless rag doll, collapsing on the ground. His eyes were still open, pupils unfocused, the corner of his mouth even carrying a trace of a strange smile—that was the trace left by the moment when death and climax overlapped.

Dead.

Number G-536, dog beastman, died after shooting eighteen spurts of semen.

**Scene-9 The Silence After Battle**

The warehouse quieted down.

Five corpses lay scattered on the ground, the air thick with the fishy, musky smell of semen, mixed with sweat, urine, and the body odor of fear. The olive-green uniforms were soaked with various liquids, clinging tightly to the dead fat-over-muscle bodies.

Lei Yanhuan stood among the corpses, the red glow on his body gradually fading. He looked down at his hands—those two claws that had just taken five lives, now returned to normal temperature.

"Control..." he whispered. "Still need practice."

He remembered the Commander-in-Chief's words: "The Scorching Flow Hidden Force is a double-edged sword. It can let you kill with one strike, but it can also make you addicted to it, degenerating into a beast of slaughter. A true strongman understands the measure of controlling power."

Today, he had killed five Beastshield grunts. But he hadn't tortured them to death, just... spent a little more time.

Lei Yanhuan sighed, pulling out his communicator from his pocket.

"Mission complete. Five G-rank, confirmed kills. Numbers G-533 to G-537."

He paused, then added: "I... still need more control training."

A light laugh came from the other end of the communicator: "Very good, Yanhuan. You've improved a lot since your graduation internship. Come back, there's a new mission tomorrow."

Lei Yanhuan turned off the communicator, taking one last look at this messy warehouse.

Moonlight poured in through the broken window, illuminating the five corpses. Their white socks gleamed faintly in the darkness, the numbers on their soles blurred, like some forgotten code.

Lei Yanhuan turned and left, his crimson figure disappearing into the night.

The warehouse returned to silence, only the mixed liquids of semen and urine slowly seeping into the ground through the cracks in the floor.

### Their Final Stories

【Living → Dead - G-533 - Wild Boar Beastman - Zhu Dazhuang】

His name was Zhu Dazhuang, number G-533. A black-brown wild boar beastman, with a typical fat-over-muscle build—a thick layer of fat over muscles built up by years of moving goods, chest muscles slightly swollen due to hermaphroditism, pushing out a full curve beneath the olive-green tight uniform. He died, killed by a red tiger beastman using some kind of "scorching inner strength." He felt magma flowing through his veins, felt his pig whip erecting and shooting uncontrollably. Ten spurts of semen took away a part of the life he had accumulated over thirty-one years.

### Story Reflections

This story shows another side of the Beastshield worldview—not the cold hunting of bounty hunters, but the "justice execution" of Special Agent Bureau members.

But the word "justice" seems so ambiguous in this story. What is the difference between the five Beastshield grunts Lei Yanhuan killed and Shi Ya, killed by bounty hunters in "The Night Watch"? They are all bottom-level employees, all small people who put on the green skin just to earn a mouthful of food.

The difference is that bounty hunters do it for money, while Lei Yanhuan does it for "justice." But death is the same—semen spent, life flowing away, numbers forgotten.

Lei Yanhuan's struggle is quite interesting. He possesses power (Scorching Flow Hidden Force), but he is still learning to control it. His "slow kill" of G-534 and his "maximum power" on G-536 both expose some dark impulse deep in his heart—the fascination with power, the disrespect for life.

The Commander-in-Chief knows this. So he gave Lei Yanhuan this mission, not to test his strength, but to test his temperament.

In this world of only male beastmen, power is the only language. But how to use power determines whether you are a man or a beast.

Lei Yanhuan is still growing. He has recognized his own problem; this is the first step. But whether he can truly control the beast inside depends on future cultivation.

As for those five dead Beastshield grunts... their stories are just five footnotes among countless tragedies in this world.

Numbers G-533, G-534, G-536. Three numbers, three lives, three unfulfilled dreams.

And in some archive room, these numbers have already been replaced by new ones. Beastshield's giant trash can never runs out of stock.
