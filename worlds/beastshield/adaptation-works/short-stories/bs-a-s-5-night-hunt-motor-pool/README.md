## 一、简介 / Introduction

**一句话钩子 / One-Line Hook**：灰狼赏金猎人夜袭兽盾车辆调配场。六名夜班兽盾兵，六种死法，一个文件夹。

**故事概要 / Synopsis**：

深夜，灰狼赏金猎人翻进兽盾车辆调配场。目标：二楼调度办公室的 D-1147 号文件夹。

调配场有六名夜班人员——门岗棕熊在刷交友软件、水牛机修工在车底哼歌、野猪清洁工在走廊拖地、黑熊巡逻兵在例行巡视、白虎休息兵刚泡好一碗面、黄狗调度员刚整理完档案。

六个人，六种死法，一个接一个安静地"睡着"。

任务完成。灯还亮着。

A gray wolf bounty hunter infiltrates a BeastShield vehicle depot at night. His target: folder D-1147 in the second-floor dispatch office.
The depot has six night shift staff — a brown bear guard scrolling a dating app, a water buffalo mechanic humming under a truck, a boar janitor mopping the hallway, a black bear patrolman on rounds, a white tiger soldier making instant noodles, and a yellow dog dispatcher who just finished organizing his files.
Six men, six deaths, each one quietly "falling asleep."
Mission complete. The lights are still on.
