## 一、简介 / Introduction

**一句话钩子 / One-Line Hook**：一只黑制服的棕虎兽人走进早饭摊，摊子就静了。他去后头上了个厕所。门被踹响了。

**故事概要 / Synopsis**：

棕虎兽人一进门，摊子就静了。靠门那桌的柴狗兽人端着没吃完的半碗面走了。摊主知道下多少面、搁不搁葱——这只虎兽人从来不开口，摊主也从来没问过。

他吃到一半，去后头的厕所。

门被踹响。一个小孩在骂，嗓门还没变声。他蹲在里面，等自己上火。火没上来。

他试着想一句话。想出来的两句——「干什么的」「出去」——全是命令。这几年从这张嘴里出去的话，就剩命令了。

小孩骂完走了。他出来，那小孩就坐在隔壁桌。灰毛的一只羊兽人，个子那么小。抬头看了他一眼，然后就动不了了。

他坐回去，把面吃完。隔壁那小孩一动不动，在等一句问话、一个手势、等人过来把他拽起来。

什么都没来。

他站起来，走到灶台边，把两张钱按在台面上，下巴朝隔壁那张桌抬了一下。摊主看懂了，没问，端了一碗新面过去。

小孩没伸手。

A brown tiger in a black BeastShield uniform walks into a street stall and the talking stops. A dog at the front table picks up his half-finished bowl and leaves. The cook already knows his order — the tiger has never spoken a word here, and the cook has never asked.

Halfway through his noodles he goes to the back toilet.

Someone starts kicking the door. A kid, voice not yet broken, swearing. Inside, the tiger waits to get angry. It doesn't come.

He tries to form a sentence. The two he comes up with — "What do you want." "Get out." — are both orders. Orders are all this mouth has produced for years.

The kid leaves. The tiger comes out. The kid is at the next table — a small gray sheep, wool at the collar. He looks up once and then can't move.

The tiger sits back down and finishes his noodles. The kid sits frozen, waiting for a question, a gesture, a hand dragging him out of his chair.

Nothing comes.

He gets up, puts two bills on the counter, tilts his chin at the next table. The cook understands, doesn't ask, and carries over a fresh bowl.

The kid doesn't reach for it.
