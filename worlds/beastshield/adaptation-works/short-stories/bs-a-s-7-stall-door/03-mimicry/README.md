## 一、简介 / Introduction

**一句话钩子 / One-Line Hook**：一只蓝制服的监工蹲在隔间里，门外那个小孩骂的话，是他自己的话。

**故事概要 / Synopsis**：

清晨，早饭摊。蓝制服的杜宾兽人吃到一半，去了后头的厕所。

门被踹响。一个小孩在骂，嗓门还没变声。杜宾兽人蹲在里面听着——那几句话他今天早上刚说过，就在营区门口，冲着迟到的绿制服说的。一样的腔调，一样的断句，一样的字眼。

他一点火都上不来。

他天天说这些。这些话从他嘴里出去的时候，听的人会站直，会把手里的东西放下，会低头看鞋。现在它们从门缝底下钻进来，就什么都不是了。就几声响。

小孩踹完走了。他出来，那小孩正站在摊子中间——灰毛的一只鹿兽人小崽，个子才到大人膝盖往上一点。

小孩的生父在门边。看清楚那身蓝制服之后，脸就白了。他两步跨过去把小孩拽到身后，弯腰，一口一个长官，越说越快：那几句话是跟着巷口那块屏学的，天天站底下看，学了一嘴，打过，打了也不改。

然后他说：“我本来要带他去的。他说他会上。他非说自己一个人会上——”

小孩从他胳膊底下探出脑袋，又喊了一遍。喊得挺响，尾音还往上挑了一下。

生父一把捂住他的嘴。

杜宾兽人看着那小孩。小孩两只手还是湿的——他刚才在里头自己洗过手，湿手在衣服前襟上蹭过，蹭出一片深色的印子。

他自己洗的手。

杜宾兽人什么也没说，走了。

A Doberman in a blue BeastShield uniform sits at a street stall. Halfway through his bowl he goes to the back toilet.

Someone kicks the door. A kid, voice not yet broken, swearing. The Doberman listens — and the sentences are his own. He shouted those exact words this morning at the compound gate, at a grunt who showed up late. Same cadence, same breaks, same words.

He can't get angry.

He says these things daily. When they come out of his mouth, people stand up straight, put down what they're holding, look at their own shoes. Coming in under the door, they're nothing. Just noise.

The kid kicks twice more and leaves. The Doberman comes out. The kid is standing in the middle of the stall — a small gray deer, knee-high.

The kid's father is by the door. When he sees the blue uniform, his face goes white. He drags the kid behind him, bows, says "sir" over and over, talking faster and faster: the words came off the screen at the mouth of the alley, the kid stands under it every day and picked them up, they beat him for it, he didn't stop.

Then: "I was going to take him to the toilet. He said he could go himself. He insisted he could go alone—"

The kid pokes his head out from under his father's arm and shouts it again. Loud this time, with a little lift at the end.

The father clamps a hand over his mouth.

The Doberman looks at the kid. The kid's hands are still wet — he washed them himself in there, and wiped them on his own shirt front, leaving a dark smear.

He washed his own hands.

The Doberman says nothing and leaves.
