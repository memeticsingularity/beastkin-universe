## Scene-1 Medicine and Panting

Chen Mo didn't like the smell of the underground fighting arena.

Not because it was unpleasant — although it was indeed filled with the sour-salty reek of evaporated sweat, the rusty sweetness of dried blood, the burnt smell of muscles rubbing against leather, the pungent chemical fragrance of stimulant spray, and the turbid odor of cheap tobacco, alcohol, and frenzied hormones lingering in the spectator seats. He wore a dark blue, slightly worn one-piece work uniform, with a forged subcontractor badge of Beastshield hanging on his chest, and carried a half-old silver metal toolbox in his hand.
