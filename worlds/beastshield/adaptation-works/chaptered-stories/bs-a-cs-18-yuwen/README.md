## 一、简介 / Introduction

**一句话钩子**：一场一夜之间被清空的基地，尸体在失去体温后仍被高潮余韵钉在原地——余温散尽之前，没有人真正死透。

赏金猎人炎与魉夜袭兽盾新兵训练基地。从巡逻的虎守卫到会议室里的犀牛组长，名单上的名字被一个个划去；炎以灼流暗劲令猎手在极致快感中殒命，魉则以隐身能力负责记录编号、处理善后。但当整座基地被屠尽之后，真正的转折才刚刚开始——那些本该死透的兽盾兵却在急救室的无菌单上，用仅存的节律继续回应着被强加的欲望。三个月后，获救的幸存者被重编为一支"特殊适应性编队"，却要为一场自己一无所知的袭杀再度走进废弃厂房。

本作为改编自原作 `bs-o-cs-1-yan-liang`（炎魉）的独立篇章。与 `bs-a-cs-4-yanliang`（memeticsingularity 版）为同一原作的不同改编版本；本作在完整前段基础上，新增了"余温"（濒死急救与意识回望）与"三个月后新编队"两大延长线。

---

**One-line Hook**: In a base emptied overnight, corpses pinned by the afterglow of climax refuse to fully die before the residual warmth fades.

Bounty hunters Yan and Liang raid a Beastshield recruit training base at night. From the patrolling tiger guard to the rhino squad leader in the meeting room, names on the list are crossed out one by one—Yan ends lives with the Scorching Dark Force, Liang records numbers and cleans up with his invisibility. But when the base lies fully murdered, the real turn begins: those who should be dead keep answering to forced desire on the operating table. Three months later, the rescued survivors are re-formed into a "special adaptation unit," only to be led back into an abandoned plant for a raid they know nothing about.

Adapted from the original `bs-o-cs-1-yan-liang` (Yanliang). A separate adaptation from `bs-a-cs-4-yanliang` (memeticsingularity edition); this work adds two extended threads — "Residual Warmth" (near-death rescue and the gaze of consciousness) and "Three Months Later: New Unit."
