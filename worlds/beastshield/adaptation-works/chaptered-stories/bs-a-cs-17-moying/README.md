## 一、简介 / Introduction

**一句话钩子**：他以黑暗为皮肤，以恐惧为武器，在兽盾的缝隙中狩猎那些迷失在灯光下的猎物。

雨禾执笔的魔影改编版。赏金猎人**魔影**以兽盾大楼为猎场，从一楼更衣室开始，层层向上狩猎。而在大楼之外，蟒蛇岛的密林深处，埋藏着魔影获得特殊能力的起源秘密……

### 版本说明

本作基于原作魔影角色进行改编创作，与 `bs-a-cm-2-moying`（memeticsingularity 版）为同一原作的不同改编版本。

---

**One-line Hook**: He wears darkness as skin and fear as weapon, hunting prey lost in the light within the cracks of Beastshield.

Yuhe's adaptation of Moying. Bounty hunter **Moying** takes the Beastshield building as his hunting ground, moving upward floor by floor from the first-floor locker room. Beyond the building, deep in the jungles of Python Island, lies the secret origin of Moying's special abilities...
