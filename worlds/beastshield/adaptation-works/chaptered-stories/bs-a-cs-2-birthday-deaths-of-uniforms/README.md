## 一、简介 / Introduction

**一句话钩子**：生日本该是庆祝生命的日子，但在兽盾，它往往成为生命的终点。

这是一个以"生日"为线索的黑暗系列，讲述不同兽盾员工在生日当天遭遇的致命命运。每章聚焦一个独立的员工，通过生日这一特殊时刻，展现他们在兽盾世界中的卑微存在、破碎愿望与不可避免的死亡。系列深度挖掘官方设定中的日常暴力，赋予每个编号以血肉与灵魂。

---

**One-line Hook**: Birthdays should be days to celebrate life, but in Beastshield, they often become life's endpoint.

A dark series centered around the theme of "birthdays," telling the fatal fates of different Beastshield employees on their birthdays. Each chapter focuses on an independent employee, using the special moment of their birthday to reveal their humble existence, shattered wishes, and inevitable deaths in the Beastshield world. The series deeply explores the everyday violence within official settings, endowing each serial number with flesh, blood, and soul.
