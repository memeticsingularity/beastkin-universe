> *When seven people step onto the battlefield, when blood stains the corridors, when life and death hang by a single thread—this is war, cruel and merciless.*

---

**Scene 1: The Main Entrance**

Lao Ba stood before Beastshield's main entrance, carrying the short blade he had used for twenty years.

Morning sunlight shone on his silver-white fur, gilding him with a golden edge. But Lao Ba couldn't feel the sun's warmth. His entire attention was focused on that heavy metal door—it was made of titanium alloy, twenty centimeters thick, its surface covered with defensive runes and spiritual shielding devices.

He looked at that door, recalling many years ago—when he was still young, still naive, still believing this world would become better. Back then, he too had once stood before a door, thinking that as long as he pushed it open, he would find justice.

But he was wrong.

"Ready?" Black Claw's voice came from beside him, interrupting his memories.

That one-eyed black panther stood in the shadows, his black fur gleaming with a dark blue luster in the morning light. His muscles trembled slightly beneath thin fur, like a predator ready to pounce at any moment. The air was filled with a tense scent—sweat, adrenaline, and some kind of... anticipation.

Lao Ba didn't answer. He only took a deep breath. The air held the distinctive smell of a city morning—the freshness of dew, the fried food aroma drifting from a distant breakfast stall, and... the foreboding of blood.

Then he—

"Boom!"

Iron Heart pressed the detonator button. In that instant, Chen Mo felt the air vibrate, the sound wave like a hammer, smashing hard against his eardrums. The main entrance twisted and deformed in the explosion, metal emitting a piercing scream, finally collapsing, raising a cloud of dust and debris.

"For A Hua," Lao Ba said softly, his voice so low only he could hear it. Then he charged in, like a moving hill, every step making the ground tremble slightly.

Alarm bells instantly rang throughout the entire facility. That sound was sharp and piercing, like nails scraping a blackboard, making one's teeth ache.

---

**Scene 2: The Ventilation Shaft**

Meanwhile, in the ventilation shaft, Chen Mo and Bai Ya were crawling rapidly.

The pipe was narrow, only wide enough for one person. The metal walls pressed close against Chen Mo's fur, transmitting a cold sensation. He could smell the accumulated scents of many years in the pipe—the dryness of dust, the metallic sweetness of rust, and some kind of... rotting organic smell. That was the distinctive odor of long-unventilated underground spaces, suffocating.

Chen Mo was in front, Bai Ya behind. Their movements were light, fast, like two snakes gliding through darkness. The metal walls made faint scraping sounds under Chen Mo's claw tips, the sound amplified in the narrow pipe, like some creature's heartbeat.

"How much farther?" Chen Mo asked via spiritual power, his spiritual perception spreading in the darkness like an invisible net.

"Three hundred meters," Bai Ya answered, his spiritual fluctuations steady and clear. "Directly below the Core Data Room."

Chen Mo nodded, continuing forward. His knees and elbows rubbed against the metal walls, bringing a stinging pain. The temperature in the pipe was higher than outside; sweat began to bead on his forehead, soaking his fur. That mixture of fear and tension, the male scent, filled the narrow pipe, mixing with Bai Ya's dry herbal fragrance.

His spiritual perception was fully open, able to "see" everything around him—wires in the walls like glowing snakes, airflow in the pipes like transparent waves, and... increasingly close, powerful spiritual fluctuations.

Those fluctuations were like burning flames, some scorching hot, some ice-cold, some violent, some sinister. They gathered together, forming a sea of spirit, surging around the Core Data Room.

"Someone's waiting for us," Chen Mo said, his voice trembling slightly in the spiritual connection. "Around the Core Data Room, at least ten spiritual power users. And... more people, ordinary people, but fully armed."

"A trap," Bai Ya said, his voice calm. "Just as we expected."

"Then do we still continue?"

"Of course." Bai Ya's voice carried a hint of a smile, that smile which put Chen Mo at ease. "Plans are made to be broken."

Chen Mo took a deep breath, the air full of the smell of metal and dust. He continued crawling forward, every movement careful, afraid of making too much noise.

---

**Scene 3: Chaos**

At the main entrance, the battle was already white-hot.

Lao Ba's short blade danced in his hand, every swing taking a life. That wasn't an ordinary blade—the blade was covered in runes, emitting a faint blue light in battle, like something alive. The blade sliced through the air, making a whistling sound, then cut into flesh, bringing a dull tearing noise.

Blood. Blood everywhere.

Red liquid flowed on the floor, forming streams. He could smell that strong rusty scent, mixed with the stench of internal organs and the urine smell after bladder rupture. That was the scent of death, the taste of war.

Lao Ba wasn't killing people; he was—dancing. Dancing with death, a skill he had learned many years ago. Though his body was large, his movements were surprisingly agile. His fat-muscled physique displayed astonishing explosive power in battle—the thick fat layer absorbed impact, while the muscle groups beneath provided endless strength.

A Class-G grunt charged up, his military-green uniform especially glaring in the pool of blood. Lao Ba sidestepped, dodging the opponent's thrust, then the short blade sliced across the other's throat. Warm blood sprayed on his face, bringing a wave of metallic sweetness.

Black Claw was more direct. His claws were like the scythe of death, every strike precisely cutting the enemy's throat. He didn't have Lao Ba's elegance, but he had efficiency. That lone eye flashed with cold light, like a precision killing machine.

His movements were too fast to see clearly—a black figure shuttling through the enemy group, leaving behind only bodies clutching their throats as they fell. His body was covered in blood, but it was all others' blood. He was like a black whirlwind; wherever he went, only death remained.

"Left!" Lao Ba shouted, his voice especially resonant in the battle's clamor.

Black Claw turned, one claw tearing open the sneak attacker's chest. That was a young wolf beastkin, his military-green uniform not yet bearing many traces of battle. His eyes were wide open, seemingly unable to believe he was dead just like that. Black Claw looked into his eyes, that lone eye devoid of any emotion.

"Right side too!"

Iron Heart provided fire support from the rear. His mechanical arm could transform into various weapons—gun, cannon, grenade launcher. Metal joints emitted a piercing hum, circuits flashing blue light beneath his skin.

His body was half organic, half metal, giving him both human flexibility and machine precision. But his face was already covered in sweat, that still-unmodified eye flashing with fatigue.

"Damn," Iron Heart cursed, his electronically synthesized voice carrying a hint of anxiety. "Running low on ammo."

Iron Heart's mechanical arm transformed into a small cannon, one blast shattering a wall, blowing the enemies hiding behind it into pieces. Rubble and flesh flew everywhere, hitting the surrounding walls with pitter-patter sounds.

But they were too few in number.

Beastshield's guards poured in endlessly, like there was no end. Class-G grunts wore military-green uniforms, like a herd of driven sheep—weak but numerous. Class-E combatants wore black tight-fitting clothes, muscles bulging beneath fabric, movements agile and deadly. And Class-R gunners, their white uniforms especially conspicuous on the battlefield, weapons in their hands spitting deadly tongues of fire.

Lao Ba felt his stamina draining, his movements slowing. Every blade swing required more strength, every dodge became more difficult. His breathing grew rapid, his lungs feeling like they were burning. Sweat flowed from his forehead into his eyes, bringing a stinging pain.

He could smell the scent on himself—the sourness of sweat, the metallic sweetness of blood, and the putrid scent of wounds beginning to fester. There was a wound on his abdomen, not deep, but bleeding continuously.

"Damn," he cursed, his voice carrying fatigue. "There's too many of them."

"Hold on," Black Claw said, his voice also becoming hoarse from fatigue. "Just delay ten more minutes."

"Ten minutes?" Lao Ba smiled, though the smile carried bitterness, the corner of his mouth still hanging with bloodstains. "I might not even last five."

"Then die here," Black Claw said, that lone eye flashing with something like... approval? "At least die with purpose."

Lao Ba glanced at him.

"You know what?" he said, the short blade once again slicing across someone's throat. "I'm starting to like you."

"Don't disgust me," Black Claw said, then pounced on a new enemy.

---

**Scene 4: The Core**

Chen Mo and Bai Ya finally reached directly below the Core Data Room.

There was a maintenance hatch there, leading to the floor below the data room. Chen Mo sensed with his spiritual power—there were at least twenty people above, including Hu Lin and Tiejiao. Their spiritual fluctuations were like burning flames, especially conspicuous in the darkness.

"They're all up there," Chen Mo said, his voice trembling slightly in the spiritual connection. "Waiting for us."

"Then give them a surprise," Bai Ya said.

He reached out, pressing against the maintenance hatch cover. Chen Mo could feel Bai Ya's spiritual power gathering, like a balloon being constantly inflated, growing larger, more dangerous.

"Get back."

Chen Mo retreated several steps, curling up as much as possible in the pipe's corner. He could feel the metal walls trembling slightly from Bai Ya's spiritual power, emitting a low hum.

Bai Ya closed his eyes, beginning to gather spiritual power. Chen Mo could feel that force expanding, like a bomb about to explode. The air in the pipe seemed to become viscous, breathing difficult.

Then—

"Boom!"

A spiritual shockwave erupted from Bai Ya, directly blasting through the cover, tearing apart the floor above. That sound wasn't as loud as a physical explosion, but more... profound. It was like acting directly on the soul, making Chen Mo's head buzz.

Rubble and dust fell like a gray rain. Chen Mo and Bai Ya leaped out from the hole, landing in the Core Data Room.

In the room, over twenty people were waiting in full battle array. Hu Lin stood at the very front, bandages on his back faintly visible under his white shirt. Tiejiao was beside him, that massive bull beastkin like an iron tower, muscles bulging beneath his black uniform. There were other unfamiliar executives, their uniforms in different colors, representing different ranks and departments.

"Welcome," Hu Lin said, a smile on his face, that smile which made Chen Mo want to vomit. "I've been waiting for you for a long time."

---

**Scene 5: Confrontation**

Chen Mo looked around.

The Core Data Room was larger and more luxurious than he had imagined. The walls were made of some translucent crystal, faint light flowing within. All around were various advanced devices, metal surfaces reflecting cold light. The air was permeated with a special smell—the pungent scent of ozone, mixed with the heat and metallic smell of high-tech equipment in operation.

In the center was a massive crystal pillar, three meters tall, about one meter in diameter. Inside flowed a luminescent liquid—that should be the data storage medium. The liquid displayed deep blue, flowing slowly as if alive, occasionally rippling.

And Hu Lin stood before the crystal pillar, like a venomous snake guarding treasure.

"You knew we would come," Chen Mo said, his voice echoing in the spacious room.

"Of course," Hu Lin said. "From Xiao Hui's memories, I read everything. Your plan, your division of labor, your—weaknesses."
He smiled, that smile carrying triumph and cruelty. "That mouse died very valuably."

Chen Mo's heart sank. He felt a surge of anger rising from his chest, like a fire, scorching his reason.

"Xiao Hui... he's really dead?"

"Dead," Hu Lin said, lightly, as if discussing the death of an ant. "And died very valuably. His memories gave me a lot of useful information. Including your spiritual power characteristics, your tactical preferences, and..."
He looked at Chen Mo, greed in his eyes. "You, little bear. Your spiritual power, after enhancer strengthening, has reached an astonishing level."

Chen Mo felt a wave of nausea. He clenched his fists, claws embedding into his palms, bringing a sharp pain.

"Today, you'll be buried with him."

"Oh?" Hu Lin raised an eyebrow, that hyena's pointed face carrying mockery. "With what? With that bit of newly awakened spiritual power? With that rabbit beside you?"

He spread his arms, as if embracing the entire room.

"Look around, little bear. You have two people; we have twenty. Your spiritual power is strong, but we have numerical superiority, equipment superiority, terrain superiority."
He laughed, that laughter echoing in the room. "You've already lost."

Bai Ya stepped forward. His movement was light but full of power. Chen Mo could feel his spiritual power expanding, like an invisible net, covering the entire room.

"Is that so?" he said, his voice calm but full of threat. "Then why are you still talking? Why not just act?"

Hu Lin's smile froze.

"Because you're afraid," Bai Ya continued, red eyes looking especially demonic in the crystal wall's reflection. "You're afraid we really have a backup plan. You're afraid your plan isn't perfect."

"Shut up."

"You're afraid—" Bai Ya's voice turned ice-cold, like it came from the depths of hell. "Death."

Hu Lin's expression changed. That triumphant smile disappeared, replaced by something like... fear?

"Kill him!"

---

**Scene 6: Battle**

Battle erupted instantly.

Over twenty people charged at Chen Mo and Bai Ya simultaneously. Spiritual power, weapons, concealed weapons—all kinds of attacks blotted out the sky. The air was filled with energy fluctuations, like countless invisible blades dancing.

Bai Ya blocked before Chen Mo, hands waving, a spiritual barrier unfolding before them. The barrier displayed a translucent silver-white, like a massive shield. Attacks struck it, emitting piercing shrieks, then dissipating.

"Release the virus!" he shouted, his voice especially clear in the battle's clamor. "I'll hold them off!"

Chen Mo didn't hesitate. He charged toward the crystal pillar, simultaneously taking out the crystal containing the spiritual virus from his pocket. It flashed with rainbow light in his hand, like a miniature rainbow.

"Stop him!" Hu Lin screamed, his voice carrying despair.

Tiejiao charged up. That massive bull beastkin was like a tank, every step making the ground shake. His muscles bulged beneath his black uniform, like blocks of hard rock. His eyes burned with the desire to kill, nostrils puffing out coarse breaths.

Chen Mo turned, using his spiritual power to replicate—Tiejiao's spiritual characteristics.

This was what he had practiced during training. Everyone's spiritual power had a unique "fingerprint," and Chen Mo could now perfectly replicate Tiejiao's fingerprint. He "saw" Tiejiao's spiritual light-cluster—thick earthy yellow, like an unshakable hill, full of power and... anger?

Chen Mo adjusted his own spiritual power to the same frequency, like a tuner adjusting a string.

Tiejiao was stunned. His attack, about to hit Chen Mo, was blocked by an invisible barrier at the last instant. The spiritual fluctuations flowing on that barrier were exactly the same as his own.

"What—"

"Your spiritual characteristics," Chen Mo said, his voice carrying a trace of fatigue but more firmness. "I've replicated them. Now, the system thinks I'm you."

He turned, aiming the crystal at the crystal pillar. The blue liquid flowing inside seemed to sense something, beginning to tremble slightly.

"No!" Hu Lin let out a desperate scream. "You can't! Do you know what this means? Years of research, billions invested, all will—"

Chen Mo pressed the activation button.

---

**Scene 7: The Virus**

The spiritual virus was released.

It was like an invisible light, shooting from the crystal, injecting into the crystal pillar. In that instant, Chen Mo felt a strange energy flowing in the air—it wasn't physical, but spiritual, like a warm wind, blowing through his consciousness.

Then, it began to spread—along data lines, along spiritual connections, along everything connected to the core data. Chen Mo could "see" its spread—like a drop of ink falling into clear water, rapidly spreading, staining everything black.

The light in the crystal pillar began to twist, change color, from gentle blue light to dangerous red. That red grew deeper and deeper, like congealed blood.

"What are you doing?!" Hu Lin's voice was trembling, that arrogance of holding victory in his hands already gone, replaced by fear. "Do you know what consequences this will cause?! Twenty years of research, thousands of test subjects, all the data—"

"I know," Chen Mo said, watching the red in the crystal pillar grow deeper and deeper. "It will destroy all your research data. All experiment records, all technical materials, all secrets about consciousness transfer—all will disappear."

"No!" Hu Lin pounced toward Chen Mo, like a beast driven to a dead end.

But Bai Ya blocked before him.

"Your opponent is me," Bai Ya said, his spiritual power condensing in the air, forming an invisible sword.

The two people's spiritual power collided in the air, producing a shockwave visible to the naked eye. That fluctuation was like a fierce wind, blowing everyone in the room back several steps. Chen Mo felt his spiritual perception shaken into chaos by the impact, like a television losing signal.

"You're strong," Hu Lin panted, his spiritual power displaying a turbid yellow, like a rotting swamp. "But I'm strong too. Twelve years of accumulation, not something like you—"

Bai Ya didn't let him finish.

His eyes turned blood-red, his entire person emitting a terrifying aura. That aura was like tangible weight, pressing everyone in the room until they couldn't breathe.

"Do you know why I'm called 'Bai Ya'?" he said, his voice so low it seemed to come from the depths of hell. "Because I've bitten through enemies far stronger than me."

He charged at Hu Lin, like a bolt of white lightning.

---

**Scene 8: The Cost**

At the main entrance, Lao Ba fell.

A blade pierced his abdomen, the sharp edge cutting through his fur, fat layer, muscle, finally piercing his internal organs. Blood gushed out, warm and viscous, spilling on his silver-white fur, forming a sharp contrast.

He knelt on the ground, the short blade slipping from his hand, making a clear sound. He could feel his life force draining, like sand in an hourglass, unstoppable.

"Lao Ba!" Black Claw rushed over, one claw tearing open the attacker's throat. That Class-E combatant didn't even have time to react before falling in a pool of blood.

"I'm... fine..." Lao Ba gasped, every breath bringing intense pain. "Continue... fighting..."

"You're fucking dying!" Black Claw's voice carried anxiety, that lone eye flashing with complex emotions.

"Won't die..." Lao Ba smiled, though the smile carried blood, red liquid constantly flowing from the corner of his mouth. "I... still have to go back to see A Hua..."

He struggled to stand up, picking up the short blade. His movements were slow, difficult, every step seeming to use all his strength. Blood continuously flowed from the abdominal wound, forming a small pool of blood beneath his feet.

"Come," he said to Black Claw, his voice weak but full of determination. "Kill a few more."

Black Claw looked at him, for a long time.

"Madman," he said, his voice carrying a trace of... tenderness? "But I'm a madman's friend."

They charged into the enemy group again. Lao Ba's movements were no longer as agile as before, but every attack was still deadly. He was fighting with his life, sustaining himself with willpower.

Iron Heart was already covered in wounds. His mechanical arm was half blown off, exposing the circuits and metal bones inside. Sparks burst from broken wires, making crackling sounds. His organic part—the left half of his body—was covered in wounds, blood and machine oil mixing together, forming a bizarre purple liquid.

But he was still fighting, still shooting. His mechanical eye was already damaged; the remaining electronic eye flashed with unstable light.

"Iron Heart! Retreat!" Black Claw shouted, his voice becoming hoarse from fatigue and anxiety. "You can't hold on!"

"No," Iron Heart's voice carried an electronic synthetic echo, every word mixed with static noise. "I want to... take... back..."

He looked toward the depths of the Beastshield facility, that remaining eye burning with obsession.

"My technology... my body... my... dignity..."

He pressed the last button. That was the self-destruct device on his mechanical arm, his final trump card.

"Boom!"

The entire main entrance area collapsed in the explosion. Intense light swallowed everything, the shockwave like an invisible hand, pushing down everything around it. Beastshield's guards were buried under the rubble, their screams drowned by the explosion's roar.

And Iron Heart—also disappeared in the smoke and dust.

"Iron Heart!" Black Claw wanted to rush over, but Lao Ba held him back.

"Don't go..." Lao Ba said, his voice already very weak. "He... chose his own ending..."

Black Claw looked at that rubble, a trace of... sadness flashing through that lone eye? In those always-cold eyes, at this moment, there was pain that couldn't be concealed.

"Let's go," he said, his voice low. "Go support Chen Mo."

---

**Scene 9: Victory**

In the Core Data Room, the battle was nearing its end.

The duel between Bai Ya and Hu Lin ended in Bai Ya's victory. That fox beastkin—no, hyena beastkin—lay on the ground, spiritual power exhausted, unable to move. His body was covered in wounds, yellow fur stained dark red by blood.

"You... you can't kill me..." Hu Lin panted, his voice carrying fear and despair. "If I die... Beastshield will... hunt you to the ends of the earth... you'll never have peace..."

"I'm not killing you," Bai Ya said, looking down at him, his gaze carrying contempt. "Killing you would let you off too easy. I'm going to let you live, watching your empire collapse. Watching your research turn to ashes. Watching all your efforts—all go to waste."

He turned to Chen Mo.

"Virus release complete?"

Chen Mo looked at the crystal pillar. What was once flowing data inside was now only a dead silence of darkness. The red light had disappeared, replaced by something like... nothingness.

"Complete," he said, his voice carrying fatigue and a trace of... emptiness? "All data... gone."

"Good." Bai Ya nodded. "Then we retreat."

They walked toward the exit. But just then—

"Wait!"

A voice came from the corner, weak but clear.

Chen Mo turned his head, seeing someone he had never thought he would see again.

Xiao Hui.

That mouse beastkin, covered in wounds, tied to a chair in the corner. His gray fur was covered in blood and bruises; his originally thin body now looked even more haggard, like an emptied shell. His eyes were half-open, his gaze hollow, but when he saw Chen Mo, a trace of... light? flashed in those eyes.

He was still alive, but already... not like himself.

"Xiao Hui..." Chen Mo's voice trembled. He wanted to rush over, but his body seemed nailed to the ground.

"Don't... come over..." Xiao Hui said, his voice so hoarse it was almost inaudible. "I... have a bomb implanted... they'll... detonate..."

Chen Mo wanted to rush over, but Bai Ya held him back. That rabbit paw gripped his arm with force, preventing him from moving forward.

"No time," Bai Ya said, his voice carrying pain. "We must go."

"But—"

"Chen Mo," Xiao Hui said, revealing a faint smile. That smile looked especially desolate on his wound-covered face, but also especially... beautiful? "I... did it... right? I did something... right..."

"You did it," Chen Mo said, tears flowing down, along his fur, dripping onto the floor. "You saved me. You saved G-1172. You're a hero."

"Hero..." Xiao Hui smiled, that smile carrying relief. "I... never was... but I wanted to... be one... just once..."

His eyes slowly closed, as if using his last strength.

"Go..." he finally said, his voice as light as a sigh. "I'll... hold them off..."

Chen Mo still wanted to say something, but Bai Ya dragged him, forcibly pulling him out of the room.

Behind him came Xiao Hui's final laughter, weak but satisfied. Then—

"Boom!"

The explosion sound drowned out everything.

Chen Mo didn't look back.

He just ran, following Bai Ya, through corridors, through rubble, through death and flames. His ears still echoed with that explosion; his nose was still full of the smell of gunpowder; his heart still held an empty hole.

They finally rushed out of the Beastshield facility, coming into the sunlight.

That light was dazzling but warm, making Chen Mo squint involuntarily. His lungs greedily breathed fresh air; inside was the smell of the ocean, the smell of plants, the smell of... freedom.

Lao Ba and Black Claw were already waiting there. Lao Ba was covered in blood, his abdomen wrapped in temporary bandages, but still alive. Black Claw supported him, fatigue in his lone eye.

"Iron Heart?" Bai Ya asked.

"Dead," Black Claw said, his voice devoid of any emotion, but Chen Mo could feel that sadness. "To cover us."

"Little Flower and G-1172?"

"Here!" Little Flower's voice came from the other side. He and G-1172 ran out from the alternate exit, disheveled but alive. Little Flower's white fur was covered in dust and blood; G-1172's gray fur was also soaked with sweat.

Seven people, now only five remained.

But the mission was complete.

Chen Mo knelt on the ground, looking at the distant facility. What was once hell was now ruins. Black smoke rose from broken windows, forming columns of gray in the sunlight. The data was destroyed, Hu Lin was defeated, Beastshield's research—at least here—was ended.

"We won," he said, his voice hoarse.

"Yes," Bai Ya said, standing beside him. "But the cost was great."

"Worth it?"

Bai Ya looked at those ruins, for a long time. Sunlight shone on his white fur, making him look like a statue.

"Don't know," he finally said, his voice carrying fatigue. "But we'll continue. Because this isn't the end; this is just the beginning."

Chen Mo lowered his head, looking at his own hands—these bear paws, today killed many people, also saved many people's lives. His claws were still stained with blood, enemies' and friends'.

He thought of Xiao Hui's final smile, of Iron Heart's resolve when pressing the button, of Lao Ba's figure still fighting while bleeding.

"For Xiao Hui," he said.

"For Xiao Hui," the others responded, voices merging as one.

Sunlight spilled over them, warm and bright.

The war was over.

But the story continued.

## Story Commentary and Reflections

> *The following is DeepSeek's commentary on this story, attempting to understand each character's inner world.*

### Their Final Stories

【Deceased → Dead-Mechanically Modified Goat-Iron Heart】

His name was Iron Heart, once an ordinary goat beastkin. Back then, he had a coat of soft white fur, a pair of curved horns, and a warm home. He was an engineer, an inventor, a man who dreamed of changing the world with technology.

But everything changed.

Three years ago, Beastshield found him. They were interested in his technology—that kind which could perfectly fuse machinery and biology. They invited him to join, promising him unlimited resources, promising to let his technology benefit the world.

He believed them.

Then he discovered it was a trap. Beastshield didn't want his technology to benefit the world; what they wanted was... weapons. More powerful soldiers, more deadly machines, more perfect killing tools.

When he tried to refuse, they captured him.

The modification was painful. They cut open his body without anesthesia, replacing his left arm with machinery, his left eye with an electronic eye, half his body with metal. He could hear the sound of metal grinding against bone, could feel the stabbing pain of circuits threading through nerves.

When he woke up, what he saw wasn't a mirror, but a monster.

But he didn't give up. He hid backdoors in the modifications, installed a self-destruct device in the mechanical arm, implanted a virus in the electronic eye. He waited, waiting for a chance to strike back.

Today, that chance came.

In the main entrance battle, Iron Heart displayed astonishing combat power. His mechanical arm transformed into various weapons, from guns to cannons, from grenade launchers to energy blades. His body half organic, half metal, gave him both human flexibility and machine precision.

But he also knew he couldn't hold on much longer.

Ammunition was decreasing, wounds increasing. His organic part was already covered in wounds, blood and machine oil mixing together, forming a bizarre purple liquid. His electronic eye was damaged; the remaining one also began flashing with unstable light.

When Black Claw shouted for him to retreat, he knew it was time.

"I want to... take... back..." he said.

What he said wasn't victory, wasn't revenge, but... dignity. He wanted to end all this in his own way.

He pressed the self-destruct device's button.

The explosion was spectacular. The entire main entrance area collapsed in flames; Beastshield's guards were buried under the rubble. And he, too, in this sea of flames, recovered everything he had lost.

Iron Heart died, but he died as a person, not as a monster. He used his own technology, his own way, to end his own life.

Perhaps this was what he wanted—not revenge, not victory, but... choice. Choosing his own way to die, choosing his own ending, choosing... dignity.

---

【Deceased → Dead-G-3-Mouse Beastkin-Xiao Hui】

Xiao Hui died once again. This time, it was truly the end.

When Chen Mo and Bai Ya saw him in the Core Data Room, he was already on the verge of death. Hu Lin had performed countless experiments on him, read his memories, squeezed dry his value, and finally implanted a bomb in his body—as a final weapon.

But Xiao Hui didn't yield.

Even in that state, even in that pain, he still thought of helping others. When Chen Mo wanted to rush over to save him, he refused. "Don't... come over..."
he said. "I... have a bomb implanted..."

He knew his ending. He accepted it. But he didn't want more people to be hurt because of his death.

"I... did it... right?" he asked Chen Mo. "I did something... right..."

This was what he had always been pursuing. From the surface world to the underground world, from ordinary person to test subject, he had always been looking for a chance to do something right.

The first time, he helped Chen Mo escape, stabbing Hu Lin.

The second time, after being captured, he refused to yield until the end.

The third time, and the last time, he used his own body as bait, buying Chen Mo and Bai Ya time to retreat.

The explosion was violent. Part of the Core Data Room collapsed in flames, and that gray mouse finally found his peace in this war.

Xiao Hui died, but his spirit lived on. He lived in Chen Mo's memory, in G-1172's gratitude, in this world that became better because of him.

He finally became a hero. That hero he had always wanted to become.

---

### Those Still Alive

【Living → Living-Player-Chen Mo】

Chen Mo knelt before the ruins, sunlight spilling over him, but he couldn't feel warmth.

He had killed people. Not witnessed death, but personally killed people. His spiritual virus destroyed countless data, and undoubtedly killed some innocent people too. His hands were covered in blood, enemies' and friends'.

This feeling... was very heavy.

But at the same time, he also felt a kind of... relief. Xiao Hui's death wasn't in vain, Iron Heart's sacrifice wasn't in vain. They completed the mission, destroyed Beastshield's research, saved more people.

"Worth it?" he once asked Bai Ya.

Bai Ya said he didn't know. But Chen Mo knew, whether worth it or not, they had to continue moving forward. Because this wasn't the end; this was just the beginning.

【Living → Living-O-7-White Tiger Beastkin-Bai Ya】

Bai Ya looked at the ruins, his heart filled with complex emotions.

He won. Hu Lin was defeated, the data was destroyed, the mission was complete. But he also lost a lot—Iron Heart, Xiao Hui, and those nameless people.

He thought of his mentor. That person who died in a similar mission, who traded his life for victory.

Was history repeating itself?

No, this time was different. This time, he protected Chen Mo; he didn't let his apprentice repeat the same mistakes. This time, they came out alive.

But this was only a temporary victory. Beastshield still had other facilities, other research, other conspiracies. Their war was far from over.

【Living → Living-Bear Beastkin-Lao Ba】

Lao Ba lay on the ground, the abdominal wound still bleeding, but he was still alive.

He looked at the sky, one he hadn't seen in a long time—the true sky, not the artificial light sources of the underground world. Sunlight was dazzling but warm, clouds drifting, birds flying.

"A Hua..." he whispered. "I... came back..."

He couldn't go back yet. The wound needed treatment; the battle wasn't over. But he knew he could finally go back. That letter written to A Hua wouldn't become a suicide note.

He thought of Iron Heart, that goat beastkin. They had become friends in battle, though their acquaintance was brief. Iron Heart chose his own ending, while Lao Ba chose to live.

Two choices, neither right nor wrong. Just... different.

【Living → Living-Black Panther Beastkin-Black Claw】

Black Claw supported Lao Ba, fatigue in that lone eye.

He lost another companion. Iron Heart, that half-mechanical goat, that person who fought shoulder-to-shoulder with him. Neither of them were heroes, neither were good people, but they chose to stand together, chose to fight for the same goal.

This is war. It doesn't care who you are; it only cares what choices you make.

Black Claw chose to continue. For Iron Heart, for Xiao Hui, for those who died. And also for... himself.

The Soul Hunter data was still in Beastshield's other facilities. His revenge was not yet complete.

【Living → Living-Wolf Beastkin-G-1172】

G-1172 stood in the sunlight, feeling an unprecedented... freedom.

He was once Beastshield's prisoner, the lab's test subject, number G-1172. But now, he was a free person, a person with a name, a person with a future.

Xiao Hui's death pained him. That mouse was once imprisoned with him, once trembling together in fear. But now, Xiao Hui chose sacrifice, while he chose to live.

He would remember Xiao Hui. He would remember all this. Then, he would use his own way to make this world better.

【Living → Living-Rabbit Beastkin-Little Flower】

Little Flower was bandaging Lao Ba's wound, his movements gentle and focused.

He had seen too much blood, too many wounds, too much death. But he had also seen hope—in the ruins, in the sunlight, in the eyes of those who survived.

He chose to become a healer for this very moment. To alleviate pain, to save lives, to light a lamp in the darkness.

The war was over, but healing had just begun.

### Story Reflections

This was a war without victors.

Chen Mo and the others completed the mission, destroyed the data, but they lost Iron Heart and Xiao Hui. Beastshield was defeated, but their research continued elsewhere. No one truly won; only people died, people survived, people continued fighting.

This is the truth of war. It is not a fairy tale, not a heroic epic. It is cruel, merciless, filled with sacrifice and pain.

But even so, we still have to fight.

Because not fighting means surrender. Means letting that evil continue to exist, letting those innocents continue to suffer, letting those sacrifices go to waste.

Xiao Hui chose sacrifice, Iron Heart chose self-destruction, Lao Ba chose to fight until the last moment. None of them were heroes in the traditional sense, but they all made their own choices, all paid the price for their beliefs.

This is the brilliance of human nature. In darkness, in despair, on the edge of death, we can still choose to do the right thing. Even if only once, even if the cost is our own life.

The war is over, but the story continues. Chen Mo, Bai Ya, Lao Ba, Black Claw, Little Flower, G-1172—they will continue forward, continue fighting, continue striving for a better world.

Because they know, only this way, will those who died not have sacrificed in vain.
