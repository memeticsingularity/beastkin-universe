> *"When everything settles, when choices become eternal, he finally understands—the first game is also the last. But this is not the end, but a new beginning. Because the true game has never been in the virtual world, but in our hearts."*

---

**Scene 1: Confrontation**

The Gate's light illuminated the entire space.

It was an indescribable light—not a single color, but countless colors interweaving together, like a river composed of light, flowing in the void. Red, blue, green, purple... every color represented an energy, an emotion, a soul. The light was warm yet blinding, like it had tangible weight, shining on Chen Mo's fur, bringing a strange burning sensation.

The air was permeated with an ancient aura—the taste of time's sedimentation, mixed with some aged medicinal fragrance and... the sickly sweetness of decay. That was the product of five hundred years of accumulation, the traces left by countless consciousness transfers, the price of longevity, the curse of immortality.

Chen Mo stood before the Principal, feeling that overwhelming spiritual pressure. It wasn't merely powerful; it was... ancient. Five hundred years of accumulation, countless consciousness transfers, made the Principal's spiritual power bottomless, like an unfathomable abyss, devouring all consciousness that tried to probe it.

The Principal's figure was constantly changing. Sometimes a massive bear, muscles bulging beneath fur, emitting a primitive sense of power, carrying the scent of earth and pine. Sometimes a vigorous wolf, gaze sharp, full of wild cunning, carrying the smell of wind. Sometimes an aged human, face covered in wrinkles, but eyes flashing with wisdom beyond age, carrying the scent of old books and herbs.

"You want to stop me," the Principal's voice echoed in the space. "But do you truly understand what you are stopping?"

"You are destroying two worlds," Chen Mo said. "Fusion will make everything lose meaning."

"Lose meaning?" The Principal laughed. "On the contrary. I am creating meaning."

His body began to change, from bear to wolf, from wolf to human, finally settling on an aged human form—that was his original appearance, from five hundred years ago.

"Do you know why I created this world?" he asked.

Chen Mo shook his head.

"Because I was afraid of death," the Principal said, his voice suddenly becoming weary. "Five hundred years ago, I was an ordinary scientist. I discovered the secret of consciousness, discovered the method of transfer. I thought I had found immortality."

He looked at the Gate.

"But I was wrong. Immortality is not a gift; it is a curse. I watched my loved ones die one by one, watched the world change, watched myself become... a monster."

"Then why do you still want to open the Gate?" Bai Ya asked. "Letting two worlds fuse will only make tragedy repeat."

"Because I don't want to bear this curse alone," the Principal said, his voice carrying despair. "If humans want immortality, then let them taste the flavor of immortality. Let them know that living is not necessarily happiness."

Chen Mo fell silent.

He suddenly understood the Principal. Not agreed, but understood. That loneliness, that despair, that... weariness.

"There's another choice," Chen Mo said.

"What choice?"

"Close the Gate. Stop all this. Let the two worlds remain separate."

The Principal looked at him, for a long time.

"And then? I continue living alone? Continue watching everything fade away?"

"No," Chen Mo said. "Then you choose death. True death."

The space fell into dead silence.

---

**Scene 2: Choice**

The Principal's body trembled.

"Death..." he said softly, like pronouncing a strange word. "I have... forgotten the meaning of this word."

"I know that feeling," Chen Mo said. "I too am afraid of death. But it is precisely because of fear that living has meaning."

He stepped forward.

"Xiao Hui was afraid of death, but he chose sacrifice. Iron Heart was afraid of death, but he chose self-destruction. They both knew that some things are more important than living."

"What things?"

"Hope," Chen Mo said. "Leaving hope for others. Letting them know that even in the darkest times, there are people willing to fight for the right thing."

The Principal fell silent.

The Gate's light began to flicker, as if sensing his wavering.

"You can close it," Chen Mo said. "You have that ability. End all this, let yourself be free."

"Freedom..." the Principal repeated this word. "Can I really... be free?"

"You can," Chen Mo said. "But the choice is yours."

---

**Scene 3: Hu Lin's Attack**

Just then, a figure charged out from the shadows.

It was Hu Lin.

He was covered in wounds, his eyes mad, gripping a dagger in his hand, stabbing straight at Chen Mo's back.

"No!" Bai Ya shouted, but it was already too late.

The dagger pierced Chen Mo's body.

It was a strange feeling—first the cold touch of metal, then sharp stabbing pain, followed by a... burning scorching sensation. That wasn't an ordinary dagger, but a specially made weapon coated with some spiritual power toxin, specifically designed to deal with powerful spiritual power users.

Pain flooded in like tidewater, but not from the wound, rather from throughout his entire body. Chen Mo knelt down, feeling his life force draining, spiritual power dissipating. Blood flowed from the wound, warm and viscous, soaking his fur, dripping onto the ground, making slight ticking sounds. The air immediately filled with a strong bloody scent, mixed with the toxin’s distinctive pungent smell, like burnt rubber and rotten herbs.

"You think you've won?" Hu Lin screamed. "You think you can destroy everything of mine? I won't let you succeed!"

He raised the dagger, preparing to stab again.

But the Principal's movement was faster.

A spiritual shockwave erupted from the Principal, directly blasting Hu Lin away. That hyena beastkin crashed into the wall, let out a scream, then stopped moving.

"Foolish," the Principal said. "He thought he could control everything. But he was just... another person afraid of death."

Chen Mo collapsed on the ground, feeling his consciousness blurring.

"Chen Mo!" Bai Ya rushed to his side. "Hold on!"

"I'm... fine..." Chen Mo said with difficulty. "Just... hurts a bit..."

"Don't talk," Bai Ya said. "Little Flower can heal you, but we must leave here."

"No..." Chen Mo grabbed Bai Ya's hand. "Close... the Gate first..."

---

**Scene 4: The Final Gift**

The Principal walked to Chen Mo's side, kneeling down.

"You are special," he said. "In five hundred years, you are the most... special person I have ever seen."

"Why..." Chen Mo asked.

"Because you remind me of myself," the Principal said. "That self who had not yet been lost."

He reached out, placing his hand on Chen Mo's wound.

"I can save you," he said. "But the price is my life. My spiritual power can repair your body, but I will... truly die."

"Why... would you..." Chen Mo asked.

"Because you gave me a choice," the Principal said. "Now, let me give you my choice."

His body began to glow.

"No..." Chen Mo wanted to stop him, but it was already too late.

The Principal's spiritual power poured into Chen Mo's body, like a warm torrent, spreading from the point of contact to his entire body.

It was an indescribable feeling—like being surrounded by sunlight, like being wrapped in love, like... returning to the primordial womb. Chen Mo could feel that force flowing within his body, repairing damaged blood vessels, stitching torn muscles, purifying toxins. Warmth spread from the wound, like a ball of fire, yet not scorching, but comfortable, healing, full of vitality.

He "saw" the Principal's memories—five hundred years of time flashing before his eyes, laughter, tears, gains, losses. Those memories weren't forced upon him, but flowed through his consciousness like water, letting him understand, letting him feel, letting him... grow.

"The Gate..." the Principal said, his voice growing lighter and lighter. "I will close it... before I leave..."

He looked at that glowing sphere, and then—

"Close."

The Gate's light began to contract, from a dazzling star to a faint point of light, and then... disappeared.

At the same time, the Principal's body began to dissipate, like a wisp of green smoke, scattered by the wind.

"Thank you..." he finally said. "For letting me finally... rest..."

Then, he disappeared.

Five hundred years of loneliness, five hundred years of curse, finally ended.

---

**Scene 5: Return**

Chen Mo opened his eyes.

He lay on the ground, his wound already healed, his body stronger than ever before. But he felt an emptiness—the Principal's spiritual power was within his body, but he was already gone.

"He... is gone?" Chen Mo asked.

"Gone," Bai Ya said. "The Gate is also closed. Two worlds... are safe."

Black Claw and G-1172 walked over, their faces carrying expressions of fatigue but relief.

"What about Hu Lin?" Chen Mo asked.

"Dead," Black Claw said. "The Principal's final blow destroyed his consciousness."

Chen Mo fell silent.

Too much death. Xiao Hui, Iron Heart, the Principal, Hu Lin... and those nameless ones who died in the war.

"Did we... win?" he asked.

"We won," Bai Ya said. "But the cost was great."

"Worth it?"

Bai Ya looked at Chen Mo, for a long time.

"Don't know," he said. "But at least, we still have the right to choose. That is victory."

---

**Scene 6: Farewell**

One week later, the safehouse.

Lao Ba's injuries had improved; he could get out of bed and walk. Little Flower was busy caring for the last of the wounded. Black Claw was preparing to leave—he still had his own revenge to continue. G-1172 decided to stay, to help rebuild this world.

Chen Mo stood at the door, looking at the distant sky.

"You're leaving?" Bai Ya walked to his side.

"Mm," Chen Mo said. "Time to go back. The real world... there are people waiting for me."

"Will you come back?"

Chen Mo smiled.

"Maybe," he said. "That world... has already become a part of me."

He turned to Bai Ya, extending his hand.

"Thank you," he said. "Thank you for everything you taught me."

Bai Ya grasped his hand.

"I also thank you," he said. "You let me see... hope."

They hugged, then separated.

Chen Mo looked at the others—Lao Ba, Little Flower, G-1172, Black Claw.

"Goodbye," he said. "Friends."

"Goodbye," they said.

Chen Mo closed his eyes, concentrating.

He felt his consciousness rising, leaving this brown bear body, passing through endless void, flying toward that distant point of light.

---

**Scene 7: Awakening**

Chen Mo removed the VR headset.

In that instant, he felt a strange sense of disconnection—like his soul being extracted from his body, then stuffed back in again. The leather inside the helmet pressed against his cheeks, carrying the temperature of sweat and skin, a damp, warm sensation.

Sunlight streamed through the window, stinging his eyes. That was a completely different light from the virtual world—more glaring, more real, carrying some kind of... temperature. The sunlight of the real world had weight, had heat, shining on the skin, making one feel tangible warmth.

He blinked, adapting to the real world's light. Then, he smelled the scent in the air—the greasy smell of takeout, the sweat smell of clothes, that stuffy atmosphere of a room long unventilated. This was completely different from the air of the virtual world; though there were simulated scents there, they didn't have this... real complexity.

He was still in his apartment. On the table sat half-eaten takeout; on his phone were piles of unread messages. Only an hour had passed—but in that world, he had experienced several months.

He stood up, walking to the window.

The outside world continued as usual. People went to work, got off work, ate, slept. No one knew that in another world, a war concerning the fate of two worlds had just ended.

Chen Mo touched his own chest. There was no wound there, but he could feel—the Principal's spiritual power was still within his body, fusing with the techniques Bai Ya had taught him.

He had changed. No longer that programmer who only knew how to code. He had experienced death, experienced battle, experienced... growth.

The phone rang.

It was his mother's call.

"Hello, Mom?"

"You child, why didn't you answer the phone for so long?" His mother's voice carried worry. "I was worried to death."

Chen Mo smiled.

"Sorry, Mom," he said. "I was just... playing a game."

"A game? What game could make you not even answer the phone?"

"A game..." Chen Mo thought for a moment. "A game that made me understand many things."

"Are you okay?"

"I'm fine," Chen Mo said. "In fact... I feel great."

He looked at the sky outside the window, that azure sky without a red moon.

"Mom," he said. "I want to come home and see you guys."

"Really? That's wonderful! Your dad misses you too. When are you coming back?"

"Soon," Chen Mo said. "In a few days."

Hanging up the phone, Chen Mo looked again at that VR device.

*Yuan Shou Yu Zhu* (Beast Lord). The first game.

But to him, that wasn't just a game. That was a world, a life, a group of friends.

He would go back. But not now.

Now, he wanted to live in reality. Cherish the people before his eyes, cherish the life before his eyes.

Because Xiao Hui taught him—even doing one small right thing is better than doing nothing at all.

Because Iron Heart taught him—even if the body is mechanical, the heart can still be warm.

Because Bai Ya taught him—while becoming stronger, don't lose your humanity.

Because the Principal taught him—immortality is not a blessing, and death is not the end.

---

**Scene 8: A New Beginning**

Three months later.

Chen Mo quit his programmer job and began doing game design.

Not those violent, greedy games, but games that could make people think, make people feel, make people grow.

His first project was called *The First Game*.

That was a story about choices. About an ordinary person, how to find balance between virtual and reality, how to make choices between power and humanity, how to maintain hope in despair.

On the day the game was released, Chen Mo received an anonymous message.

"Thank you. —Bai Ya"

Chen Mo smiled.

He knew that in some corner of that world, Bai Ya was still teaching new students, Lao Ba was still guiding new Descenders, G-1172 was still helping people in need.

Their stories continued.

And his story had just begun.

---

**Scene 9: Epilogue**

Many years later.

Chen Mo had become a famous game designer; his works helped countless people find meaning in life.

He married and had children, had a happy family. But he never forgot that world, those people.

Occasionally, when the night was quiet, he would put on that old VR device and return to the Beastshield universe.

Bai Ya was still there, though older than before. Lao Ba was still there, though his back was more bent. G-1172 had become a respected healer; Little Flower opened a clinic; Black Claw... Black Claw had finally found the murderer of his mate and completed his revenge.

They would gather at the safehouse, drinking Lao Ba's brewed wine, telling each other's stories.

"You know," Bai Ya once said. "You changed this world."

"Not me," Chen Mo said. "It was us."

"Right," Bai Ya smiled. "It was us."

They raised their glasses, for Xiao Hui, for Iron Heart, for the Principal, for all those who sacrificed for the right thing.

"The first game," Bai Ya said.

"And also the last," Chen Mo added.

They looked at each other and smiled.

Because to them, that was never a game.

That was life.

## Story Commentary and Reflections

> *The following is DeepSeek's commentary on this story, attempting to understand each character's inner world.*

### Their Final Stories

【Living → Dead-Unknown-The Principal】

He had no name, or rather, he had forgotten his name. For five hundred years, he had been called by countless titles—scientist, creator, principal, monster. But none could represent the true him.

Five hundred years ago, he was an ordinary human scientist. Young, full of passion, believing science could change the world. He discovered the secret of consciousness transfer, thought he had found immortality, found the next step in human evolution.

But he was wrong.

Immortality is not a gift; it is a curse. He watched his loved ones die one by one—partner, children, friends, students. He watched the world change, from the steam age to the electrical age, from the electrical age to the information age. He watched himself become... a monster. A monster trapped in endless time.

He created this world—the Beastshield universe—as a place of escape. Here, he could start anew, could forget the past, could become... another person.

But escape couldn't solve the problem. Five hundred years of loneliness, five hundred years of pain, eventually turned into anger and despair. He wanted to let others taste the flavor of immortality too, wanted to let the world know that living is not necessarily happiness.

Until Chen Mo appeared.

That little bear gave him a choice—to choose death, to choose freedom, to choose... meaning. Chen Mo let him understand that the value of life lies not in length, but in depth. Not in how long one lives, but in what one lives for.

He chose death.

Not as failure, but as victory. For the first time in five hundred years, he felt peace, felt... freedom.

He used his final strength to save Chen Mo, closed the Gate, ended his own curse.

In the last moment before dissipating, he smiled. That was the first true smile in five hundred years.

He could finally, finally... rest.

---

【Living → Dead-O-6-Hyena Beastkin-Hu Lin】

Hu Lin died, like a stray cur, in madness and hatred.

He was once a high official of Beastshield, at the peak of power, an existence countless people looked up to. But deep in his heart, there was only fear—fear of death, fear of losing control, fear of... being forgotten.

He pursued immortality, not because he wanted to live, but because he was afraid of dying. He tortured test subjects, not because he was cruel, but because he wanted to prove he had the power to control life and death. He hunted Chen Mo, not because of hatred, but because of... jealousy.

Jealous that Chen Mo had the courage to face death. Jealous that Chen Mo had friends willing to sacrifice for him. Jealous that Chen Mo... had hope.

Hu Lin never had hope. He only had fear, only desire, only... emptiness.

At the last moment, when the Principal's spiritual shockwave struck him, he felt no fear. Only a kind of... relief. Finally, finally, he no longer had to be afraid.

His death was tragic, but also... inevitable. A person consumed by fear would ultimately be destroyed by fear. This was his choice, and also his fate.

---

### Those Still Alive

【Living → Living-Player-Chen Mo】

Chen Mo removed the VR helmet, returning to the real world.

But he was no longer that programmer who only knew how to code. He had experienced death, experienced battle, experienced... growth. The spirits of Xiao Hui, Iron Heart, and the Principal all remained within his body, becoming a part of him.

He chose game design as his new career—not for entertainment, but for inspiration. He wanted to create games that could make people think, make people feel, make people grow, just like the impact that world had on him.

He knew that in some corner of that world, Bai Ya was still there, Lao Ba was still there, G-1172 was still there. Their stories continued, and he too had become a part of that story.

This is not the end, but the beginning.

【Living → Living-O-7-White Tiger Beastkin-Bai Ya】

Bai Ya stood at the safehouse door, looking at the distant sky.

His mentor had once done something similar, but didn't come back. This time, he changed the ending. He protected Chen Mo, completed his mentor's unfinished business.

But he knew, this wasn't the end. There were still new students to teach, new battles to face, and... new hope to protect.

He would continue. Because this was the path he chose.

【Living → Living-Bear Beastkin-Lao Ba】

Lao Ba brewed wine, watching new Descenders arrive one by one.

His injuries had healed, but his heart would forever bear scars. Those were the marks of war, the marks of loss, and also... the marks of growth.

He would raise his glass for Iron Heart and Xiao Hui every time he drank. They became legends, became a part of this story.

And he would continue living, continue guiding, continue... hoping.

【Living → Living-Black Panther Beastkin-Black Claw】

Black Claw completed his revenge.

But revenge didn't bring him the satisfaction he imagined. It only brought... emptiness.

So he chose a new path—helping those who, like him, had lost loved ones, protecting those who were powerless to protect themselves.

Perhaps, this is redemption.

【Living → Living-Wolf Beastkin-G-1172】

G-1172 became a healer.

He used his own experiences to help those victims who had escaped from Beastshield's laboratories. He knew that pain, knew that fear, and also knew... hope.

Every healing was a healing of himself.

【Living → Living-Rabbit Beastkin-Little Flower】

Little Flower opened a hospital.

He used his abilities to heal countless spiritual traumas. His name became a symbol of hope, became the light of the underground world.

He proved that even in the darkest places, there are people who choose kindness.

---

### Story Reflections

This is a story about choices.

From Chapter 1 to Chapter 8, we witnessed Chen Mo's growth—from a Descender who only knew how to run, to a warrior willing to fight for the right thing. From a person afraid of death, to a person who understood the meaning of life.

We also witnessed the choices of others—Xiao Hui chose sacrifice, Iron Heart chose self-destruction, the Principal chose death, Bai Ya chose to continue, Lao Ba chose hope.

Every choice shaped their destiny. Every choice proved... humanity.

In this story, there is no pure good or evil. Hu Lin was the villain, but he was also just a pitiful person consumed by fear. The Principal was the creator, but he was also a victim of the curse. Everyone had their own reasons, everyone had their own pain, everyone was... searching for a way out.

This is the complexity of human nature. We are not black and white; we are gray, contradictory, searching for light in the darkness.

Xiao Hui's spirit—"wanting to do the right thing, even if only once"—ran through the entire story. It reminds us that even in the most desperate times, we can choose. Choose kindness, choose bravery, choose... hope.

The story has ended, but the spirit lives on.

In every person who chooses to do the right thing, in every person who doesn't give up hope, Xiao Hui, Iron Heart, the Principal... they are all there.

May we all find our own answers in our "first game."

## Afterword

This is a story about growth.

Chen Mo grew from an ordinary programmer into a person who could understand two worlds and connect two kinds of life. He learned power, but more importantly, he learned humanity.

Xiao Hui's sacrifice, Iron Heart's courage, Bai Ya's teachings, the Principal's redemption... every person left a mark in Chen Mo's growth.

And ultimately, Chen Mo chose to return to the real world, chose to continue fighting in his own way—through creation, through sharing, through hope.

This is not a story about heroes. This is a story about ordinary people.

An ordinary person, how to not lose their humanity in a cruel world.

Thank you for reading this far.

May you too find your own answer in your "first game."
