## 一、简介 / Introduction

**一句话钩子**：他以黑暗为皮肤，以恐惧为武器，在兽盾的缝隙中狩猎那些迷失在灯光下的猎物。

赏金猎人**魔影**的死亡线故事。在兽盾公司这座钢铁与权力浇筑的帝国里，魔影是最不可捉摸的幽灵。他潜伏在通风管道的阴影中、更衣室的角落里、走廊灯光照不到的盲区——任何被视为"安全缝隙"的地方，都是他布下的捕兽夹。

他的狩猎方式独特而致命：不依赖刀枪，而以精准的暗劲、毒针和对雄性兽人身体弱点的极致掌控，将猎物一步步推入快感与死亡交织的深渊。狗、熊、牛、虎、野猪、黑豹、灰狼……不同种族的守卫在他手中沦为待宰的羔羊，而堆积的尸山与弥漫的腥甜，不过是通往下一个猎物的路标。

这不是正义的审判，也不是复仇的烈焰。这是一场关于支配与剥夺的私人仪式——黑暗不是庇护所，而是猎手的皮肤。

**One-line Hook**: He wears darkness as his skin and fear as his weapon, hunting prey lost in the light within the cracks of Beastshield.

The death-line story of bounty hunter **Moying**. Within the steel-and-power empire of Beastshield Company, Moying is the most elusive ghost. He lurks in the shadows of ventilation ducts, the corners of locker rooms, and the blind spots beyond the reach of corridor lights — any place considered a "safe gap" becomes his trap.

His hunting method is unique and lethal: not relying on blades or firearms, but on precise dark-force techniques, poison needles, and an extreme mastery of male beastkin anatomical vulnerabilities, pushing prey step by step into an abyss where pleasure and death intertwine. Dog, bear, bull, tiger, boar, black panther, gray wolf... guards of different species become lambs to the slaughter in his hands, and the mounting pile of corpses and permeating sweetness of bodily fluids are merely signposts to the next prey.

This is neither a trial of justice nor a flame of vengeance. It is a private ritual of domination and deprivation — darkness is not a shelter, but the hunter's skin.
