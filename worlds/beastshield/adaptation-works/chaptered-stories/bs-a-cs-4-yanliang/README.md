## 一、简介 / Introduction

**一句话钩子**：当死亡成为一门生意，杀手与清道夫在欲望与鲜血中丈量生命的边界。

赏金猎人炎与魉潜入兽盾新兵训练基地，一个负责收割，一个负责善后。炎修炼的"灼流暗劲"能通过操控欲望来终结生命，而魉的隐身能力让他成为最完美的清道夫。在这个没有人会在意的夜晚，名单上的名字被一个个划去——虎、犬、狼、熊，每一个编号背后都是一个未曾实现的梦想。这不是正义与邪恶的对抗，而是生存与利益的冰冷计算，以及被碾碎的、一个个具体而微的生命。

本仓库同时保留两个改编版本：
- **v1-20ch**：较早的 20 章扩写版，对原作三章进行了大幅情节扩充。
- **v2-expanded**：正在更新的重写版，基于原作三章结构，以 lanse 原作者风格为锚点，强化感官极致化、慢节奏与人物互动，并将章节拆分为五章，进一步降低重复感。

---

**One-line Hook**: When death becomes a business, a killer and his cleanup partner measure the boundary of life through blood and desire.

The bounty hunters Yan and Liang infiltrate a Beastshield recruit training base—one reaps lives, the other cleans up the aftermath. Yan's "Scorching Dark Force" can end lives by manipulating desire, while Liang's invisibility makes him the perfect undertaker. On this unremarkable night, names on a list are crossed out one by one—tiger, dog, wolf, bear—each number hiding an unfulfilled dream. This is not a battle between justice and evil, but a cold calculus of survival and profit, and the crushing of individual, fragile lives.

Two adaptation versions are maintained:
- **v1-20ch**: The earlier 20-chapter expansion.
- **v2-expanded**: A rewrite in progress, anchored to the original three-chapter structure and lanse's style, with heightened sensory detail, slower pacing, and five chapters.
