## Story Synopsis

The amnesiac black canine beastkin **Hei Gou (Black Dog)**, bearing the mysterious martial arts legacy of the master **Mo Quan (Ink Hound)**, struggles to survive in the mountains while pursued by Beastshield Corporation. He is lustful, cruel, yet carries a touch of merciful tenderness— in a world where shooting empty means death, every battle is an interweaving of eroticism and life-and-death struggle.
