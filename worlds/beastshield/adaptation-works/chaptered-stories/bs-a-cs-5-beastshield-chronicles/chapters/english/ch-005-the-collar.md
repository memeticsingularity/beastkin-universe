# Chapter 5 — The Collar

When Xu Yi woke up, an unfamiliar white ceiling came into view. The unfamiliar space was filled with the pungent smell of disinfectant and the ticking sound of instruments. He shook his head, which was supported by a pillow, to survey his surroundings, and found that apart from medical equipment, he was alone in the hospital room.

The Samoyed, just emerging from unconsciousness, was immediately caught up by the pain left from his battle with the white dog. Multiple parts of his body were wrapped in tight bandages, and the unbearable sore, swollen pain hidden beneath the bandages attacked along with his awakening.

Hiss, did I really sustain so many wounds just now? Though the situation had nearly turned one-sided due to his misjudgment, Xu Yi clearly remembered that he had tried his best to avoid injury.

Xu Yi lay on the bed pondering who exactly that white dog was. That skill and... those methods — they didn't look like they belonged to an ordinary person at all.

Also, why did he want me to stay inside Beastshield as an inside man? But he had already discovered that I wasn't a Beastshield employee, hadn't he?

Furthermore... who wants to stay in Beastshield and mingle with these scum! That bastard white dog... I must find a way to take revenge on him!

Thinking this, the Samoyed became furious. He used his arms to barely prop himself up to sit on the hospital bed to vent his emotions, but the process of sitting up caused sore, swollen pain in many areas, making him grit his teeth and suck in a breath of cold air.

At this moment, he twitched his ears, keenly catching the sound of the hospital room door being pushed open.

"Awake? Then things are easier. Best not move around recklessly, or I'll kill you." A voice with a rich, steady tone reached Xu Yi's ears.

The visitor had dark gray fur with tiger stripes, wearing a black Beastshield tight uniform. Even the long socks clinging to his lower legs were black — an entirely black appearance from head to toe.

Xu Yi quietly sized him up. From his understanding of Beastshield, those who could wear this black uniform were the highest-ranking existences within this Beastshield branch.

He was a gray tiger beastkin with a truly astonishing physique, robust in build, with strong and powerful limbs. The Beastshield uniform clinging to his body clearly outlined his muscle lines — something extremely rare even in a Beastshield branch full of burly, rough men.

At this moment, he was emitting a mixed scent of alcohol and male hormones. Xu Yi deduced that this was the high-ranking Beastshield soldier who had parachuted into the branch tonight, coming to visit the wounded after the branch's welcome banquet.

Wait, in Beastshield's style of operation, does something like visiting the wounded really exist?

After the other entered the hospital room, he first looked down at the stack of paper reports in his hand, then directed a solemn gaze at Xu Yi lying on the hospital bed.

This gaze made Xu Yi feel uneasy in his heart, and the words the other spoke next confirmed that this sense of crisis was not unfounded.

"Though you're wearing the uniform of Beastshield Soldier No. 16, your appearance doesn't match the records at all, and there are no records related to you in the system. This proves you don't belong to Beastshield at all."

Damn! Knew it! Xu Yi quietly tugged down the corner of his mouth.

The gray tiger's eyes sharpened. In just three strides, he came to the bedside and gripped Xu Yi's neck. Speaking of which... wasn't I supposed to be sent here as a spy? Damn, they can tell my identity at a glance, what's the point of pretending? That idiot white dog, did he really not prepare anything for me at all...

The Samoyed couldn't help but curse in his heart. He had just caught his breath when someone came to clean house again. Forget spy activities, dying directly was entirely possible.

"Not bad combat power. It'd be a pity to kill you just like that. I'll give you a choice."

The gray tiger nodded and smiled, seemingly quite satisfied with Xu Yi. He came to the monitoring equipment in the hospital room, operated the instruments, and took out a technologically advanced collar, extending it toward Xu Yi who had just recovered.

"Put on the collar here and become a Beastshield soldier for my use, or... be killed by me."

Though it was an old-fashioned approach and line, the gray tiger deliberately dragged out the final syllable of "or," making it abundantly clear which option he expected the other to choose.

Though Xu Yi couldn't speak, his tightly furrowed brows clearly showed vigilance. However, what he was thinking inside was another matter entirely.

"Does this person have to speak this way? It feels like a villain dug out from some old-timey literary work."

Seeing the Samoyed's prolonged refusal to give an answer, and his patience being worn down bit by bit, the gray tiger simply reached out roughly to capture the other party and used his body to press him down, forcibly putting the collar around Xu Yi's neck.

Xu Yi was not made of steel either. One collapsing force strike pulled at his entire upper body where injuries hadn't healed, causing secondary tearing of his wounds. The bone-igniting pain made him comply with the other party.

After successfully putting on the collar, the gray tiger proudly raised the corner of his mouth, revealing his tiger fangs.

This collar was called the Dominance Collar. Once worn, it could only be removed through death or Beastshield's backend authority.

When the collar suffered external impact or disassembly, it would trigger a punishment mechanism: releasing electric current to shock the wearer into complete paralysis. The Samoyed, whose body had been penetrated by electric current to the point of being crispy on the outside and tender on the inside, strongly supported his nearly exhausted body, still glaring back at Mo Wu unwillingly.

"A doggy with backbone, but backbone isn't meant to be used this way, especially against your master!"

Damn! Why does everyone call me little doggy? And this one even put a collar on me. Do they all like playing master-servant service?

Mo Wu appreciated Xu Yi's backbone, but the other's attitude indeed made him unhappy. So he solidly rewarded Xu Yi with a punch, knocking him out for a while. He himself then used some authority to tamper with the branch's Beastshield personnel list, having Xu Yi replace the original Beastshield Soldier No. 16.
