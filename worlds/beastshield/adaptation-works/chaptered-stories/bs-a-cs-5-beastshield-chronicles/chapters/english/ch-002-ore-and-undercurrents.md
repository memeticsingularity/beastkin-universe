# Chapter 2 — Ore and Undercurrents

The next morning, Xu Yi got out of bed early to mail his letter, but was interrupted by an urgent knocking at the door.

Xu Yi opened the door to see two burly beastkin wearing police caps standing one before the other at his doorway, their police uniforms stretched tight and defined by bulging muscles.

"To what do I owe the honor of your visit, officers? Has something happened nearby recently?"

Xu Yi immediately became vigilant, half-opening the door and standing behind it to probe. The white bear police officer standing in front stepped forward to the door, his thick claw gently pushing the door open to its widest and looking into the sparse interior. The other, a fox beastkin holding a form, stood shoulder-to-shoulder with the white bear and delivered the official line.

"Hello, sir. We are from the police investigation team."

The fox beastkin gestured with his eyes for the white bear to show his badge with him, and reached out to stop the other from entering to search. The white bear didn't speak, only silently retreating behind the fox beastkin to observe the Samoyed's every move.

"At 22:53 last night, someone was attacked near here. According to eyewitness descriptions, a white rabbit was seen in the area at the time of the incident. Though I hate to admit that we're doing this to help Beastshield, I must ask... Mr. Xu Yi, who lives in Room 204, have you seen the other party?"

Damn! Did my meddling backfire and get reported by the other party?!

Xu Yi's pupils dilated slightly when he heard this news, but he then composed himself and put on a poker face.

"I know nothing of this matter. I was in my room writing a letter to my teacher all last night and never left." As he spoke, Xu Yi kept his face as straight as possible, not letting the two police officers find any flaw in his expression.

Though Xu Yi said this, the suspicious gazes of the two police officers never shifted an inch from the Samoyed. Especially the white bear who had just observed the small movement — he smiled slightly and directly placed his thick paw on Xu Yi's shoulder.

"If you don't believe me, I can provide the letter I wrote as evidence." Xu Yi took out the letter he had written to his teacher and handed it to Heng Ning. The fox police officer also returned it after a brief inspection.

"Then we apologize for the disturbance, sir. Also, recently there have been unidentified ores circulating. The source is unknown, but touching them will likely cause instant death. Please be vigilant and pay attention to suspicious people or matters. If anything happens, be sure to notify the police immediately."

The white bear's paw on Xu Yi's shoulder was gently pulled back by the fox. After leaving this reminder, he led the white bear away.

"I won't see you out." Xu Yi watched the two police officers until they completely disappeared from view before he let out a sigh of relief.

"Why didn't you expose him just now? That little mutt was clearly lying." In the apartment hallway, the white bear frowned and lowered his voice, his dissatisfaction and confusion evident.

"You know, we don't need to go that far with civilians. This is Beastshield's own business; there's no need to take it so seriously."

The fox beastkin sighed lightly, taking a cigarette from the pack in his pocket and holding it between his teeth.

"Even if the police department is under pressure from the other side, we don't need to completely follow their instructions. Just go through the motions and put on a show. If we can't produce results, the Beastshield branch can't do anything to us either."

After hearing this, the white bear stopped speaking, only smiling and nudging the fox beside him with his elbow.

On the other side, Xu Yi, having obtained new intelligence from the police, began using existing information to infer the current situation.

Those two police officers just said there were unidentified ores circulating, but I've been gathering intelligence here for so long and haven't seen any new mines opened, nor have there been any newly constructed mines recently... Could it be those two abandoned mines? But I went to investigate them not long ago, and there were clearly no recent mining traces there.

Lethal ores, huh... Could that just be a lie Beastshield Company uses to cover themselves? But that possibility can't be ruled out either... The worst possibility is that the lethal ore is the "Blood Essence Stone" that Teacher wants to find and destroy, and Beastshield Company may already be using the Blood Essence Stone for beastkin body experiments.

"Looks like I need to go again, but before that, I must notify Teacher first." Xu Yi organized the clues in his mind, directing his gaze toward the two long-abandoned mines. After confirming his target, he took out his paw phone to contact his teacher.

"Paw phone is off... Teacher, what are you doing."

Xu Yi was somewhat helpless, rubbing his eye sockets with two fingers. Could Teacher have locked his paw phone in a cabinet again without charging it? After lurking for a year, he finally gathered intelligence but couldn't reach anyone...

There was no other way. He could only write it in a letter and send it to him, hoping he would come immediately after seeing it.

"It's the modern era, why does Teacher still insist I use letters to contact him... He said I could call in emergencies, but every time I call, I can't reach him."

Helpless, Xu Yi sighed again. He could only take out pen and paper to add the new intelligence to the letter, seal the envelope, and then come to the rooftop to leap all the way to the post office.

After mailing the letter, Xu Yi turned and walked toward a quaint traditional Chinese medicine shop near the post office. The wooden sign above the door was engraved with three vigorous characters: "Fu Yuan Tang."

Pushing open the wooden door, as soon as he stepped inside, Xu Yi was enveloped by the astringent, bitter aroma of roots, stems, and herbal medicines. The shop's interior was simple and classical, with rows of neatly arranged traditional medicine cabinets all bearing exquisite handwritten labels.

"Hello, what medicine would you like... oh, it's you, Little Rabbit."

Xu Yi had just prepared to speak when he was stopped by an old, low voice. The tone rose at the end of the nickname, mixed with uncontainable laughter and joy.

"Don't call me Little Rabbit in public, Grandpa Fang." The Samoyed shook his head helplessly, turning back to look at the old sheep physician standing before the medicine cabinet, wearing a long gown, reading glasses, a goatee, with a focused and gentle gaze.

The sheep Fang Qiu Yang before Xu Yi was the second person besides Ji Yu who knew Xu Yi was a Mao Beast. He was not only an old friend of Ji Yu's but also a descendant of one of the Twelve Beasts' physicians.

"You ungrateful little thing. I've been prescribing medicine for you since you were by your teacher's side. Calling you that isn't too much. Here, take it."

Refuted, Fang Qiu Yang still smiled kindly and teased the other two sentences. Then he turned around, bent down, and took out medicine he had prepared in advance for Xu Yi from the cabinet at his feet, placing it on the table.

"Hmm... Grandpa Fang, I need a few extra doses today. I won't be coming to pick up medicine after this." Xu Yi directly explained the situation; he didn't want to drag a physician unskilled in combat into this.

"Going undercover again, right? Little Rabbit is truly like a brick — moved wherever needed." The sheep let out a long sigh, then turned his back to prescribe medicine for the Samoyed.

"Can't be helped. Who else can move now except Little Rabbit?" Xu Yi spread his paws to express helplessness.

It turned out that the Twelve Beasts was a special forces unit born from the Chinese zodiac. This special forces unit transformed themselves into warriors hunting Phage Beasts through pills and runes.

But after hunting the Phage Beasts' leader, the "Blood Monarch," the Twelve Beasts retreated from the front stage of history, and most of the methods to transform beastkin into Twelve Beasts were lost in the river of time.

"Stay safe. Remember to visit this old man's medicine shop more often when you have time." Fang Qiu Yang joked, his sheep paws marked by the passage of years caressing Xu Yi's head with concern, then using his thumb to gently wipe Xu Yi's face.

"Yes, goodbye, Grandpa Fang." Xu Yi took a deep breath, bid farewell to Fang Qiu Yang, picked up the several packages of wrapped Chinese medicine, and left Fu Yuan Tang.

Strangely, along the way back, Xu Yi saw quite a few beastkin standing outside shops or houses crying.

Asking one of the crying cow beastkin, he learned that Beastshield Company was using yesterday's employee attack incident as a pretext to arrest beastkin everywhere for forced service. Many beastkin had voluntarily surrendered out of fear of implicating their families.

"I must go to Beastshield Company."

After hearing the cow beastkin's grievances, Xu Yi's gaze sharpened. He secretly set his itinerary, simply comforted the cow beastkin a few words, and waited until the other's emotions stabilized somewhat before returning to his rental apartment to prepare.

In the rental apartment, he boiled the Chinese medicine according to the dosage into medicinal soup and drank it. The side effect symptoms that could flare up at any time in his body dissipated with the soup entering his throat. That controllable sensation only made Xu Yi feel his body was very relaxed.

"Though I'd really like to wear a long robe for action, it's not suitable for infiltrating Beastshield."

Then the Samoyed opened his wardrobe, changed into the gray-black short-sleeved shorts he wore during previous operations, put on a baseball cap, and quietly waited for nightfall.

"Beep beep beep." The alarm on his paw phone rang, signaling that the time had come. Xu Yi turned it off, tossed it on the bed, and headed to the rooftop to leap and sprint at full speed toward Beastshield across the building rooftops.
