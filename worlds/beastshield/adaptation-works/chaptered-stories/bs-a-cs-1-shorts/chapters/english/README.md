## 1 📖 系列简介 / Series Introduction

**中文**  
**作品编码**: `bs-a-cs-1-shorts`  
**作品性质**: 改编作品 (Adaptation Work)  
**作品类型**: 支线分章故事 (Chaptered Side Story)  
**创作模式**: AI与人类共创  
**世界观**: 兽盾世界观 (Beastshield Universe)  
**系列状态**: 更新中 (4章已完成)

这是一个基于**兽盾世界观**
的日常短篇故事集，通过四个独立成篇的故事，展现兽盾公司不同场景下的生活切片。系列采用AI与人类共创模式，深度挖掘官方设定中的日常场景，为每个G级杂兵赋予完整的生命故事。

**English**  
**Work Code**: `bs-a-cs-1-shorts`  
**Nature**: Adaptation Work  
**Type**: Chaptered Side Story  
**Creation Mode**: AI-Human Co-creation  
**Universe**: Beastshield Universe  
**Status**: Updating (4 chapters completed)

This is a collection of daily short stories based on the **Beastshield Universe**, presenting slices
of life in different scenarios within the Beastshield Company through four independent stories. The
series adopts an AI-human co-creation model, deeply exploring daily scenes from official settings,
and endowing each G-rank grunt with a complete life story.

**中文**
