aroma of birthday
cake mingles with the metallic scent of blood, the fishy pungency of semen, and the faintly sour

story of warmth and chill reveals the cruelest everyday reality within the **Beast Shield World**.*

## Scene-1 After Work

At 5:20 PM, in the **training room** on the third floor of the **Beast Shield Company** building,
the **bull beastman**
Azhuang finished his last set of squats.

His thick bull-leg muscles tightened like rocks during exertion, the layer of fat covering them
undulating like waves.
The barbell plates on the squat rack collided, producing dull metallic clangs. Sweat seeped from
between his short,
stiff yellowish fur, converging into thin streams that trickled down the muscular grooves of his
neck, soaking the
collar of his **olive-green tight-fitting uniform**.

Azhuang took a deep breath, his chest expanding, his round, thick pectoral muscles stretching the
uniform into full
curves. He slowly returned the barbell to the rack, the contact between metal and metal making a
crisp "clang." He
straightened up, the thick muscles of his waist and abdomen relaxing, the layer of fat covering his
abdominal contours
swaying slightly. He wiped his face with a hand, his palm registering the combined sensation of his
rough skin, short
stiff fur, and warm sweat.

A dozen or so **Beast Shield employees** were still working out in the **training room**. The air
was thick with the
mingled scents of male beastman sweat, body odor, metal, and rubber. A **brown bear beastman** was
bench-pressing, veins
bulging on arms thick as tree trunks, his pectoral muscles swelling like two solid boulders beneath
a tight tank top.
Further away, several **wolf beastmen** were practicing combat moves, swift and fierce, their lean
muscle lines rapidly
contracting and extending beneath their uniforms.

Azhuang walked towards the water cooler by the wall. His steps were heavy but steady, his *
*olive-green sneakers**
making faint "squeaks" on the rubber floor. The thick muscles of his calves were clearly outlined
beneath his **white
long socks**, the olive-green stripe at the sock cuff stretched somewhat out of shape.

He filled a cup to the brim and drank it down. As the water passed his throat, he could feel the
muscles on the side of
his neck sliding. After drinking, he looked down at the **Beast Shield LOGO** on the left side of
his chest—the
shield-shaped emblem had been washed somewhat pale, its edges slightly frayed.

Seven years.

He thought.

Today was his birthday. Thirty-one.

No colleague knew. At **Beast Shield Company**, no one cared about the birthday of a **G-rank grunt
**. They only cared
about your number, your mission completion rate, how much labor you contributed each month. Azhuang
was long used to it.
He was just an ordinary yellow-skinned bull beastman, number **G-277**, monthly salary one hundred *
*RUB**, duties
including patrol, guard duty, loading goods—all purely physical tasks that required no thought.

He walked to his locker and twisted the combination lock open with thick fingers. The click of the
lock bolt was faint.
He opened the door, took out his belongings: a towel, a small bottle of cheap shower gel, and a set
of clean casual
clothes. When he shut the locker door, the metal frame collided with the cabinet with a dull "thud."

He began to take off his sweat-soaked uniform. Rough fingers unzipped the side of the short sleeve,
then grabbed the hem
and pulled up. The wet fabric clung to his skin, making a slight "rip" sound as it peeled away. His
burly upper body was
exposed to the air—yellowish skin covered in short, stiff fur, solid muscle groups beneath a thick
fat layer. His round
pectoral muscles were full and prominent, two deep brown **nipples** the size of cherries embedded
in the center of
each, surrounded by a darker areola. His shoulders were broad, deltoids rising like small hills, his
arms thick, the
contours of his biceps and triceps clearly visible.

He dried himself with the towel. The rough fibers rubbed against his skin, carrying away sweat and
heat. He could feel
the warmth of his muscles, the rough texture of his skin, the sensation of his fur being flattened
and springing back
beneath the towel. When wiping his chest, he accidentally brushed his own **nipple**, sending a
faint tingling
sensation. He paused.

Bull beastmen's **nipples** were sensitive. He remembered his mother—though in this all-male
reproduction world, mothers
were also male, but they all called the birth-giver that—saying it was one of their racial traits;
with enough
stimulation, they could secrete milk. Azhuang had never verified it. He thought it was too private,
too... weird.

He shook his head, dismissing the thoughts, continued drying, then changed into clean casual
clothes—a loose gray
T-shirt and dark work pants. The fabric was rough but much more comfortable than the tight uniform.
Finally, he put on
his worn sneakers, their soles already somewhat flattened.

He stuffed the wet uniform and **white long socks** into a plastic bag, picked it up, and walked
towards the **training
room** door.

Passing a mirror, he caught a glimpse of his reflection: an ordinary bull beastman, stocky but not
imposing,
honest-looking, eyes gentle. The two curved horns on his head gleamed with a light brown luster
under the light, their
tips slightly worn. His nose was broad, nostrils flaring slightly with each breath. His lips were
thick, the corners
naturally downturned, giving him a simple, even somewhat clumsy, appearance.

He pushed the door open and entered the corridor.

## Scene-2 Corridor and Farewell

The corridor in the **Beast Shield Company** building was long, walls painted gray-white,
fluorescent tubes on the
ceiling emitting cold white light. The floor was covered in dark green vinyl, polished shiny by
countless shoe soles.

Azhuang's footsteps echoed in the empty corridor. He could hear faint sounds from distant
floors—voices, doors opening
and closing, the low hum of equipment. The air was filled with a mix of disinfectant, dust, and male
body odor.

He reached the elevator and pressed the down button. The button was metal, its surface somewhat
dented from repeated
pressing, cool to the touch. Waiting, he looked down at his hands. They were broad and thick,
knuckles large, palms
covered in calluses. These were hands for manual labor.

The elevator doors opened, empty inside. He entered and pressed the button for the first floor. The
elevator descended
slowly; he could feel a slight sense of weightlessness. The metal cabin emitted a low hum.

He remembered seven years ago when he first entered this building. He was twenty-four, fresh from
his hometown to the
city, unable to find a job. Hearing that **Beast Shield Company** was hiring, with food and lodging
provided and a
stable income, he came. The interview was simple—a strength test, a look at his build, and he
passed. When he received
his first **green uniform**, he even felt a bit proud.

Now... now he just felt tired.

Not physically tired. That heavy, indescribable kind of tiredness in his heart.

The elevator stopped at the first floor, doors opening.

The lobby was spacious, floor tiled with polished marble. Directly facing the elevator, a huge *
*Beast Shield Company**
emblem hung on the wall—a black shield with an interlocking claw pattern in the center. Below the
emblem were a few
potted plants, their leaves covered in a thin layer of dust.

Azhuang walked towards the main entrance. Passing the security booth, he slowed down.

Inside the booth, a **gray bear beastman** was dozing in a chair. He wore the same **olive-green
uniform**, his burly
body filling the chair completely, his round belly bulging beneath the tight fabric. His bear paws
rested on the desk,
the claws at his fingertips gleaming with a dull sheen in the light. His eyes were closed, his chest
rising and falling
slowly with breath, soft snores emanating from his throat.

Azhuang hesitated, then raised a hand and gently tapped on the glass window.

The gray bear beastman jerked awake, eyes wide open. Seeing it was Azhuang, he relaxed, yawned,
revealing sharp teeth.

"Azhuang," he said, voice coarse and husky, tinged with sleepiness. "Leaving so early today?"

"Yeah, got some things," Azhuang said. His voice was low, carrying the resonant timbre
characteristic of bull beastmen.

The gray bear beastman—everyone called him Brother Bear—rubbed his eyes, thick fingers brushing
through the short fur on
his face. "See you tomorrow."

"See you tomorrow." Azhuang nodded.

He turned to leave, then stopped, looking back to add, "Brother Bear, be careful on duty tonight.
Heard there are *
*Bounty Hunters** active nearby recently."

Brother Bear grinned, the smile somewhat fierce. "Let them come. I'm itching for a fight anyway."

Azhuang said nothing more. He knew Brother Bear was strong, a **G-rank** but with plenty of combat
experience. He
himself wasn't. He was strong but slow, only knew the most basic fighting moves. If he really
encountered a **Bounty
Hunter**...

He shook his head, dispelling the ominous thought.

Today was his birthday. He should think positive.

He pushed open the heavy glass doors, and the evening sunlight hit him.

## Scene-3 Walking to the Cake Shop

The sun at 5:30 PM was already low, its light tinged orange-red. The sky was gradient, from pale
blue overhead to
orange-yellow in the distance, a few clouds gilded at the edges.

The **Beast Shield Company** building was located at the edge of the industrial district of **White
City**, surrounded
mostly by factories, warehouses, and low-rise office buildings. The streets were wide but not busy.
The cement pavement
of the sidewalk had a few cracks, stubborn weeds growing from within.

Azhuang took a deep breath. The outside air was much fresher than inside the building, mixed with
sunlight, dust,
distant vehicle exhaust, and faint food scents. He picked out a hint of sweetness—from the bakery a
few blocks away.

His destination was "Sweet Corner," a small cake shop. It was where he went every year for his
birthday.

He began walking along the sidewalk. His pace wasn't fast, each step planted firmly. The soles of
his sneakers made a
soft "shushing" sound against the cement. His thick arms hung naturally at his sides, swaying
slightly with his steps.

Occasional passersby walked by. A **dog beastman** holding hands with a **cat beastman**, both in
casual clothes,
talking softly. A truck drove past, its cargo bed stacked with cardboard boxes, the engine's roar
growing louder then
fading. At a street corner, an elderly **goat beastman** had a fruit stall piled with apples,
oranges, and bananas,
their fresh scent drifting in the air.

As Azhuang passed, the old goat glanced up, his gaze pausing on Azhuang's face for a moment before
lowering again.

Azhuang knew his identity was obvious—the stocky build, bull beastman features, and that
ever-present alertness and
weariness that came with working at **Beast Shield**. Maybe the old goat recognized him as a **Beast
Shield employee**,
maybe not. It didn't matter.

He kept walking.

Turning a corner, the environment changed. This was an old commercial street lined with various
small shops: a grocery,
a tailor, a hardware store, food stalls. Signs were colorful, some faded and peeling. Puddles on the
ground reflected
the sky's colors.

The scents in the air grew complex too. The greasiness of fried food, the richness of a spice shop,
the faint rot of
garbage bins, the laundry detergent scent of drying clothes... all mingling together.

Azhuang knew this road well. He'd been walking it for seven years.

He remembered the first year. He'd just been employed three months, received his first salary.
Counting those crumpled *
*RUB** notes, he thought about what to buy to celebrate his twenty-fifth birthday. Passing "Sweet
Corner," he was drawn
to the cakes displayed in the window. Pink cream, bright red fruit, and "Happy Birthday" written in
chocolate sauce.

He hesitated a long time before pushing the door open.

The cake that day was sweet. So sweet he almost cried.

Every year after, he came.

This was the seventh time.

He reached the middle of the commercial street and, next to a convenience store, saw the familiar
glass door.

"Sweet Corner."

The sign was hand-painted in pink letters, surrounded by drawings of cream and strawberries. The
display window was
clean, showcasing a few sample cakes: a six-inch cream fruit cake, a chocolate mousse, a cheesecake.
A paper was stuck
to the glass listing "Today's Specials" and prices of some drinks.

The door was wood-framed glass, a string of bells hanging from the handle.

Azhuang stopped at the entrance.

First, he checked his attire—gray T-shirt, work pants, old sneakers. Not wearing the **Beast Shield
uniform**. Good. He
didn't want to scare the owner or attract attention.

Then, he took a deep breath, raised his hand, and pushed the door open.

The bells rang.

A clear "ding-a-ling," like a gentle reminder.

## Scene-4 Inside the Shop

The interior wasn't large, about twenty square meters. Walls painted light yellow, a few pastoral
landscape pictures
pasted on them. A vintage-style chandelier hung from the ceiling, casting warm yellow light. To the
left was a glass
counter displaying various pastries; to the right, two small round tables with chairs. At the back
were the cash
register and a door leading to the kitchen.

The air was thick with a rich, sweet aroma—the warm scent of cream, sugar, flour, eggs, and fruit
mixed and baked. This
scent enveloped everyone who entered, like a soft embrace.

Azhuang stood at the door, letting his eyes adjust to the indoor light.

"Azhuang's here!"

A cheerful voice came from behind the counter. Then, a furry head popped out—the **squirrel beastman
** shop owner.
He—also male, but Azhuang was used to using "she" for this petite, gentle, soft-spoken
shopkeeper—had reddish-brown fur,
round black eyes, a small nose and mouth. She wore a pink apron with a few flour stains.

"Hello, boss," Azhuang said. His voice sounded even deeper in the confined space.

"Knew you'd come today." The owner came out from behind the counter. She was very short, only
reaching Azhuang's chest.
She looked up at him, eyes curved like crescent moons. "This year is... the seventh, right?"

"Yeah, seventh year." Azhuang nodded. He noticed a few more fine lines at the corners of her eyes,
but her smile was as
warm as seven years ago.

"The usual? Six-inch fruit cream cake, with 'Happy Birthday' written on top?" she said, walking
towards the refrigerated
display case. Her movements were quick and nimble, the apron hem swaying with her steps.

"Yeah, the usual." Azhuang said.

The owner opened the glass door of the refrigerated case, cold air rushing out carrying an even
stronger cream scent.
She took out a pre-packaged pink cake box. The box was tied with a ribbon, a simple bow at the top.

She placed the cake box on the counter with a soft "thud."

"Got it ready for you early," she said, a hint of pride in her voice. "Made fresh this morning.
Cream is real
buttercream, fruit is all fresh, strawberries especially sweet."

Azhuang looked at the pink box. He could imagine its contents: white buttercream frosting, a border
of piped
decorations, surface covered with sliced strawberries, mango, and kiwi. Right in the center, "Happy
Birthday" written in
red fruit jam.

The same every year.

That made him feel at ease.

"Thank you, boss," he said. Then he took out money from his work pants pocket—a few crumpled **RUB**
notes. He counted
out twenty RUB and placed them on the counter. The edges of the notes were worn, the paper surface
glossy.

The owner took the money, her fingertips brushing Azhuang's palm. Her fingers were slender, skin
smooth, temperature
slightly cooler than Azhuang's. He could feel that slight coolness.

"Wait, I'll get your change." The owner turned towards the cash register. Opening a drawer, coins
clinked. "Twenty
RUB... cake is eighteen... change is two RUB."

She returned, placing two coins in Azhuang's palm. They were metal, cool to the touch, edges
slightly sharp.

Azhuang pocketed the coins, his fingers brushing his own palm. It was warm, with thin calluses.

"Azhuang," the owner didn't immediately push the cake box over but leaned against the counter,
looking up at him. "You
should find a partner. It's so lonely spending birthdays alone."

Azhuang was silent for a moment. His thick fingers unconsciously rubbed the seam of his pants. The
fabric was rough,
loose threads slightly prickly.

"Not in a hurry," he finally said, voice even lower.

"How can you not be?" The owner sighed. "You're thirty-one now, right? In this world, between
males... though it's not
easy, you have to try. I think you're honest, hardworking, you'll surely find someone suitable."

Azhuang didn't know how to respond. He thought of the relationships at **Beast Shield Company**: the
brief entanglements
that sometimes followed blatant physical clashes between **Combat Soldiers**, the domination and
control of **overseers
** over **grunts**, and those unspoken transactions in the dark.

He didn't want any of that.

He wanted... he didn't know what he wanted either. Maybe just someone to eat cake with, to talk to,
to share warmth with
on long nights.

But it was too hard.

In this world where all characters were male beastmen capable of reproduction through special
physiology and hormonal
regulation, procreation was possible. But Azhuang had never delved into that. It felt distant to

"I'll think about it," he finally said. It was a polite phrase.

The owner looked at him, seemed to see through his evasion but didn't press. She just smiled and
pushed the cake box
towards him.

"Well then, happy birthday, Azhuang."

"Thank you, boss."

Azhuang reached out to take the cake box. His fingers were thick, his movements somewhat clumsy. The
pink box surface
was smooth, slightly cool to the touch. Carefully holding the box, he felt its weight—the solidity
of the cake, the
softness of the cream, the moisture of the fruit, all faintly transmitted through the cardboard.

He turned, ready to leave.

The bells rang again.

But not from the door he pushed.

## Scene-5 The Intruder

The door was flung open violently, with such force the bells shook wildly, emitting a harsh "
ding-a-ling clatter." The
door hit the wall, bounced back with a dull "thud."

A figure blocked the doorway, cutting off most of the light.

Azhuang froze. Holding the cake box, he stood still, his eyes adjusting to the silhouette backlit.

It was a **wolf beastman**.

A lean, wiry build, muscle lines clearly visible under a tight black tactical vest. His fur was dark
gray, mixed with
some dark brown markings. His wolf ears stood erect, pointed, slightly tilted forward. His face was
lean, cheekbones
prominent, eyes gleaming with cold, pale gold in the shadows. His mouth was slightly open, revealing
sharp canine teeth.

He wore work pants, a multi-functional tactical belt around his waist holding several items: a
sheath for a **steel
dagger**, two magazine pouches, a small first-aid kit, a coil of rope. The dagger's hilt was
exposed, black with
anti-slip grooves. On his feet were heavy tactical boots, soles caked with mud and dust.

He stood at the door, scanning the shop—first the owner behind the counter, then landing on Azhuang.
More precisely, on
Azhuang's burly build, bull beastman features, and the pink cake box in his hands.

The wolf beastman's mouth corners twitched upward, not quite a smile.

"Well, a **Beast Shield** minnow here."

His voice carried the raspy quality characteristic of wolves, with a mocking lilt at the end.

Azhuang's heart jumped. Instinctively, he took a half-step back, his back pressing against the edge
of the counter. The
cool marble surface seeped through his thin T-shirt.

The owner's face instantly paled. Her small hands clutched the edge of her apron, knuckles white
with force. She opened
her mouth but made no sound.

The wolf beastman entered the shop. His steps were light, tactical boot soles making almost no sound
on the floor. But
each step carried a predator's ease and pressure.

The bells slowly stopped swaying behind him.

The door closed automatically, cutting off most outdoor light. Inside, only the warm yellow glow of
the chandelier
remained, casting long, shifting shadows of the three figures.

The wolf beastman walked to the center of the shop and stopped. Less than three meters between him
and Azhuang. Azhuang

his arms, his throat's Adam's apple sliding slightly with each breath. He could smell the scents

"G-rank, huh?" The wolf beastman tilted his head, pale gold eyes appraising Azhuang up and down. "
Green uniform, tsk,
one hundred RUB."

"I... I'm off duty," he said. His voice was drier than usual. "I just came to buy cake."

"Buy cake?" The wolf beastman repeated, tone rising, clearly mocking. He took a step forward.

Azhuang retreated again, but his back was already against the counter, nowhere to go. His back
pressed fully against the

The wolf beastman took another step forward. Now less than two meters apart. Azhuang could see more

He was half a head
shorter than Azhuang, but looking up at him, his presence was completely overwhelming.

Too fast. Azhuang didn't even see the movement, just felt his hands empty—the cake box was snatched

"Happy Birthday."

Rounded font, vivid color, strikingly visible against the white cream.

Then he looked up at Azhuang. In those pale gold eyes, the mocking glint was gone, replaced by

Then he smiled.

A real smile this time. Mouth corners stretching, revealing more sharp teeth, fine lines deepening
at his eyes. But the

"So today's your birthday?" he said. "What a coincidence, let me give you a big gift."

## Scene-6 Violence Begins

The wolf beastman, still holding the cake box in his left hand, suddenly thrust his right hand
forward, fingers splayed,

Azhuang didn't even have time to react—he was just a **G-rank grunt**, not subjected to the brutal

Soldiers**, nor the agility of **Firearm Soldiers**. His mind was still stuck on "the cake was

All he felt was a tremendous force from the back of his neck.

Not a push, not a pull, but a precise, powerful, irresistible downward pressure.

His head was forced downward.

His vision changed rapidly: the chandeliered ceiling became the counter's marble surface, then the
open interior of the
cake box. The pink cardboard interior enlarged before his eyes, then the white cream, red

"Ugh—!"

Suffocation followed immediately.

He couldn't breathe. His nasal passages were filled with cream, the viscous fluid clogging his

He began to struggle.

Thick arms flailed wildly, hitting the counter. Fists pounded the marble surface with dull "thuds."
His legs kicked,

But that hand was like an iron clamp.

The wolf's palm wasn't large, but his fingers were strong, fingertips pressing precisely near the
Dazhui point at the

"Mmm... ugh... hck..."

30%...

Not here.

Not like this.

nauseating yet strangely sad olfactory combination.

Azhuang's story leaves my chest tight. He so ordinary, so insignificant, his death like a crushed
leaf in an alley,
nothing left but that faint "crunch." No earth-shattering past, no epic dreams, his biggest wish
just "eat cake on
birthday each year." In this all-male beastman world full of violence, power, and sexual tension (
manifested as **Lust
Gauge**), such simple wishes seem luxurious instead.

The wolf's indifference is systemic product. In a world encouraging hunting, exchanging white socks
for bounties,
compassion is unnecessary burden. He eats the semen-smeared cake not from perverse pleasure, but a
complete
objectification of prey: your body is bounty, your semen is seasoning, your life just part of my
drinking money. This
coldness chills more than direct cruelty.

And the squirrel owner, she's bystander to this cruel world, also innocent victim. Her "Sweet
Corner" should provide
warmth and sweetness, now forever stained with death and semen's fishiness. Her fear is the system's
most direct
oppression on ordinary civilians: you never know when violence will crash into your daily life,
turning everything to
nightmare.

What stings most in this story is that "meaningless death." Azhuang did nothing wrong, just clocked
out, went to buy
cake. His death holds no "value," won't advance plot, reveal conspiracies, awaken anyone's
conscience. It just happened,
like a gust of wind, then everything continues. The wolf will keep hunting, Beast Shield keeps
operating, bounty system
keeps recycling white socks. Only Azhuang gone, along with his minuscule anticipation for sweetness.

In this all-male beastman world, gentleness is scarce, sweetness luxury, and life... life often just
a number on a sock
sole, waiting to be exchanged for one hundred RUB.

Happy birthday, Azhuang. May you, in your next life, in a world with sunlight, cake, and no numbers,
grow old slowly.
