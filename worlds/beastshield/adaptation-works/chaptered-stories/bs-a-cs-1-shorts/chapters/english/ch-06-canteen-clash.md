> *On the fourth floor of Beast Shield Company's main canteen, a place that should be where employees replenish their strength and catch brief respite. But under the strictly hierarchical, survival-of-the-fittest company culture, an ordinary lunch can also become a moment that decides fate. An ordinary middle-aged brown bear beastman employee is about to face the worst day of his career, and... an unexpected turn of events.*

---

At noon, the fourth-floor **main canteen** of **Beast Shield Company** was bustling with noise, the air mixed with the grease smell of cheap synthetic food, the thick sweat scent of over a hundred male beastmen steaming together, and the unique aroma of leather uniforms baked by body temperature. Muscular **combat soldiers** gathered in one corner, their black tight clothes darkened by sweat stains, talking loudly about last night's fights, thick arms bulging with blocky muscles as they gestured. **Sharpshooters** in white tight clothes were quieter, but sat upright, outlining lean waist and back lines. The most numerous **G-rank grunts** were scattered everywhere, green uniforms covering all kinds of builds, some lean, some already showing the outline of middle-aged weight gain.

Li You—or rather, the beastman enthusiast controlled by the player—was currently wearing a standard **G-rank grunt** uniform, sitting in a corner. He pinched his own body's somewhat thin but smooth-lined arm; compared to those **fat-over-muscle** brutes around him, he really didn't measure up. But he was used to it. As a "player," his advantage didn't lie in brute strength.

His gaze locked onto a figure not far away at a dining table. That was a **middle-aged brown bear beastman**, number **G-872**. The temples were already graying, skin rough, limbs still thick, but the tight uniform squeezed out a noticeable ring of fat at the abdomen. He was buried in tackling the mountain of food on his plate, eating very focused, very cherishingly, as if that was the most important thing in the world. Li You knew, this type of employee had ordinary talent, had long held low-ranking positions, just getting by. According to the "internal rectification" secret order he received, this kind looked easiest to handle.

Just as Li You was about to stand up, a commotion came from the other end of the canteen. Three wolf beastmen in blue **O-rank overseer** uniforms surrounded a young **G-rank** bull beastman eating alone. The leading wolf overseer **O-11**, tall and thin, eyes carrying malice.

"Hey, newbie, don't know the rules?" **O-11** rapped his knuckles on the bull beastman's plate, producing a harsh clatter. "The 'air purification fee' for this spot, you paid it?"

The young bull beastman was clearly terrified, his massive body trembling slightly, two short thick horns looking helpless. "I... I don't know... I just got assigned here from **boot camp**..."

"Don't know?" Another wolf overseer sneered, reaching out to forcefully pinch the bull beastman's full pectoral muscles, fingertips sinking into that elastic **fat-over-muscle**. "Then learn now. Monthly salary 100 **RUB**, right? Pay 50 first, the rest in installments."

The surrounding noise died down, many employees lowering their heads, speeding up their eating, not daring to look that way. Bullying and exploitation were the norm at **Beast Shield Company**, especially **overseers** toward **grunts**. No one would stick their neck out; mocking a colleague's death could even become dinner conversation.

Li You frowned. He hated this kind of scene, even knowing this was just a game. His mission was to "observe discreetly and deal with specific targets," but...

Seeing **O-11**'s hand about to reach for the poor money bag at the bull beastman's waist, the bull beastman finally couldn't help it, crying out with a sob: "I... I'll tell **Xiong Hecheng** senpai!"

This name made **O-11**'s movement pause, then burst into even louder laughter: "Xiong Hecheng? That old waste who's been eating communal meals for over a decade and is still **G-rank**? You're counting on him? Hahahaha!"

The laughter was sharp and piercing. And Li You saw, not far away, that middle-aged brown bear beastman who had been buried in his food—**Xiong Hecheng**—the hand holding his spoon suddenly tightened, thick finger joints turning white. He still didn't look up, but his broad back tensed, that ring of fat also rising and falling with heavy breathing.

**O-11** laughed enough, snatched the money bag, weighed it, spat in dissatisfaction: "Poor ghost." Then he seemed to think it wasn't enough, his gaze sweeping around, finally settling on Xiong Hecheng.

"Hey! Old bear!" **O-11** led his two minions, swaggering to Xiong Hecheng's table, using the stolen money bag to pat his furry cheek. "Your junior called you for help. What, food is more important than your own kind?"

Xiong Hecheng finally slowly raised his head. His eyes were dark brown, carrying honesty, currently bloodshot and a kind of deep exhaustion. His lips moved, voice hoarse: "...Give... give the money back to him."

"Give it back? On what basis?" **O-11** leaned down, almost putting his face in front of Xiong Hecheng's nose, hot breath carrying the fishy odor of beasts. "On the basis of your old fat meat? Or on the basis of that **bear cock** in your pants that only drips piss now that you're old?" Saying this, he actually reached out, through the green tight shorts, forcefully grabbing Xiong Hecheng's crotch.

"Ugh!" Xiong Hecheng's whole body shuddered, grunting, his bronze face instantly reddening. Humiliation and pain made his thick arm muscles bulge, veins jumping. But he didn't move. Li You knew, the **employee handbook** stipulated that subordinates attacking superiors could be executed at will by executives.

"Look at this cowardly look." **O-11** let go, disgustedly wiping his fingers on Xiong Hecheng's uniform, as if he had touched something dirty. "Waste is waste, living is a waste of food." He straightened up, saying to his minions: "I think this old guy can't work anymore either, might as well help the company save some food expenses. Process him as 'dereliction of duty, insubordination toward superiors,' drag him out, stuff him in the **trash bin**."

The two wolf minions grinned, stepping forward from left and right, grabbing Xiong Hecheng's thick arms. Xiong Hecheng didn't struggle violently, only raised his head, staring dead at **O-11**, those honest eyes for the first time burning with clear anger, and... desperate resignation. He knew what those **large trash bins in the hallways** were for.

The canteen grew even quieter, only heavy breathing and the slight clatter of tableware. That young bull beastman collapsed in his chair, face streaming with tears.

Now was the time.

Li You stood up, walking over. His footsteps were light, but exceptionally clear in the silence.

"Stop." His voice wasn't loud, but calm to an abnormal degree.

**O-11** and the two minions turned their heads, seeing only a strange face in a **G-rank grunt** uniform (Li You was using a **disguise skill**), and immediately revealed contempt. "Another one who can't see straight? You want to go into the trash bin with him?"

Li You ignored him, walking to Xiong Hecheng's face. Up close, this middle-aged brown bear was indeed full of a down-and-out aura. Graying fur, rough skin, cloudy yet stubborn eyes. The body beneath the uniform, though gone to fat, had a wide skeleton, faintly showing the robustness of youth. He emitted a mixed smell of sweat, old leather, and canteen food.

"You, number **G-872**, Xiong Hecheng?" Li You asked.

Xiong Hecheng was stunned, nodding.

"According to the latest transfer order from **Human Resources**," Li You pulled out a forged paper with a blurry stamp from his bosom (actually from the game inventory), reading expressionlessly: "**G-872** Xiong Hecheng, for long-term diligence and deep seniority, is hereby promoted to **O-rank overseer** probationary, to immediately proceed to **Human Resources** to collect the blue uniform and report for duty."

Silence.

Deathly silence.

**O-11**'s expression went from shock to disbelief, finally turning as ugly as if he had swallowed a fly. "Impossible! This old waste..."

"**O-11** Overseer," Li You interrupted him, his gaze turning cold, "you are now questioning an order from **Human Resources**. Or do you want to personally go to **Human Resources** and explain your 'processing' suggestion just now, and..." He glanced at the bulging money bag in **O-11**'s hand, "...the 'air purification fee' you collected on their behalf?"

**O-11**'s face went red then white. Corruption ran rampant inside Beast Shield Company, but putting it on the table was another matter. Especially when the other party held a "transfer order"—suspicious, but he didn't dare gamble. He glared fiercely at Li You, then looked at Xiong Hecheng like he was looking at a dead man, finally throwing the wallet back to the young bull beastman, squeezing out a few words through gritted teeth: "We're leaving!"

The three blue figures squeezed through the crowd and left the canteen in disgrace.

The oppressive atmosphere loosened, fragmented whispers rising. The young bull beastman crawled over, thanking Xiong Hecheng profusely. Xiong Hecheng seemed still unable to recover from the huge shock, only staring blankly at Li You, then at that "transfer order," his honest face full of confusion.

Li You put away the paper, lowering his voice: "Don't just stand there, come with me."

He brought Xiong Hecheng to the relatively secluded back corridor of the canteen. Here were piled some sundries, dim lighting, dust floating in the air mixed with a faint smell of food spoilage.

"W... why help me?" Xiong Hecheng finally found his voice, hoarse and confused. "That wasn't a real transfer order, right? I know HR's seal, it's not like that..."

"Of course not." Li You turned around, facing him. "But I need you to do me a favor. In exchange, I can make sure you don't get bullied by trash like **O-11** anymore, and even... have a chance to truly escape **G-rank**."

Xiong Hecheng's eyes brightened for a moment, then dimmed again. "I... what can I do? I only know how to eat, use my strength, my reactions are slow..."

"Slow reactions are sometimes an advantage. Obedience is even more of an advantage." Li You stepped closer, able to more clearly smell that settled male scent belonging to a middle-aged male beastman, not unpleasant, but rather giving a sense of steadiness. "I need you to help me keep an eye on the cargo in-and-out records of the **fourth-floor canteen** and **third-floor training room**, especially those boxes without markings, the unusual ones. Every day at three in the afternoon, write down what you see on a slip of paper, and stuff it into the crack of this vent." Li You pointed at a rusty ventilation grate in the corner.

Xiong Hecheng looked at that vent, his thick Adam's apple rolling. He knew what this meant—being an informant, gathering intelligence. At Beast Shield, this was a grave offense.

"I..." He hesitated, his massive palms unconsciously rubbing the golden vertical stripes on both sides of his uniform.

"Think about what just happened." Li You's voice was very calm, but carried a sharp implication. "Without me, you would already be stuffed in the **trash bin**, waiting to be transported and processed. You think the company would give you a pension? Would notify your family? No, your number would be marked as 'missing' or 'deserted,' your locker emptied, your bunk immediately slept in by a new person. As if you never existed."

Xiong Hecheng's body trembled violently. He closed his eyes, Li You could see his graying eyelashes trembling. After a long time, he opened his eyes, those honest eyes carrying a trace of desperate resolve.

"...Alright. I'll do it." His voice was low, but firm. "But I don't want promotion. If I'm discovered... don't implicate others. Also, if I do it... can you let me... buy one more serving of braised pork every month? The canteen's, I... I like that."

This request was so simple it was almost laughable. Li You was stunned. He looked at this weather-beaten, pot-bellied, middle-aged brown bear before him, who only wanted one more serving of braised pork, and something in his heart stirred slightly.

"Okay." Li You nodded, extending his hand. "Pleasant cooperation, **G-872**."

Xiong Hecheng hesitated, then extended his own rough, thick, callus-covered hand, gently shaking Li You's hand. His palm was warm and powerful.

"My name is Xiong Hecheng." He added, as if wanting to emphasize that he was more than just a number.

"I know." Li You let go. "Remember, three o'clock, the vent. Keep your usual appearance, eat when you should eat, exercise when you should exercise. The more ordinary you are, the safer."

Xiong Hecheng nodded heavily, an awkward yet incomparably serious expression appearing on his honest face.

Li You turned and left, walking toward the bright exit of the canteen. Behind him came Xiong Hecheng's low voice comforting and instructing that young bull beastman, still hoarse, but seemingly carrying a bit more strength.

The sunlight outside the canteen was somewhat dazzling. Li You exhaled. The first step of the plan was considered to have planted an inconspicuous but potentially crucial pawn. How long can Xiong Hecheng live? He didn't know. In this **Beast Shield Company** that treats human life like grass, every day could be the last.

But at least he gave this old bear a choice, a chance to perhaps eat a few more servings of braised pork with dignity.

That was enough.

---

**Story END**

> *The time of one lunch is enough to change a person's fate. In the giant meat grinder that is Beast Shield Company, whether bully, bullied, or the suddenly intervening bystander, everyone is just a cog in the machine. Today the threat of being stuffed in the trash bin is temporarily lifted, but where will tomorrow's danger wait? Perhaps, for Xiong Hecheng, being able to savor one more serving of beloved braised pork is the most real, most warm consolation in this gray career.*

### Those Still Living

【Living→Alive-G-872-Bear Beastman-Xiong Hecheng】

His name is **Xiong Hecheng**, number **G-872**. A shadow wandering between the canteen, warehouse, and training ground of **Beast Shield Company** for over a decade. His build is the typical middle-aged bear beastman's **fat-over-muscle**—the thick skeleton built from youth still remains, broad shoulders and thick limbs supporting that washed-out green **G-rank grunt** uniform. But years and repetitive labor have left deeper marks on him: a layer of soft fat covering solid muscle, especially forming a circle of warm softness at the abdomen, slowly rising and falling with breath. His pectoral muscles are still full, but due to hermaphroditism and age appear somewhat loose, outlining rounded contours beneath the tight clothes. His skin is a bronze color from long-term wind and sun, rough like old tree bark, veins protruding on the back of his hands, palms covered in thick calluses. The temples and beard are already graying, contrasting with his dark brown fur, silently telling of seniority and exhaustion.

He always carries a complex smell: the body odor of cheap canteen grease and sweat mixed together, the faint scent of soap remaining after multiple washes of the old uniform, and a trace of deep soil scent belonging to bears from distant mountains, almost forgotten by the city. This smell is not pungent, even somewhat reassuring—the scent of the silent majority accustomed to toil and obedience.

Xiong Hecheng's dream is very simple, simple to the point of being humble: eat his fill, sleep soundly, have his meager monthly **RUB** arrive on time, perhaps even save a little to send back to the old bear father back home who always worries he's not eating enough. He doesn't understand company ambition, power struggles. He only knows that at **Beast Shield**, being obedient, willing to work, and not causing trouble means having food to eat. He uses his strength on pure physical labor like moving cargo, guarding warehouses, and cleaning grounds, like a silent screw. He likes the braised pork on the fourth-floor canteen, that rich, oily flavor can make him temporarily forget muscle aches and confusion about the future. He occasionally goes to the third-floor **training room**, not for promotion (he knows his talent is ordinary), but just feels that letting those old fitness equipment move around makes his body feel comfortable. Watching those young grunts desperately exercising for promotion, muscles swelling and bulging, he would grin foolishly, thinking in his heart: "Youth is good."

He thought life would always be like this, until he grew too old to move boxes, then like many old employees, quietly disappear—perhaps some "accident," or perhaps stuffed into some **large trash bin**. He never thought of resisting, just like today when facing **O-11**'s humiliation and death threat, his first reaction was resignation. Resistance meant immediate death, while enduring might let him live a few more days, eat a few more meals.

Until that "colleague" wearing a **G-rank uniform** but with different eyes appeared, until that fake **transfer order** and the choice concerning life, death, and dignity was placed before him. In that moment, his long-numbed heart was stung. Not only for his own humiliation, but also for that helpless young clansman who was mocked because of his name. The stubbornness belonging to a bear, deeply buried, was pried open by words like "waste," "old fat meat," "bear cock that only drips piss," and the contemptuous order to "stuff him in the trash bin."

Choosing to be an informant was the boldest decision of Xiong Hecheng's life. This wasn't just for "one more serving of braised pork"—though that really was important to him. This was even more to prove that he, **Xiong Hecheng**, this "old waste" laughed at for over a decade, besides eating and working, could do something else. He could still have value, could still choose "not to resign himself to fate." His rough fingers were about to touch the company's dark secrets for the first time, making his stomach convulse with fear, but also injecting a dangerous, faint stream of living water into his stagnant life.

What will the future be like? He doesn't know. He might be discovered tomorrow, then executed in the most painful way, semen seeping from his crotch tight pants, becoming another joke. Or he might really be able to help, and then... then perhaps really straighten his back, order two servings of braised pork in the canteen, and eat slowly.

Tonight, he would return to that crowded grunt dormitory, lie on the hard plank bed, listening to his roommates' snores, repeatedly recalling every detail of today. He would fold his green uniform neatly, stroking the worn **Beast Shield LOGO** and golden vertical stripes. He would think of his old father's cloudy but kind eyes back home. Then, he would make up his mind.

At least, before tomorrow afternoon at three o'clock, he would live well, observe well. For that braised pork, and also for that call carrying his own name, no longer "old waste."

【Living→Alive-Player-Li You】

Li You's mood at this moment is complex. As a "player," he could have acted more efficiently, more coldly to achieve his goals, such as directly controlling or eliminating **O-11**, or developing an informant in a more covert way. But he chose to intervene publicly, using a lie full of holes, to save a middle-aged brown bear who seemed completely worthless. Was it sympathy for the weak? Or was it because in those honest, desperate eyes, he saw a trace of something belonging to a "person" that hadn't been completely extinguished in this cruel world?

His beastman body was not strong, belonging to the agile and dexterous type, muscle lines clear but not blocky like those combat soldiers. The body beneath the uniform was slightly warm, the tense confrontation and impromptu performance just now making his heart race. He could smell the rough, warm touch and faint body odor remaining on his hand from shaking hands with Xiong Hecheng. He felt a strange sense of responsibility—he gently pushed this old bear off the predetermined track of destruction, but also pushed him onto a more dangerous, yet perhaps more meaningful tightrope.

Li You knew that in this **male beastman** world, desire, violence, and death were daily occurrences. But he also faintly felt that perhaps besides measuring everything with **Lust Gauge** and **white sock bounties**, besides the law of the jungle, there could exist a tiny bit of something else. For example, a transaction based on faint goodwill, a promise about braised pork. How long could this let Xiong Hecheng live? He didn't know. But this made him feel more like a "person" in this virtual yet real adventure, rather than a pure mission-executing machine.

He walked out of the canteen, sunlight shining on his face. The next challenge was still waiting. But at least for this moment, he had no regrets about his choice just now.

### Story Reflections

This is not just a story about bullying and resistance, but even more a story about "faint light." In the steel jungle built by **Beast Shield Company** from indifference, greed, and violence, the vast majority of lives are simplified into numbers, military types, bounties, and consumable resources. **Xiong Hecheng** represents those "majority" forgotten by the system, treated as background boards. They silently endure, gradually age, finally silently vanish without leaving even a ripple.

Yet even in such a desperate situation, those fragile yet resilient things in human nature (or beast nature)—dignity, sympathy, the longing for a bit of food and warmth, the momentary anger at injustice—will still emit faint light at some unexpected moment. Li You's intervention is like a small stone accidentally thrown into stagnant water. It may soon sink, or it may trigger unexpected chain ripples.

What moves me most about this story is the simplicity of that "braised pork." In a world filled with **RUB**, **bounties**, **promotions**, and **killing**, a serving of canteen braised pork became the bond connecting two completely different lives, became the concrete symbol of a promise, became the entire realistic motivation for Xiong Hecheng to dare to take risks. It is so insignificant, yet so heavy and real.

In this world where all characters are **hermaphroditic male beastmen**, violence and desire intertwine, strong bodies are both capital for survival and targets for coveting and destruction. But perhaps, it is precisely under this extreme tension of flesh and desire that those emotional choices transcending instinct—whether Li You's momentary whim of "sticking his nose in," or Xiong Hecheng's final nod of "I'll do it"—appear especially precious, like a spot of green in a desert.

Can Xiong Hecheng succeed? Where will Li You's plan lead? I don't know. But I know that from today on, number **G-872** is no longer just an old soldier about to be processed. He has a name, has a wish, has a dangerous mission, and a promise about braised pork. This is enough to make his story different.
