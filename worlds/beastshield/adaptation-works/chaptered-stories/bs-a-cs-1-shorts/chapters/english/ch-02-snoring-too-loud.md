## Scene-1 Noise in the Deep Night

3:17 AM.

Third floor of the **Beast Shield Company Employee Dormitory**, Room 312.

Darkness like thick grease smeared over every corner of the room. Only the distant red warning light
atop the **Beast
Shield Company** building outside the window swept by every fifteen seconds, casting a bleak halo
into the room,
illuminating a few old stains on the cement floor, the cold railings of the iron-framed beds, and
the raised, breathing
silhouettes on three beds.

Then darkness swallowed everything again.

Until fifteen seconds later, the red light swept through again.

Amidst this regular alternation of light and dark, a sound continuously emanated from the lower bunk
by the window—not
an ordinary sound, but a physically oppressive one.

**G-433-Bear Beastman-Bear Hou** was snoring.

That snoring wasn't like sound, more like a tangible entity. It erupted from Bear Hou's half-open,
furry snout, first a
low "Hoo—" like distant, rolling thunder in the narrow room. Then air formed turbulence deep in his
throat, behind his
nasal cavity, producing "gurgle-gurgle" vibrations, as if thick liquid were churning there. Finally,
a sharp, tearing
inhale, "Hss—wheez!", like a leaking bellows or a blunt saw pulling through wet wood.

A complete snoring cycle lasted about eight seconds.

Then repeated.

Had repeated for three hours and forty-two minutes.

Wolf Xun knew very clearly. He was counting in his heart.

**G-312-Wolf Beastman-Wolf Xun** lay on the upper bunk by the door, eyes open. His wolf ears—those
pointed ears covered
in gray short fur, edges tinged silver—were fully erect, pinnae turning slightly like precision
radar, capturing every
faint sound in the room. But besides Bear Hou's snoring, he could hardly hear anything else.

No, listen carefully, he could.

From the lower bunk opposite, **G-277-Bull Beastman-Bull Wen**'s breathing, heavy but even. That was
a bull beastman's
characteristic resonance from deep within a broad chest, with a slight nasal quality. But compared
to Bear Hou's
snoring, it was almost silence.

And his own heartbeat. Thump-thump. Thump-thump. Getting faster, louder, almost synchronizing with
Bear Hou's snoring.

Wolf Xun had tried every method.

He used a pillow to cover his ears—useless. Bear Hou's snoring penetrated feathers and cotton,
vibrating directly into
his skull.

He tried lying on his side, pressing one ear against the bed board—worse. The wooden structure of
the bed board became a
sounding box, amplifying the snoring and adding the "buzzing" vibration of the iron frame.

He tried counting sheep in his mind. One sheep jumps over the fence, two sheep jump over the
fence... When he reached
the three hundred seventy-fourth, an especially plump sheep jumped halfway, suddenly turning into
Bear Hou's face
mid-air, that furry, wide-open-mouthed face, then a loud "SNORT!" and the sheep vanished.

He opened his eyes, staring at the crack on the ceiling. The crack appeared when the red light swept
over, winding and
twisting like a sneering mouth.

Now it was the three hundred seventy-fifth sweep of red light.

Wolf Xun slowly turned his head, looking towards the bed by the window.

The red light swept over Bear Hou's body at that moment.

For an instant, Wolf Xun saw details.

Bear Hou lay on his back, wearing only the gray, loose shorts issued by **Beast Shield Company**,
fabric washed pale,
the elastic waistband already losing its elasticity, loosely cinched around his thick waist and
hips. His upper body was
completely naked.

That was the typical bear beastman "**fat-over-muscle**" physique.

A thick, yellowish-brown layer of fat covered his entire body, like soft but solid armor. Beneath
that fat layer were
massive, solid muscle groups. His pectoral muscles weren't sharply chiseled squares but two round,
full hills, slowly
rising and falling with his breath, the **nipples** at their peaks—deep brown, the size of copper
coins—tightening
slightly with each inhale, the surrounding areolas developing fine goosebumps.

His abdomen was also round, like half an inflated ball, navel sunken in folds of fat. But when he
turned or exerted
force, the outlines of abdominal muscle segments could vaguely be seen beneath that fat layer, marks
left by years of
physical labor.

His arms were thick as ordinary beastmen's thighs, biceps and triceps noticeably bulging even when
relaxed, their
circumference possibly exceeding Wolf Xun's calf circumference. Those muscles were covered in short,
stiff
blackish-brown fur, glistening with an oily sheen under the red light.

Bear Hou's face was buried in shadow, but his square jaw and slightly open mouth were visible. With
each exhale, a heavy
breath sprayed out—the smell of last night's cheap liquor, mixed with the bear beastman's
characteristic, slightly
pungent body odor, and the scent of half-digested **cafeteria** slop: cabbage, potato, cheap
synthetic meat.

That scent drifted over with each bout of snoring.

Wolf Xun's nose twitched.

He smelled it.

He also smelled the scent from Bull Wen—gentler, the faint grassy smell characteristic of
herbivores, mixed with sweat
and cotton. And his own scent: the wolf's cool body odor, a bit of sour sweat, and dust not fully
washed off after
yesterday's training.

But these scents were all overwhelmed by Bear Hou's snoring and alcohol breath.

As if the entire room was occupied by this sleeping brown bear.

Not just sound and scent.

Wolf Xun could even feel that with each snore from Bear Hou, the iron bed frame beneath him emitted
an extremely faint "
creak." Then the vibration transmitted through the cement floor, through the walls, through the air,
to Wolf Xun's bunk,
making his bed board resonate almost imperceptibly.

That resonance went directly to his spine.

Like someone poking his tailbone with a very blunt needle. Once. Again.

Wolf Xun closed his eyes.

His claws—the slightly sharp nails at his fingertips—dug deep into his palms. Pain came, but not
enough, far from enough
to distract.

He heard Bear Hou turn over again.

The bed frame groaned under the strain.

Bear Hou turned from lying on his back to lying on his side, facing Wolf Xun's direction. Now his
snoring changed
slightly, from sharp inhales to low, continuous "Hoo... hoo..." sounds, like a broken engine idling.

The red light swept through again.

This time, Wolf Xun saw Bear Hou's face.

That honest, short-furred bear face completely relaxed, eyes closed, eyeballs under the eyelids
moving rapidly—he was
dreaming. A trace of glistening drool at the corner of his mouth slowly crawled down the fur of his
chin, dripping onto
the pillowcase where a small dark wet patch already existed.

Bear Hou's lips moved again, mumbling something indistinct.

"...Mom... money..."

Then the snoring continued.

Wolf Xun stared at that face.

Three hours and forty-three minutes.

He had to get up at six tomorrow morning. At six-thirty, he had to assemble at the **training room**
for morning
training. At eight, he had to stand guard in the lobby on the first floor of the **Beast Shield
Company** building for
four hours. In the afternoon, he had to follow an **O-rank overseer** to the warehouse district to
inventory supplies.

He needed sleep.

At least four hours. That was the minimum he needed to maintain basic functions.

But he had gotten less than three hours of sleep per day for seven consecutive days.

All because of this snoring.

Wolf Xun's temples throbbed, a jumping pain. His eyeballs felt dry, like two grits rubbing in their
sockets. His throat
tightened; his stomach spasmed from fatigue and tension.

He knew he was losing judgment.

But he couldn't control it.

Another thought emerged, like mold growing in the dark: What if Bear Hou... stopped snoring?

What if that mouth closed?

What if that massive body... quieted down?

Once this thought appeared, it began to grow wildly.

## Scene-2 Slow Rising

3:31 AM.

Wolf Xun opened his eyes.

He didn't know when he had closed them, maybe only dozed off for a few minutes, maybe never fell
asleep. His sense of
time was already scrambled.

The snoring continued.

But the rhythm changed. Bear Hou seemed to have entered deeper sleep; the snoring became more
regular: Hoo—wheez,
Hoo—wheez. Each "Hoo" drawn out long, as if using all the air in his lungs, then a short "wheez" of
inhalation.

Wolf Xun slowly, extremely slowly, propped himself up on his elbows.

The bed board made a faint "creak."

He froze.

The snoring paused for a second.

Wolf Xun held his breath, all muscles tense, ears straight up, capturing any movement from Bear
Hou's bed direction.

One second. Two seconds.

Then, louder snoring erupted: "SNORT—!!!"

Bear Hou had just adjusted his breathing.

Wolf Xun slowly let out a breath, white mist briefly visible in the cold air before dissipating.

He continued moving, like a rusted machine, frame by frame. First, he pushed aside the thin blanket
covering him—the *
*Beast Shield Company**-issued olive-green blanket, filled with cheap synthetic cotton, almost
non-insulating. Cold air
immediately enveloped his body.

He wore only brief-like sleeping shorts, upper body naked. The wolf beastman's physique was lean,
muscle lines distinct,
covered by a thin, even layer of fat. His pectoral muscles weren't round and full like Bear Hou's
but two tight, clearly
contoured slabs. Ribs were faintly visible under the skin, abdomen with six defined abs, but
currently tense from
nervousness.

His skin had a gray-blue tint, covered in extremely short, almost invisible downy fur, feeling like
fine sandpaper to
the touch.

Wolf Xun stepped barefoot onto the edge of the lower bunk—Bull Wen's bed. His soles felt the rough
bedsheet fabric and
the hard bed board beneath. Bull Wen slept soundly below, breathing evenly.

Wolf Xun glanced down.

Bull Wen lay on his side, back to him. The bull beastman's broad back raised the thin blanket into a
huge curve, that
solid, yellowish-brown **fat-over-muscle** like a small mountain in the dark. Bull Wen's
horns—short, thick, curved
backward—rested against the wall, rising and falling slightly with his breath.

He wasn't awake.

Wolf Xun curled his toes slightly, feeling the cold cement floor. Then he stepped down.

The floor was rough, covered in a thin layer of dust. Cold sensation shot up from his soles, making
him shiver.

He stood up straight, height about 185 cm, medium-tall for a wolf beastman. But in this room, he was
at least a head
shorter than Bear Hou and slimmer than Bull Wen.

The room was small, about twelve square meters. Three iron-framed beds, three small storage
cabinets, a cement sink, a
squat toilet. No window, only a palm-sized vent high on the wall, its wire mesh rusted.

Air didn't circulate. Smells of sweat, body odor, dust, and faint urine odor from a corner mixed
into the **Beast Shield
Company** lower-level dormitory's characteristic, suffocating atmosphere.

Wolf Xun walked towards his storage cabinet.

His steps were extremely light, soles landing first, then heels, like a real wolf stalking. Only a
very faint "shushing"
sound, the sound of bare skin rubbing against cement.

Three steps. He stopped before his storage cabinet.

The cabinet was sheet metal, green paint peeling and flaking, rust stains on the door. The lock was
a simple padlock;
the key was under his pillow.

Wolf Xun didn't get the key.

He reached out, fingers—slender, knuckles distinct, nails slightly sharp—probing the narrow gap
between the top of the
cabinet and the ceiling.

There was a narrow space there.

His fingertips touched something. Cold, metallic.

He gently hooked it, slowly, slowly pulling it out.

A dagger was pulled out.

The red light swept over timely.

The blade reflected a cold gleam.

It was a **steel dagger**, about twenty centimeters long, single-edged, fine serrations along the
spine. The hilt was
wrapped in black anti-slip cloth, somewhat worn, revealing the metal beneath. This wasn't **Beast
Shield Company**
standard issue; it was spoils Wolf Xun had secretly taken from a dead bounty hunter during a "
perimeter clean-up
mission" three months ago. He had hidden it, not reported it.

He liked this knife.

Weight perfect, balance just right. The blade was sharpened; he had secretly tested it in a corner
of the training
ground, easily cutting through three layers of cardboard.

Wolf Xun gripped the hilt.

Familiar feel. The rough texture of the anti-slip cloth rubbed against his palm, the metal parts
cool. He flipped the
dagger, blade tip down, held against his leg.

Then he turned.

Facing Bear Hou's bed.

## Scene-3 Approaching and Observing

Three steps away.

Wolf Xun took a full minute.

First step, he lifted his right foot, sole suspended, paused two seconds, confirming Bear Hou's
snoring hadn't changed,
confirming Bull Wen's breathing remained steady. Then the sole landed, weight slowly shifting
forward.

The floor made no sound.

Second step, left foot.

His soles could feel every grain of dust on the floor, every tiny crack. The cold sensation kept
coming, but his soles
began to sweat, a slippery feeling making his toes unconsciously grip the ground.

Third step, right foot.

Now he stood at the foot of Bear Hou's bed.

The snoring sounded right before him, so loud he could feel the air vibrations hitting his face.
That scent mixed with
alcohol, stomach acid, and bear body odor was stronger now, warm, humid with sleep.

Wolf Xun looked down at the massive creature on the bed.

Bear Hou lay on his side, facing him. This angle showed Bear Hou's full side profile: from broad
shoulders, to round
chest side, to protruding belly, to thick thighs.

Bear Hou's shorts had ridden up from his sleeping posture, exposing most of his thighs. The fur
there was denser,
yellowish-brown short hair covering thick muscles. Thigh muscles appeared incredibly thick even when
relaxed, thicker
than Wolf Xun's waist.

Further up, the shorts' waistband cinched at his hip bones, the elastic digging into fat, forming a
deep indentation. At
the front crotch of the shorts, a noticeable bulge—Bear Hou's sleeping genitalia, forming a soft
lump under the **Beast
Shield Company**-issued loose shorts.

Wolf Xun's gaze swept over that area, then moved away.

He looked at Bear Hou's upper body.

Bear Hou's left arm was pinned under his body, right arm resting on his belly, palm naturally open.
That palm was thick
and broad, palm and finger pads covered in thick calluses, nails short and blunt, edges dirty.

Bear Hou's chest rose and fell with his snores.

With each inhale, those two round pectoral hills would rise, the deep brown **nipples** at their
peaks slightly
protruding, surrounding areolas contracting. With each exhale, the pectorals sank, **nipples**
relaxing.

Wolf Xun noticed that below Bear Hou's left **nipple**, there was an old white scar about five
centimeters long, as if
slashed by something sharp. The fur over the scar was white, contrasting with the surrounding
blackish-brown.

His gaze continued upward to Bear Hou's neck.

One of the bear beastman's most typical features: a neck thick as a tree trunk. Thick fat and muscle
wrapped around
vertebrae, trachea, and arteries, making the neck appear almost as wide as the head. Short, stiff
fur covered the entire
neck, forming a slight bulge at the Adam's apple.

Bear Hou's head tilted to one side, neck fully exposed.

On the right side of the neck, when the red warning light swept over, bluish blood vessels could
faintly be seen under
the skin. At the carotid artery location, with each heartbeat, there was an extremely faint pulse.

Thump. Thump. Slow and powerful.

Wolf Xun tightened his grip on the dagger.

The hilt was already warmed by his palm, but the blade remained cool.

He was thinking, where to strike?

Neck? Stab the trachea from the front? Or cut the artery from the side? Or stab upward from under
the chin, through the
mouth and brain?

He had learned these in training. **Beast Shield Company**'s **new recruit camp** taught basic
anatomy, taught where the
vital points were. But they taught for dealing with "enemies," not roommates.

Bear Hou's snoring changed again.

He took a deep breath, then that breath caught in his throat, making a "gurgle" sound. His body
twitched, right leg
kicked, ankle hitting the bed frame with a dull "thud."

Wolf Xun's whole body froze, the dagger almost slipping from his hand.

But Bear Hou just adjusted his posture. He turned over, from lying on his side to lying on his back.

Now he faced upward.

The snoring paused for a few seconds, then resumed, becoming louder, erupting directly from deep in
the throat: "
Hah—SNORT!! Hah—SNORT!!"

His mouth opened wider.

Wolf Xun could see inside Bear Hou's mouth: pink palate, slightly trembling uvula, and yellowish
molars at the back.
Drool continuously flowed from the corners of his mouth, wetting the fur on his cheeks and the
pillowcase.

Bear Hou's hands now both rested on his belly, fingers twitching occasionally, as if grabbing
something in a dream.

His chest and abdomen were fully exposed.

Pectoral muscles rose and fell with breath, the round fat layer on the abdomen also undulating
slightly. Navel deeply
indented, surrounding skin with fine folds. The shorts' waistband still cinched tightly, pressing a
red line into the
abdominal fat.

Wolf Xun's gaze fell on Bear Hou's chest.

Left chest, the heart's location.

Separated by a layer of fat, a layer of muscle, a layer of ribs, that fist-sized heart was beating,
pumping blood
throughout the body, maintaining the operation of this massive body, maintaining this damn snoring.

What if he stabbed there?

What if the blade tip pierced the skin, pierced the fat, pierced the pectoralis major, slipped
between ribs, stabbed
into that beating lump?

Would the snoring stop?

Surely.

Wolf Xun's breathing grew rapid.

He didn't realize it himself; he began mimicking Bear Hou's breathing rhythm: deep inhale, then
hold, then slowly
exhale. But his inhales were too shallow, exhales too hurried, lungs making a wheezing sound like a
broken bellows.

His hand shook.

Not from fear. Something more complex, mixed with anger, fatigue, despair, and resolve.

He needed sleep.

He needed quiet.

He needed this bear to shut up.

Now.

## Scene-4 The First Stab

3:49 AM.

Wolf Xun raised his left hand.

His left hand fingers spread, hovering about ten centimeters above Bear Hou's left chest, over that
thick, furry
pectoral. He hesitated, where to press.

Finally, his left hand slowly descended, pressing on the lower edge of Bear Hou's left chest, near
the armpit.

Sensation transmitted.

First, temperature. Bear Hou's skin was hot, like a small furnace. In sleep, bear beastmen's body
temperature was higher
than usual, their physiological characteristic.

Then, texture. Short, stiff fur pricked Wolf Xun's palm, somewhat itchy. Skin under the fur was
thick, elastic, like
aged rubber. Further down, that thick layer of fat, soft, but resistance could be felt. Under the
fat, solid pectoralis
major, slightly undulating with breath.

Wolf Xun's palm could clearly feel Bear Hou's heartbeat.

Thump. Thump. Thump.

Slow, powerful, like someone beating a drum inside.

Each heartbeat transmitted through his palm to his arm, to his shoulder, to his chest, gradually
synchronizing with his
own heartbeat.

Thump. Wolf Xun's heartbeat.

Thump. Bear Hou's heartbeat.

The two rhythms tried to merge but failed, forming an irritating off-beat.

Wolf Xun's right hand tightened on the dagger.

He adjusted his stance, left foot forward, right foot back, body slightly lowered, like preparing to
pounce. Right hand
raised, dagger tip aimed at that spot on Bear Hou's left chest—between the fourth and fifth ribs,
slightly left, the
projected area of the heart.

His muscles tensed.

Deltoids, biceps, forearm muscle groups, all contracted, storing power for the coming thrust. His
core tightened, abs
hard like planks. Calf muscles tensed, toes gripping the ground, seeking the most stable footing.

He took a deep breath.

Lungs expanded, ribs lifted, diaphragm lowered.

Then he held his breath.

Time seemed to slow.

The red warning light swept over, casting his shadow on the wall, that shadow holding a blade high
like some ancient
totem.

Bear Hou's snoring continued: "Hah—SNORT! Hah—SNORT!"

Drool dripped from his mouth.

Bull Wen turned over in the opposite bed, uttering indistinct sleep-talk: "...grass... tasty..."

Wolf Xun's eyes fixed on the point where the blade tip would pierce.

On that skin, a small dark spot, maybe a birthmark, maybe old dirt. The surrounding fur slightly
sparser than elsewhere.

Right there.

Wolf Xun's right hand began to descend.

Muscle contraction generated force, transmitted from shoulder to upper arm, to elbow, to forearm, to
wrist. The blade
tip cut through air with a faint "hiss."

Then—

Contact.

The blade tip touched skin.

First, resistance. Bear Hou's skin was tougher than imagined, like thick leather. The tip slightly
indented, but hadn't
pierced yet.

Wolf Xun applied more force.

Wrist pressed down, body leaned forward, putting body weight into it.

"Pfft."

A faint, almost inaudible sound.

The blade tip pierced the epidermis.

In that instant, Bear Hou's body moved.

Not a large movement, just an unconscious contraction of the pectoral, skin tightening. The snoring
paused for half a
second.

Wolf Xun froze, all blood seeming to solidify.

But Bear Hou didn't wake. His eyelids twitched, eyeballs moving rapidly under them, but eyes didn't
open. Snoring
continued, becoming deeper, more even: "Hoo... hoo..."

The blade tip had penetrated about half a centimeter.

Wolf Xun could feel the change in sensation from the blade: after breaking through the outermost
skin, next was the
subcutaneous fat layer. Softer there, less resistance, the blade slid in easily.

Warmth transmitted from the blade.

Blood.

Not much yet, just capillaries cut, seeping a small amount of blood. But the blade already felt the
temperature change,
from cold to lukewarm.

Wolf Xun continued pressing down.

The blade went deeper.

One centimeter. Two centimeters.

Now encountered new resistance—pectoralis major muscle fibers. Those dense, striated muscle tissue
wrapped around the
blade, trying to stop its advance. Wolf Xun needed more force.

He clenched his teeth, jaw muscles bulging.

Right hand continued forcing, while left hand pressed hard on Bear Hou's chest, preventing him from
struggling suddenly
from pain.

The blade continued deeper.

The sensation of cutting muscle fibers was peculiar, like using a blunt knife to cut thick rubber, a
faint "shh-shh"
sound, tissue separating. Resistance from the blade varied, different directional muscle fibers
resisting.

Three centimeters.

The blade should have penetrated the pectoralis major, entered the pectoralis minor layer, or
already approached ribs.

At that moment, Bear Hou's body jolted violently!

Not a small twitch, a full-body, intense tremor!

Wolf Xun's heart almost leaped from his throat.

Bear Hou's eyes opened.

In the dim light, those bear eyes opened blankly, pupils reflecting eerie light when the red warning
light swept over.
His gaze unfocused, first looking at the ceiling, then slowly, extremely slowly, turned towards Wolf
Xun's direction.

He saw Wolf Xun.

Saw Wolf Xun's hand pressing on his chest.

Saw the dagger in Wolf Xun's other hand, stabbed into his chest.

But he didn't seem to understand what was happening yet.

Sleep too deep, alcohol still in effect, pain signals not fully reaching the brain.

Bear Hou's lips moved, making a muffled sound: "...Huh?"

Then his gaze moved downward, looking at his own chest.

He saw a dagger hilt standing on the furry skin of his left chest. Most of the blade was buried in
his body, only about
five centimeters length outside. Skin around the hilt began oozing blood, not spurting, slowly,
thickly welling up,
flowing down the curve of the pectoral, staining the blackish-brown fur red.

Bear Hou's eyes widened.

Blankness rapidly faded, replaced by confusion, then confusion turned to shock, shock turned to...
fear.

He opened his mouth, seemed to want to shout.

But Wolf Xun moved faster.

His left hand abruptly changed from pressing the chest to covering Bear Hou's mouth!

Palm completely covered Bear Hou's furry face, fingers pressing hard, fingertips sinking into the
fat of his cheeks.
Bear Hou's lips pressed under the palm, teeth scraping the edge of Wolf Xun's hand.

Bear Hou's throat emitted "mmph" sounds, low, muffled.

His body began to struggle.

Real struggle.

## Scene-5 Struggle and Suppression

Bear Hou's right hand rose, that thick, broad palm waving wildly in the air, trying to grab
something. Fingers spread
then clenched, nails slicing air.

Wolf Xun leaned forward, using his upper body weight to press down on Bear Hou. His left knee
lifted, kneeling on the
bed edge, right leg still standing on the ground, forming an unstable support.

Bear Hou's left hand also moved, trying to grab the dagger stabbed in his chest. But the angle was
wrong, his arm too
short, couldn't reach.

His legs began to kick.

Thick thigh muscles tensed, shorts stretched almost tearing. Soles kicked the bed board, making "
Thud! Thud! Thud!" dull
sounds. The entire iron-framed bed began shaking violently, bed legs scraping cement floor, emitting
piercing "creaks."

Wolf Xun could feel that tremendous power erupting from within this massive body beneath him.

Bear Hou was a **G-rank grunt**, bottom-tier laborer, but his strength was real. Years of carrying
heavy loads, lifting
barbells in the **training room**, accumulated huge potential in his muscle groups. Now, under
threat of death, this
potential was activated.

Wolf Xun was almost thrown off.

He gritted his teeth, every muscle tensed to the limit. His left hand clamped tightly over Bear
Hou's mouth, while his
right hand, gripping the dagger, began to twist!

Not pulling out, twisting.

Wrist rotated clockwise, making the blade churn inside Bear Hou's chest cavity!

"Ugh—!!!"

A muffled, agonized scream squeezed out from between Wolf Xun's fingers.

Bear Hou's eyes widened almost to splitting, eyeballs bloodshot, pupils contracted to pinpoints. His
body arched as if
electrocuted, chest and abdominal muscles all spasming, that round belly contracted inward,
abdominal muscle outlines
instantly becoming clearly visible.

The blade twisted inside, cutting muscle tissue, possibly already touched ribs, scraped periosteum.

More blood gushed out.

Now not oozing, but gushing. Warm, thick blood gushed from the wound, flowing down the pectoral,
soaking Wolf Xun's arm
pressing on his chest, soaking the bedsheet, drip-dripping onto the floor.

The smell of blood instantly exploded.

Strong, cloying metallic tang mixed with the bear's characteristic body odor, filling the small
room.

Bear Hou struggled more violently.

His right hand finally grabbed Wolf Xun's left arm, fingers like iron pincers digging into Wolf
Xun's upper arm muscles.
Nails pierced skin, blood flowing from Wolf Xun's arm, mixing with Bear Hou's blood.

Pain came, but Wolf Xun didn't let go.

He instead twisted the dagger harder!

Wrist continued rotating, counterclockwise this time.

He could feel resistance the blade encountered inside—maybe severed a larger blood vessel, or cut a
lung lobe. A "
squelch" feeling, like puncturing an air sac, transmitted from the blade.

Bear Hou's body jolted violently!

His back arched, head thrown back, neck veins all bulging like twisted earthworms. His legs stopped
kicking, instead
stiffening straight, toes curled, insteps taut.

Between Wolf Xun's left fingers, he felt warm liquid.

Not blood. Clearer liquid.

Bear Hou was crying.

Tears welled from his wide eyes, flowing down the corners, into his fur, to the edge of Wolf Xun's
hand.

In those eyes, fear had turned to pain, deep within the pain, a trace of... confusion?

As if asking: Why?

Wolf Xun avoided those eyes.

He stared at the dagger, at the wound, at the continuously gushing blood.

Bear Hou's struggle began to weaken.

Strength flowed from that massive body. The grip of his right hand on Wolf Xun's arm loosened,
fingers slowly slipping,
leaving five deep bloody scratches on Wolf Xun's arm. Legs no longer taut, began twitching slightly,
ankles trembling
rhythmically.

His breathing changed.

No longer snoring, nor normal breathing, but rapid, broken "hck... hck..." sounds from the covered
mouth, with
blood-flecked "gurgles."

That was the sound of a punctured lung, blood flowing into the trachea.

Through his left hand, Wolf Xun could feel Bear Hou's mouth moving, as if trying to say something.
But pressed firmly by
the palm, only indistinct syllables escaped.

"...why... wh..."

Wolf Xun didn't answer.

He just kept pressing, right hand gripping the dagger, feeling the sensation of life ebbing from the
blade.

The rising and falling of Bear Hou's chest became irregular. Sometimes violent twitches, sometimes
long pauses. Each
inhale shorter, each exhale with more blood-flecked foam.

The red warning light swept over.

Illuminated Bear Hou's paling face. His lips began losing color, from deep brown to grayish-white.
Eyes half-open,
pupils beginning to dilate, but still a faint light remained.

His body was cooling.

Wolf Xun's palm could feel Bear Hou's skin temperature dropping. From furnace-hot, to warm, to

changed. He could never go back to being that Wolf Xun who just complained about snoring and tossed
and turned, unable
to sleep.

He would sleep well. Tomorrow, he would go about his missions as if nothing had happened and collect
his meager salary.
But in his heart, he knew he had crossed a line. On the other side of that line lay deeper darkness,
but also a faster
path to promotion—at Beast Shield, the ruthless often lived better.

**【Living→Alive-G-277-Bull Beastman-Bull Wen】**

His name was Bull Wen, number G-277. A yellow-skinned bull beastman, powerfully built with solid
muscle, yet now he
curled under his blanket like a frightened calf. Into his potent male scent had mixed the sour odor
of fear.

He had actually been awake the whole time. From the moment Wolf Xun climbed down from his bunk, to
the dagger being
drawn, to Bear Hou's final struggles, he heard everything clearly. He didn't dare move, didn't dare
breathe, could only
squeeze his eyes shut and pray for it all to end quickly.

But when Wolf Xun dragged the body past his bed, he couldn't help but open his eyes. He saw the
blood, saw the corpse,
saw Wolf Xun's eyes glowing coldly in the dark.

In that moment, boundless terror washed over him. He knew that if he made a single sound, he would
be the next one
stuffed into the trash bin. So he nodded, he shrank back under the covers, he stifled his sobs.

He would remember every detail of this night: Bear Hou's final snore, the muffled sound of the
dagger piercing flesh,
the gurgle of flowing blood, Wolf Xun's icy threat. These images would torment him every night,
making him never dare to
sleep deeply again, never dare to trust anyone again.

Starting tomorrow, he would be even quieter, even more submissive, drawing as little attention to
himself as possible.
He would tell himself: survival is what matters most. As for justice, conscience, the feeble bond of
fellowship
between底层 employees—at Beast Shield, such luxuries were not for the likes of him.

## Story Reflections

This is a story about "sleep," yet it is filled with sleepless dread.

In the twisted ecosystem of Beast Shield Company, even the most basic physiological need—sleep—could
become grounds for
murder. Bear Hou's snoring was a noise, a disturbance, an "error," and so he was "corrected." In the
cruelest, most
permanent way possible.

Wolf Xun's choice was an inevitable product of Beast Shield's culture. Here, violence was the most
direct way to solve
problems; survival of the fittest was the only rule. When he crossed that line, there might have
been a moment of
hesitation, but it was quickly replaced by the pragmatism of "fixing the problem." He became part of
the system, a more
efficient cog in the machine.

Bull Wen's silence is the epitome of countless low-level employees. He saw what he shouldn't have
seen, knew what he
shouldn't have known. His only choice was to close his eyes, cover his ears, and pretend everything
was normal. Because
the cost of speaking out could be his life.

And Bear Hou… he just wanted a good night's sleep.

In this world where all characters are hermaphroditic male beastmen, violence and desire are
intertwined, and survival
overrides humanity. Everyone adapts to this brutal environment in their own way: Bear Hou with
alcohol, Wolf Xun with a
dagger, Bull Wen with silence.

But some nighttime sounds, once heard, can never be erased from memory. Some bloodstains, even when
washed away, leave
permanent marks upon the soul.

The snoring has stopped.

But silence, at times, is louder than any noise.
