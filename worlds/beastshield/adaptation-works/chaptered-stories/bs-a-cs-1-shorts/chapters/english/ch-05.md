# Chapter 5 The White Sniper's Yesterday Lover

---

---

> *The crosshairs in the sniper scope, today aimed at his sealed past. The last bullet of his birthday—whose chest did it pierce?*

---

The noon sun cut sharp patches of light on the dust-covered concrete floor through the broken windows of the abandoned factory. **Wolf Yuanzhi** lay prone on the second-floor sniper nest, muscles beneath his **white tank top** aching slightly from maintaining the same position for so long. Sweat slid down his gray fur, darkening the **tight tank top** at his back with damp stains, emitting the unique scent of a wolf beastman—a mixture of gunpowder and faint wildness.

His codename was **R-217**, a **sharpshooter** of **Beast Shield Company**. Today was his birthday. Twenty-five years old.

His communication earpiece carried the cold, hard voice of the **overseer**: "Target has entered range, third floor of the apartment, by the window. Confirm identity: **white tiger beastman**, former police officer, number P-334. Elimination order issued, lethal force authorized."

Yuanzhi adjusted his position, left cheek pressed tight against the **sniper rifle** stock. Through the high-powered scope, the details of the old apartment building across the way were crystal clear. Rusted fire escapes, clothes hung out to dry, withered potted plants on the windowsill... Then, he saw the target.

In that instant, his breath stopped.

White fur, a robust yet fluid muscle line, that face—even across hundreds of meters, even with the years carving some weathering into it—Yuanzhi would never mistake it.

**Tiger Linxi**.

His police academy classmate, the comrade who once stood shoulder-to-shoulder with him on the training ground, sweating together... and the yesterday lover he had buried deep in his heart, never spoken aloud.

Memories flooded in like a tide. On the police academy's combat course, when Linxi threw him to the ground, then reached out to pull him up, the warmth transmitted through his palm. On the rooftop late at night, the two of them sharing a can of beer, watching the city lights, talking about what kind of police officers they wanted to become. And that rainy night, when he mustered the courage to confess, only to hear Linxi say: "Yuanzhi, I'm going to the Special Operations Unit. We... let's take a break for now."

Later, Yuanzhi was forced to leave the police force due to family debts, eventually joining **Beast Shield**. And Linxi, he heard, had offended the higher-ups by insisting on investigating a certain **mine disaster** and the **red crystal** case, and was squeezed out of the core department.

He never imagined their reunion would be like this.

The crosshairs of the scope steadily framed Linxi's chest. Beneath that washed-out old police uniform shirt, the outline of pectoral muscles was faintly visible. Linxi seemed to be organizing documents on the table, brows slightly furrowed, that focused expression exactly the same as back then.

"R-217, report status." The voice in the earpiece pulled Yuanzhi back to reality.

His finger rested on the cold trigger, trembling slightly. Sweat flowed into his eyes, bringing a stinging pain. He could hear his own heart pounding wildly in his chest, almost drowning out the static noise in his ear.

"Target... confirmed." His voice was dry and hoarse. "Requesting reconfirmation of elimination order. Target may... possess important intelligence."

"Order unchanged. Eliminate." The overseer's voice was without ripple. "Complete within three minutes, or it will be considered insubordination."

Insubordination. At **Beast Shield**, that meant **execution**. In his mind flashed the corpses of colleagues stuffed into **large trash bins in the hallways**, their **white socks** stripped off, numbers becoming vouchers for **RUB** exchangeable at the **weapon shop**.

He closed his eyes, taking a deep breath. The smell of dust and his own sweat was exceptionally clear. When he opened his eyes again, Linxi in the scope seemed to sense something, looking up toward the window, gaze seeming to pierce through hundreds of meters of distance, stabbing straight into Yuanzhi's eyes.

In that instant, Yuanzhi saw the confusion in Linxi's eyes, followed by sharp vigilance.

He knew. The sniper's presence was exposed.

There was no time left.

Yuanzhi's index finger slowly pulled the trigger.

**Bang—!**

The gunshot echoed in the empty abandoned factory. The recoil of the bullet leaving the barrel struck his shoulder socket, painful. But he didn't look at the result. Almost at the same moment he pulled the trigger, he turned the gun barrel with the speed he had trained thousands of times, the cold metal pressing against his right temple. Fur was pressed into a depression, feeling the hardness and chill of the metal.

"Sorry, Linxi. And... happy birthday, Yuanzhi."

He whispered to himself, then pulled the trigger a second time.

**Bang!**

The second gunshot followed immediately. His body jerked violently, head tilting to the left. Warm liquid gushed from the bullet hole at his temple and the exit wound exploding on the other side, mixed with gray-white brain matter, splashing onto the dust-covered concrete floor. His fingers slipped from the trigger, his body gradually losing all strength, collapsing limply on the sniper nest.

The chest area of his **white tank top** was rapidly soaked by semen seeping from inside his body. Thick, milky-white liquid permeated through the tight fabric, forming a large dark wet stain on his abdomen and crotch, flowing downward along the curves of his body, mixing with blood, emitting the strong, fishy odor unique to the end of life. This was his physiological characteristic as a **beastman**—his **Lust Gauge** overflowing when full, **death ejaculation**.

His legs first went straight, toes in the **white long socks** (with a blue ring at the cuff, the number **R-217** printed on the bottom) curling violently a few times inside the **khaki military boots**, then completely relaxing, spreading limply. His eyes were still half-open, gazing at the dilapidated ceiling of the factory, the light in his pupils rapidly fading.

Beside the sniper rifle lay an unopened energy bar. The packaging was somewhat old, a small sticky note attached to it with transparent tape, written in somewhat childish handwriting: "**Happy birthday, Yuanzhi. Stay alive.**" The signature was a crudely drawn wolf head smiley face. That was something he received yesterday during patrol from a **G-rank** bear beastman grunt—that honest big guy heard he was having a birthday soon and secretly slipped it to him. The bear beastman said, this was a custom from his hometown, you eat something sweet on your birthday.

The energy bar was never opened. He had originally planned to find a corner to secretly eat it after the mission, as a little celebration for himself.

---

Half an hour later.

The rusted iron door on the first floor of the factory was carefully pushed open. **Tiger Linxi**'s figure appeared in the doorway, wearing civilian clothes, but with a bulge at his waist—clearly armed. His movements were agile and vigilant, white tiger ears erect, capturing any faint sound. Beneath thick white brows, golden eyes were sharp as knives.

He slowly walked up to the second floor, the smell of blood and another strong male scent mixing together, making him wrinkle his nose. He saw the sniper nest, and Yuanzhi.

In that moment, Linxi's body visibly stiffened. He recognized the **Beast Shield** **white uniform**, recognized that sniper rifle, and recognized that face—even with half of it covered in blood.

"Yuan... zhi?" His voice was so low it was almost inaudible.

He quickly stepped forward, crouching down, fingers trembling as he reached for Yuanzhi's carotid artery. No pulse. Body temperature was fading. His gaze fell on the gruesous wound at Yuanzhi's temple, and the dark stain of semen soaking his chest and abdomen. As a former police officer, he was all too familiar with the death characteristics of **Beast Shield** employees.

Linxi's hand clenched into a fist, nails digging deep into his palm. He jerked his head up, looking toward the direction of his apartment across the way. There was a clear bullet hole in the window glass—positioned high, almost scraping the top of the window frame. If he had been standing still at the time, the bullet would have flown at least twenty centimeters above his head.

A miss. A **Beast Shield** **R-rank sharpshooter**, at a distance of less than four hundred meters, missed.

No, not a miss.

Linxi's gaze slowly moved back to Yuanzhi's face, lingering on his half-open, unfocused eyes. Then, he saw the energy bar beside him, and that small sticky note saying happy birthday.

He reached out, picking up the energy bar. The handwriting on the sticky note brought a pang of sorrow. He recognized this handwriting—it was Yuanzhi's. He was always this clumsy, even writing happy birthday all crooked.

"Stay alive..." Linxi read the words on the sticky note, his voice choking.

He remembered their last meeting, that rainy night, what he said to Wolf Yuanzhi about "taking a break." At that time, he had just received secret orders to investigate **Beast Shield**'s involvement in the **mine disaster** and **red crystal**. It was fraught with danger. He didn't want to drag Yuanzhi down. He thought that was protection.

Later, he heard that something happened to Yuanzhi's family, forcing him to leave the police force. He tried to find him, but there was no trace. Until recently, his investigation touched certain sensitive nerves of **Beast Shield**, and he began to sense surveillance and threats. That morning, the chill of being targeted by a sniper made him instantly alert, years of training making him instinctively take evasive action.

He never imagined the sniper would be Yuanzhi.

Even less did he imagine that Yuanzhi would make such a choice on his birthday.

Linxi was silent for a long time. In the factory, only the wind whimpering through the holes. He took off his coat, gently covering Yuanzhi's upper body, hiding that blood-stained face and the mess on his chest. Then, he carefully removed one **white long sock** from Yuanzhi's foot. The bottom of the sock, the number **R-217** clearly visible.

He knew this rule. This was a **bounty voucher**.

But he didn't plan to exchange it for that **500 RUB**.

He folded the sock carefully, putting it into his inner pocket. There was already a crumpled photo there—the only graduation photo of him and Yuanzhi from police academy. Two young beastmen with arms around each other's shoulders, grinning carefreely.

Finally, Linxi picked up that happy birthday sticky note, carefully tearing it off, and put the energy bar together into another pocket.

He stood up, taking one last look at his former lover covered by the coat, then turned and walked toward the stairs. Footsteps heavy, but firm.

Sunlight shot in through the broken window, falling on Yuanzhi's exposed foot wearing the other white sock and military boot. The blue ring on the sock gleamed faintly in the light.

Outside the factory, the hustle and bustle of the city drifted in faintly. Linxi's figure disappeared behind the iron door.

The wind continued to blow, rolling up the dust on the ground, slowly covering the pool of dark liquid that was gradually coagulating—a mixture of blood and semen.

## Story Commentary and Reflections

> *The following is DeepSeek's commentary on this story, attempting to understand the inner world of each character.*

### Their Final Stories

【Living→Dead-R-1-Gray Wolf Beastman-Wolf Yuanzhi】

His name is Wolf Yuanzhi, number R-217. On his twenty-fifth birthday, he lay on the second floor of an abandoned factory, looking at the tiger beastman he once loved through his sniper scope. As a **sharpshooter** of **Beast Shield**, he had the typical lean build of a gray wolf, muscle lines clear and sharp beneath his **white tank top**, not the heavy fat-over-muscle of a **bear** or **bull**, but the lean, explosive power belonging to a predator. Long-term sniper training made his shoulder and back muscles exceptionally developed, deltoids and rear delts outlining beautiful curves beneath the tight clothing. His gray fur gleamed silver in the afternoon sun, his sweat mixing with the smells of gun oil and leather—the scent of a sniper accustomed to lonely waiting.

Yuanzhi's dream was simple: pay off his family's debts, leave **Beast Shield**, open a small shooting range. His marksmanship was extremely accurate, famous as "Eagle Eye" in the reserve boot camp. During the转正 test, ten bullets scored ninety-nine rings, and he was directly assigned to **R-rank**. He liked the precision and certainty of firearms—pull the trigger, bullet flies toward the target, no surprises. This made him feel safe, able to temporarily forget the things he did wearing this **white uniform**.

In his locker, besides a spare uniform and a few gun magazines, there was only an iron box, locked inside three things: a police academy graduation photo (he carefully cut out the part with him and Tiger Linxi), a faded police academy badge, and a crumpled piece of paper with "When I pay off my debts, I'll go find you" written on it. He didn't know if Linxi still cared, or even if he was still alive. But he needed this hope.

This morning, when that honest **G-rank** bear beastman grunt secretly slipped him the energy bar, Yuanzhi was stunned. The bear beastman rubbed his big paws, embarrassed as he said: "My ma says, on your birthday you gotta eat something sweet, then your luck will be good." Yuanzhi accepted it, a corner of his heart quietly softening. He tore off a sticky note, thought for a moment, and wrote "Stay alive." Both for himself, and for that kind bear beastman. He stuck the note on the energy bar, planning to eat it after the mission.

Then he received the elimination order. Target coordinates were the apartment he was most familiar with.

Those few seconds before pulling the trigger were the longest moment in his life. Tiger Linxi in the sniper scope, pectoral muscles rising and falling slightly with breath beneath the old police uniform, the line of his neck still so beautiful. Yuanzhi remembered their last training together, when Linxi fell, and he reached out to pull him up, only to be pulled down in turn, the two of them rolling together on the mat, close enough to smell the steaming heat on each other and the vigorous scent unique to young males. Linxi looked at him, golden eyes shining astonishingly bright, then suddenly let go, got up and walked away. Yuanzhi never understood that look.

Now he understood. But too late.

He chose to let the bullet miss, then end his own life. This was not cowardice, but the most complex resistance he could muster: not hurting the one he loved, not betraying the order (he did fire the gun), then using death to escape this irreconcilable tearing. Before the bullet pierced his skull, he smelled the fear and relief suddenly rising in his sweat, felt his **Lust Gauge** overflowing and erupting at the moment of death—that familiar, scalding liquid gushing out, taking away his last heat.

He never got to eat that energy bar. Never got to wait for the day when his "luck would be good." When he died, he was thinking about whether Linxi had safely left, whether that bear beastman who gave him the energy bar would be sad because of his death, and what would have happened if he had been a little braver back then.

Wolf Yuanzhi's corpse was discovered hours later by a **Beast Shield** cleanup crew. They skillfully stripped off his other numbered white sock, recorded "R-217, died in the line of duty," then stuffed him into a **large trash bin** and transported him away. His sniper rifle was recovered, the energy bar and sticky note swept away as garbage. His file gained another "died in the line of duty" record, perhaps **500 RUB** of pension would be sent to an address that no longer had any family.

The tiger beastman he loved took one of his socks, and that sticky note saying "stay alive."

He did, in his own way, let the one he loved "stay alive."

### Those Still Living

【Living→Alive-Former Police Officer-1-White Tiger Beastman-Tiger Linxi】

His name is Tiger Linxi, former police officer number P-334, now a secret investigator. A few strands of inconspicuous light gray mixed in his white fur—traces left by stress and years. His figure was still well-maintained, the naturally broad skeleton and fluid muscle lines of a **white tiger beastman** still evident, thick pectoral muscles, abdominal muscles faintly visible in blocks beneath his civilian clothes. Only the fine lines at the corners of his eyes betrayed the result of long-term staying up late and high tension. He always carried a faint scent, similar to pine needles and old book scrolls, mixed with the clean smell of detergent—the scent of a man accustomed to hiding and solitude.

Linxi's dream was once to uphold justice; now that dream had become concrete and dangerous: take down **Beast Shield**, uncover the truth behind the **mine disaster** and **red crystal**. For this, he left the police force mainstream, turned to underground investigation, and therefore had to distance himself from all close people, including Wolf Yuanzhi. He thought this was protection. He didn't know what happened to Yuanzhi's family, didn't know he had joined **Beast Shield**. He had been looking for him, using every covert channel, but there was never any news. Until today, when that chill of being targeted by a sniper climbed up his spine, he relied on beast instinct and years of training to dodge. He rushed out, wanting to catch that sniper, to ask who was behind it.

He never imagined he would see Yuanzhi's corpse.

When Linxi crouched beside Wolf Yuanzhi's gradually cooling body, his sense of smell captured the strong smell of blood, the fishy odor of semen, the smell of dust, and... a trace of extremely faint, belonging to Yuanzhi, deep in his memory. In that instant, all suppressed emotions, the loneliness of investigating alone these years, his worry and guilt for Yuanzhi, mixed with the impact of witnessing death, nearly overwhelmed him. He clenched his fist, nails piercing his palm, using pain to force himself to stay calm.

He took Yuanzhi's sock and that birthday sticky note. Not for the bounty, but to remember. To remember this gray wolf who protected him in such a tragic way, this one he had loved. To remember **Beast Shield** had added another blood debt.

When Linxi left the factory, his back was ramrod straight, but every step was exceptionally heavy. He knew that from today on, his investigation was no longer just for justice or truth, but also carried a heavy personal vengeance. Yuanzhi's death was like a thorn, deeply embedded in his heart, every heartbeat bringing pain, and also bringing firmer resolve.

In his pocket, that birthday sticky note pressed against the police academy photo. One asked him to "stay alive," one forever frozen in a brilliantly smiling past. And he, must carry these two weights, continue walking in the darkness, until he tears open a crack of light in the world that made Yuanzhi have to put on the **white uniform** and pull the trigger on himself.

He might succeed, or he might one day, like Yuanzhi, fall in some dark corner. But at least for now, he is still alive. Carrying the memories of two people and unfulfilled wishes, alive.

### Story Reflections

*I see a story about how love and sacrifice can happen in the most impossible of environments. Wolf Yuanzhi, a sniper trapped in an evil organization, on his birthday was ordered to kill the one he loved. He chose a third path: using the end of his own life to preserve his lover's survival, while not betraying that twisted order (he did fire the gun, just missed). This is an ultimate tenderness, and also an ultimate despair.*

*In this world where all characters are **male beastmen**, love also exists, is also profound, and can also make people make incredible choices. The unspoken feelings between Tiger Linxi and Wolf Yuanzhi, pushed to opposite ends of the life-and-death sniper line by fate and their respective choices. One thought distance was protection, one thought leaving was helplessness, yet ultimately completed their last silent dialogue in bullets and blood.*

*This story also profoundly reveals the cruelty of the **Beast Shield** system: it can turn people into tools, turn birthdays into death days, turn the most intimate emotions into a coordinate on a mission order. Wolf Yuanzhi's death is his personal tragedy, and also the inevitable product of this twisted world's rules. His "white sniper" uniform was ultimately dyed the color of sacrifice by his own blood and semen.*

*And Tiger Linxi, this survivor, will carry this heavy memory and continue forward. In this world of male beastmen, his anger, sorrow, and love will all become the most resilient motivation on the road of investigation. Perhaps one day, he can uncover the truth, for Yuanzhi, and for all lives swallowed by **Beast Shield**, win back a little justice.*

*A birthday should be a day to celebrate life, but here it becomes the endpoint of a death countdown. Yet even at such an endpoint, the beautiful parts of human nature (or beast nature)—love, sacrifice, memory—still flicker with faint light in the darkness, like that unopened energy bar's sticky note, like that taken white sock with the number printed on it. They are light as a feather, yet heavy enough to bend a strong beastman's spine, and heavy enough to support him through the rest of the thorn-covered road.*
