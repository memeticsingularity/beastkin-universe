## 一、简介 / Introduction

**一句话钩子**：在兽盾，连死亡都变得如此日常，如此廉价——但每一个死去的角色，都曾是一个完整的生命。

这是一个基于**兽盾世界观**的日常短篇故事集，通过多个独立成篇的故事，展现兽盾公司不同场景下的生活切片。从公司门口的蛋糕店到员工宿舍的深夜，从新兵营的霸凌现场到食堂的等级冲突，从毕业实习的荒郊仓库到狙击镜里的昨日恋人——每个故事都为一名普通的G级杂兵赋予完整的生命故事。

系列采用AI与人类共创模式，深度挖掘官方设定中的日常场景。不止于暴力描写，更探索暴力背后的系统性原因：一个生日蛋糕如何成为死亡祭品，一声鼾声如何点燃杀戮，一次正义干预如何换来悲剧结局，一顿午餐如何决定职业生涯的生死。在兽盾，死亡毫无价值，杀得顺手，死后像垃圾处理——但数字背后，是否也曾有过温度？

---

**One-line Hook**: In Beastshield, even death becomes so routine, so cheap — but every deceased character was once a complete life, with dreams, fears, and insignificant hopes.

This is a collection of daily short stories based on the **Beastshield Universe**, presenting slices of life in different scenarios within the Beastshield Company through multiple independent tales. From the cake shop at the company gate to the late-night employee dormitory, from bootcamp bullying to cafeteria hierarchy clashes, from graduation internships in desolate warehouses to yesterday's lover in a sniper scope — each story endows an ordinary G-rank grunt with a complete life story.

The series adopts an AI-human co-creation model, deeply exploring daily scenes from official settings. Beyond mere violence depiction, it explores the systemic causes behind violence: how a birthday cake becomes a death offering, how a snore ignites murder, how an act of righteous intervention leads to tragedy, how a lunch decides a career's life or death. In Beastshield, death is worthless, killing is casual, and corpses are disposed of like garbage — but behind the numbers, was there ever warmth?
