**Scene 1: The Depths of Night**

**Scene 6: The End of Night**

But, they had no choice.

"Let's go." Leizhao finally said; he slowly sat up, feeling every pain and weakness in his body. "We... we walk together."

Niu Pan looked at this young tiger beastman, suddenly feeling a... long-lost emotion. That feeling of a father wanting to protect his son, that impulse to sacrifice himself to let the young live.

But they were no longer just two strangers. They were... a father and son depending on each other, in this cruel world, becoming each other's only family.

They didn't know what awaited them ahead. Didn't know whether they could successfully escape, didn't know whether they could survive in this cruel world.

But at this moment, they had each other.

And perhaps, that was enough.

**Scene 7: Final Preparations**

Before leaving, they needed to make some preparations.

"Okay?" Niu Pan asked, "Can you hold on?"

"Can." Leizhao nodded, then smiled bitterly, "Just... the taste isn't very good."

Niu Pan let out a low laugh; that laugh carried a... comfort. In this desperate situation, the young man could still joke, indicating his spirit hadn't been crushed.

"Let's go." Niu Pan said, extending an arm for Leizhao to support. "Follow me closely. The road is still long."

Leizhao gripped that thick arm, feeling the power transmitted through that rough skin. That touch reminded him of his father's hand — that pair of hands that had once held him walking mountain roads, taught him hunting, and finally worked to death at a construction site.

"Call me Uncle." Niu Pan seemed to understand his hesitation, saying in a light voice, "I'm over twenty years older than you... calling Uncle, appropriate."

"Uncle Niu." Leizhao called in a light voice.

Niu Pan's body slightly trembled. Those simple two words made him feel a... long-lost warmth. That was the feeling of family, the feeling of being needed, the feeling of being respected as an elder.

"Mm." He responded, voice somewhat hoarse, "Let's go, child. Together... leave this damned place."

They took one last look at this corpse pile — this place that had witnessed their "death" and "rebirth."
Those corpses, those beastmen who had once been alive, breathing, dreaming like them, were now just... piles of garbage about to be processed.

"We will remember you." Leizhao said in a light voice, not knowing whether he was speaking to those corpses or to himself.

Then, he and Niu Pan supported each other, beginning to walk toward the depths of the wilderness.

Their backs gradually grew smaller in the morning light, finally disappearing into that gray horizon.

Two "dead men," began their escape.
