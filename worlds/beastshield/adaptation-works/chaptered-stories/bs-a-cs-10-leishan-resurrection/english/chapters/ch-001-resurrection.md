# Chapter 1: Breathing in the Trash Can

---

**Scene 1: Waking in Darkness**

The first sensation to return was cold.

Not the kind of cold from winter wind against skin, but a bone-deep, inside-out cold. As if his entire body had been soaked in ice water. Every limb had lost its warmth; only his heart — if it was still beating — maintained the bare minimum signs of life.

Leizhao's consciousness rose like a drowning man being slowly pulled up from the bottom of the deep sea, penetrating the heavy darkness bit by bit, with agonizing difficulty.

He didn't know where he was. The last image in his memory was: a corner, shadows, a large hand reaching out from the darkness, then the suffocating feeling of his throat being squeezed, the shame and terror of his genitals being gripped, that scorching flow of energy rushing into his body, forcing him erect to the point of pain, and then —

Then he knew nothing.

Now, he tried to open his eyes, but his eyelids were as heavy as if filled with lead. He tried to move his fingers, but his limbs seemed not his own, offering no response.

**Am I still alive?**

This question surfaced in his chaotic consciousness. If he was dead, why could he still feel? If he was alive, why wouldn't his body obey?

He tried to take a deep breath, but his lungs felt crushed, able to draw in only the tiniest amount of air. That air was saturated with complex odors — rot, sourness, and a strong, fishy male musk. It was his own smell, only dozens of times stronger than usual, mixed with sweat, fear, and some bodily fluid he didn't want to think about.

Trash can.

The word suddenly leaped from the depths of memory. He remembered — that small, naked figure, that being called "Liang," dragging him toward a green plastic container. He remembered the process of being stuffed inside, his waist caught at the rim, two legs sticking out, his upper body forced into the dark interior.

He was in that trash can right now.

**Scene 2: The Body's Betrayal**

As time passed second by second, more sensations returned. And these sensations brought not comfort, but deeper confusion and terror.

He felt his posture — upper body curled at the bottom of the trash can, head tilted to one side, cheek pressed against something rough and damp. It was a garbage bag, or some other discarded debris. His neck was twisted at an uncomfortable angle, his spine aching dully.

His waist was caught at the rim of the can, numb from prolonged pressure. But he could feel the plastic edge of the rim squeezing his lumbar vertebrae; that blunt pain was real, proof that he was alive.

And then there was his lower body.

Two legs exposed above the knee, sticking out at an awkward angle, pointing upward. Night wind blew past his ankles, bringing waves of coolness. His calves, clad in white knee-high socks, were exposed to the air. The sock cuffs had loosened from the earlier dragging, sliding down to reveal more rough tiger fur.

His right foot — he tried to feel his right foot — the shoe was gone. That olive-green sneaker had fallen off somewhere, and now his right foot wore only the white sock, the sole directly touching the cold air.

His left foot still seemed to have a shoe on, but the laces were loose, the shoe hanging loosely on his foot, ready to fall off at any moment.

What he found hardest to accept was the state of his genitals.

His olive-green tights were still on, but the condition of his crotch made him afraid to think too hard. The fabric was wetly plastered against his skin, heavy, sticky, gently rubbing against sensitive skin with each faint breath. That level of wetness wasn't something sweat could achieve, nor was it simply urine. That thick texture, and the lingering fishy musk, were all reminders of what had just happened.

He had been forced to ejaculate. Under the grip of that large hand, he had come, and he had come a lot.

**Scene 3: The Return of Heartbeat**

Leizhao began trying to sense his own heartbeat.

At first, he felt nothing. His chest cavity seemed utterly silent, no pulsation, no sound of blood flow. This plunged him into deeper panic — if his heart wasn't beating, what was he? A zombie? A ghost? Or some monster caught between life and death?

But when he focused all his attention on his chest, he finally detected it.

Thud.

Faint, almost imperceptible, but it was indeed a heartbeat. Very slow, perhaps only twenty or thirty beats per minute, and very weak, like a drumbeat from far away.

Thud.

After another long second, the second beat.

This discovery gave him a strange sort of comfort. He was still alive, in an extremely faint, extremely dangerous way. His body was maintaining life at the lowest possible metabolic rate, like a hibernating animal, like a frozen insect, waiting for spring.

But spring would not come on its own.

He realized that if he continued lying here, the final outcome would only be one of two: either he would truly die from hypothermia, or he would be discovered by the morning sanitation workers, and then —

And then what? Thrown into an incinerator like garbage? Or sent back to Beastshield, to be disposed of as a violated waste?

No. He could not allow that to happen.

**Scene 4: The First Attempt**

Leizhao began trying to control his body.

He chose the simplest starting point — his fingers.

He concentrated all his spirit, trying to make his right index finger move. At first, nothing happened. His fingers were like frozen sticks, unresponsive. But he didn't give up, sending the command over and over in his mind: move, move, move.

Then, he felt it.

Very faint, but undeniably real — the fingertip of his right index finger twitched slightly.

This tiny success was like lighting a fire. He began trying more fingers, then his entire palm. Each successful control brought more confidence, and more confidence accelerated his body's recovery.

But the process was excruciatingly slow, and accompanied by immense pain.

As blood began to flow back into his limbs, the tingling pain after numbness faded nearly made him cry out. It was like countless needles stabbing into his blood vessels at once, like being gnawed by millions of ants. His fingers began to tremble uncontrollably, muscles spasming from prolonged stillness.

He clenched his jaw — he discovered his jaw could move too — enduring the pain. His tiger fangs pierced the soft flesh on the inside of his lip, and the taste of blood spread through his mouth. This taste made him more awake.

**Scene 5: The World Outside the Trash Can**

While struggling to regain control of his body, Leizhao began trying to perceive his surroundings.

His ears — fortunately his ears had been working all along — captured sounds from outside the trash can.

The night wind blew gently, bringing the rustling of distant leaves. Occasionally there would be patrol footsteps in the base, but very distant, probably in the inner perimeter. He could hear his own breathing, faint and rapid, and his heartbeat, gradually speeding up.

No voices. No sounds of those two hunters.

They were gone. This realization brought him both relief and terror. Relief that they wouldn't return to finish the job; terror that he was now truly alone, with no help.

He tried to open his eyes.

At first, his vision was blurry. In the darkness, there were only faint shadows flickering. But as his eyes gradually adapted, he began to distinguish more details.

What he saw was the interior of the trash can — stains hanging on the green plastic walls, the rim of the can above his head revealing a patch of starry sky. It was the real night sky; White Capital's light pollution wasn't that severe, and the stars were clearly visible.

He looked at those stars and suddenly felt an uncontrollable sadness.

Not long ago, he had been planning his future. Opening a martial arts hall, teaching kids to practice martial arts, saving up to buy a small house in the city. Those dreams now seemed so distant, so unrealistic. He had nearly died, in this trash can, like a discarded piece of meat.

But the stars were still there. And so was he.

**Scene 6: The True State of the Body**

As more sensations returned, Leizhao began to truly face the state of his body.

First, he realized he needed to deal with his pants.

That wet crotch was growing cold; night wind blew in from outside the can, carrying away heat. If he didn't clean up soon, he might truly die from hypothermia. And that sticky feeling — the feeling after semen dries — made him extremely uncomfortable.

But he had no ability to stand up now, let alone find water to wash.

Second, he felt extreme hunger and thirst.

That hunger wasn't ordinary stomach hunger, but a bone-deep craving from his body demanding energy supplementation. His stomach was twitching, emitting faint gurgling sounds. He remembered the synthetic meat steak he had eaten at dinner; that bit of energy had long been exhausted.

The thirst was even more unbearable. His throat was as dry as if sandpapered, each swallow carrying a stinging pain. He craved water, any water, even dirty water.

Then, he noticed *that*.

At the bottom of the trash can where he was curled up, some liquid had pooled into small puddles. That had flowed from his own body — sweat, semen, perhaps some other bodily fluids. The liquid was indistinguishable in color in the darkness, but by smell, he knew what it was.

A thought surfaced in his mind.

He pushed the thought away. That was too crazy, too humiliating. He would rather die than —

But his body made the decision for him.

When he tried to shift his body, adjusting his posture, his lips — dry and cracked — came into contact with that liquid. The sensation was wet, sticky, carrying a strong salty taste and fishy musk. His first reaction was nausea, wanting to spit it out.

But his tongue betrayed him.

The taste buds on his tongue, upon contact with the liquid, sent a strong signal to his brain. That was water, electrolytes, protein. That was what he needed most right now.

His body craved survival more than his reason did.

**Scene 7: Licking**

Leizhao closed his eyes, letting himself sink into that humiliating act.

His tongue extended from between his lips, slowly, tentatively licking across the plastic surface at the bottom of the trash can. The liquid was thicker than he imagined, its temperature slightly lower than his body heat, carrying a strange warmth.

The first swallow made his stomach convulse violently, as if protesting the source of this food. But his body quickly accepted it, beginning to greedily demand more.

He began to more actively collect that liquid, licking with his tongue, sucking with his lips. His movements were clumsy because his body was still stiff, his posture awkward. But he didn't stop.

In that moment, he was no longer a Beastshield recruit, no longer a young man with dreams, no longer a dreamer who wanted to open a martial arts hall. He was just a creature, a creature stripped of dignity, forced to survive in the most primitive way.

And that liquid — his own bodily fluid — was indeed having an effect.

He could feel that as the liquid entered his stomach, his body was undergoing some change. That bone-deep weakness began to lessen, the temperature in his limbs began to rise, and the clarity of his thinking improved.

This wasn't some magical recovery, just the most basic physiological reaction after having a fundamental need met. But for him, it was enough.

**Scene 8: Struggle and Freedom**

When his strength had recovered somewhat — really only a little, perhaps enough to support a few minutes of activity — Leizhao began trying to get out of the trash can.

This was more difficult than he imagined.

His upper body was stuck inside the can, his waist tightly caught by the rim edge. He tried pushing the can walls with his hands, but the flexibility of the plastic greatly reduced his efforts. He tried twisting his body, but that only made his waist hurt more.

He began to regret eating so much cafeteria food. His weight of nearly three hundred pounds was normally a symbol of strength; now it was a shackle.

He changed strategy.

He no longer tried to push straight out. Instead, he first shrank his upper body deeper into the can, then grabbed the upper edge of the can wall with his hands, using the strength of his arms, coordinating with the twisting of his body, pulling himself upward bit by bit.

The process was excruciatingly painful.

His waist was squeezed by the rim edge, leaving a deep red mark. His skin rubbed against the rough plastic, bringing a fiery pain. His arm muscles trembled, biceps and triceps bulging with the effort, veins popping. Sweat poured from the pores all over his body, mixing with the previous bodily fluids, making his body even more slippery.

But he didn't give up.

Once, twice, three times. Each attempt brought him closer to freedom.

Finally, after a particularly forceful pull, he felt the constriction at his waist loosen. He seized this opportunity, twisting his body violently while finding a support point outside the can with his hand.

*Splash.*

He slid out of the trash can, his upper body falling heavily onto the cold ground.

**Scene 9: Gasping in the Night Wind**

Leizhao lay prone on the ground, gasping for breath.

The night wind blew directly on his exposed skin — his upper garment was still on, but already a mess, exposing large patches of chest and abdominal fur. The wind was cold, but to him, it was the air of freedom, proof that he was alive.

He maintained this posture, lying on the ground like a beached fish, for several minutes.

His body was trembling, not from cold, but from extreme muscle fatigue and nervous tension. His heart was pounding in his chest, perhaps over a hundred beats per minute, in stark contrast to his previously faint heartbeat.

He slowly raised his head, surveying his surroundings.

It was a corner of a wall, just as he remembered. The green trash can stood by the wall, its lid half-open, revealing the dark space inside. His shoe — the right one — lay beside the trash can, sole up, showing the number G-147.

He reached out with a trembling hand and grabbed that shoe.

The shoe was olive-green, heavy, the upper covered with dust and some white stains. He pulled the shoe to his side, then, with the slowest speed, bit by bit, stuffed his foot into it.

This simple action exhausted most of his strength. But when both feet were in shoes, he felt a strange sense of completeness. He was still wearing his uniform, he still belonged to Beastshield, he still had an identity, still had a number.

He wasn't garbage. At least, he wasn't just garbage.

**Scene 10: The Next Step**

Leizhao leaned against the wall and sat up.

This posture made him dizzy, black spots appearing at the edges of his vision. But he held on, not falling again.

He looked at his hands. Those hands covered in yellow tiger fur, with thick knuckles and sharp claws. Not long ago, those hands couldn't even make a fist. Now, though still trembling, they could execute his commands.

He touched his throat.

There was a bruise there, very deep, sending a sharp pain when pressed. That was the mark of being strangled, the handiwork of that hunter called Yan. He remembered the feel of that hand — rough, powerful, carrying the chill of death.

He touched his own genitals.

The crotch of his olive-green tights had dried somewhat, but was still stiff, like a piece of starched fabric. The skin inside was aching dully, the sensitivity from overstimulation intertwined with discomfort from friction.

But he was still alive.

This was the most incredible fact. He had experienced that degree of ejaculation, that intensity of suffocation, that humiliation of being discarded like garbage, but he was still alive.

He had heard some legends about beastmen — about those individuals with incredibly tenacious vitality, about that mysterious ability to enter a feigned-death state on the brink of death. He had never thought he would possess this ability, let alone discover it in such circumstances.

But now was not the time to think about these things.

He needed to leave this place. Before the next patrol arrived, before dawn, before he was discovered by anyone.

He propped himself against the wall and slowly stood up.

His legs trembled, his knees weak, but he stood. Nearly three hundred pounds of weight pressed on two legs — that was the weight he was familiar with, the symbol of his strength.

He took a deep breath. Night wind rushed into his lungs, bringing stinging pain and clarity.

Then, he took the first step.
