>
> **Type**: IF Branch / Side Story
> **Perspective**: Leizhao (G-147 Tiger Guard from the original Yan Liang story)
> **Setting**: A derivative plot based on the "Sperm Retention Feign Death" mechanism from the main storyline

---

## Story Synopsis

This is the story of Leizhao — that tiger beastman who, while patrolling the outer perimeter of the Beastshield recruit training base, was subdued by the bounty hunter Yan, forced to ejaculate, and thrown into a trash can.

But this time, he did not die.

On the brink of death, his young body erupted with an incredible survival instinct. When Yan and Liang left, when the night grew deeper, when the other corpses in the trash can slowly turned cold, Leizhao opened his eyes in a mixture of semen and sweat.

This is a story about coming back from the dead, about escape and survival, about two beastmen marked as "dead" by the system who depend on each other in the shadows.

## Worldview Setting

This story is based on the following settings of the Beastshield universe:

- **Sperm Retention Skill (Biejingong)**: Some beastmen (especially bull species with astonishing semen storage) can, through special training, retain a portion of their semen during ejaculation and enter a feigned-death state
- **Feigned Death**: Beastmen in a feigned-death state have extremely low metabolic rates; heartbeat and breathing are almost undetectable
- **Recovery**: Those who have feigned death need time to recover. During this period, they are extremely weak and need nutritional supplements (usually... their own bodily fluids)
- **Two Accidental Feigned-Death Survivors**:
    - **Leizhao** (G-147): Tiger beastman recruit who inadvertently triggered his feigned-death ability, knowing nothing of Beastshield's darkness
    - **Niu Pan** (E-011): Bull beastman senior officer, proficient in sperm retention skill, deeply aware of Beastshield's cruel rules
