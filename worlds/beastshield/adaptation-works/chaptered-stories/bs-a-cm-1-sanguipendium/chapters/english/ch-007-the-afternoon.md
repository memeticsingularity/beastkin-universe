Hu Zhaojun glanced at Hu Liuhui. That young tiger beastman's eyes held curiosity, no malice. He hesitated for a moment, then handed the ring over.

Hu Liuhui took the ring and examined it carefully. It was an ancient ring, a silver setting inlaid with a spreading eagle—that was the crest of Hu Zhaojun's family.

"Very beautiful." Hu Liuhui said. "Why... are you here?"

"Because my father was branded a traitor." Hu Zhaojun's voice was calm, like narrating a story that had nothing to do with him. "The family fell, I was reduced to E-level. I came here to restore my honor."

"How to restore it?"

"Become champion." Hu Zhaojun took back the ring. "Or, die with dignity."

Hu Liuhui fell silent. He looked at Hu Zhaojun's naked body—that demeanor that remained dignified despite his fall, those eyes that remained proud even after being reduced to E-level.

"Can I sit next to you?" he asked.

Hu Zhaojun glanced at him, then nodded slightly.

The two tigers sat side by side on the bench, their naked upper bodies exposed to the sun. One was a fallen noble, one was a young contestant. Ten years separated them, but at this moment they shared the same patch of sunlight.

---

Xiong Linsheng and Xiong Wugang sat on a bench in the courtyard, keeping each other company in silence.

The forty-two-year-old old bear and the middle-aged bear with hollow eyes, naked from the waist up, their fur grayish-white and dark gray respectively.

"My partner died in the 'Cage.'" Xiong Wugang suddenly spoke, his voice hollow.

Xiong Linsheng didn't speak, just listened quietly.

"He traded himself to save me." Xiong Wugang's voice held no emotional fluctuation, like stating a fact. "Three months later, I received his corpse."

"I came here to die with dignity." Xiong Linsheng said, his voice hoarse. "Forty-two years old, sixteen years at E-level, no hope whatsoever."

"You want to die?"

"I want to choose the manner of my own death." Xiong Linsheng turned his head, looking at Xiong Wugang. "Dying on the arena is better than dying in a corner."

The two bears sat naked side by side, the afternoon sun shining on them. Their body temperatures transferred in the air, about two degrees higher than the surrounding temperature.

"If we meet on the arena," Xiong Wugang said, "I won't go easy."

"Me neither." Xiong Linsheng responded. "That is the final respect for each other."

---

Near the dog dorm, Quan Zhengwei was telling stories of the past.

That thirty-five-year-old Rottweiler was naked from the waist up, sitting on the steps, surrounded by several young dogs—Quan Songliang, Quan Xiejun, Quan Heshou, Quan Yong.

"Ten years ago at the competition," Quan Zhengwei's voice was low, "I saw a man who had three legs broken on the arena, but still didn't give up."

"Why?" Quan Songliang asked, his ice-blue eyes full of curiosity.

"Because he had someone waiting for him to go back." Quan Zhengwei said. "His wife, his child."

"Did he win in the end?"

"No." Quan Zhengwei's voice was calm. "But he died smiling. Because he knew, he had given his all."

The young dogs fell silent. The "smile" at the corners of Quan Xiejun's mouth hung once more, but this time, there was a hint of understanding in his eyes.

"This is the Blood-Bounty Elimination Tournament," Quan Zhengwei said. "Not for winning, but to prove that we once lived."

---

The wall clock pointed to 17:00.

The cafeteria door opened, beginning to serve dinner.

Niu Xuqiang, naked from the waist up, walked toward the cafeteria with Niu Majun, Niu Huhui, and Niu Guoming. Their fur displayed different hues in the afternoon light—grayish-white, brownish-yellow, grayish-white, dark gold.

"Five o'clock." Niu Xuqiang said, glancing at his phone. "Dinner time."

"Then..." Niu Majun's voice carried some kind of premonition, "it's night."

"Yeah." Niu Xuqiang put his phone back in his pocket. "Night."

They walked into the cafeteria naked, the sunlight behind them slowly sinking in the west.

---

**Chapter 7 END**

> *The afternoon sunlight has dissipated, but the body still remembers that temperature. Training, conversation, sweat, comfort—Dorm A and Dorm B are not two worlds, they are different figures in the same courtyard. They are naked from the waist up, brushing past each other in the corridors, sitting side by side on benches, assessing each other in training. Tomorrow is the lottery day, but at this moment, they are just thirty-two male beastmen, in the same cage, waiting for the same night.*
