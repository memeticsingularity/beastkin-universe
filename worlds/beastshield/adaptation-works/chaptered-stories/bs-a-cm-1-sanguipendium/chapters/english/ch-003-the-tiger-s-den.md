It was a completely different lighting scheme from the Ox Camp. The Ox Camp used high-wattage industrial lamps that emitted a harsh, bluish-white cold light, illuminating everything and leaving no shadow. The Tiger Camp, by contrast, used specially designed warm-toned lights; the rays were golden-yellow, simulating natural light at sunrise, providing sufficient illumination while preserving a certain degree of shadow and depth.

This lighting design was no accident. The tiger beastmen's visual system had a different sensitivity to light than the ox beastmen—their eyes were better suited to capturing detail in dim environments, and intense light actually caused them discomfort. Therefore, the Tiger Camp's lighting design took this physiological difference into account, creating the most suitable visual environment for tiger beastmen.

No matter what happens tomorrow, no matter what opponent the drawing brings, no matter whether the tournament's ending is life or death, at this moment, they were all still alive. And this was the foundation of everything, the beginning of everything, the hope of everything.
