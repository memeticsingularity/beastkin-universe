## Act 1 The Wolf's Struggle

The gray wolf was the first to awaken.

When his consciousness surfaced from the anesthetic mist, the first thing he felt was restraint—his wrists tied behind his back, his body immobile, like a beast caught in a trap.

Then, he saw that face.

Pale, ordinary, carrying a certain hair-raising smile. Those amber eyes were looking down at him, as if admiring a delicacy about to be savored.

"Awake?" The man's voice was soft to the point of near-tenderness. "Don't rush to move. Your body still needs time to recover."

"What... what do you want?" The gray wolf's voice was hoarse, his throat as if scorched by fire.

"I want to watch you struggle," the man smiled, his fingers gently parting the gray-white fur covering the wolf's face. "I want to watch you go from resistance to submission, from hope to despair. That feeling... is more fascinating than the result alone."

He began undoing the gray wolf's uniform, his movements slow and deliberate, like unwrapping a precious gift.

The gray wolf tried to struggle, but his paralyzed muscles refused to obey. He could only lie there, feeling that hand wandering across his body, sliding from chest to abdomen, lingering at his waist.

"Your body is very sensitive," the man murmured, his fingers threading through that layer of gray-white fur. "Wolf-kind fur is coarse, but the skin beneath is very delicate. That contrast... is quite interesting."

His hand enveloped the gray wolf's most vulnerable part, that burning temperature making the gray wolf gasp.

"Don't..." The gray wolf closed his eyes, tears seeping from the corners.

"Yes," the man's voice was like the temptation of a demon. "Your body has already begun to respond. See? It's more honest than you are."

He began to move, slowly and rhythmically.

The gray wolf felt his body betraying him. Those forced waves surged one after another, washing away his reason, washing away his dignity. He tried to resist, tried to think of other things, but that stimulation was too intense, too intense to ignore.

"Please... spare me..." His voice trembled, carrying a crying tone.

"Beg me?" The man laughed, his hand moving faster. "Fine, beg me. Let me hear your pleading."

The gray wolf clenched his jaw, refusing to make another sound. But his body was still responding under that forced stimulation, that physiological reaction occurring uncontrollably.

The man was not impatient. He savored this tug-of-war—the prey's resistance, the prey's shame, the prey's tearing between physiology and psychology. He used his fingers to tease the gray wolf's most sensitive parts, fast then slow, light then heavy, making that wave recede when it was about to reach its peak, then pushing it up again.

"How long can you endure in this situation?" the man asked curiously. "One minute? Two minutes?"

The gray wolf's body began to tremble violently, that prolonged prelude making sensitivity painful. His breathing grew rapid, his chest heaving violently, gray-white fur soaked with sweat, clinging to his skin in strands.

"Can't endure it anymore?" The man laughed. "Then release. Let me see how wolf-kind trembles."

When the wave finally struck, the gray wolf's body arched, a suppressed wail squeezed from his throat. That release brought not relief, but deeper despair—he felt his life force flowing away with that liquid, felt something essential leaving him.

But the man did not give him time to rest.

"The first time," he murmured, but his hand did not stop. "Wolf-kind should be able to do more times, right?"

"No... can't anymore..." The gray wolf gasped. "Too sensitive... can't endure..."

"You can endure," the man's fingers began teasing again. "Your body is still young, still full of vitality. Let me see where your limit is."

The second round of stimulation came even more fiercely. The man used more techniques, his fingers wandering across the gray wolf's body, from nipples to navel, from inner thighs to the most private parts, every sensitive point carefully teased.

The gray wolf began to weep, truly weep, tears blurring his vision. His body was emptied again and again, each time weaker than the last, more painful.

When the third wave struck, he no longer had any strength to resist. His body lay limp on the floor, eyes open, but his pupils already dilated.

The man checked his pulse, his fingertips feeling no beat beneath. That body that had once struggled so fiercely was now completely relaxed, gray-white fur in disarray upon sweat-soaked skin, like a stray dog drenched by rain. He stood up, his gaze passing over that face still carrying tear stains—those eyes were still open, but the pupils had dilated, losing all luster.

The basement air was permeated with a mixture of sweat and despair. The man took a deep breath, as if collecting this scent into memory, then turned, his footsteps tapping out crisp sounds on the concrete floor, walking toward the next waiting body.

## Act 2 The Bear's Heaviness

The brown bear awoke to a feeling of heavy oppression.

Not from fear, but from his own body—that massive build, that thick chest and belly, making breathing heavy even in unconsciousness.

The man's hand was wandering across his chest, sinking into that thick layer of fat, pressing the muscle beneath.

"Your fat layer is very thick," the man's voice carried marvel. "Feels like a quilt, soft, warm, with solid power hidden beneath."

"Let me go..." The brown bear's voice was deep and slow, like thunder rumbling from deep within his chest.

"Don't be in such a hurry," the man laughed. "Your body tells me you can endure a great deal. Let me see how much life force is hidden within this massive body."

He began undoing the brown bear's uniform. That massive build made this process difficult, the uniform stretched tight, buttons almost popping open.

When the last piece of fabric was lifted, the sight before him made the man hold his breath—a broad chest covered in dense brown fur, pectorals thick and full, wrapped in a layer of fat. The abdomen round and firm, the back broad, the thighs thick.

"Magnificent," he murmured. "Such a massive body..."

His hands began to wander across the brown bear's body, from thick pectorals to round abdomen, feeling that transition from soft to hard.

Unlike the way he treated the gray wolf, this time the man adopted a slower rhythm. He knew bear-kind's life force was tenacious, knew this body could endure more, so he intended to savor this process.

His fingers threaded through that fat, pressing the muscle beneath, feeling that slow and powerful pulse. The brown bear's reaction was also slow, each wave needing longer to brew, but each one more substantial.

"No rush," the man murmured. "We have plenty of time. Let me slowly... empty you."

He used an hour to bring this bear to his first release. That substantial liquid gushed forth, showing this body's vigorous life force.

But the man was not satisfied. He continued working, his fingers wandering across that massive body, from thick buttocks to thick thighs, from broad back to firm pectorals.

The brown bear's breathing grew heavier and heavier, that slow, rhythmic breathing gradually becoming rapid and chaotic. His heart was like an overloaded machine, pounding heavily in his chest.

"Can you still continue?" the man asked.

The brown bear did not answer, but his body was still responding, still trembling under that forced stimulation.

The man laughed, continuing his work. He used various techniques to stimulate this bear—fingers, palms, even lips and tongue, threading through that dense fur, tasting that salty sweat.

When the fifth wave struck, the brown bear's body had already begun to collapse. His breathing became intermittent, his heartbeat irregular.

But the man still did not stop. He used drugs to enhance the brown bear's sensitivity, making that already exhausted body harden again, tremble again, release again.

Sixth time... seventh time... eighth time...

Each time more difficult, each time more painful. There was no fear on the brown bear's face, only a deep exhaustion, like finally freed from some heavy burden.

When the ninth wave struck, it was already a dry spasm, almost no liquid, just the body's last struggle.

The man stood up, looking down at that massive body. That chest that had once been as heavy as a mountain range was no longer rising and falling; beneath that fat layer that had once wrapped solid power, the heart had stopped beating. The brown bear's eyes were half-open, pupils dilated, no fear in his gaze, only a finally-unburdened exhaustion, like a traveler who had walked too long finally finding a place to rest.

"A mountain has collapsed," the man murmured to himself, his fingers finally brushing through that dense brown fur, feeling that gradually dissipating warmth. He turned and left, his footsteps especially light beside that massive body.

## Act 3 The Tiger's Pride

The yellow tiger awoke with angry fire burning in his eyes.

Even with his body controlled by paralysis, those amber eyes still flickered with unyielding light. He looked at the man looking down at him, his gaze full of contempt and anger.

"You coward!" he roared, his voice echoing in the basement. "If you have the guts, let me go! Fight me fair and square!"

The man laughed, that smile carrying genuine appreciation.

"Tiger-kind's pride, unwilling to lower its head even in the direst straits. I like it." His fingers traced the tiger's pectorals, feeling that rock-like hardness. "But pride cannot save you. Here, you are just prey."

"Shut up!"

"I want to watch your pride shatter piece by piece," the man began undoing his uniform. "Watch your eyes, watch your dignity collapse before physiological reaction."

Unlike the previous prey, this time the man adopted a more humiliating approach.

He forced the yellow tiger to watch him. Using his fingers to prop open his eyelids, making him unable to look away, making him watch himself being toyed with, being forced to respond.

"Look at me," the man commanded. "I want you to watch with your own eyes how your body betrays you."

He began to move, slowly and forcefully.

The yellow tiger clenched his jaw, refusing to make any sound. But his body was still responding under that stimulation, that physiological reaction occurring uncontrollably.

"Not talking?" The man laughed. "Fine. Let me see how long you can endure."

He used edging techniques—again and again pushing the yellow tiger to the edge of his limit, then stopping, letting him fall, then pushing him up again. That prolonged prelude turned pain into torture, turned sensitivity into agony.

Cold sweat beaded on the yellow tiger's forehead, orange fur soaked. His body trembled, muscles spasmed, but he still clenched his jaw, refusing to make any sound.

"Very enduring," the man praised, his fingers accelerating their rhythm. "But your body has already betrayed you."

When the first wave struck, the yellow tiger's body arched, but he still bit his lip, only emitting a suppressed low growl.

"Still have strength?" The man laughed. "Fine."

Second round, third round... The man gave him no time to rest, forcing him to harden again right after release, to tremble again, to release again.

Fourth round, fifth round... The yellow tiger's tears began to flow, tears of anger and humiliation. His gaze was still proud, but his body had already begun to collapse.

Sixth round, seventh round... His voice was already broken, that suppressed whimper squeezing out from between clenched teeth.

Eighth round... When the final wave struck, the yellow tiger's eyes were still open, those amber pupils still burning with unyielding flame, even though life had already left him.

The man reached out, gently closing those eyes that refused to close even in death. Even in death, that tightly locked brow still carried an unyielding stubbornness, as if the soul were still roaring somewhere invisible. On the yellow tiger's cheeks remained dried tear stains, the final weakness of a proud one, frozen upon this still-warm body.

"Even a tiger," the man murmured, his voice carrying a certain near-reverent sigh, "is ultimately only flesh and blood."

He took one last look at that golden mane, then turned, his figure merging into the shadows of the basement.

## Act 4 The Ox's Silence

The black ox awoke with only confusion in his eyes.

"I... I didn't do anything wrong..." His voice was deep and slow. "I just followed orders..."

"I know," the man's hand had already begun wandering across his body. "You were wronged. A scapegoat. A sacrifice."

"Then why do this to me?"

"Because this is your fate," the man's voice was flat. "Because you are too obedient, too compliant. You don't even know how to resist."

Unlike the previous prey, this time the man adopted a more mechanical approach.

He did not expect the black ox's response, did not expect his struggle or pleading. He just worked mechanically, like operating a machine, like completing a task.

His fingers pressed against the black ox's thick pectorals, feeling that solid muscle beneath that layer of fat. The black ox's reaction was also mechanical—no emotion, no fluctuation, just the body responding instinctively.

"You can't even cry?" The man was somewhat disappointed.

"What's the use of crying?" The black ox's voice was flat. "Dead either way."

"At least let me see some reaction."

"I have no reaction."

The man began using more intense techniques. He used drugs, used tools, used every means to stimulate this ox, trying to get a little emotional fluctuation from him.

But the black ox just lay there, eyes fixed on the ceiling, as if thinking about something, or as if thinking about nothing. There was no expression on his face, only a deep exhaustion.

First time, second time, third time... The man constantly stimulated him, emptying him again and again.

Fourth time, fifth time, sixth time... The black ox's body responded mechanically, but his gaze was still hollow.

Seventh time, eighth time... The man's techniques became more intense, trying to break that silence.

Ninth time... When the final wave struck, the black ox's heart finally stopped beating. There was still no expression on his face, only a relieved peace.

The man stood up, meeting those still-open black eyes. There was no resentment in that gaze, no questioning, only a hollow peace, as if asking "is it finally over," or as if thinking nothing at all. The black ox's silence would continue forever—no more endless compliance, no more mechanical responses, only this body lying here quietly, until forgotten.

"Even death is so compliant." The man shook his head and turned to leave. His footsteps echoed in the basement, while the black ox still lay there in silence, like a machine finally unplugged.

## Act 5 The Dog's Fragility

The yellow dog was the last to awaken.

When he awoke, tears were already streaming down his face.

"I want to go home..." His voice trembled, carrying a crying tone. "I want my mom... I'm only twenty-one... I don't want to die..."

The man's hand gently caressed his face, as if comforting a child.

"Shh, don't be afraid. It won't be long. Your body is very young, very sensitive... it will end soon."

Unlike the previous prey, this time the man adopted a gentler approach.

He knew this dog was too young, too fragile, unable to endure too much torture. So he intended to end it quickly, letting this young prey suffer less.

His fingers wandered across that green body, from not-yet-fully-developed pectorals to flat abdomen, from slender thighs to the most private parts.

The yellow dog's body responded very quickly, that sensitivity the sensitivity of youth, each touch bringing violent trembling.

"Don't... too sensitive... can't endure..." He wept, tears blurring his vision.

"It will be over soon," the man murmured, his fingers accelerating their rhythm. "Let me help you end all this."

The first wave came fast and fierce, the yellow dog's body trembling violently, a suppressed whimper squeezed from his throat. That release brought not pleasure, but deep shame and despair.

The man gave him no time to rest, immediately beginning the second round. He knew this young body recovered quickly, but also knew it couldn't endure many times.

When the second wave struck, the yellow dog was already lying limp on the floor, eyes unfocused, lips moving slightly, unable to speak.

Third time... When the final wave struck, the yellow dog's eyes rolled back, his body spasming violently, then falling still. His heart was too young, too fragile, unable to endure three strong impacts.

The man checked his pulse, his fingertips feeling no sign of life beneath. That still-childish face was unusually pale now, the corners of his eyes still holding un-dried tears, lips slightly open, as if there were words left unsaid, dreams left unfinished. A twenty-one-year-old life should have been running under the sun, playing on the grass, not ending in this dark basement in such a humiliating way.

"Too young." The man's voice rarely carried a trace of fluctuation, but that fluctuation was quickly suppressed. He straightened up, his gaze sweeping across the five bodies already lying on the floor—wolf, bear, tiger, ox, dog, each reaching their end in their own way.

The shadows of the basement seemed to grow a few shades darker. He straightened his clothes and walked toward the other five still-unconscious bodies.
