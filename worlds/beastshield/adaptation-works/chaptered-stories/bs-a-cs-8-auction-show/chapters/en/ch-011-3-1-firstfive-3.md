## Act 1 The Pup

He didn't know where he was.

Consciousness surfaced from the deep sea, carrying that post-drowning vertigo. G-203 tried hard to open his eyes, but his eyelids were as heavy as if glued shut. He could feel—could feel his own existence, feel his body contacting some cold surface, feel the faint flow of air in his nostrils.

But that feeling was strange.

As if his body was no longer his own.

As if his soul were stuffed into a container about to fall apart.

He tried to move a finger. No response. He tried to bend a knee. Still no response. Panic began spreading from his chest, like a ball of cold fire, burning his heart into wild pounding.

"Awake?"

That voice was very close, as if coming from above his head. G-203 finally barely opened his eyes—vision blurry, as if looking through a layer of water mist, but he could see a figure, a figure crouching at his side.

Pale face. Amber eyes. That hair-raising calm.

"Don't..." He wanted to speak, but his throat was as if blocked by something, only able to emit hoarse breath sounds.

"Shh." The man's finger pressed against his lips, that touch making G-203 shudder all over. That finger was hot, carrying a certain dangerous temperature. "Don't waste your strength. Your body... is very young, very sensitive. You won't have to endure for too long."

That voice sounded like comfort, yet made G-203's tears instantly well up.

He remembered.

He remembered everything.

That auction. That buyer. That van. Then... then came the gas. That sweet, irresistible gas, pouring in from the ventilation opening, like some living entity, drilling into his lungs, drawing his consciousness away bit by bit.

They had been bought.

All ten of them.

And now...

The man's hand began to move. That finger slid from G-203's lips down to his chin, then along the side of his neck downward, lingering at the collarbone. Every inch of touch was like burning; young skin had no defense against this stimulation, that sensitivity was the sensitivity of inexperience, the sensitivity of facing danger for the first time.

"Twenty-one years old," the man's voice was like talking to himself, or like introducing to some invisible audience. "Employed for two weeks. Made a mistake on the first mission... what a young body."

His palm pressed against G-203's chest.

Through that layer of olive-green uniform fabric, G-203 could feel the shape of that palm—broad, carrying thin calluses, full of controlling power. That palm lingered on his chest for a few seconds, as if feeling the rhythm of his heartbeat, then began sliding downward.

G-203 wanted to struggle.

He wanted to scream, wanted to run, wanted to do something—but his body wouldn't obey. That paralytic feeling spread from his spine to his limbs, making him lie there like a corpse, passively accepting every touch.

"Please..." He finally squeezed out a voice, carrying a crying tone. "I don't want to die..."

"I know," the man's voice was terrifyingly gentle. "But death is not something you can choose."

The uniform was undone.

Buttons were undone one by one, emitting slight sounds, like some countdown in the silent basement. G-203 could feel the instant cold air contacted his skin—that stimulation made his skin break out in goosebumps, every yellow short hair standing on end, like a frightened cat.

The man's gaze fell upon his body.

Young body. Well-proportioned build. Skin not yet marked by time and labor.

"Too young," the man's finger drew circles on that flat abdomen. "So young that even your muscles are still soft..."

He began to move.

G-203's body trembled violently. That stimulation was too intense—not because the technique was so fierce, but because this body was too young, too sensitive, every nerve ending screaming, every touch bringing electric-like shudders.

"Don't... too sensitive..." G-203 wept, tears flowing down his cheeks, dripping onto the cold floor. "Please... don't..."

But the man did not stop.

His fingers wandered across that green body, from not-yet-fully-developed pectorals to flat abdomen, from slender thighs to the most private parts. Every touch brought violent trembling, that sensitivity was the sensitivity of youth, the sensitivity that had not experienced pain.

G-203 felt his body betraying him.

That physiological reaction occurred uncontrollably, that shame flooding over him like a tide. He was only twenty-one, he had only been employed for two weeks, he still had so many things he wanted to do—he wanted to go home, wanted to see his mom, wanted to tell his dad he was wrong, he would be more careful next time—

When the wave struck, his body arched, a suppressed whimper squeezed from his throat.

That release brought not pleasure, but deeper despair. He felt his life force flowing away with that liquid, felt something essential leaving him.

But the man gave him no chance to catch his breath.

The second round of stimulation came faster. That young body had no time to recover from the first impact before being pushed to a second climax. G-203's vision began to blur, white spots appearing before his eyes, like some warning signal.

"No... can't anymore... heart..." He gasped, feeling his heart pounding wildly in his chest, as if about to break through the cage of his ribs.

"You can," the man's voice seemed to come from very far away. "Your body can still endure once more."

Third time...

When the wave struck again, G-203's eyes rolled back. His body spasmed violently, as if struck by electric current, then fell still. That heart—too young, too fragile—finally stopped beating after three strong impacts.

The man's finger lingered at the side of his neck, feeling the disappearance of pulse.

That still-childish face was unusually pale now, the corners of his eyes still holding un-dried tears, lips slightly open, as if there were words left unsaid, dreams left unfinished.

The basement fell into brief silence.

Only that young body lying there quietly, body temperature still there, but life already gone.

The man stood up, his gaze sweeping across that face, then turning toward another corner of the basement.

There were still nine bodies waiting.

## Act 2 The Cheetah

G-612 awoke to a strange stinging sensation.

Not from one place, but from his whole body—every pore, every inch of skin, screaming sensitivity. This was leopard-kind's nature; their nerve endings were denser than ordinary people's, their whole body a sensitive point.

He wanted to spring up, wanted to rush out like the wind, but that lean body completely refused to obey. He could only tremble slightly, like a trapped moth, wings stuck to the spider web.

"Leopard-kind," the man's voice rang out beside his ear, carrying a certain near-reverent emotion. "Your whole body is a sensitive point. Every inch of skin can feel the slightest touch. In the wild, this is your advantage, your hunting weapon. But here..."

His finger lightly pressed against the side of G-612's neck. "This is your curse."

The syringe pierced the blood vessel.

A few seconds later, G-612 felt that sensitivity amplified tenfold, a hundredfold. That body had already been pushed to the limit by drugs and innate sensitivity, every nerve ending screaming, every inch of skin able to feel the slightest change in airflow.

"Where... where is this..." His voice was hoarse and alert, carrying feline-kind's unique hissing sound.

"Your terminus," the man's hand had already begun wandering across his body, sliding from the side of his neck toward his chest. "Also the final stage of your life."

He began to move.

For leopard-kind, he didn't need to slowly build up. That body had already been pushed to the limit; every touch brought violent trembling. His fingers wandered across G-612's body, from behind the ear to the side of the neck, from chest to abdomen, from inner thighs to the most private parts.

Every touch brought a bout of violent trembling, a suppressed whimper, a bodily spasm.

"Don't... too sensitive... can't endure..." G-612's voice already carried a crying tone, that mask of the proud hunter was shattering. "Will die... really will die..."

But the man did not stop.

He adopted a completely different approach. He didn't use his hand to directly stimulate the most private parts, but began multi-point stimulation across the whole body—a feather lightly brushing behind the ear, a soft brush drawing circles on the chest, fingers wandering on inner thighs.

That multi-point simultaneous stimulation brought not pleasure, but torture. G-612's nerves were overstimulated, every signal screaming, every touch like an electric shock.

When the first wave struck, his body arched, like a fully drawn bow, a wail mixed with pain and humiliation squeezed from his throat.

But the man did not stop stimulating other parts.

Second wave, third wave... He constantly stimulated the leopard from different angles, different parts, making him unable to adapt, unable to resist. That whole-body sensitivity characteristic made him with nowhere to escape, nowhere to hide.

"Please... too intense..." G-612 was already bathed in tears, that lean body trembling violently under overstimulation, like a leaf in the wind. "Will die... really can't endure..."

Fourth wave, fifth wave...

Every wave was stronger than the last, more unbearable. G-612's nerves were already at the edge of collapse, that whole-body sensitivity characteristic making him with nowhere to escape.

Sixth wave...

When the final wave struck, G-612's body had completely collapsed. His heart stopped beating in violent spasms, those amber eyes still open, but pupils dilated, losing all luster.

The man gently stroked the black leopard's still-warm body, that once-sensitive flesh now only silence.

When he stood up, the shadows of the basement seemed to grow a few shades darker.

## Act 3 The Mountain

G-556 awoke to a feeling of heavy oppression.

Not from fear, but from his own body—that massive build, that thick chest and belly, making breathing heavy even in unconsciousness. He was bear-kind, brown bear race, height one meter ninety, weight over one hundred ten kilograms. That body was like a mountain; even death needed time to climb.

The man's hand was wandering across his chest, sinking into that thick layer of fat, pressing the muscle beneath.

"Your fat layer is very thick," the man's voice carried marvel. "Feels like a quilt, soft, warm, with solid power hidden beneath."

G-556 wanted to speak, but his throat was as if blocked by something. He could only emit a deep whimper, like thunder rumbling from deep within his chest.

"Don't be in such a hurry," the man laughed. "Your body tells me you can endure a great deal. Let me see how much life force is hidden within this massive body."

He began undoing the bear-kind's uniform. That massive build made this process difficult, the uniform stretched tight, buttons almost popping open. When the last piece of fabric was lifted, the sight before him made the man hold his breath—a broad chest covered in dense brown long fur, pectorals thick and full, wrapped in a layer of fat. The abdomen round and firm, the back broad, the thighs thick.

"Magnificent," he murmured. "Such a massive body..."

Unlike the previous prey, this time the man adopted a slower rhythm. He knew bear-kind's life force was tenacious, knew this body could endure more, so he intended to savor this process.

His fingers threaded through that fat, pressing the muscle beneath, feeling that slow and powerful pulse. The bear-kind's reaction was also slow, each wave needing longer to brew, but each one more substantial.

"No rush," the man murmured. "We have plenty of time. Let me slowly... empty you."

He used an hour to bring this bear to his first release. That substantial liquid gushed forth, showing this body's vigorous life force.

But the man was not satisfied. He continued working, his fingers wandering across that massive body, from thick buttocks to thick thighs, from broad back to firm pectorals.

G-556's breathing grew heavier and heavier, that slow, rhythmic breathing gradually becoming rapid and chaotic. His heart was like an overloaded machine, pounding heavily in his chest.

Sixth time... seventh time... eighth time...

Each time more difficult, each time more painful. That massive body began to collapse, breathing becoming intermittent, heartbeat irregular.

Ninth time...

When the final wave struck, it was already a dry spasm, almost no liquid, just the body's last struggle. That chest as heavy as a mountain range was no longer rising and falling; the heart had stopped beating.

The man stood up, looking down at that massive body. The brown bear's eyes were half-open, pupils dilated, no fear in his gaze, only a finally-unburdened exhaustion, like a traveler who had walked too long finally finding a place to rest.

"A mountain has collapsed," he murmured to himself.

## Act 4 The Torn Silk

G-891 awoke with angry fire burning in his eyes.

Even with his body controlled by paralysis, those amber eyes still flickered with unyielding light. He was tiger, yellow tiger race, proud royal kind, unwilling to lower his head even in the direst straits.

"You coward!" he roared, his voice echoing in the basement. "If you have the guts, let me go! Fight me fair and square!"

The man laughed, that smile carrying genuine appreciation.

"Tiger-kind's pride, unwilling to lower its head even in the direst straits. I like it." His fingers traced the tiger's pectorals, feeling that rock-like hardness. "But pride cannot save you. Here, you are just prey."

"I want to watch your pride shatter piece by piece," the man began undoing his uniform. "Watch your eyes, watch your dignity collapse before physiological reaction."

This time, the man adopted the most humiliating approach.

He forced the yellow tiger to watch him. Using his fingers to prop open his eyelids, making him unable to look away, making him watch himself being toyed with, being forced to respond.

"Look at me," the man commanded. "I want you to watch with your own eyes how your body betrays you."

He began to move, slowly and forcefully.

The yellow tiger clenched his jaw, refusing to make any sound. But his body was still responding under that stimulation, that physiological reaction occurring uncontrollably.

He used edging techniques—again and again pushing the yellow tiger to the edge of his limit, then stopping, letting him fall, then pushing him up again. That prolonged prelude turned pain into torture, turned sensitivity into agony.

When the first wave struck, the yellow tiger's body arched, but he still bit his lip, only emitting a suppressed low growl.

Second round, third round... The man gave him no time to rest, forcing him to harden again right after release, to tremble again, to release again.

Fourth round, fifth round... The yellow tiger's tears began to flow, tears of anger and humiliation. His gaze was still proud, but his body had already begun to collapse.

Sixth round, seventh round... His voice was already broken, that suppressed whimper squeezing out from between clenched teeth.

Eighth round...

When the final wave struck, the yellow tiger's eyes were still open, those amber pupils still burning with unyielding flame, even though life had already left him.

The man reached out, gently closing those eyes that refused to close even in death.

Even in death, that tightly locked brow still carried an unyielding stubbornness, as if the soul were still roaring somewhere invisible.

## Act 5 The Empty Shell

G-445 awoke with only confusion in his eyes.

"I... I didn't do anything wrong..." His voice was deep and slow. "I just followed orders..."

"I know," the man's hand had already begun wandering across his body. "You were wronged. A scapegoat. A sacrifice."

"Then why do this to me?"

"Because this is your fate," the man's voice was flat. "Because you are too obedient, too compliant. You don't even know how to resist."

This time, the man adopted the most mechanical approach.

He did not expect the black ox's response, did not expect his struggle or pleading. He just worked mechanically, like operating a machine, like completing a task.

His fingers pressed against the black ox's thick pectorals, feeling that solid muscle beneath that layer of fat. The black ox's reaction was also mechanical—no emotion, no fluctuation, just the body responding instinctively.

"You can't even cry?" The man was somewhat disappointed.

"What's the use of crying?" The black ox's voice was flat. "Dead either way."

First time, second time, third time... The man constantly stimulated him, emptying him again and again.

Fourth time, fifth time, sixth time... The black ox's body responded mechanically, but his gaze was still hollow.

Seventh time, eighth time...

The black ox felt his consciousness beginning to drift away. That dissociative feeling was like his soul being drawn out from his body, floating to the ceiling, coldly looking down at everything happening below.

He watched his own body being toyed with, watched that black-fur-covered body trembling under a stranger's hands, watched that white liquid gushing forth again and again.

But that was no longer "him."

That was just an empty shell, a container, a body being executed.

"He" was already elsewhere.

Ninth time...

When the final wave struck, the black ox's heart finally stopped beating. There was still no expression on his face, only a relieved peace.

The man stood up, meeting those still-open black eyes.

There was no resentment in that gaze, no questioning, only a hollow peace, as if asking "is it finally over," or as if thinking nothing at all.

The black ox's silence would continue forever.
