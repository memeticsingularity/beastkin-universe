## Act 1 The Leopard's Multi-Point

The black leopard awoke to a strange stinging sensation.

Not from one place, but from his whole body—every pore, every inch of skin, screaming sensitivity. This was leopard-kind's nature; their nerve endings were denser than ordinary people's, their whole body a sensitive point.

The man knelt at his side, holding several tools in his hand—a feather, a soft brush, essential oil.

"Do you know?" The man's voice carried a certain pathological excitement. "Leopard-kind's whole body is a sensitive point. This means... I can start from anywhere, simultaneously from multiple places."

"No... don't..." The black leopard's voice already carried a crying tone.

"Yes."

This time, the man adopted a completely different approach. He didn't use his hand to directly stimulate the black leopard's private parts, but began multi-point stimulation across the whole body.

He used the feather to lightly brush behind the black leopard's ear, that being one of leopard-kind's most sensitive areas. At the same time, he used the soft brush to draw circles on the black leopard's chest, brushing past the nipples, bringing a bout of shudders. Another hand wandered on the black leopard's inner thighs, close but not touching the most private parts.

"Feel it?" The man murmured. "Your whole body is burning."

The black leopard's body trembled violently; that multi-point simultaneous stimulation brought not pleasure, but torture. His nerves were overstimulated, every signal screaming, every touch like an electric shock.

The man continued his work. He took the black leopard's nipple into his mouth, lightly licking with his tongue, while his fingers drew circles around the navel, another hand probing toward the base of the thigh.

"Please... too intense... will die..." The black leopard was already bathed in tears.

"It's this intensity I want."

When the first wave struck, the black leopard's body arched, like a fully drawn bow, a heart-rending wail squeezed from his throat. But the man did not stop stimulating other parts, that whole-body sensitivity characteristic making him with nowhere to escape.

Second wave, third wave... The man constantly stimulated the black leopard from different angles, different parts, making him unable to adapt, unable to resist.

Fourth wave, fifth wave... The black leopard's body had completely collapsed, tears flowing down his cheeks, those amber eyes filled with despair.

Sixth wave... When the final wave struck, the black leopard's heart stopped beating in violent spasms.

The man gently stroked the black leopard's still-warm body, that once-sensitive flesh now only silence. When he stood up, the shadows of the basement seemed to grow a few shades darker.

## Act 2 The Boar's Indifference

The wild boar awoke with no fear in his eyes, only a numb calm.

The man looked at him, suddenly losing interest.

"You're the traitor, right?" The man's voice was cold. "You sold out your companions, thinking you could buy your own safety."

"Yes." The wild boar's voice was flat.

"But do you feel regret now?"

"No." The wild boar closed his eyes. "I just had bad luck."

This time, the man adopted an indifferent approach. He didn't invest passion like he did with other prey, but mechanically, emotionlessly carried out his work.

His fingers wandered across the wild boar's body, but without appreciation, without marvel, just completing a task. That indifference was more terrible than torture—being ignored, being treated as a lifeless object.

"You're not worth my spending too much energy on," the man said coldly. "You're just a greedy traitor."

The wild boar's body responded mechanically, but the man had no emotional fluctuation. He didn't prolong foreplay, didn't play with edging, just quickly brought the wild boar to release, then started again.

Again and again, mechanically repeating. The wild boar felt a strange emptiness—not from the body, but from the spirit. He found himself craving attention, craving to be treated as a valuable prey, not abandoned garbage.

"Look at me," the wild boar suddenly said. "At least look at me."

The man stopped his movements, looking into the wild boar's eyes.

"You're begging me to pay attention to you?" The man laughed. "Why? Didn't you not care?"

"I..." The wild boar fell silent.

The man continued his indifference. He used the fastest way to empty the wild boar, again and again, giving no chance to catch his breath.

When the sixth wave struck, the wild boar's heart stopped beating. There was no fear on his face, only a strange emptiness—he finally got the ending he wanted, yet discovered he had never truly lived.

He swept one last glance over the wild boar's hollow pupils, no fear inside, no remorse, only an annoying blankness. When the man turned, even his footsteps carried weariness, as if what he had just handled was merely a lifeless object.

## Act 3 The Deer's Gentleness

The white deer awoke with a gaze as calm as a deep pool.

"You know what I'm going to do?" The man asked.

"I know." The white deer's voice was soft. "You're going to kill me."

"You're not afraid?"

"Afraid. But I hope to die more peacefully."

This time, the man adopted the gentlest approach. He gently undid the white deer's uniform, movements so soft they were like treating a fragile dream.

"You released civilians," he murmured. "You chose mercy."

"Yes."

"Then, I shall give you mercy too."

His fingers wandered across the white deer's body, but not stimulation, rather comfort. He used his warm palm to cover the white deer's chest, feeling that calm heartbeat. He used his lips to lightly kiss the white deer's forehead, as if giving blessing.

"Let me help you," the man murmured. "Let you depart in peace."

He began to move, but the rhythm was the most soothing, like playing a gentle lullaby. The white deer's body slowly relaxed under that gentle touch, that release not forced, but guided, like drifting with the current.

When the first wave struck, the white deer's body trembled slightly, but a smile hung on his face.

"Like wind," he murmured. "Like clouds."

The man continued his gentleness. He used essential oil to coat the white deer's body, using warm palms to massage every inch of skin, letting that release come slow and deep.

Second wave, third wave... Each time carried a certain near-sacred sense of ritual.

Fourth wave, fifth wave... The white deer's gaze grew more and more unfocused, but the smile at the corner of his mouth never disappeared.

When the fifth wave struck, the white deer's heart stopped beating in that gentle embrace. His eyes were still open, but his pupils carried a certain tranquility transcending life and death.

The man gently closed his eyes.

He closed the deer's eyelids, like closing a poetry collection finished reading. That smile remaining at the corner of the mouth gave this dark basement a trace of holiness that shouldn't exist. The man was silent for a moment, then quietly retreated into the shadows, as if unwilling to disturb this rare peace.

## Act 4 The Lion's Dignity

The male lion awoke with the flame of a king burning in his eyes.

"I want you to maintain your dignity," the man said, "until the final moment."

"I will." The male lion's voice was like thunder.

This time, the man adopted the most intense approach. He wanted to use the strongest stimulation, the most extreme edging, to test how long this lion's dignity could persist.

He used all tools—drugs, devices, techniques, all skills brought to the extreme. The male lion's body trembled violently under that intense stimulation, but his gaze was still firm, still burning with unyielding flame.

"The first time." The man counted.

The male lion clenched his jaw, refusing to make any sound.

The man increased stimulation intensity, that pain surpassing pleasure, becoming a pure test.

"The second time."

The male lion's body was in spasms, sweat soaking his golden mane, but his gaze was still proud.

"The third time, fourth time, fifth time..."

Again and again, the man constantly challenged the male lion's limit. He used various ways to stimulate this king's body, trying to break that dignity.

Sixth time, seventh time... The male lion's voice was already broken, that suppressed whimper squeezing out from between clenched teeth, but his gaze was still unyielding.

Eighth time... When the final wave struck, the male lion's eyes were still open, those amber pupils still burning with the dignity of a king.

The man bowed to that body.

The man bowed deeply to that body. Those eyes were still open, amber pupils freezing pride that refused to extinguish even in death. Even in this filthy basement, this body still emitted the afterglow of a king, as if death could not conquer this dignity.

## Act 5 The Fox's Torment

The red fox awoke to find himself placed in the center of the basement.

Surrounding him was a circle of already cold bodies—wolf, bear, tiger, ox, dog, leopard, boar, deer, lion. Nine companions he had once betrayed, now all dead, their eyes mostly open, as if accusing him of his crimes.

"See?" The man's voice rang out beside his ear. "They all died because of you."

"No..." The red fox's voice trembled.

"You betrayed them," the man's fingers slid across the red fox's cheeks. "Now, you shall suffer the cruelest punishment."

This time, the man adopted the most prolonged torture. He wanted to push the red fox to the limit, then stop, let him look at those corpses, let his conscience suffer, then push him up again.

"The first time." The man began.

When the wave was about to strike, the man suddenly stopped his movements.

"Look at them," he forced the red fox to turn his head. "Look at those people you sold out."

The red fox's tears gushed out, that spiritual torment more painful than the physical.

The man waited ten minutes, letting that suffering reach its peak, then began again.

"The second time."

Again stopping when about to reach the peak, letting the red fox look at those corpses once more.

"The third time, fourth time, fifth time..."

Again and again, the man constantly repeated this torture. The red fox collapsed under the dual torment of body and spirit, crying echoing in the basement.

Two hours later, when the sixth wave finally struck, the red fox's heart stopped beating. His eyes were still open, looking at that circle of former companions, that remorse forever frozen upon his face.

The man stood up, looking down at those eyes filled with remorse even in death. In the red fox's pupils were frozen the reflections of nine faces, a debt he would carry to his grave. This spiritual lingchi was far more thorough than physical torture.

## Act 6 The End

Ten bodies lay quietly in the basement.

The wolf died struggling, the bear was exhausted in his heaviness, the tiger's pride was shattered, the ox left in silence, the dog's fragility was ended, the leopard collapsed under multi-point stimulation, the boar was ignored in indifference, the deer rested in gentleness, the lion's dignity endured forever, the fox regretted in torment.

The man stood in the center of the room, looking at his collection.

"Perfection," he murmured. "Ten different ways, ten different endings."

He walked toward the telephone, calling the cleanup personnel.

The garbage truck transported the ten bodies away, to the incinerator. The man stood at the doorway, watching the vehicle disappear into the night.

"Until next time," he turned and disappeared into the darkness.
