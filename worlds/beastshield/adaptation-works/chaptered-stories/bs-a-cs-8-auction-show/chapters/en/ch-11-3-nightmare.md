## Story Commentary and Reflections

> *The following commentary is based on this chapter's story content, attempting to understand the meaning of this dark reversal.*

### A World Without Salvation

This chapter is a story about "shattered hope."

In 11-2, that mysterious buyer raised his paddle at the critical moment, giving readers a false hope—maybe someone would save them, maybe they would have a second chance, maybe there was still kindness in this world.

But this chapter shattered that hope.

The buyer was not a savior, but another demon. He bought them not to save, but to enjoy alone. He used the price of 1 RUB, bought ten lives, then personally ended them.

This is a death crueler than Beastshield's auction house—because they once thought they were saved.

Notably, this chapter's death method is essentially the same as the previous auction house—sexual stimulation leading to "death by semen exhaustion."

But the difference is, this time it was in an enclosed room, with a buyer "enjoying" alone. This privacy made death more shameful, more lonely.

Why did that buyer wait until the last moment to raise his paddle?

Why did he buy everyone at the starting price?

Why did he say to the fox "I want you to watch those people you sold out—they're all alive too"?

These details all have answers in this chapter.

He waited until the last moment to enjoy that critical stimulation.

He bought everyone at the starting price because he only planned to use them once.

He let the fox watch because he knew the fox would die—he just wanted the fox to see hope before death, then personally shatter that hope.

This is the greatest cruelty.

Not death itself, but the process of hope shattering.

**This is our world.**

**Hope is often false, salvation is often a trap, and death is the only end point.**
