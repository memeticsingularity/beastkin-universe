## 2. Story Overview / 故事总览

The fates of the Twelve Divine Weapons' inheritors intertwine, and the final battle between Beastshield Company and the Agent Bureau is imminent. From the conspiracy of the promotion test and the dojo massacre, to the earth-shattering battle on the floating islands of the East Sea, the resurrection of the Blood King and the fall of the Dragon Martial God, the entire beastkin world faces unprecedented change.

十二神武传人的命运交织，兽盾公司与特工局的最终决战一触即发。从晋升测试的阴谋、武馆灭门惨案，到东海浮岛的惊天大战，血君王复活、龙武神陨落，整个兽人世界面临前所未有的变革。

This volume consists of 22 chapters, the final chapter of the main trilogy, tying up all foreshadowing, revealing the truth of the Blood Essence Stones, the secret of humanity, and the ultimate fate of the beastkin world.

本卷共22章，是主线三部曲的最终章，将所有伏笔收束，揭示血精石的真相、人类的秘密，以及兽人世界的终极命运。

### 4.2 Key Truths / 关键真相

- **Human Truth / 人类真相**: What is imprisoned in the Heavenly Prison is not humans, but something even more dangerous / 天牢中关着的并非人类，而是比人类更危险的存在
- **Blood King Origin / 血君王来历**: Leader of Semen-Eater Beasts five hundred years ago, soul sealed in Blood Essence Stones / 五百年前嗜精兽领袖，灵魂封印于血精石中
- **Chairman Ya's Goal / 牙董目的**: Ostensibly searching for humans, actually finding a suitable body for the Blood King / 表面寻找人类，实则为血君王寻找合适的肉体
- **Chairman Hui's Betrayal / 辉董背叛**: Jealous of Dragon God's status, secretly sabotaged leading to Dragon God's fall / 嫉妒龙神地位，暗中破坏导致龙神陨落

### 4.3 Final Organizational Landscape / 组织最终格局

- **Beastshield Company / 兽盾公司**: Suffered heavy losses, but Chairman Ya retreated with the Blood King / 损失惨重，但牙董带着血君王撤退
- **Agent Bureau / 特工局**: Suffered over half casualties, but protected the humans/mysterious beings / 伤亡过半，但保住了人类/神秘存在
- **Light Cult / 光照会**: Chairman Hui was counter-killed by the Blood King, leaderless / 辉董被血君王反杀，群龙无首

## 5. Main Characters / 主要角色

### 5.1 Agent Bureau / 特工局

- **Lao Lei / 老雷**: Avenges Bear Dojo Master, battles A Bao, uses the Sun-Burning City-Breaking Cannon / 为熊馆长复仇，与阿宝决战，使用焚阳破城炮
- **Mr. Qu / 曲先生**: Battles the Blood King with Celestial Sound, showcasing Divine Weapon inheritor prowess / 以天籁对战血君王，展现神武传人实力
- **Yin / 银**: Master of disguise, infiltrates the Light Cult / 易容大师，潜入光照会
- **Shadow Demon / 魔影**: Searches for the Mouse Divine Weapon, rushes to join the floating island battle / 寻找鼠神武，赶往浮岛参战
- **Commander-in-Chief / 总司令**: Coordinates the overall situation, communicates with Dragon God / 统筹全局，与龙神对话

### 5.2 Divine Weapon Inheritors / 神武传人

- **Dunchang / 墩常**: Kui Niu Bow inheritor, searches for his master, joins the floating island battle / 夔牛弓传人，寻找师父，参战浮岛
- **Da Ben / 大奔**: Chairman Lan's guard, lends the Xianxian Bei Shi Armor / 岚董护卫，借出先贤卑豕甲

### 5.3 Beastshield High Command / 兽盾高层

- **Chairman Ya / 牙董**: True goal is to resurrect the Blood King, using Dragon God's body / 真正目的是复活血君王，利用龙神肉体
- **Chairman Hui / 辉董**: Light Cult leader, betrayed Dragon God and was counter-killed by the Blood King / 光照会教主，背叛龙神后被血君王反杀
- **Chairman Lan / 岚董**: Forbidden spell master, casts Yellow Spring Earth Shrink to save humans / 禁咒大师，施展黄泉缩地救走人类
- **Flowing Ruin / 流墟**: Liquid monster, police station invader / 液体怪物，警局入侵者
- **A Bao / 阿宝**: Battles Lao Lei, ultimately defeated / 与老雷决战，最终被击败
- **Minister Lei / 儡部长**: Ambushes Dunchang in the desert, controls undead beastkin / 沙漠伏击墩常，操控不死兽人

### 5.4 Legendary Characters / 传说级角色

- **Dragon Martial God / 龙武神**: World's strongest, creator of the Five Elements Seal, ultimately possessed by the Blood King / 天下第一，五行封禁的创造者，最终被血君王占据肉体
- **Blood King / 血君王**: Progenitor of Semen-Eater Beasts, sealed five hundred years ago, resurrected through Dragon God's body / 嗜精兽始祖，五百年前被封印，借龙神肉体复活
