## 2. Story Overview

Focuses on the past and present of the Agent Bureau's top assassin "Demon Shadow." From infiltrating the Beastshield building to carry out assassination missions, to fighting alongside his partner "Lao Lei," to recalling his first mission and mutation experience on Python Island five years ago. This volume reveals the origin of Demon Shadow's special abilities and the deeper conspiracies behind the Beastshield organization.

This volume consists of 15 chapters, starting from Demon Shadow's infiltration of the Beastshield building, through encounters with Lao Lei, battles in Bear Clan territory, confrontation with the Hell Bull, and finally recalling the first mission and Red Crystal mutation on Python Island five years ago.
