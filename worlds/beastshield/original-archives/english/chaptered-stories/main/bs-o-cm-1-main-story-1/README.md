## 2. Story Overview / 故事总览

A furry enthusiast acquires a mysterious VR game called *Beast World*. In the game, all damage and attacks are converted into "Lust Value." When Lust Value reaches its limit, the character will "ejaculate" to death. Players must utilize this unique mechanism to survive, grow, and gradually uncover the secrets of the Beastshield Company.

一名兽圈爱好者获得了一款神秘的VR游戏《兽游世界》。在游戏中，所有攻击伤害都会转化为性欲值，当性欲值达到上限，角色便会"喷射"死亡。玩家必须利用这一机制，在游戏中生存、成长，并逐步揭开兽盾公司的秘密。

This volume is the first part of the overarching story, consisting of 13 chapters. It follows the player from entering the game, experiencing the tutorial, initially exploring Beastshield headquarters, encountering the Agent Bureau, to finally uncovering the secret of the Blood Essence Stone within their body.

本卷为整个故事的第一部分，共13章，从玩家进入游戏开始，经历了教程关卡、初探兽盾总部、遭遇特工局、最终解开体内血精石秘密的全过程。
