## Synopsis / 故事简介

An amnesiac black dog awakens in darkness, finding himself in a cave with a beastkin corpse beside him. Facing pursuit from a **Beastshield** search team, the mysterious power hidden within him awakens at the moment of life and death. This is a journey of exploration into identity, power, and survival.

失忆的黑狗在一片昏暗中醒来，发现自己身处洞穴，身旁还有一具兽人尸体。面对**兽盾**搜捕队的追杀，他体内潜藏的神秘力量在生死关头觉醒。这是一段关于身份、力量与生存的探索之旅。
