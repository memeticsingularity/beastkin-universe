## 📖 关于本作

《潜伏》是一部由**骸虎**创作的**黑暗科幻军事题材支线长篇小说**，是"基尅兵宇宙"《帝国万岁》世界观下的核心支线作品。

本作聚焦于帝国殖民扩张中的一颗**特殊星球**——该星球地下蕴含的神秘矿物能干扰、甚至直接摧毁帝国士兵脑中的控制芯片。围绕这一核心设定，故事展开了两条交织的叙事线：

- **狼兽人养子线**：一名被退役蓝袜兵"老爹"收养的年轻狼兽人，在老爹的教导下掌握帝国士兵的弱点，通过潜入、暗杀与策反，逐步组建反抗力量。他从最初的猎杀黑袜兵获取装备，到最终捕获并洗脑帝国士兵建立秘密据点，完成从猎物到猎人的蜕变。
- **帝国刺客兵线**：一名直属皇帝陛下的豹兽人刺客兵被派往该星球，监视并清除腐败的白袜指挥官。他的潜入与处决展现了帝国内部权力结构的残酷与高效，同时也揭示了皇帝对这颗星球的特殊重视。

故事穿插着**熊兽人小队长**的崛起与堕落、**黑虎指挥官**作为实验型白袜兵的嗜血 feast、以及**两个觉醒的虎兽人黑袜兵**在体制裂缝中寻找温情的悲剧。作品通过密集的战术潜入、芯片 hack、性弱点处决与父子羁绊，构建了一个在绝对黑暗中孕育反抗火种的残酷世界。

"潜伏"不仅是主角的战术状态，更是整个星球反抗意志的隐喻——在帝国的绝对统治下，唯有最深藏不露的忍耐与最精准的弱点打击，才能撕开一线生机。

## 📖 About This Work

*Lurking* is a **dark sci-fi military side-story novel** created by **骸虎**, set within the "Kiki Soldier Universe" of *Long Live the Empire*.

The story focuses on a **special planet** during the Empire's colonial expansion—its subterranean minerals can interfere with and even directly destroy the control chips implanted in Imperial soldiers. Around this core setting, two intertwined narrative threads unfold:

- **The Wolf Adopted Son Arc**: A young wolf beastman raised by a retired Blue Sock "Father" learns the weaknesses of Imperial soldiers. Through infiltration, assassination, and subversion, he gradually builds a resistance force, transforming from prey to predator.
- **The Imperial Assassin Arc**: A leopard beastman assassin directly under the Emperor is dispatched to the planet to monitor and eliminate corrupt White Sock commanders, revealing the brutal efficiency of internal Imperial power structures.

The work weaves together tactical infiltration, chip hacking, sexual weakness executions, and father-son bonds to construct a world where the faintest ember of resistance can only survive through absolute patience and precision strikes against fatal vulnerabilities.

### 🏛️ 世界观核心命题 | Core Worldview Theses

《潜伏》延续了《帝国万岁》的核心命题，并将其聚焦于**个体反抗的可行性**：

1. **弱点的武器化**：当帝国的绝对控制建立在生理弱点之上时，这些弱点是否也能成为反抗者最锋利的武器？
2. **身份的重写**：通过芯片 hack、伪装与洗脑，个体能否在系统内部重建自由意志？
3. **父子羁绊的悖论**：老爹对养子的教导既是生存之道，也是将帝国暴力逻辑内化的过程——这种传承究竟是救赎还是诅咒？
4. **星球的意志**：当整个自然环境都成为帝国的敌人时，文明扩张的边界在哪里？

---

### 🏛️ Core Worldview Theses

*Lurking* continues the core theses of *Long Live the Empire*, focusing on **the feasibility of individual resistance**:

1. **The Weaponization of Weakness**: When the Empire's absolute control is built upon physiological vulnerabilities, can these same weaknesses become the rebel's sharpest weapons?
2. **Identity Rewriting**: Through chip hacking, disguise, and indoctrination, can individuals reconstruct free will from within the system?
3. **The Paradox of Father-Son Bonds**: The Father's teachings to his adopted son are simultaneously survival skills and the internalization of Imperial violence logic—is this inheritance salvation or curse?
4. **The Will of the Planet**: When the natural environment itself becomes the enemy of the Empire, where lie the boundaries of civilizational expansion?
