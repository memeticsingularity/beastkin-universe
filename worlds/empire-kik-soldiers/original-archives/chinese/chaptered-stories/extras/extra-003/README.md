## 简介 / Introduction

帕图斯（Patuus），野猪兽人少年，帝国军事学院学生。在星舰充能期间意外发现自己可自由操控精液——无论自己的还是他人的。他利用此能力暗杀巡逻黑袜兵，制造袭击事件后成功混入基尅兵内部。然而在一次行动中因大意被黑袜兵小队长识破，遭枪击濒死。最后一刻，他将意识转移至小队长的精液中，完成夺舍。此后他以虎兽人小队长的身份活动，逐步向帝国核心渗透，目标直指皇帝陛下与虎猛少主。

---

## I. Introduction

Patuus, a young boar beastkin and student at the Imperial Military Academy, accidentally discovers during a starship recharge that he can freely manipulate semen—his own or others'. He uses this ability to assassinate patrolling black-sock soldiers and, after staging an attack, infiltrates the Kiki ranks. But during one operation he is discovered by a black-sock captain and shot to the brink of death. At the last moment, he transfers his consciousness into the captain's semen, completing a possession. Thereafter he moves as a tiger beastkin captain, steadily penetrating toward the imperial core, with the Emperor and Heir Humeng in his sights.
