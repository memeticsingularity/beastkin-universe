本卷为《帝国万岁》后日谈短篇合集。各章时间线不完全一致，既有虎猛晋升为帝国少主后在行星级星舰中的巡查日常，也有帝国崛起初期白袜长官尚稀缺时的沼泽清缴往事。所有短篇共享同一世界观与核心设定，可独立阅读。

---

## I. Introduction

This volume is a collection of post-empire short stories for *Long Live the Empire*. Chapters vary in timeline, covering both Humeng's daily inspections aboard the planetary starship after his promotion to imperial heir, and the early empire's swamp campaigns when white-sock commanders were still rare. All stories share the same worldview and core settings, and may be read independently.
