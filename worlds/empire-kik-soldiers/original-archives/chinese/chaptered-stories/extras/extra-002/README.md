## 简介 / Introduction

虎猛已进化为以精液为食的实验型少主。皇帝派遣他前往行星级星舰底部炮台区调查短路事故。在飞船上、炮台区、渔村旧址，虎猛一路处决玩忽职守与知晓过多的各级士兵，甚至发明了新型处决方式。

---

## I. Introduction

Humeng has evolved into an experimental heir who feeds on semen. The Emperor sends him to investigate a short-circuit accident in the planetary starship's bottom turret zone. Aboard the ship, in the turret area, and at the ruins of a fishing village, Humeng executes soldiers at every level who have been derelict or know too much, even inventing a new mode of execution.
