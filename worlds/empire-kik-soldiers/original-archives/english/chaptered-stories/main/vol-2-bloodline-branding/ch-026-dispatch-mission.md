# Chapter 26 Dispatch Mission

Hu Meng quickly stood at attention and saluted when he saw the blue socks officer arriving. Then, under the gaze of the three-stripe green socks soldier, he was taken away. He jogged all the way to the white socks officer's office. Hu Meng didn't know what was happening. Just as he was about to lie down, the white wolf called him to his side and told him His Majesty the Emperor's orders.

After hearing this, Hu Meng was at a loss. His two feet stepped on each other, and his head lowered. He hadn't expected that he would be temporarily promoted to a blue socks soldier! And he was being sent to another base to study. His master also told him that as long as he did well, he could become a blue socks soldier by exception!

Once he became a blue socks soldier, he could stay by his master's side at any time! This was truly wonderful. At the same time, he didn't have to worry about being milk-executed! Because they would be following missions there, increasing combat experience and accumulating merit!

Then the white wolf master's tone changed, telling him that this external dispatch had an even more arduous mission! Find that base's secret! His Majesty the Emperor suspected that the officer of that base was planning to rebel! As long as he found a shred of evidence, that white socks soldier would be finished!

At the same time, he also exhorted his tiger cub to be sure to pay attention to safety. Due to the location of that base, all low-ranking black socks soldiers had already been upgraded to version 2.0. He also told Hu Meng the characteristics of the version 2.0 black socks soldiers.

Hu Meng opened his mouth in surprise, not knowing what to say. It turned out that the new generation of Kik Soldiers had already been successfully cultivated. They were no different from ordinary black socks soldiers! Only after death, they would vaporize or turn into powder within 1-5 minutes. Only their own equipment and chips would remain; semen could be extracted, and if left alone, it would also form ordinary cum stains on the clothes.

As long as someone else put on those clothes and equipment, they would be immediately identified! At the same time, the residue on the clothes would also transform into a deadly poison, killing those infiltrators.

However, Kik Soldiers didn't need to worry, because those substances would not have any reaction upon contact with Kik Soldiers. After hearing the explanation, Hu Meng saluted and said: "Master! Your slave, Tiger Cub, will complete the mission! Let me go!"

The white wolf officer was very satisfied and ordered his subordinate to bring over the prepared items. That canine beastkin personal guard delivered a pair of blue socks. Hu Meng once again fainted under the chip's control. The white wolf officer removed his ankle guards and green socks. He personally kept those green military socks in safekeeping. Then he put those blue military socks on his tiger cub.

He put the ankle guards back on and also put the blue socks soldier's helmet on him. That helmet was also pure black with a matte surface, featuring super night vision and filtration systems. It also had noise and bright light protection functions. The face of the helmet was a very ferocious expression. It had a built-in communication device, so when they spoke during combat, their voices were muffled and had an electronic sound effect.

After waking up, Hu Meng spent several minutes adapting. He told his master his current status in a muffled voice. The white wolf master patted his head and told him to go to the tarmac! At the same time, there was also a secret communication channel. He could report through the Empire's network!

Hu Meng walked to the tarmac just like that. The Kik Soldiers he encountered along the way were all equipped like this. After entering the fighter jet, Hu Meng was forcibly shut down...

After five hours of flight, he finally landed at Base No. 16. This was not at the foot of the snow mountain but in a forest; Base No. 16 was situated between the forest and the city.

After Hu Meng rebooted, he walked down from the tarmac. Following the chip's guidance, he came to the queue of externally dispatched soldiers. He discovered that besides the usual races, there were also some other races of Kik Soldiers here!

Those standing guard and patrolling were boar beastkin and cattle beastkin, while the squad leaders leading the teams were tiger, wolf, and bear beastkin. And the Kik Soldiers at the outer perimeter base were more than half boar beastkin and cattle beastkin! Compared to these, the black socks soldiers were what his master called version 2.0!

Because the highest officer of this base was not present, a blue tiger beastkin was in command. He introduced himself as the officer's trusted confidant; when the master was not present, everything was under his management.

Then he brought them into the base and gave them a tour. Every battle annihilating the resistance organization was recorded, and combat experience was imparted at the same time. Because this was what the white socks tiger officer had instructed! This blue socks tiger beastkin was also one-eyed; according to him, this was a mark left by his master! This was his master's favor!

The base tour and imparting of combat techniques were matters for the next day. When Hu Meng arrived, it was already evening. There were thirty such externally dispatched soldiers in total. They lived in the main building of the inner circle of the base, in a specially designated area.

After the tour, they were also told that external dispatch team members, except for certain areas where they did not have authority, could arbitrarily command lower-ranking soldiers to conduct combat and participate in various missions! If they encountered anyone slacking, they did not need to apply to Base No. 16; they could directly milk-execute them!

Hearing this, the other Kik Soldiers went to carry out their missions. Hu Meng had a special mission, so he still wandered around the base to familiarize himself with the situation. That one-eyed tiger officer also knew that Hu Meng was from the Snow Mountain base.

Before leaving, his master had specially instructed that the externally dispatched Kik Soldiers from the Snow Mountain base were not to be touched! But they must pay attention to his movements at all times, everything done in secret without letting him notice! The one-eyed tiger had been following Hu Meng, appearing very enthusiastic.

Hu Meng also responded enthusiastically. He told the one-eyed tiger officer about his time at the Snow Mountain base, roughly that the climate here was very good. Then he immediately asked about those boar Kik Soldiers. The one-eyed tiger officer explained very patiently. Because the version 2.0 Kik Soldiers had already been deployed in other bases one after another. It's just that the Snow Mountain base was too remote, and the environment was very harsh; these boar and cattle beastkin Kik Soldiers simply couldn't perform!

These were all facts, because Kik Soldiers who had served for a long time all knew! The Snow Mountain base was the Empire's exile area! This was a tacitly understood secret. Whatever new weapons and equipment there were, they were always given to the Snow Mountain base last!

Then Hu Meng asked again: "Sir! This one wants to know how to annihilate those resistance organizations! Can this one ask those black socks soldiers who fought in the battles?"

The one-eyed tiger looked at this rookie Hu Meng and immediately relaxed a lot. During the introduction earlier, their authority had already been explained, yet he still had to ask him. It seemed he really was freshly cultivated; the Snow Mountain base must have been very helpless to send him here! As expected of an exile area. Then he patted Hu Meng's shoulder and told him: "Brother, you're too polite! Within the scope of the Empire's orders, you can handle it yourself! It's all for the Empire!"

Hu Meng quickly saluted and then went to the outer perimeter base. He recorded many weapons and equipment he had never seen here. Then he strolled to the garage at the outer perimeter of the base, found several vehicle models he had never seen before, and recorded them.
