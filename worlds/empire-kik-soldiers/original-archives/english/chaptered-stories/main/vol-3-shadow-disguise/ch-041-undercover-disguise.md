# Chapter 41 Undercover Disguise

Bai Lang still felt this plan wouldn't work! Defensive measures were necessary. The Empire had done this before. It's just that back then, the chips didn't match the body, and the self-destruct mechanism and loyalty mechanism would interfere with each other. Certain sensitive words being triggered would cause interference, making those informants die on the spot!

Hu Meng's idea still left Bai Lang dissatisfied. Fortunately, the current chip parasitism methods were much gentler. Becoming an acquired Kik soldier wouldn't result in such extreme behavior! The self-destruct mode was also changed to simply blowing up the brain.

Taking advantage of the gap while the program was being written, Bai Lang punished Hu Meng remotely. He shut down all his privileges, which also included the death mechanism! He also ordered the miscellaneous soldiers at the watchtower to find him a set of tattered clothes to change into!

This punishment caused Hu Meng to be stripped of all his clothes and changed into tattered clothes. The clothes were also a specially selected costume, torn and damaged on the spot, then taken outside and stained with dirt.

When the miscellaneous soldiers trembled as they jerked off Hu Meng's socks, what they expected to happen based on their knowledge didn't occur. They also put a mining slave's matching explosive collar around Hu Meng's neck. At this point, the miscellaneous soldiers had also beaten him severely for several hours.

With some of the chip's privileges shut down, those black-socked soldiers also forgot their memories of Hu Meng. Now, Hu Meng was curled up on the ground, some of his memories modified. At this moment, he thought he was a miner slave who had escaped from the mine, caught and locked up here. Only in certain states would some of his memories and partial privileges be restored.

If his plan failed, he wouldn't need to come back! He could just fend for himself outside! This was the decision Bai Lang made. Even with His Majesty the Emperor's favor, he couldn't harm the Empire's interests!

For this, Hu Meng was also thrown outside the watchtower and frozen from the middle of the night until dawn! At this time, the fox beastkin had also been modified almost completely. He staggered and struggled up from the floor of the cell, his whole body aching terribly! His tongue comforted the painful places in his mouth!

Those black socks had knocked out his teeth! He swore that as long as he could find a chance to get out, he would definitely kill them all! In the way they feared!

His ears heard sounds coming from the cell. He pressed his head against the door to listen. The sounds of shackles rubbing against the ground and pushing and shoving! Followed by the sound of a heavy object falling...

"Beat him hard for me! A guy who escaped from the Snow Mountain Mine! And he escaped with secrets. The orders from above are that if we can't get any information out of him, we'll be finished too!"

Then, a piercing sound reached his ears. The cell door opened, and a black-socked soldier discovered he was eavesdropping. He raised his foot and kicked him flying into the wall! In that instant, he saw a dark object on the ground. Then the force from his chest sent him into the wall, and he fainted again...

At this time, his chip had already written the command mission into his subconscious. His behavior and thoughts would unconsciously complete the set tasks. At the same time, under the stimulation of a certain code word, the chip would take over and turn him into a killing machine!

He wiped the blood he spat out with his sleeve, grabbed the floor with all his might, his whole body covered in wounds! Those damned black socks! Then he lay on the ground gasping for breath. A whole day and night had passed, and they hadn't even given him a sip of water!

Just as he was thinking about this, the cell door opened, and two black socks threw an object in. The fox beastkin on the ground instinctively got up and rolled into the corner. With a thud, a large guy was thrown onto the spot where he had just been lying.

There was light in the cell. The fox beastkin approached and saw that it was a tiger beastkin covered in blood. He was only wearing a pair of tattered pants, with shackles on his hands and feet and an explosive collar on his neck, the same as his own.

He figured this was another poor beastkin. He slowly crawled over to check if he was still breathing. Looking at his body, he couldn't help but touch this tiger beastkin's chest. His hand felt a wet sensation, and the smell of blood came through. He panicked and quickly wiped the bloodstains onto himself.

At this time, the resistance organization members in the town had also been sleepless all night. They quietly observed the situation at the watchtower. In the middle of the night, they brought a beastkin out, tortured him with fists and gun butts! They also threw him out in the deep of night to freeze, and dragged him back in at dawn.

After that, they slowly approached the watchtower and indeed discovered bloodstains on the ground! Sympathy made them redraw their plans; they would find a way to rescue the leader and that big guy!

One more person meant one more share of strength. In the cell, the tiger beastkin's body moved. The sound of shackles made the fox beastkin look over. Only after a long while did he slowly approach him. He still maintained a safe distance; who knew if he would attack when he woke up!

Then the cell door opened again, and two black-socked soldiers walked in, grabbed the tiger beastkin's head, and dragged him out. The current on his collar activated, and the tiger beastkin woke up screaming. His whole body convulsed as he struggled on the ground...

"Hurry up and tell us what you know! Otherwise, we'll hang you outside until you freeze to death!" one black-socked soldier said, his hands not stopping either! He continued to use a wire-wrapped stick to commit violence against his body.

That tiger beastkin screamed: "I... ah! I don't know anything! I'm just a miner slave who escaped! I want to go home!"

"Still not talking, huh! We have plenty of experience dealing with tough guys like you!" The other black sock kneed him hard in the chin. He knocked him to the ground and said.

He took a dagger from his calf and stabbed it into his palm with one strike. Another scream! The entire cell shook. At this moment, no one paid any attention to him at all. The fox beastkin thought; is this a good chance to escape? It's just that there are still heavy troops guarding outside! His brothers couldn't save him either! They couldn't even get through the main gate!

Following a feeble scream. Another strike stabbed into the sole of his foot. The fox beastkin curled up in the corner, shivering. Watching the blood flow out, those black socks kicked him in the head with their feet stained with blood.

Every kick shook him left and right, saliva flying. At this moment, the alarm went off, and those two black socks hurriedly threw the big guy back into the cell, closed the door, and left in a hurry.

"Help... help me..." the big guy said weakly.

Only then did the fox beastkin walk over, wanting to let him lean against the wall and sit down. But he simply didn't have the strength. He could only tear off his sleeve and try to bandage his palm...

**Chapter 41 END**
