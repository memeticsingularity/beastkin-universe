# Chapter 45 Resistance Plan

In the underground burrow, the resistance organization leader was inspecting weapons, thinking that the big guy still didn't have a suitable weapon. There weren't many weapons either. He should have gone to their armory to loot some. Actually, the tactical vest on that set of clothes would suit him very well. Thinking this, he returned to his home.

Moving stealthily inside the house, he found it had been turned upside down. It was obvious it had been searched. Outside, a group of townspeople were being inspected one by one. A loudspeaker blared some threatening words. After finding that set of gear, he quickly vanished and threw it onto the big guy's sleeping mat.

"Put it on! You'll need it for combat later!"

The big guy opened his bleary eyes, took one look, and put it down. He wrapped himself tighter in the blanket and went back to sleep. The fox beastkin looked at his exposed feet. The bandages on them no longer had fresh bloodstains. So he walked over and shook him awake, asking: "Besides milking-killing those black-socks to death, do you know any other secrets!"

"No, that's all I heard. Can you let me sleep a bit more? I'm so tired..." The big guy said before his snoring resumed.

So he organized his thoughts. A figure emerged from the cave entrance. It was a short red panda beastkin. Upon seeing the leader, he said: "Those black-sock soldiers are at it again in town! This time they aren't killing people. Instead, they've tied up the elderly townspeople in the square! If you aren't handed over within a day, they'll keep them there! What should we do?"

The fox beastkin gripped his weapon and said: "Now that we've grasped their weakness. How confident are you? If your grip isn't strong enough, you won't be able to kill them, and you'll be killed by them instead!"

"Maybe we can make some tools to help us! Since they're so afraid of ejaculation, their fatal weak point has been found. We can make something of this!" the fox beastkin thought.

That little red panda beastkin ran over to the sleeping Hu Meng, looking at this big sleeping cat and flicking his ears. He said: "Could he be an undercover agent?"

"No! I tested him last night. It was so bad I had to take a bath! I have to say, the smell was awful!" The fox beastkin felt depressed just thinking about it.

Then the red panda picked up that oversized tactical vest and took out the contents. Two bottles of medicine and a strange device, that was all.

If Hu Meng had regained his memories, he would have known one bottle was a berserker serum and one was a stamina supplement. That device was an extraction device. These were all standard issue for Kik soldiers. The infinite milking-kill serum was only carried at specific times.

"Get the blacksmith! I've thought of a way! As long as he can make what I need according to my requirements, we'll act tonight!" the fox beastkin said excitedly.

The blacksmith was an ox beastkin. He made all the homemade weapons and trap parts. After hearing the leader's idea, he immediately rushed to the secret hideout and began working on the weapons!

The doctor happened to be there; he needed him to make some stretchers. After hearing the blacksmith's relayed message, he thought of something and hurriedly ran back to his clinic. He completely forgot about the stretchers, only pitying the patients left in his clinic...

The blacksmith worked until the early hours of the morning before stopping. He had only made prototypes, untested in actual combat. Considering how tough those black-socks were, he had deliberately increased the power! Two pairs of electric-shock gloves. As long as they touched the crotch area, a slight curl of the hand would discharge an electric current!

Guaranteed to make those black-sock soldiers so pleasured they couldn't stand up. They just needed to carry batteries on their backs or waists. If they could get more materials and parts, the gloves could be used more times!

There were also a few hairpin-like devices. Once clipped on, the inner protrusions would move rapidly. At the same time, they carried a weak electric current stimulation, guaranteed to make them surrender within 20 seconds!

These two types of tools should be enough. Thinking this, the blacksmith, who hadn't slept for a day and a night, fell asleep on the table. It wasn't until the leader arrived that he woke up. The leader had actually gone to the doctor's first. The doctor had given him two grenades.

The fox beastkin looked at the two grenades. They were lighter than usual, and when shaken, there was a sound of liquid inside. At this moment, the doctor quickly explained: "Don't! Don't! That's liquid made from purified livestock aphrodisiac! Once opened and thrown, it will produce a colorless gas. Even a long-haired beast from the snow mountains couldn't resist going into heat after smelling it! The drug concentration is so potent that just a little bit would make the entire town... you know..."

Hearing this explanation, the fox beastkin nearly dropped and activated these two weapons of mass destruction! The doctor was truly impressive. If they threw one into a vehicle while those guys were inside, the scene would definitely be wild! With this, even storming the watchtower was possible.

It's just that the watchtower didn't only have black-sock soldiers; it also had active defense systems! If those weren't disabled, it was still a terrifying weapon capable of wiping out the town! Capturing their commander alive might be feasible. Thinking this, that big black guy could be of use again. It would just require some grievance on his part, and they needed to mark him!

So they wouldn't accidentally harm him too. Although he wouldn't die, he would wish he were dead! If he went into heat then, it would be terrible! So he gathered all the members to discuss. They would rescue the townspeople after making their move!

It's just that those townspeople probably couldn't withstand it either, so he asked the doctor if there was an antidote. The doctor scratched his head and said: "No, not yet!"

"Hurry up and think of something! Otherwise, those townspeople will go into heat too! Those are our elders. If our elders are beaten to death, just say it outright! Or do you still want to get married in the future!"

Hearing the leader say this, the doctor finally understood! The father of the girl he liked had also been captured! If he let his future father-in-law end up like that, what hope would he have of getting married!

So he began flipping through books looking for an antidote formula. Without it, those two grenades could only be kept in reserve.

Those members discussed the rescue plan. The black tiger big guy listened, half-understanding. He only understood that he was to continue disguising himself as a black-sock soldier, run to the watchtower entrance to ask for help, or directly blend in with the black-sock soldiers in the square.

Quietly untie the townspeople's ropes. Once the attack started, the townspeople wouldn't need to be managed. They could also take the opportunity to take out a few! To avoid accidentally injuring the big black guy, they required him to leave a mark after disguising himself!

Thinking of a mark, the fox beastkin leader brought over paint and painted the tip of his tail white. This made him very easy to identify. The big black guy nodded and continued listening to their plan.

This plan was simultaneously transmitted to Commander White Wolf's computer. He listened to the plan and sneered. Their small stature indeed made them difficult to deal with, and they had targeted weapons and an inside man! The black-sock trash on duty were indeed going to be done for!

It's just that storming the watchtower was something they couldn't do! Even if Hu Meng was well-disguised, without a chip, relying solely on military sock permissions wouldn't work! It was good that the resistance organization wasn't mobilizing in full force. Now was not a good time to wipe them out!

**Chapter 45 END**
