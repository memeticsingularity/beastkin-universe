# Chapter 56 New Battlefield

"Master? A new battlefield?" Hu Meng reluctantly licked his tongue. Looking at his master's white-socked foot claws, he asked.

"Yes! A new battlefield. This planet no longer has any value! By then, all bases will enter sleep mode! Including all the Kik soldiers inside. They will only be reawakened when His Majesty needs them!" Commander White Wolf said excitedly.

Grabbing Hu Meng's mask, he signaled him to follow. By then, all bases would rise from the ground. They would find a place to enter hibernation mode, or become bombs to reduce the entire continent to zero!

Hu Meng followed his master all the way to the top of the main building. Watching the platform on the top descend and then rise back up with a spaceship. White Wolf kicked Hu Meng's butt, signaling him to hurry up and climb in! As the spaceship took off vertically, it instantly pierced through the clouds and shot into the cosmos.

Hu Meng saw things he hadn't learned about in the cultivation pod. A massive warship floated in the boundless black background. The Snow Mountain Base he knew was as insignificant as an insect compared to this!

The spaceship entered its docking position and was guided to the internal port. Hu Meng followed his master off the spaceship. He saw patrolling Kik soldiers and various things he had never seen before. He didn't dare look too much and just followed his master. Passing through the area where the spaceships were docked, they took an elevator to another area.

Here, there were soldiers standing guard at intervals, and patrol soldiers came in batches one after another. They would stop and salute when they saw his master. As they walked, he saw a white-sock commander come out of a room on either side of the corridor.

This white-sock commander was a tiger beastkin. He said something to his master. His master jogged to speed up, and Hu Meng also kept pace with his master. All the way to the end of the corridor, the white-sock bear at the door had been waiting for a long time.

He hurriedly stepped forward to stop White Wolf and said: "His Majesty is already waiting." Then he looked at Hu Meng behind White Wolf and gave him a meaningful glance.

Hearing that His Majesty was inside, Hu Meng tried hard to calm himself down. His hands grabbed his pants, at a loss. That white-sock bear commander patted Hu Meng's shoulder and said: "His Majesty wants you to go in too. I still have a mission, so I'll be going first."

The door slowly opened. Commander White Wolf and Hu Meng slowly walked in. The throne slowly turned. His Majesty sat there, full of majesty. Commander White Wolf quickly stepped forward and knelt down. His Majesty's upper body was bare, revealing his robust muscles. His golden fur shone brilliantly, and his majestic tiger face was awe-inspiring without even being angry!

A golden metal ring hung behind him. The armor and metal skirt armor at his waist were magnificent. The belt was very wide, and the metal buckle in the middle was engraved with the Empire's emblem.

The armor on his wrists was also of the same material. His bare feet were a symbol of his status. He slowly walked to White Wolf's side and stroked his wolf head. White Wolf let out a doting sound, his tail wagging like a propeller.

Hu Meng also knelt down behind his master. He looked at the smooth, mirror-like floor. It reflected his terrified eyes. His mask seemed a bit crooked. If His Majesty saw him like this, he would be done for!

"Hu Meng? Come here!" His Majesty spoke in a kind voice. That voice was like magic. After Hu Meng heard it, he got up and came to His Majesty's side.

His Majesty stretched out his left foot and stepped on his head, rubbing it on his head a few times. Then he said: "Beast Corps Captain! It seems you have seen the Empire's heavy weapons! A battlefield is about to be opened! As long as you perform well! There will be even more rewards!"

Hu Meng answered while bearing the weight on his head: "Yes! Your Majesty! This humble one will definitely kill those enemies for Your Majesty!"

"White Wolf, your subordinate is very good! Tonight, let your subordinate witness the true power of the Empire! You stay and accompany me!" His Majesty said, throwing a metal plate to Hu Meng from the throne.

Hu Meng held the metal plate and went out. He didn't dare disturb His Majesty. After coming out, the two blue-sock soldiers standing guard at the door of His Majesty's office saw the plate in Hu Meng's hand and their eyes went wide!

This plate was a key used to upgrade Kik soldiers. Only soldiers promoted to white-sock could obtain it. Hu Meng held this plate and walked for a long time in the starship according to the directions before arriving at the indicated location.

The door lock sensed the key and opened automatically. Hu Meng saw more than thirty special cultivation pods. Seeing one cultivation pod flashing, he walked over. As soon as he approached, the plate in his hand vibrated and transformed into a key.

Inserting the key into the slot, the cultivation pod opened. Hu Meng was completely taken over by the chip. He took off his clothes in a panic beside it and lay down. More than ten tubes inserted into Hu Meng's body.

They began injecting drugs for enhancement. At the same time, the chip was also being modified and upgraded. This was a white-sock soldier-exclusive cultivation pod. The chip in Hu Meng's brain began writing information about the next battlefield. The structure and functions of his own starship, as well as the permissions to command these Kik soldiers.

In His Majesty's bedroom, White Wolf had been completely drained by His Majesty. Now he was being used as his cushion.
