# Chapter 42 Cell Breakout

The fox beastkin's eyes were wide, staring hollowly at the door. The black tiger beastkin had told him everything he knew, and only then did the fox realize that the despicable Kik soldiers' weakness truly was their crotch! He also learned what "milking-kill" (撸杀) was. By now, the wounds on the big guy's body had stopped bleeding.

Hearing these things shocked him, but he had only managed to eavesdrop on this much before being discovered. He had fled for his life without any food along the way. By the time he reached the edge of the town, he was exhausted and fainted. Those black-socked soldiers had injected him with nutrient solution to extract what he knew, which was the only reason he hadn't died!

This was what he himself said—all his memories from before becoming an undercover agent had been sealed. He mentioned the name of a village, and the fox beastkin believed him. Because that village had truly existed, vaporized by an Imperial warship's aerial strike. The villagers who had been captured alive were all turned into miners and consumed...

The fox beastkin began thinking of a way to escape. Even if the two of them could take out the guards, they still couldn't get past the system. Not to mention, under the influence of drugs, he had divulged a lot of intelligence!

Thinking of this, he couldn't help but worry about his brothers! He didn't know what he had said. Perhaps that alarm just now was them assembling to go arrest his comrades! At this thought, he grabbed his head and sank into deep self-blame!

The entire Watchtower No. 2 entered automatic mode, leaving only four black-sock soldiers to hold the fort. One team was in the control room, one in the cell. After who knew how long, they heard snoring from outside.

The fox beastkin used the big guy as support to look out the window in the cell door. He only saw the two black-socks sleeping, and if he listened carefully, he could even hear their snores!

Meanwhile, in the underground facility of the Snow Mountain Base, the White Wolf commander was observing the latest experiment with the meat blob. Two black-sock soldiers were brought into the meat blob's containment room. After the door closed, they observed the meat blob's condition.

The meat blob, originally curled up in a corner, began to move. Its entire body wriggled as it approached the two black-sock soldiers. They immediately opened fire. Blue beams struck the meat blob, emitting a charred smell...

Then its speed increased as it slammed into one black-sock soldier, knocking him over before making an unscientific aerial U-turn. The entire meat blob flattened into a meat patty, sticking directly to the other black-sock soldier's foot. It quickly wrapped around his head!

The black-sock soldier struggled, grabbing the meat blob with both hands, trying to pull it off his head! He could no longer breathe, and no matter what he did, he couldn't get the meat blob off his head. At this point, the meat blob was still tightening. After 30 seconds, the black-sock soldier had run out of oxygen and lay on the ground struggling. His flailing legs drove his body through its final throes.

At 49 seconds, the black-sock soldier stopped moving, though his legs still twitched. The meat blob extended a tentacle and drilled into his bulging groin guard. Accompanied by rhythmic undulations, it voraciously devoured his semen before the black-sock soldier fully expired.

During this time, his companion tried to rescue him. Whether using weapons fire or a dagger to stab, all attempts ended in failure. After his companion was milked-killed, he ran to the door and knocked frantically, shouting. Commander White Wolf listened to the researcher's explanations and records, feeling very puzzled.

His Majesty the Emperor had sent this thing and only told them to conduct experiments. He hadn't said what exactly this thing was, only knowing that it had grown a bit since last time.

Watching the meat blob retract its tentacle and jump onto the remaining black-sock soldier's head. Using the same method, it devoured his semen clean before returning to a dormant state. Data from the detectors indicated it had entered hibernation...

After dispatching a team of soldiers to clear out the corpses, they also conducted research. Meanwhile, those Kik soldiers who had learned the truth would also be sent in as subsequent food for the meat blob!

Commander White Wolf felt that this meat blob obtained nutrients from the semen it devoured to grow. Then it would mature and perhaps become some kind of creature. After being stationary for 46 minutes, the temperature at the center of the meat blob rose.

The scanner detected tissue resembling an embryo. Just as they were about to zoom in, the meat blob secreted a substance that cut off the scanning device's signal. They could only observe its state through the camera...

The researcher said: "Commander, perhaps it needs more food?"

White Wolf: "Perhaps your idea is correct. Order that team of black-sock soldiers to stand by in the containment room! Use puppet mode to send them in!"

After saying this, Commander White Wolf left. Two blue-sock soldiers emerged from the elevator and followed behind him as he began inspecting the inner circle of the base.

"Is there any way, little guy?"

"No, not yet. Even if we could get out of this cell, how would we get outside? With your build, you can't crawl through the ventilation ducts! The important thing is, with your injuries, walking is already a problem."

"Then why don't you just leave me behind and escape! Spread the news about their weakness and end the Empire's rule; that would be revenge enough for me!"

"That won't do! We have to act together for the best chance of escape! Come here, I have a plan..."

The fox beastkin whispered the plan into the big guy's ear. The big black tiger blinked and asked if this would really work? The fox beastkin swished his big tail, indicating no problem. Because they had no idea how many secrets he knew. He could say he knew everything, and their attention would definitely be on him!

As long as he acted very scared and continued to draw their attention. After he made his move, the big guy would follow suit, and half their plan would be complete! Then they would interrogate them on how to get out, and they could escape!

It sounded easy, but he had to act as bait. However, if they didn't execute this plan, their only fate was death! At this thought, he decided to give it a shot—a desperate gamble could turn a bicycle into a motorcycle!

So they waited for a shift change or bathroom break. During this time, the big black tiger even fell asleep. The fox beastkin had to cover his mouth and press on his wounds to wake him up. Looking at this big black tiger, the fox beastkin calculated that their chances of success had dropped even further...

There was movement outside the door. One black-sock soldier got up to go to the bathroom. The fox beastkin patted the big guy's head, signaling him to act.

"I... I've figured it out! If I tell you everything, will you let me go!" the black tiger shouted, banging on the door.

The black-sock soldier at the door was sleeping soundly and was woken from a good dream. Angrily, he slammed the gate! He stood up impatiently and yelled: "Hmph! You only figured it out now! I'm opening the door! If you're lying, I'll blow your brains out immediately!"

Then the door lock opened. The black-sock soldier was a wolf beastkin, his tail wagging irritably!

**Chapter 42 END**
