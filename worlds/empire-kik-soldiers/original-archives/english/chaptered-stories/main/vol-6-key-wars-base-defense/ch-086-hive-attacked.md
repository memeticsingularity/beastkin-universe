# Chapter 86 Hive Attacked

Five days after the hero's successful operation, Hu Meng led his subordinates to the location of the ruins. On the surface, there was only a lonely fortress, capable of accommodating a squad's garrison. The entire black fortress was circular, 8 meters high with a diameter of 16 meters, seamless. Entry and exit were only possible via the elevator from directly above.

The underground facility was the real base. In addition to the necessary facilities, it was all integrated into one. It was in the shape of a giant hive. It was excavated through an exclusive tunnel. While constructing, the excavated soil and rubble were transported to the hive. They were transformed into building materials for the hive...

Hu Meng came to the Hive Base via the elevator. Every time he descended a certain distance, a gate would close. Weapons would also extend from the tunnel walls, aiming upward.

After arriving at the hive, Hu Meng took his dinosaur guard straight to the main control room. A Green Socks tiger beastman was already standing here. Looking at this squad leader, Hu Meng didn't say any superfluous words. He directly called up the excavation progress and related plans on the screen.

That Green Socks squad leader was about to report something when he was stopped by the dinosaur guard. The officer would check the system himself and didn't need a subordinate to point fingers!

After a long time, he sat in the commander's seat, put his feet on the table, and started shaking them. The Green Socks squad leader quickly ran to Hu Meng's side and massaged the officer's feet. At the same time, three Black Socks soldier squad leaders and one company commander also ran over. They stood in a row before Hu Meng...

"I've looked at your plans. This base was built to analyze the items in the ruins. This is a matter of key focus for His Majesty. You also know the news from the other bases! Our base only has one entrance! We must tighten our defenses even more!" After Hu Meng finished speaking, those squad leaders answered in unison: "For the Empire! Long live the Empire!"

Then the dinosaur guard had those squad leaders stay right here. Commander Hu Meng was going to conduct a surprise inspection! Right after the guard finished speaking, Hu Meng disappeared. He went all the way to the surveillance room and discovered that the soldiers here were not slacking off. They were staring at many screens at all times, especially that only entrance and exit.

Then he came to the next level down. The further down this base, the more important it was! The main control room was on the second-to-last level. Hu Meng first went up one level and then immediately changed his route. He came to the very bottom level. There were only 30 Kik soldiers here. That was still too few.

He communicated with the guard through his chip, having him apply to the main base for two more companies to come over! After all, there were still many empty posts! After contacting the ruins, a large number of personnel would also be needed for exploration!

The situation on the bottom level was different. Everything revolved around a huge cylindrical device. This huge cylinder was rotating. Hu Meng knew this was a drill, constantly digging downward. At the same time, it transported the soil upward. A squad of Black Socks soldiers was guarding here.

When they saw the blue socks on Hu Meng's feet, they quickly stood at attention. Hu Meng walked up and looked at them. He smelled them with his nose. A strong male odor and sweat odor. At this time, a Black Socks soldier ran over, slowing his pace as he approached Hu Meng...

Hu Meng turned around and asked: "Why aren't you in the formation!"

That Black Socks soldier answered: "Reporting, sir! I just went to the restroom."

Hu Meng didn't ask any further. It was quite a while before he returned to the main control room. Those squad leaders were only then dismissed and returned to their posts. He didn't know that this was His Majesty the Emperor protecting him.

The hero had already told the king about his experiences. The king was very excited. He finally knew those guys' weak points. The king wanted to train an elite force to follow the hero and slowly reclaim the kingdom's territory. This proposal was vetoed by the hero, because only he alone could do it!

Then the hero disappeared, leaving behind a king full of hope that had been ravaged into pieces by that hope. He could only place all his hope on the hero. Because the Empire had sent a message ordering them to surrender within ten days!

The hero returned to his own base, first took a bath to clear his head. He formulated a plan. The place he was going to was the last city the Empire had captured. It was the closest to the capital! And the military base in that city was also the weakest one.

So the hero chose this place. He put on special gloves. He wore a tight-fitting one-piece short-sleeve and shorts garment. Several slender cylinders hung on the belt at his waist. On his feet were boots of the same style. But the texture was more like those Kik soldiers' socks!

There were also some mechanisms on the gloves, equipped with special bullets developed these past few days! After being hit, the bullet would dissolve. After the drug was absorbed, it would rapidly cause ejaculation and death! This was his special improvement based on the demon king's subordinates from before.

Those gloves were the same, enhanced to increase jerking-off speed. He could make them surrender with just a touch of a Kik soldier's crotch! As for the items at his waist, those were for when there was no other choice...

Then he opened the teleportation device in the base. Because hundreds of thousands of years had passed. Although there had been regular maintenance during this period, and although it had withstood the test of time, the remaining energy was not much.

At this time, the alarm system in the base flashed. An object was approaching from directly above. This was not good news. Helpless, he could only delay the plan. The entire facility should be buried deep underground. The only ones who could detect and reach here were the demon king's subordinates!

The hero, having made up his mind, protected the most important parts of the base. He went to face the enemy alone! Anyway, he now knew a quick way to replenish energy.

He walked to the launch shaft part of the base and entered a torpedo-shaped vehicle. In the preparation room earlier, he had already locked onto the position of the huge object! The hero deduced that this should be a drill. After reaching the destination, they would be able to come here through the pipe!

So his hiding place had to be changed! At the very least, the demon king would have some scruples about him. At the same time, the equipment and supplies in the base could also be preserved! He looked at the screen inside the torpedo-shaped vehicle and avoided the drill from a very far distance.

At the same time, he concentrated the energy in the vehicle at the front and the propulsion system. Even the hardness of the earth's crust couldn't stop him. The Black Socks soldiers on the very bottom level of the Hive had no idea that their lives were about to come to an end.

The floor behind them began to shake, gradually producing cracks. Then a torpedo-shaped vehicle burst out in a line. The alarm in the base sounded. The stationed Black Socks soldiers quickly turned around. They saw the torpedo-shaped device was already in mid-air...
