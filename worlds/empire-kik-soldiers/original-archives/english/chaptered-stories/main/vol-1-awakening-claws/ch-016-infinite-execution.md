# Chapter 16 Infinite Execution

    In the main base's room, that chameleon-like synthetic beastkin was crawling on the ceiling. His neck could rotate 720 degrees; he didn't have to worry about his neck being twisted off at all!

    The wolf beastkin officer lay sideways on the sofa, toying with his own tail, listening to that personal guard's report.
    "Sir! After investigation, those directionally cultivated Kik Soldiers have not yet been completely rooted out. Your black socks slave is completely fine; I have made a record. When the time comes, they will be gathered together uniformly! Does Sir have any instructions?"
    "At the same time, the missing Armored Vehicle No. 5 from 26 days ago can be confirmed to have been attacked! We discovered traces of explosions in the surrounding area and determined it was the resistance organization's operation. The black socks corpses at the scene were relatively well preserved. According to the chip's final records, the milking execution mechanism may have been leaked!"
    Hearing this, the wolf beastkin officer looked up at the ceiling; that personal guard also looked at himself. Being looked at by the officer like this made him somewhat uneasy, especially knowing that this officer was very special!
    "That's easy to handle! Demote those blue socks soldiers. Have my slave lead the team as bait and take them to find the resistance organization. I will temporarily turn off the milking execution mechanism in Hu Meng's chip. Let that trash celebrate for nothing. At the appropriate moment, have those blue socks soldiers go to the rescue. This will dispel their suspicions and also allow my little slave to help me get rid of those blue socks soldiers! There won't be any suspicion..." the white wolf beastkin said leisurely.
    His meaning was just this: after dealing with the resistance organization, have his tiger cub milk-execute them. In the report, it would be written that all were killed in action, the cause being that their military socks got caught and detached from their footpads, triggering the mechanism and resulting in death! Finally, clean up the battlefield, and that would be it!
    With this merit, the tiger cub could get promoted — truly killing three birds with one stone! After hearing this, that personal guard's eyes kept blinking; this was indeed a method. Some time ago, dealing with a batch of trash had caused the base's red alert to persist for a long time!
    The wolf beastkin officer gave some more details, after which that personal guard became invisible again to carry out the mission. At this moment, at Watchtower No. 6, all the Kik Soldiers were lying on the ground. They were all in a coma; only that tiger beastkin squad leader was still conscious. He was pinned against the wall by the tall crocodile beastkin, his tail wagging back and forth, very impatient.
    His subordinates had already gone to the control room to modify the surveillance and operation records.
    "Listen, who we are is not important! What is important is that this place will be our temporary camp from now on. You and your subordinates must enter hibernation, understand!" the crocodile beastkin said, the tip of his tail extending a thorn pressing against that tiger beastkin's forehead.
    "U-understood... Sir."
    That crocodile beastkin's tail retracted the thorn and slapped him across the face. That black socks squad leader passed out dead; he then ordered his own subordinates to transfer them all into hibernation pods. If they didn't fit, put two in one pod...
    Ever since becoming the white wolf officer's slave, Hu Meng had taken extra care of his socks. He knew these socks could even withstand bullets, but he still cherished them, washing them several times every day. On this day, that bear beastkin officer called him over. Ten minutes later, that bear beastkin officer stood up. Slowly, he unfastened his belt and reached both hands into his own crotch, milking himself...
    About half a minute later, his body trembled and he fell to the ground, dead. That familiar smell made Hu Meng freeze. This... this was suicide by self-milking! Once an officer issued this kind of order, it could not be retracted; the Kik Soldier who received this kind of order could only execute it.
    "Long... live... the Empire!" After saying this, that bear beastkin officer completely kicked the bucket.
    Hu Meng called over his subordinates and had them process the officer. Then he ran to the designated mission location. Walking into that dormitory, he saw two squads of blue socks soldiers standing there. The mission brief said these were his subordinates, and the day after tomorrow, he would lead the team in the resistance organization elimination mission.
    And he, the lowest-ranking black socks soldier, was actually going to command blue socks level officers! Besides their usual equipment, they also had a crotch guard. This made Hu Meng very envious...
    At the same time, the resistance organization's second-in-command had also formulated a plan. These past few days, whenever they encountered a lone Kik Soldier while staking out, they would capture him to see if they could bring him back as a prisoner to test the milking execution. After all, they all held doubts about this idea. They thought that at the time, the leader must have remembered wrong or used too much force and directly pain-killed that black socks soldier or something.
    Only after the leader repeatedly advocated for it did they decide to give it a try. This operation included the leader, totaling six people. And the Empire side dispatched two 10-man squads. The armored vehicle was all bait, including Hu Meng, while invisible fighter jets in the sky were responsible for the raid. Hu Meng was to give the resistance organization hints and explicit signals, inducing them to milk-execute him. After discovering it was ineffective, the second squad would launch an attack and let one resistance organization member escape. This was the entire plan; the remaining mission was still confidential. When the time came, Hu Meng would know what to do! The white wolf beastkin officer had also prepared a backup plan to bring his tiger cub back safely!
    "Squad leader! The vehicle body is severely damaged! We are ambushed!"
    At this moment, white smoke billowed inside the vehicle; the entire armored vehicle was overturned on the road. Hu Meng was stuck in the vehicle due to the sensor. The soldier in the passenger seat opened the door and escaped first, pulling Hu Meng out before being headshot by a hidden sniper. The rear door also opened, and the remaining Kik Soldiers quickly took their positions and opened fire toward the sniper's location.
    These blue socks soldiers had already had their abilities restricted for the purpose of confusing the enemy. The resistance organization's goal was very clear: find the squad leader and capture him; kill all the rest!
    The sniper naturally wouldn't stay in one location for very long. Relying on his mastery of the area, he quickly moved to the next location. Two bangs, and two more Kik Soldiers were executed. The other team members flanked and threw their homemade grenades. Two thuds, and several more blue socks soldiers were blown over.
    The Kik Soldiers hiding in the vehicle, seeing this, also jumped out of the vehicle under the chip's orders. A blue socks soldier stepped on his companion's corpse, blood splattering out, but he didn't care at all. His socks had already been dyed a strange color. Now only three Kik Soldiers remained, plus one unconscious Hu Meng...
    At this moment, all the resistance organization members surrounded and took care of the remaining two blue socks soldiers as well. They looked at the corpses in confusion because they had never seen soldiers with other colored socks. Now it seemed these dark blue socks Kik Soldiers were inferior to the black socks soldiers. Therefore, they judged that these were freshly trained rookies!
