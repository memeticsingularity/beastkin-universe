# Chapter 18 Promotion to Green Socks

As soon as Hu Meng landed, he was ordered to lie in a capsule pod. That pilot also committed suicide by self-milking after setting the program; shortly after, specialized black socks soldiers would come to clean up. The capsule pod was transferred through a special passage to the white socks officer's room.

The pod door opened, and Hu Meng saw his master. He quickly got up and crawled down, awaiting his master's orders. The white socks officer was very satisfied, still using his foot to step on his face to show affection. He also ordered him to take off his dirty clothes; a new frog-suit set was already beside him.

Then he led Hu Meng to the bathroom; the white socks wolf beastkin wanted to give his tiger cub a good wash! Especially to remove the smell of semen! After the mission was completed, his chip was activated; expelling a large amount of semen in a short period of time would shorten a Kik Soldier's lifespan!

After washing Hu Meng clean, Hu Meng fell asleep. Looking at the scar on his face, he felt a pang of heartache. After all, this was his slave; apart from himself and His Majesty the Emperor, no one could treat him like this!

Then he stroked Hu Meng's head and leaned against his body, falling asleep. The personal guard lurking on the living room ceiling disdained to record such matters. Most of the Empire's high command had a few slaves like this; it was a very common thing. At the same time, he also developed an interest in this black socks soldier.

However, this interest only lasted a few seconds. Seeing that the white wolf beastkin was temporarily not in danger, he also slipped out to continue rooting out those directionally cultivated Kik Soldiers. His mission was to protect this esteemed one's safety...

It was not until evening that Hu Meng woke up. Feeling his master leaning against his body, he continued maintaining his prone posture. Ten minutes later, the white socks officer also woke up. He patted Hu Meng's head and led him to the living room.

Sitting on the sofa, he threw that new military uniform to Hu Meng; Hu Meng quickly put it on. After tidying up the military uniform, he was about to lie down on the ground when he was stopped. He saw the white socks officer take out a pair of green military socks from a box and shake them before his eyes.

Taking advantage of Hu Meng's inattention, he activated his chip. Dizziness came over him, and he passed out. The white socks officer pulled off Hu Meng's black socks and put those brand-new green socks onto those tiger feet. Only then did he discover that Hu Meng, a new recruit of just over a month, actually had gray-black paw pads!

Generally, after a Kik Soldier matured from cultivation, the paw pads on his foot claws would only change color after three months! He sighed, adjusted the sock cuffs for Hu Meng, tucked the pant legs into the socks, and then the other foot! After putting them on, he couldn't stop admiring Hu Meng's black military uniform equipment and deep green socks.

After a Kik Soldier was promoted to green socks, his protective gear would include a crotch guard and ankle guards. This was to better secure the military socks on his feet. This could guarantee the Kik Soldiers' survival rate; even if the enemy really hit a vital spot, he could be guaranteed to keep his life and continue serving the Empire!

Blue socks soldiers would additionally receive a helmet and mask. They were only equipped during combat; as for their kind of white socks soldiers! During combat, they would put on a black tight one-piece suit and wrap themselves in armor! It didn't affect mobility, and the skeletal module's defense was astonishing; both live ammunition and energy weapons could be greatly mitigated. Even being caught in a mine blast at zero distance would only result in minor injuries!

However, there were very few occasions requiring white socks soldiers to fight; their combat attire was stored inside their bodies. It could be activated at any time; at the same time, the armor also had some exoskeleton armor functions. In terms of combat power alone, they could tear apart an Empire's heavy mech with their bare hands!

Therefore, white socks soldiers held a very high status in the Empire and were also advanced tools carefully cultivated by His Majesty the Emperor. Before becoming a white socks soldier, the steps were all normal. After confirming promotion to white socks soldier, they would undergo secondary cultivation. Only then could they become the highest-level white socks soldier!

At the same time, white socks soldiers all wore two layers of socks! The outer military socks looked no different from ordinary military socks. Their defense was even stronger; beneath this layer of socks, there was another very thin layer of white socks. This inner layer of socks was the true vital point of a white socks soldier; even if the outer socks were pulled off during combat or a sneak attack, he could still move and fight!

If this inner layer of socks was pulled off, then it would be over. Once this inner sock detached from the foot claw, it was not just death by ejaculation! To guard the Empire's secrets, the white socks soldier would directly vaporize. That implanted equipment would become a bomb, finally leaving only the lower body ordinary military uniform.

Therefore, white socks soldiers were very rare and held a very high status in the Empire. These secrets made him somewhat lose his composure. Looking at Hu Meng lying there sprawled out, he became happy again! He picked up the crotch guard, put it on his tiger cub, and woke him up!

Hu Meng sat up; a large amount of information decompressed and then pushed his brain to digest this information. He held his head, hurting for a good while, before adapting to his new identity. At this moment, the white wolf master threw him a pair of specially made ankle guards for him to put on himself.

Hu Meng saw the deep green long military socks on his feet and only then reacted. In a few swift moves, he put on the ankle guards, then lay there licking his master's socks in gratitude. After all this was done, Hu Meng had a new position; he was assigned to the team patrolling the outer perimeter of the main base.

His original subordinates would naturally be managed by a new squad leader. Now, Hu Meng was already a green socks soldier! Even black socks soldier officers had to speak to Hu Meng in a low voice.

Hu Meng came to the new place to report for duty. The squad leader was a snow leopard beastkin. His green socks were stepping on a subordinate's head; his massive tail was very eye-catching.

After standing at attention and saluting, that squad leader told him to go rest on his bunk. He would have two patrol shifts at night. Hu Meng saluted again and ran to his own dormitory. It was still a barracks. He found a spot, lay down, listened to the snoring of the companions beside him, and also fell asleep. When Kik Soldiers slept, they only covered themselves with a blanket, with both feet exposed outside.

This made it convenient for the squad leader to wake them up. Green socks soldiers were responsible for standing guard and patrolling the outer perimeter of the main buildings in the inner circle of the base. Without special orders, they were not allowed to enter the main building; occasionally, they would also go to the outer perimeter base to transmit orders and so on.

So Hu Meng's work became much easier. They slept in a daze, waiting for the arrival of night.
