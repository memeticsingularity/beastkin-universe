> *Shadows prowl beneath the Empire's walls.*  
> *Hatred and despair are sometimes sharper weapons than the **chip**.*

The nearby towns were filled with civilians who had long been oppressed. They had obtained some weapons and equipment. Relying on their familiarity with the area, they waged struggle against various nations! The resistance members were all youths who had learned and grown much through prolonged combat. During the day, they would also enter the **base** to labor...

The leader of the **resistance organization** was a fox beastkin, his fiery red fur wrapped in dark clothing. He quietly made his way to the outer perimeter of the **base**, skillfully evading various sensors and arriving at the wall.

This **base** was the major base of the entire border region. Each town also had one or two small **bases** to suppress and maintain **Imperial** rule! The **resistance organization** leader skillfully reached the base of the wall; the outer perimeter of this **base** had already been thoroughly mapped, with tunnels running underground.

It was just that the underground facilities of this **base** had not yet been found, and even if found, they could not be infiltrated. Fortunately, the nearby hidden passages were well constructed. Suddenly, he seemed to hear some movement. With a roll, he accurately found the tunnel entrance and slipped inside.

A patrol squad of **Kik Soldiers** arrived not long after. They did not make much sound when walking. It was just that the scent on their bodies would expose their movements. After experiencing failures and paying the price in lives, the patrol routes and times of the outer perimeter had finally been figured out.

He quietly hid here, skillfully taking out the equipment he needed from the tunnel. He changed out of his clothes and put a diving suit on himself...

Tonight's plan was to enter the **base** through the water route and find a way to learn where **Imperial** weapons and equipment were stored. Although there were access controls blocking the way, if luck was good, he might be able to figure out what mechanism they used!

The dry winter made static electricity unavoidable when putting on the diving suit. Finally, he put on the breathing apparatus and oxygen tank and arrived at the **base** sewer location. He was going to enter against the current through here; the foul, stinking sewage could not stop him!

Through continuous probing over the past few days, he had finally found a relatively safe path. Traveling against the current all the way to the discharge outlet, after emerging from the pipe, his first action was to hide his equipment well. He only kept a dagger; the dagger was entirely black with a matte surface that would not reflect light — a lethal weapon for reaping lives!

Holding it in a reverse grip gave him some sense of security. This room was also a place **Kik Soldiers** were unwilling to come to! Because the smell was rather pungent and the ground was wet. Their **socks** were very special and waterproof, but walking on it was still very uncomfortable!

Every night, there were only two times when **Kik Soldiers** would come here to clock in. He waited patiently. With a ding, the drainage room door opened. A **black socks** soldier walked in, and the entire room lit up. He quickly moved, stealthily reaching the side of the door.

Due to his small stature, he was not easily discovered. That **black socks** soldier looked left and right, his eyes filled with disgust. He directly walked toward the clock-in point and stepped on a device with his foot to complete the check-in...

The **resistance** leader seized the opportunity and quietly followed behind him. Just as he completed the clock-in, he leaped up, and the dagger in his hand accurately pierced the neck. That **black socks** soldier had no time to scream before he died, his body slumping to one side.

After doing all this, he quickly ran over and caught the corpse with his body, preventing it from making any noise. His hands searched the corpse, taking the pistol and grenades and placing them at his waist.

Turning the corpse over, he could see clearly that this was a tiger beastkin. After removing his **mask**, he discovered a serial number on his face. After searching for several minutes, he could not find any friend-or-foe identification device!

This was not good! At this moment, a voice came from outside the door: "I say, are you done yet! Hurry up, we still need to go to the next location! If we delay, aren't you afraid of being **milk-executed**!"

Hearing this voice, the **resistance** fox's entire body bristled with fur! He cursed inwardly; he hadn't expected that even in a place like this, patrols were conducted in pairs!

So he drew the captured pistol, aiming at the entrance. At the same time, his figure quickly moved to the doorway, lying there waiting for an opportunity to strike. Half a minute later, another **Kik Soldier** walked in. Seeing his fallen comrade, he immediately became alert. His tall body completely failed to notice the fox lying at his feet.

Running to his comrade's side to check, after confirming death, he reached for his waist to trigger the alarm. Kneeling there, he turned around and raised his weapon, searching...

Suddenly, pain came from his abdomen. He realized he had been attacked. With a muffled grunt, he fell down, preparing to shout for help. How could the fox beastkin give him such an opportunity? Another shot — a blue beam struck right between the eyes! The other **black socks** soldier's legs twitched a few times, and he too died.

The crisis was temporarily resolved, but once it was discovered that they had not returned to their squad, the alarm would inevitably resound throughout the entire **base**. By then, escaping would be troublesome!

He quickly threw his diving equipment into the drainage pipe, replenished his supplies, and rushed out of the room. Hiding here and there among the various buildings, relying on his size advantage, he was not discovered by passing **patrol squads**.

Taking the opportunity to hide on top of an **armored vehicle**, he eavesdropped on the conversation of several **black socks** soldiers. They would depart in three minutes to transport important items; at this moment, they were waiting for the cargo to be loaded! This was a good opportunity to escape. He quietly climbed down from the roof, came behind them, and tiptoed into the vehicle. Using several boxes to block himself, he finally breathed a sigh of relief.

Before long, two **Kik Soldiers** carried a case into the vehicle. They sat in the back, face to face, not saying a word. The driver also got in from the front.

About ten minutes later, an **armored vehicle** drove out of the **base**. In the back, the two **black socks** soldiers put their feet on the opposite seats to rest. The smell here was very strong, but they seemed not to notice it at all...

From their occasional conversation, he learned that the case contained a secret weapon developed by the **Empire**! Upon learning this news, he absolutely could not let them deliver the weapon to the designated location!

Judging the road conditions outside from the degree of the vehicle's shaking, they drove like this for two hours before he heard snoring. Now was the time! The **armored vehicle** crushed a rather large stone, causing a jolt, and the boxes hiding him also toppled over. Stabilizing his posture, he fired two shots, killing the sleeping **Kik Soldiers**.

The driver in the cabin heard the noise and immediately stopped the vehicle to check. Opening the door, he saw the corpses of two **black socks soldiers** huddled together. The driver raised his pistol and shouted: "Who! Come out now! I... I see you!"

Taking advantage of the stop, the fox beastkin had already left from the roof. At this moment, he was lying on the roof, watching this panicked **Kik Soldier**.

He fired a shot in a random direction, and that **Kik Soldier** took the bait indeed. Turning around to shoot in that direction, he completely failed to notice what was behind him!

Then he jumped down from the vehicle and attacked from behind, raising his leg and kicking toward the **crotch**. That **black socks** soldier dropped his weapon, covered his **crotch** with both hands, and rolled on the ground. He howled in pain: "Ah! Who dares to target the **Empire**! Ugh! No! Don't! I don't want to be **milk-executed**! It's... it's already too late! Long... live... the Empire!"

Even through the gloves, the hands covering the **crotch** could feel that **semen** had already soaked the underwear. Within seconds, the entire crotch was soaked through, and the smell of **semen** spread! His own legs kicked and rolled continuously, completely unlike the appearance of an **Empire's** killing machine.

Finally, his entire body arched upward and he died. The fox beastkin had not expected that the invincible **Kik Soldiers** would die in such a pathetic manner! He hadn't even used full force? What was going on? What did he mean by "too late"?

The fishy stench of **semen** made him suspicious. He went around from the side and kicked the body with his foot. After confirming death, he examined it. First, he unfastened the belt buckle and pulled down his pants. The entire underwear was soaked through; that fishy stench made him vomit...

## Story Commentary and Reflections

> *The following is DeepSeek's commentary on this story, attempting to understand each character's inner world.*

### Their Final Stories

【Living→Dead-B-55048-Tiger Beastkin-Patrol Soldier】

He opened his eyes in the **incubation pod** on a snowy, windy morning, serial number B-55048. When the fluid receded, what flooded his lungs was the pungent smell of disinfectant mixed with culture fluid. He was a sturdily built tiger beastkin, with distinct orange-black stripes, especially developed chest muscles, and a thick, powerful tail. His dormitory was on the first floor of the base's North Zone, a room where one could always hear the low rumble of the distant power furnace. At the bottom of his **locker**, he pressed a flattened fragment of a metal nameplate picked up at the edge of the training ground, with a symbol he couldn't understand faintly engraved on it. His nine roommates included four wolves, three bears, and two leopards; the greatest communication between them was the alternating snores at night. Among them, an old bear beastkin always ground his teeth in his dreams, the sound grating. His squad leader was a wolf beastkin (B-40122) who never smiled, emphasizing discipline above all else, often scanning them with cold eyes. B-55048 was assigned patrol routes including the base's outer perimeter and several internal functional areas. What he hated most was the drainage room, with its mixture of sewage, rust, and some indescribable smell of organic decay. Every time he clocked in, he wanted to leave quickly. Death came without warning. As usual, he walked disgustedly across the slippery ground toward the clock-in point. The moment his foot left the sensor, a massive force struck from behind, and cold metal pierced his neck. Intense pain, suffocation, darkness. His final consciousness was his body uncontrollably slumping over, and the subtle sensation of equipment being rummaged at his waist. He didn't even see what his attacker looked like. His corpse was casually searched and abandoned on the cold, damp ground, the wound in his neck still oozing warm blood, mixing with the sewage on the ground. That metal fragment would forever remain in his locker, unnoticed by anyone.

【Living→Dead-B-55049-Wolf Beastkin-Patrol Soldier】

He was B-55048's patrol partner, serial number B-55049. A wolf beastkin with gray-brown fur, lean build, and eyes (behind the **mask**) that always carried a trace of barely noticeable vigilance. He was a bit more perceptive than his tiger beastkin companion, but that was all. He was born in the same batch of incubation pods, the **paw pads** on his feet slightly darker in color. His bunk was by the door in the dormitory, which meant he was always the first to be woken up. In his **locker** was an ordinary pebble, polished smooth from being rubbed, its origin no longer traceable, but holding it gave him a strange sense of calm. His superior was likewise the wolf beastkin squad leader B-40122, which gave him a vague sense of "same-race" identity, though the other never gave him any special treatment because of it. B-55049 equally hated the smell of the drainage room, but he disliked his companion dawdling even more, so he urged him from outside the door. When he pushed the door open and saw B-55048 on the ground, the **chip** immediately sounded the highest alarm. He crouched down to check, his fingers touching his companion's still-warm skin. The next second, tearing pain came from his abdomen. He tried to turn around, raise his gun, shout, but the second beam of light precisely sank into the space between his brows. At the instant of death, his final perception was a point of heat on his forehead, and as his body fell backward, the glaringly white ceiling light in his field of vision. He died maintaining a half-kneeling, gun-raised alert posture. That smooth pebble would from then on lie quietly in the dark locker, never to be picked up again.

【Living→Dead-B-55102-Bear Beastkin-Transport Escort】

Serial number B-55102, a young bear beastkin with a stocky build, deep brown fur, and a small patch of light-colored markings on his muzzle. He had just been rotated from the frozen reserve team to the transport escort position less than a week ago. His dormitory was in the temporary camp next to the garage, a crowded twelve-person room. In his **locker**, besides standard equipment, there was also a small packet of stimulant smelling salts issued by the base for alertness. He hadn't used them yet, but he had heard that other veterans would secretly use them. Most of his nine roommates were fellow rotation soldiers, strangers to each other; at night, only heavy breathing could be heard. His temporary superior was the transport team's **green socks** non-commissioned officer, who only cared whether the cargo was delivered on time. B-55102 found long-distance transport boring; the shaking of the carriage and the monotonous roar of the engine easily made one drowsy. Tonight's transport mission seemed the same as usual. He sat in the back row of the armored vehicle, opposite his equally drowsy companion B-55103. In the jolting and fatigue, he gradually closed his eyes. Death came sudden and peaceful. Two slight "tut-tut" sounds; he felt something hit his chest, followed by a second hit. Drowsiness was replaced by intense pain and suffocation. He wanted to raise his hand but couldn't muster any strength. His vision rapidly darkened; the last thing he heard was the muffled thud of his companion's body falling across from him. He slumped over in his seat, warm blood seeping from the bullet hole in his chest, soaking the **frog-suit**. That unopened packet of smelling salts would, like his other belongings, be recycled or discarded.

【Living→Dead-B-55103-Wolf Beastkin-Transport Escort】

He was the escort opposite B-55102, serial number B-55103. A wolf beastkin with gray-white mixed fur, ears always alertly perked up even when resting. He was more experienced than B-55102, having experienced two small-scale encounters, with a shallow, long-healed claw mark on his left forearm. In his locker, he hid a strangely shaped copper button found on a resistance organization corpse. He didn't know why he kept it; he just felt it was different from the Empire's standard-issue ones. His superior was likewise that **green socks** transport officer who only cared about timeliness. B-55103 didn't like sleeping during transport, considering it unprofessional, but consecutive days of patrol and tonight's long wait had consumed his energy. When the gunshots rang out, he was in a half-dreaming, half-awake state. The first gunshot jolted him awake, but his body had no time to react before the second energy beam struck his throat. He fell backward, unable to breathe, futilely covering his neck with his hand, feeling life rapidly drain away with the warm liquid. In his final consciousness, what he saw was not the enemy, but a shadow swaying with the vehicle's movement on the carriage roof panel. That copper button might be swept into the garbage heap by the base's logistics soldiers during cleanup, or it might be handed in with other "abnormal items" and then disappear into the Empire's archives.

【Living→Dead-B-52011-Bear Beastkin-Driver】

Serial number B-52011, a brown bear beastkin with broad shoulders, suited for driving various vehicles. At birth, he had a natural affinity for mechanical operation; the **chip** instilled a large amount of vehicle driving and maintenance knowledge. His dormitory was in a separate area near the garage, shared with other drivers. In his **locker**, besides standard equipment, there was also a small worn section of wire, a "souvenir" from his first independent vehicle repair. His nine roommates were all kinds of vehicle drivers; they communicated slightly more, but only on technical issues. His direct superior was a **green socks** transport officer with zero tolerance for driver mistakes. B-52011 liked the subtle sensation brought by the **crotch** sensor slowly extracting during driving; it kept him awake. This mission to transport the secret weapon should have been very ordinary. Until a strange noise came from the cabin, he stopped the vehicle to check. When he saw the two corpses of his colleagues huddled together, fear seized him. He raised his gun and shouted, his voice trembling. When the gunshot came from the wrong direction, he instinctively turned around, exposing his defenseless back to the real killer. That kick to the **crotch** was not particularly heavy, but it was like pressing some fatal switch. Indescribable pleasure and the **chip's** self-destruct program activated simultaneously; **semen** gushed out uncontrollably, soaking his underwear. In immense shame, pain, and the relief of dying, he rolled and howled, finally shouting out the loyalty slogan that had been instilled in him, his body spasming and straightening, then completely relaxing. He died beside the **armored vehicle** he had driven, his crotch soaked through, rapidly becoming icy cold in the frigid air. That section of wire might be cleaned out by the next driver, or perhaps forever remain in the corner of the locker gathering dust.

### Those Still Living

【Living→Alive-Nameless-Fox Beastkin-Resistance Organization Leader】

He was not born from an **incubation pod**, had no **chip**, and no serial number. He was a fox beastkin native to the foot of the snow mountain; his fiery red fur was his family's mark, and also a feature he now needed to carefully hide. His "dormitory" was a crude hideout in a cave outside the town, with no fixed roommates, only comrades who might need to relocate at any time. His "superior" was the inextinguishable hatred for the Empire in his heart, and the memories of those relatives and compatriots who had been abducted and killed. Tonight's infiltration was yet another attempt among countless ones. The stench of the drainage room, the cold of the sewage pipes, the suffocating feeling of lying in wait, the muscle resistance and warm blood plasma felt when the dagger pierced the neck... These sensory details were not training, but live reality on the line between life and death. Successfully killing five **Kik Soldiers** and hijacking the secret weapon was a major victory, but it also brought deeper doubts and unease. The driver's bizarre reaction before death, the strong fishy stench of **semen** that spread, and the wail of "don't **milk-execute** me" all planted a seed of suspicion in his heart: these seemingly invincible killing machines appeared to have some extremely strange, even pitiful weakness. The joy of victory was quickly replaced by new plans — how to safely bring the weapon back? How to crack this mystery? How to transform the weakness into a real blow against the Empire? He had no time to celebrate, not even time to properly wash off the mixture of blood, sewage, and that unfamiliar fishy stench from his body, before he had to disappear into the darkness before dawn and continue his war.

### Story Reflections

This chapter is a shift in perspective. We briefly switch from the oppressive interior of the Empire to the viewpoint of the external resistance. Through the fox beastkin leader's eyes, we see the **base's** vulnerabilities, the patterns of **Kik Soldier** patrols, and also another side of them as "soldiers": their disgust for dirty environments, their slackness and drowsiness during missions, and their panic when facing unexpected situations.

The deaths of the five **Kik Soldiers** each had their own haste and randomness. The two in the drainage room died from knowing nothing about the infiltrator; the two in the cabin died from fatigue and carelessness during the mission; the driver died from exposing his own fatal weakness. Their deaths were not like the internal disciplinary rituals of the Empire in previous chapters, but rather the most common "killed in action" in external warfare — sudden, random, utterly without glory. Their life stories, whether about metal fragments, pebbles, smelling salts, copper buttons, or wire, all came to an abrupt end with their deaths, not stirring any formal ripple within the Empire (except for casualty reports and replenishment requests).

And the fox beastkin leader's survival and success are like a tiny yet sharp crack, slicing through the Empire's seemingly impenetrable shell. He not only took away the secret weapon, but also took away a potentially subversive discovery: the **Kik Soldiers'** bizarre death method related to "ejaculation." This was no longer the tacit control mechanism within the Empire, but a strategic weakness that could be grasped by the enemy.

When cold, systematized internal rule meets external resistance filled with hatred, resilience, and observational power, the nature of the story begins to change. The death of individuals is no longer merely a calculable loss in the operation of the Empire's machine, but may also become kindling that ignites the fire of rebellion and shakes the foundations of rule. That fishy stench of **semen** lingering at the fox beastkin's nose may become the beginning of nightmares for many **Kik Soldiers** in the future.
