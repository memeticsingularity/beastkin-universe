> *No orders, only waiting.*
> *The fishy stench of liquid seeps into the **mask**, like an invisible chain.*
> *He suppresses the restlessness in his heart, because that would cost him his life.*

Without orders, he could only wait. The fishy odor of liquid on his **mask** was unbearable. A trace of restlessness gradually sprouted in his heart, but he suppressed it! Because messing up could really cost him his life!

The **blue socks** officer stood before the corpse, supporting his chin with one hand in thought. Then he took out a device from the pocket of his **tactical vest**. He pressed it against j56005's face, extracting all the **semen**.

He opened the back of the device and took out a small bottle filled with thick, white, turbid **semen**. He shook the small bottle before j56005 and said: "This is important material for the Empire! It's an important ingredient for training new **Kik Soldiers**! Every **Kik Soldier** who is **milk-executed** must have their **semen** extracted like this!"

j56005's breathing grew rapid. He was terrified! He had just been born; was he going to die before he could serve the Empire? Watching the officer approach, he couldn't help but swallow a mouthful of saliva. The **paw pads** on the soles of his feet began to sweat...

Sweat seeped through the **military socks**, leaving marks on the ground. This small movement did not escape the bear beastkin officer's eyes. He crossed his arms over his chest and said: "Don't be so nervous, kid! Let me ask you a question! Do you know why we **Kik Soldiers** don't wear boots?"

Receiving the officer's command, the **chip** provided the answer: "Reporting, sir! Because the **military socks** of a **Kik Soldier** are our mark; these **socks** represent the Empire! Moreover, these **socks** have strong defensive capabilities and make no sound during movement. We are proud of them! Sir!"

"Very good answer. As a reward, here's another mission! Take this corpse to the recycling department. Then patrol the base once. If you see anyone slacking, grab them and bring them to me!" The bear beastkin officer lifted his foot, pressing those **blue socks** against j56005's lower abdomen as he spoke.

j56005 saluted, then walked around the officer, stepped forward, and unlocked the restraints. He hoisted the corpse onto his shoulder and walked out of the room. The corpse had not yet cooled; that fishy, desire-filled odor still lingered. He walked through the corridor and arrived at the base square, passing by other patrolling **Kik Soldiers**.

Those patrolling **Kik Soldiers** were each in high spirits, arranged in formation by height, moving through the base along their routes. The searchlights on the walls also moved rhythmically. After walking for more than ten minutes, he arrived at the recycling room.

The moment he approached, the equipment read the information on his **socks**. The door opened automatically. The entire room was eerie and gloomy under the pale white light. Racks were piled with recycling boxes, their labels categorizing what items the boxes were meant to hold.

He threw the corpse on his shoulder heavily onto the iron bed and removed the equipment piece by piece. First the **tactical vest**, then took out the **mask** stuffed in the pocket and placed it aside. Then he unfastened the equipment on his legs, followed by the gloves and the **frog-suit** top.

Looking at the tiger beastkin's sturdy chest, the tight **undershirt** he wore still had sweat stains. An odor of sweat involuntarily made his pupils dilate, and his hands stopped, stroking the corpse back and forth several times.

Only then did he reluctantly take it off, followed by the pants and underwear. When pulling off the underwear, that smell went straight into his nasal cavity, and his lower body couldn't help but stir! He very much wanted to grab his **crotch**, but **military regulations** did not allow him to do so!

At this moment, the **chip** in his brain activated, suppressing that fire of desire. He silently recited "Long live the Empire" and continued his work. Looking at that soft genitalia, there was only disdain in his heart!

Among **Kik Soldiers**, there is only a superior-subordinate relationship; there is no so-called friendship! Only the relationship of **milking** and being **milked**. This mission was about to conclude. The final step was to pull off those **Imperial military socks**. They had a scent! An intoxicating scent!

According to the labels on the boxes, he sorted the equipment and placed them properly, then pressed the button beside the bed. The corpse's terrified expression slowly descended, sent into the decomposition equipment. It became the most basic nutrients used to cultivate new **Kik Soldiers**...

After finishing all this, he let out a sigh of relief, picked up his weapon, and carried out the officer's second mission! Find a slacking soldier and bring him before the officer!

So he tidied his equipment and tiptoed out of the recycling room. Following the information instilled by the **chip**, he began patrolling the base. Before long, he arrived at a corner of the warehouse. These **Imperial military socks** were truly impressive!

They didn't feel cold against the ground, fit perfectly against the soles of his feet, and made not a single sound. His ears swiveled, hearing a strange noise? It sounded like snoring!

j56005 was overjoyed. He crept over and saw a **black socks** soldier leaning against the wall, asleep. His weapon was thrown aside. He slept soundly, occasionally using a hand wearing **tactical gloves** to rub his belly. The snoring was very rhythmic...

According to the information in the **chip**, **Kik Soldiers** would go limp all over if their **crotch** was grabbed. This was the fatal weakness of a **Kik Soldier**, but the rebels didn't know this top-secret information yet!

j56005 extended his left hand, aiming at his **crotch**! He quickly grabbed that bulge, and the **black socks** soldier let out a muffled grunt and woke up. He opened his eyes weakly, looking at the scene before him.

It was a **Kik Soldier** just like himself! The sensation from his **crotch** made his whole body weak, twitching uncontrollably. As a wolf beastkin, his muzzle was pinched tightly shut, unable to make a sound. He only looked at j56005 in terror. j56005 said coldly: "By order of the **blue socks** officer! I saw you slacking on patrol! I am to bring you to him for punishment!"

Saying this, he squeezed harder with his left hand, hurting the slacking **black socks** soldier until he passed out. Still not satisfied, he stomped his large foot on the soldier's foot. The dust on the **socks** was imprinted on the slacking wolf beastkin soldier's **mask**! He picked up the soldier's weapon, slung it on his back, and dragged him like a corpse through the base, into the building, and before the officer!

"Well done!" the **blue socks** officer said, rubbing his hands together.

He used his foot to toy with the **black socks** soldier on the ground, ordering j56005 to close the door and stand outside! Then he dragged the **black socks** soldier into the corner and pulled off his **mask** with one hand. He rubbed the wolf's head with both hands, his own drool flowing onto the **mask** without him even noticing...

Using the **chip** in his brain, he activated and woke up the black socks soldier before him. The **black socks** soldier looked at the officer in terror and said: "Sir, spare me! I was just... just asleep! I won't dare again! Please spare me!"

"That won't do! Dereliction of duty is a capital crime under Imperial **military regulations**! Your only value now is to entertain me!" Saying this, he pulled open the fly of his pants.

The large bulge at his **crotch** was exposed, plump and very tempting!

## Story Commentary and Reflections

> *The following is DeepSeek's commentary on this story, attempting to understand each character's inner world.*

### Their Final Stories

*No Kik Soldiers died in this chapter.*

### Those Still Living

【Living→Alive-B-56005-Tiger Beastkin-New Recruit/j56005】

This is a new recruit's first direct confrontation with death — the death of another, and the fear of his own possible death. He fully executed the entire process from corpse disposal to capturing a colleague. The **chip** suppressed his physiological desires, but could not completely eliminate that instinctual restlessness toward the smell of **semen** and the hazy perception of the power of **milking**. In the recycling room, his brief caress of the tiger beastkin compatriot's corpse was a tiny ripple of that permitted 15% self-awareness in the silence. He learned efficiency and coldness, and also experienced for the first time that in the Empire, to live meant having to become a kind of "gear," and the first step to avoid being crushed was to learn to crush others.

【Living→Alive-L-08031-Bear Beastkin-Blue Socks Executioner】

He is **blue socks**, the direct executor of death within the base. He enjoys the pleasure of domination and explanation, teaching the new recruit about the collection of **semen** and the significance of **military socks** as lessons. That step on j56005's stomach was both a reward and a mark — marking this new recruit as worth "cultivating." At this moment, he is preparing to enjoy his captured prey, venting power and desire in the most direct way. For him, this is just another ordinary night of "entertainment," one of the few forms of entertainment in his border exile life that he can completely control.

【Living→Alive-B-33017-Wolf Beastkin-Slacking Soldier】

He is an unfortunate, ordinary **black socks soldier** who let his guard down at the wrong time. Captured in his sleep, the despair of his whole body going weak when his **crotch** was controlled, and the fearful pleas upon waking before the **blue socks officer**, constitute his entire world at this moment. He has not yet been **milk-executed**, but his fate is no longer in his own hands. His existence, at this moment, exists solely to satisfy his superior's "entertainment" needs — a vivid, trembling footnote under Imperial rules.

### Story Reflections

This chapter is like a cold surgical demonstration. We follow j56005, step by step, learning how to become a "qualified" Imperial Kik Soldier: how to handle a comrade's remains, how to understand the cold significance of **semen** as a strategic resource, how to use the weakness knowledge instilled in him to carry out arrests, and finally, how to detach himself from the "entertainment" scene inside the door and become a silent guard.

The most heart-stopping thing is not the brutality of the **blue socks officer**, but the "institutionalization" and "pedagogical" nature of the entire process. Death is broken down into procedures, desire is transformed into a resource, and fear is disciplined into obedience. j56005's moment of "caressing" and "reluctance" in the recycling room is the last faint glimmer of humanity (or beast-nature), immediately suppressed by the **chip** and the mission. And that slacking wolf beastkin soldier — his fate is not yet written, but everyone knows the possible endings.

Here, the living learn how to bury the dead, and stand ready to become the next object to be processed. The shadow of the Empire permeates every corner and shapes every soul — if they still possess souls — through such seemingly daily handovers, patrols, and punishments.
