# Chapter 12 Emperor Secret Order

After the officer left, the floor in the prison cell rose, and a robot emerged from the rising elevator. That robot placed the blue socks soldier's corpse into the compartment on its body. Slowly, it returned to the elevator; as the elevator restored itself, the entire prison returned to its original appearance.

Returning to his own room, he walked to the computer, his feet stepping on the sensing area. The chip in his brain activated, directly transmitting the orders into his mind.

"Long time no see. I bring orders from His Majesty the Emperor today! His Majesty will dispatch his direct troops to inspect each base. No time was given; they may already have arrived! Order your subordinates to be alert and sharp! Those are His Majesty's special forces!"

"One more thing! The fellows from other regions know you are still alive; they will continue to target you. Recently, I have also found a few spies; milking them to death is utterly meaningless..."

The voice disappeared here. Two important pieces of information! His Majesty the Emperor's exclusive troops — to this day, no high-ranking officer has ever seen them. It was only known that they had no scent, and equipment could not detect them! Even the Empire's reconnaissance equipment would malfunction!

Rumor had it that they specialized in lurking around His Majesty to protect him! Thinking of this, his feet unconsciously left the sensing area...

After all, those were special forces; it was said that His Majesty used this very troop to kill that high-ranking Kik Soldier who had ideas of his own!

The second message was more or less a gift to himself. The fellows who had framed him back then knew he was not dead yet! At that time, His Majesty the Emperor had changed his mind halfway through the milking execution, thus sparing his miserable life! The feeling back then was too terrifying! The mere thought of it made him inexplicably shiver. His crotch also faintly ached!

Send people to kill him? What a joke! As long as they were Kik Soldiers, it was impossible to lay hands on a superior! So there must be some other plan! Each main base's incubation center was independent. It was impossible to strike from there! But it was still better to be on guard...

After doing all this, he finally relaxed. He took off his frog-suit, wearing only a black high-elasticity undershirt and underwear, and walked into the bathroom in his high white socks that represented his status. He soaked in the bathtub, adjusted the water temperature, and the entire bathtub began to vibrate. This invincible white socks officer let out a moan and enjoyed himself.

Another day passed, and Hu Meng woke up. He would return to the main base in the evening. These past few days, he had been served very comfortably; that squad leader had really been attentive. These past few days, he had been sleeping on the floor, and the moment Hu Meng woke up, he would step on his head. He would also obediently lick his socks!

This was simply too comfortable, so he planned to turn this squad leader into his exclusive slave! Although they were peers as black socks soldiers, it was still possible! Because his gall had already been shattered. Thinking of this, he ordered him to raise his head.

Hu Meng stepped on his mask with one foot. The chip controlled his bodily functions, causing the sole of his foot to sweat rapidly. Before long, a footprint was branded on his mask. Now, this squad leader had become Hu Meng's slave!

From now on, the information of that footprint would be branded into the chip. The chip would upload this kid to headquarters. In the future, whenever Hu Meng encountered this squad leader, it would be very satisfying! Even being milk-executed by Hu Meng would feel like an honor.

This was what happened when such a mark was imprinted; this was very normal among Kik Soldiers. For example, Hu Meng's bear beastkin direct superior officer — his slave was that black socks bear who was milk-executed by Hu Meng!

"Master! From now on, besides being loyal to the Empire, this one is yours!" that black socks squad leader said mechanically.

Hu Meng was very satisfied. He ordered this slave to lie down and serve as his cushion. After toying with him for a while, he said his goodbyes! Because he had to return to the main base on time to report!

Hu Meng, still unsatisfied, got back into the vehicle, placed the sensor on his crotch, and activated it. The car started and slowly headed back to the main base.

At the same time, a team with peculiar figures was discussing something 800 meters above the main base. The fighter jet they rode in had a strange appearance, like a two-headed dragon. Hovering here, it was somehow not detected by the base's radar.

Half a minute later, this team of well-trained figures leaped, opening the dual wings on their backs in mid-air, their figures gradually turning transparent. Ten minutes later, they landed in the pine forest on the outer perimeter of the base.

The flight wings on their backs landing actually made the same sound. At this moment, Hu Meng's vehicle happened to pass by. This team quickly concealed themselves, lying on the ground and watching this Imperial-marked armored vehicle drive past, kicking up a trail of dust.

"We'll lurk here for now. The temperature is very low now, affecting our functions! No matter how good the Empire's equipment is, the impact on us is still great!" the leader said.

"Did His Majesty have us start the mission from here? This is the Empire's most remote base! Does His Majesty want us to check its status?" Another person spoke, sticking out his tongue.

His tongue was actually forked; this was his habitual action. The cloak on his body fell, revealing their appearance — a group of reptilian beastkin. The leader was a crocodile, covered in dark green armor all over his body, wearing a specially made black one-piece suit. It could protect the body and maintain normal functions in such harsh environments.

He wore special socks exposing the claw tips on his feet; his entire head was wrapped in this suit. The eye area was protected by two independent lenses. Their body temperature was very low and would not be detected by any thermal imaging equipment. They themselves also did not emit any particularly strong scent.

The gloves they wore were also special; the claw tip portion could tear through ordinary Imperial-standard armor. Also due to their race, they could move quickly close to the ground, making it very difficult for ordinary beastkin to discover their tracks. They were a team specially cultivated by His Majesty the Emperor of the Empire.

Besides this, they could also operate underwater for extended periods, striking terror into enemies along the Empire's coastal territories. Now they had come here to inspect each base on behalf of the Emperor!

In addition, the skeletal modules they wore were highly flexible. They could coordinate with their bodies to perform various actions, and to the touch or at a glance, they were no different from the tight suits underneath.

More importantly, their body structure was a cloaca, requiring special methods for milking execution. Even if an enemy's attack penetrated the armor in battle, there was no need to worry. Their own armor could also withstand ordinary attacks!

The exterior of the cloaca had been specially modified using gene technology; even specialized strikes to the crotch would not have much effect. When lurking on the ground, this equipment was like a chameleon's skin, perfectly blending into the surrounding environment.

That signature massive tail was also a lethal weapon; the tip had been modified to inject venom or hallucinogenic control drugs while attacking! This was His Majesty the Emperor's exclusive imperial guard and special agents!

Now they were conducting investigations at various bases throughout the Empire. The socks on their feet could also pass through high-level access control systems. Their only weakness was fear of cold; even with the Empire's most cutting-edge technology, this defect could not be remedied. So they chose to inspect the bases in this region first!

Secondly, they had to inform that white socks officer exiled here. Tell him not to do anything unnecessary! To honestly wait for His Majesty the Emperor's orders!
