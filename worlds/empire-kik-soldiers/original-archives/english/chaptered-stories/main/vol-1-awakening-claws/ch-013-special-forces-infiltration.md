# Chapter 13 Special Forces Infiltration

This team of special Kik Soldiers turned around, organized their equipment, confirmed their respective functions, and walked toward the base. The one at the back of the team didn't forget to erase traces. Their speed was not very fast. They advanced during the day and needed to hibernate at night; more importantly, they had to maintain their temperature!

The claw-exposing socks, like ankle guards, would cause them to slowly lose heat. When necessary, they would drink the potions they carried. These potions would temporarily allow them to overcome these weaknesses, at the cost of consuming their lifespan.

After two days and one night, they finally reached the outer perimeter of the base. Through the telescope function on their lenses, they could clearly see everything at the base's main gate. Even the stains on the socks of the team of black socks soldiers guarding the entrance were clearly visible.

Watching them walk back and forth at the entrance, they memorized their patterns and also observed the patrol shift times of the outer perimeter. Until the sun set, all the Kik Soldiers of this team lifted their masks and poured the special potion down their throats.

Taking advantage of the gap during their shift change, they crept to the roadbed beneath the base's main gate. This place was 800 meters from the base's main gate; nearby was a sentry post. A motorcycle was parked outside the sentry box, convenient for the sentries here to change shifts.

The timing was about right; the wolf beastkin black socks soldier inside the sentry box changed shifts with the soldier outside. It was during this gap that the concealed special forces suddenly struck! A black shadow appeared without warning behind the base soldier who had just stopped outside.

A massive tail struck his neck, simultaneously injecting venom. This time, the venom was only a powerful sleep agent and was not fatal. The soldier inside the sentry box sensed something was wrong, grabbed his weapon, and rushed out, only to be knocked unconscious by another crocodile beastkin. They were thrown down the slope beside the road. They wouldn't wake up for four or five hours...

What remained was to wait for the squad at the base entrance to discover the situation here and enter the base in the chaos. They waited two hours for this moment; the special forces team leader noted this drawback! When they returned to His Majesty, they would issue orders to milk-execute these useless sentries!

They smoothly entered the base; those soldiers at the entrance didn't notice. It wasn't their fault, because the special forces had activated camouflage mode. They only had two minutes, enough time for them to get in...

Coming inside the base, they evaded various patrol teams and sentry posts, taking the opportunity to assess this base. At this moment, Hu Meng had finished his daytime mission and was bathing with his subordinates in the bathhouse on the first floor of the dormitory.

In the changing room, he found his locker and stripped off all his clothes and equipment. He only wore military socks and a tight, high-elasticity black undershirt and underwear set. Only at this time could they remove their masks, revealing their true faces.

Hu Meng was escorted into the bathroom like a star surrounded by the moon. Besides the showerheads on the wall, there was also a massive pool. Inside, the steam was hazy; some black socks soldiers were bathing. Seeing the socks on Hu Meng's face, they quickly stood at attention.

Hu Meng ignored them and directly walked into the pool, leaning against the edge of the pool and enjoying the comfortable water. He exhaled a breath of turbid air, closed his eyes, and enjoyed himself. The tight underwear set began to react upon contact with water. The stains and sweat stains on it all peeled off into the water.

A wolf beastkin subordinate fawningly washed Hu Meng's back. Hu Meng comfortably stretched out his legs. Another subordinate walked over, knelt in the pool, and helped Hu Meng scrub his socks. By the way, he also massaged his own squad leader...

Hu Meng was extremely comfortable, letting out whimpering sounds. That comfortable feeling made him directly kick over the subordinate who was massaging his feet. At this moment, a tiger beastkin wearing green socks walked into the pool. These green socks soldiers were the type responsible for the outer perimeter of the main building.

They were unwilling to come to a place for these black socks trash, but this green socks soldier had come with a mission. He used his green socks to touch Hu Meng's black socks. After identifying the information in the socks, he confirmed that this was the black socks soldier needed for the mission!

He activated his authority, and Hu Meng quickly got up and stood at attention!

"Reporting, sir!" Hu Meng immediately greeted.

The green socks soldier had a tall, straight build with a perfect body of muscle wrapped in fat, making his underwear set even more eye-catching. He waved his hand, telling the other black socks soldiers to leave this place. Those Kik Soldiers nodded and left to wait outside.

"The officer sent me to transmit orders! Your mission was completed very well! That officer wants you to report to him personally!" After speaking, he walked out of the pool.

He came to a glass room at the entrance/exit area; the equipment inside the room activated. Within several tens of seconds, the moisture on his clothes was gone. He opened the door on the other side and went to put on his military uniform...

Hu Meng didn't dare delay and subsequently went to the glass room as well. After tidying up, he put on his military uniform and equipment and headed straight for the main base... When he arrived at the entrance, the canine beastkin personal guard beside the white socks officer was already waiting.

Familiar with the route, he arrived at the white socks officer's room. Two blue socks soldiers stood at the door. Their pant zippers were pulled open, and a strange device was inserted there; those two blue socks soldiers looked to be in great pain.

"They angered the esteemed one. As long as they can endure ten minutes, the device will not activate. Otherwise, they will be milk-executed, and even the chips in their brains will self-destruct along with it," the canine beastkin explained.

Hu Meng was startled and only lowered his head to follow this personal guard into the room. Upon seeing that white socks officer, he obediently crawled over in an orz posture, burying his head very low, afraid that he too would have such a device fitted on him.

"You are very well-behaved today. Were you frightened by the two trash at the door? As long as you like, I can demote them to black socks. You can discipline them!" The white socks officer said, stepping on Hu Meng's head with his foot.

"This one doesn't dare. Those two officers look very strong. This one wouldn't dare manage them," Hu Meng answered in a small voice.

Subsequently, the officer leaned back on the sofa and fell asleep, while Hu Meng continued to serve as his footrest...

On the other side, that green socks soldier who had transmitted the orders still had patrol duties after leaving the black socks soldiers' bathhouse. He casually held his weapon in his hand and began his mission. He walked to a place where someone was slacking, stretched his waist, and was about to lean against the corner of the wall to rest.

Suddenly, his crotch received a heavy blow; his entire body went limp and was about to fall when a pair of large hands grabbed his neck. His entire body was lifted up; breathing became a problem. His feet swayed wildly, and both hands also used all their strength, wanting to pry open the large hands pinching his neck.

Everything was in vain. The vision in his eyes grew increasingly blurry; the swelling in his crotch became more and more obvious! The most terrifying thing was that his feet were also grabbed.

"Quickly scan this green socks trash's information; we need to know why he came here! Could it be that we've been discovered?" the leader crocodile beastkin quietly instructed his subordinate.

A minute later, a slightly shorter subordinate replied: "We have not been discovered. I used his identification system to enter this base's system. His patrol route would bring him here. How do we dispose of this trash?"

"If that's the case, then hack his chip and make him our puppet. This base doesn't seem to have anything urgent. Later, we'll contact that esteemed one and tell him His Majesty the Emperor's latest orders!"

That green socks tiger beastkin was placed on the ground. Having already fainted from oxygen deprivation, he let these special forces do as they pleased. They pried open his mouth and fed him a bottle of medicinal liquid. The medicinal liquid contained nanobots; after entering the body, they could gather in the brain!

They would expand more functions on the original architecture of the chip. He was about to become a puppet, secretly providing information. After the mission was completed, he would also self-destruct; there was no need to worry at all.

Half an hour later, this green socks soldier sat up from the ground, completely unable to recall what had happened. He only felt that he had successfully slacked off. He quickly picked up his weapon and ran all the way back to the duty room.
