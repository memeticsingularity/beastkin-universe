These detailed "final stories" and the still-struggling "living people" together constitute the truth of the Empire's foundation: it is supported by countless tiny lives that are meticulously designed, strictly controlled, and finally silently annihilated. Every Kik Soldier has had a momentary gaze at an ice crystal, an obsession with neatness, an unconscious curl in sleep. Then, all these subtle traces belonging to "life" are ruthlessly erased, recycled, and reused by the system. When the fire of rebellion flickers at the foot of the snow mountain, what it must confront is perhaps not only the obvious violence, but also this extreme cold machine that grinds every tremor of the soul into uniformly obedient powder. These seen stories are the most powerful, silent questioning of that machine.
