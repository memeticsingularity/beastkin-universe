> *One corpse is a secret.*
> *A roomful of corpses is a truth the Empire cannot hide.*

It took him a while to recover. He regretted throwing away his diving equipment! Sitting on the ground, recalling what had just happened, he felt he had found the **Kik Soldiers'** weakness!

Just as he was getting somewhere, a voice came from the driver's cabin: "Vehicle 5! Stop immediately for inspection upon hearing this! An intrusion event has occurred at the **base**!"

The **fox beastkin** clutched his chest — he had still been discovered! There was no time to lose. He picked up the submachine gun on the ground and went to the driver's cabin. He smashed the communication and positioning equipment violently with the gun stock! Then he pushed the two **black socks** corpses in the back out of the vehicle, took everything usable from the three corpses, and finally drove away...

Along the way, he planned his route while contacting his comrades to arrange a pickup. The vehicle stopped a few kilometers from the stronghold; the tracks left in the snow would definitely expose him. After unloading the cargo with his companions, he had the vehicle drive off in the direction of the lake.

A crowd of **resistance** members surrounded the case. Under the light, the black case showed not a single seam. Knocking on it produced a solid, porcelain-like sound, and scanning it with detection equipment revealed no abnormalities. Helpless, they could only place it in the warehouse...

Next, the **fox beastkin** leader began to review the infiltration plan with his companions. Having stolen a classified item from the **Empire**, they would definitely conduct a large-scale search! So during this period, everyone should take no action. Subsequently, power to this stronghold was cut off. Everyone returned to the town through the tunnels...

At this moment, the **base** was brightly lit, with squads of **Kik Soldiers** in every corner. They turned the entire **base** upside down searching for traces of the intruder.

In the building at the very center of the **base**, several **green socks** squad leaders stood motionless. Before them was a high-ranking officer wearing **white socks**. He was a one-eyed **wolf beastkin**; at this moment, his feet were on the desk, looking up at the ceiling.

His mind was racing. News had just come in that Vehicle 5 was missing! The cargo inside was definitely lost! This was a super-weapon distributed by the **Empire**! The consequences of losing it were severe! He might even be **milked** himself!

Standing on either side of the desk were two **black socks** soldiers massaging his feet. A **green socks** soldier spoke: "Sir! The transport vehicle has not yet been found, but we have discovered the path the intruder used to infiltrate..."

"What's the use of that! If the **Empire** punishes me by **milking me to death**, then I will activate the **chips** in you wastes' brains one second before that! Knowing the path means there has always been a security risk that you failed to notice! Those two dead **black socks** wastes are as stupid as you!" The **wolf beastkin** officer roared in fury.

The several **green socks** soldiers standing on the other side of the desk all bristled with fur! The officer was angry! That **green socks** soldier who had just spoken wanted to explain something, but the officer was already impatient. He raised his hand, palm upward, making a grasping gesture.

His other hand elegantly snapped his fingers. That **green socks** squad leader suddenly leaned forward, clutching his **crotch** with both hands, groaning in pain. A few seconds later, he collapsed there dead. The remaining **green socks** squad leaders were so frightened they knelt on the ground begging for mercy.

"Vehicle 5 can't be found. I will report this news truthfully! No one will escape then! I hope His Majesty the Emperor can be merciful once! **Milk-execute** all the **black socks** soldiers responsible for last night's patrol! And bring a new batch from the **incubation center**!" The officer had the remaining **green socks** squad leaders roll out of the room.

Five minutes later, the execution room in the **base** was filled with screams. **Black socks** soldiers lay strewn about on the ground in every direction. The soldiers responsible for execution had to put on gas masks to work normally. The room was filled with the fishy stench of **semen**; due to the officer's order, their **semen** could not be recovered.

In the **recycling room**, j56005 was working non-stop. From being woken by the alarm at dawn until now, he had been working for a long time. He had been on standby in the **recycling room** since just now! Now, there was an endless stream of corpses being sent for him to process.

The fishy stench made him dizzy and seeing stars. Mechanically, he stripped off their equipment and sorted it into categories. Before he knew it, it was noon. He had to put down his work and line up to go to the cafeteria to eat, busying himself until the sun set before he finished processing those corpses.

At the same time, the towns around the **base** were also in turmoil. In the center of the town stood a tower-like structure. This was the **Empire's** stronghold **base** in the town. Its firepower was perfectly positioned to cover the entire town; the upper part of the tower could, when necessary, deploy its weapon systems to level the entire town.

The part closer to the ground was the area where **Kik Soldiers** were stationed and rested. A tower-like structure like this housed 20 **Kik Soldiers**. Two **armored vehicles**, various weapons and supplies, and an underground floor served as a warehouse.

The **watchtowers** in the surrounding towns had already received orders! All weapon ports opened, with various machine guns and missiles aimed at every part of the towns. The gates on the first floor opened, and two teams of **Kik Soldiers** formed combat formations and searched the towns house by house.

The entire town was exposed under the lights of the **watchtower**, with nowhere to hide. All townspeople were woken up and violently driven out of their homes by the charging **Kik Soldiers**.

They were not allowed to return home until dawn. Upon returning, not a single intact item remained in the house. Children cried in terror, while young adults continued to undergo inspection in the square.

The **black socks** squad leader in the **watchtower** directed his subordinates in interrogating these villagers, occasionally using gun stocks to beat those innocent townspeople. This interrogation continued until evening; those **Kik Soldiers** were also exhausted. Afterwards, they fell asleep soundly in their vehicles...

Among the townspeople, the members of the **resistance** were the most cowardly-looking group. They had already hidden their weapons properly; their main task now was to act cowardly. All the towns entered a curfew state, with patrols beginning at night in shifts.

The central **watchtower** also activated its corresponding functions. The top of the tower unfolded like a mushroom, with various weapons extending outward. The missile launch ports in the upper-middle section also all opened; with a single order from the officer, the town would be leveled!

By evening, j56005 received orders that there was one more corpse to process. So he had to work overtime. Arriving at the **recycling room**, he saw the corpse of a **green socks** officer lying there. This was the first time he had seen a **Kik Soldier** of this rank.

After closing the door, he examined it carefully. Curiously, he lifted the **mask** and discovered a slightly chubby **wolf beastkin**. His gray fur was dull and lusterless, his expression filled with fear and unwillingness, and his crotch area was very damp. His legs were slightly bent, and there was a pungent smell on his **gloves**...

Slowly, he removed the **frog-suit** top, pressing the tip of his nose close to the chest and sniffing. Just what was the difference between the officers and himself?

**Chapter 5 END**

> *The suppression continues, but the new recruit in the recycling room begins to wonder.*
> *The fear of the superior and the questions of the subordinate collide quietly in the stench.*

## Story Commentary and Reflections

> *The following is DeepSeek's commentary on this story, attempting to understand each character's inner world.*

### Their Final Stories

【Living→Dead-G-30821-Wolf Beastkin-Green Socks Squad Leader】

When he awoke from the **incubation pod**, his serial number was G-30821. As a rare **Kik Soldier** promoted directly to **green socks**, his gray fur was smoother than that of ordinary soldiers, and his build was more robust. His dormitory was a four-person officer's quarters, his bunk by the window, with a view of the flagpole at the center of the base. In his **locker**, besides standard equipment, there was also a small bottle of special cleaning agent for polishing **military socks** — a supply only **green socks soldiers** were permitted to be issued. His three roommates were **green socks** squad leaders from other squads; between them existed a subtle relationship of both competition and mutual vigilance. His direct superior was that one-eyed **white socks wolf beastkin** officer; he revered the other and also yearned to one day wear **white socks**. G-30821 was known for his rigorous work, responsible for patrol dispatch in the three outer zones of the base. His greatest anxiety was that mistakes by his **black socks** subordinates would implicate him. When the intrusion event occurred, he had硬着头皮 reported to the furious officer that the infiltration path had been discovered, inwardly harboring a trace of hope to atone for his mistakes. However, the officer's patience was exhausted. That grasping gesture and the snap of his fingers triggered a backdoor program preset in his **chip**, activatable only by higher authority. Intense pain exploded from his **crotch**, instantly sweeping through his entire body. He fell forward, his hands futilely clutching his vital spot, feeling **semen** gush out uncontrollably, soaking his crotch, in unspeakable pain and immense humiliation. Death came swiftly and humiliatingly; he didn't even have time to utter a plea for mercy, let alone "Long live the Empire." His corpse was casually thrown into the **recycling room**; that bottle of unused cleaning agent would probably be recovered by the logistics department and distributed to the next lucky (or unlucky) **green socks soldier**.

【Corpse→Dead-B-11407-Tiger Beastkin-Patrol Soldier】

He was one of the many **black socks soldiers** responsible for outer perimeter patrol last night, serial number B-11407. A young tiger beastkin with clear stripes and a tail tip that always curled slightly upward. His dormitory was on the east side of the base, a ten-person room, his bunk in the corner. In his **locker**, he hid a dried, claw-shaped leaf that had accidentally fallen on his shoulder during an autumn patrol. Among his nine roommates was a leopard beastkin born in the same batch as him; the two would occasionally exchange glances during training. His squad leader was a stern bear beastkin who often used an electric baton to poke their backs to urge them to speed up. B-11407 hated night shifts, especially the second half of the night, when the cold wind and drowsiness tormented him doubly. Last night, he had walked his fixed route as usual, not noticing anything abnormal. Until the morning shift change, he thought he could rest. Then, the assembly order came, followed by the verdict of "**milk-execute** all." He didn't even know what he had done wrong. In the execution room, he was pressed against the cold restraints; the executioner's rough technique brought intense pain and a pleasure against his will. Before his consciousness was burned away, he heard the rising and falling screams and whimpers around him, smelling the increasingly thick fishy stench of **semen** belonging to himself and others in the air. He may have shouted "Long live the Empire," or perhaps not. His **semen** was not recovered due to the order, flowing wasted onto the ground, mixing with that of his colleagues. That dried leaf would be swept into the trash can when his locker was cleaned out.

【Corpse→Dead-B-20389-Wolf Beastkin-Patrol Soldier】

Serial number B-20389, dark gray fur, with a faint scar below his left eye (accidentally scratched during training). He was B-11407's roommate, their bunks adjacent. In his **locker**, besides equipment, there was also a small section of wood polished very smooth, which he had picked up by the wall and whittled when bored. He was relatively silent by nature and paid extra attention to the ground under his feet during patrols, afraid of stepping on something he shouldn't. His squad leader was the same bear beastkin, who had once punished him with an extra shift for reporting a suspicious mark on the road surface (later proven to be animal tracks). Last night's patrol was nothing special to him; he was even somewhat relaxed because the shift change was imminent. When he was escorted to the execution room, he fell into immense confusion and terror. He didn't understand why. When the stimulation began, he tried to endure, but physiological mechanisms and **chip** control made him rapidly collapse. Before death, his final thought was perhaps that smooth section of wood. His corpse was stacked with many colleagues and sent to the **recycling room**, becoming one of the many "raw materials" that j56005 needed to process.

【Corpse→Dead-G-40115-Wolf Beastkin-Green Socks Officer】

He was a **green socks** corpse delivered separately, serial number G-40115. He did not die in this incident, but in an earlier "internal purge." He was a slightly chubby gray wolf beastkin, his fur having lost its luster. In life, he had been an officer in some internal affairs department, possessing a separate lounge. His **locker** might once have held some small privilege items only high-ranking officers had, but it was now empty. His story was already difficult to trace: perhaps he knew secrets he shouldn't have, perhaps he sided with the wrong faction, perhaps it was simply dereliction of duty. He died filled with fear and unwillingness, his crotch damp, his **gloves** stained with his own or others' bodily fluids. At this moment, he was merely an object of curious observation and study for j56005, a cold footnote to the concept of "officer." Traces of his existence, besides this corpse being processed, probably only remained as a crossed-out name and serial number in some archive.

### Those Still Living

【Living→Alive-Nameless-Fox Beastkin-Resistance Organization Leader】

After successfully hijacking the vehicle and returning to the stronghold, he faced new pressure and choices. The secret weapon could not be opened, like a hot potato. Even more urgent was the crazy retaliation the Empire would inevitably launch. He decisively ordered a power cut, evacuation, and transition to silent mode, displaying a leader's calm and decisiveness. However, the question in his heart about the **Kik Soldiers'** weakness was fermenting. That driver's reaction before death, that wail of "don't **milk-execute** me," and the spreading fishy stench of **semen** were like flickering will-o'-the-wisps in the darkness, pointing toward a direction that could overturn the war situation. He must now endure, preserve his strength under the Empire's high-pressure search, while secretly thinking about how to verify and utilize this discovery. The mixture of blood, sewage, and that unfamiliar fishy stench on his body became a unique medal of this hidden war.

【Living→Alive-W-00115-Wolf Beastkin-White Socks Officer (One-Eyed)】

He was the highest commander of this border base, a **white socks** officer exiled here yet still holding the power of life and death. His one eye was a symbol of his experiences, and possibly a memento of factional struggle. The pressure of losing the Empire's secret weapon made him furious and anxious; he feared punishment from above would fall on his own head. His method of executing G-30821 was both a punishment for an incompetent subordinate and a venting of his own fear and power. That elegant snap of his fingers when activating the **chip** backdoor program was filled with cold ritualism. His order for the large-scale **milking execution** of patrol soldiers was both an evasion of responsibility and a posture of "severe rectification" shown to his superiors, as well as using the lives of bottom-level soldiers to quench his own unease. The two **black socks soldiers** massaging his feet were the only power comfort he could enjoy at this moment. He lived in the crack between fear and anger, using even crueler violence to conceal his own vulnerability. He would report truthfully and pray for the Emperor's "mercy," but deep down, perhaps he knew that "mercy" for the exiled was always limited.

【Living→Alive-B-56005-Tiger Beastkin-Recycler/j56005】

Continuous high-intensity work left him exhausted. The mountain of corpses and the strong fishy stench of **semen** in the **recycling room** nearly overwhelmed him. In the mechanical repetitive labor, his initial fear was being replaced by numbness. However, when that **green socks** officer's corpse was delivered separately, a crack appeared in the numbness. Curiosity overpowered conditioning — he examined it carefully, lifted the **mask** to observe the face, and even bent down to sniff the scent of the other's chest. He was comparing, wondering: "Just what is the difference between the officers and myself?" This thought was extremely dangerous, touching upon a taboo possibly set by the **chip**. But at this moment, under extreme fatigue and sensory impact, that suppressed 15% self-awareness was trying to find answers from the corpse of a higher-ranking kindred. This was another subtle moment of his consciousness growing, only this time, the object of exploration was death itself.

【Living→Alive-B-55031/55032 etc.-Kik Soldiers-Search Teams】

They were **black socks soldiers** dispatched to various towns to carry out search and suppression missions. Under their superiors' fury and strict orders, they violently drove out townspeople, smashed houses, interrogated and beat them. High-intensity, hostility-filled work left them exhausted; they could only "sleep soundly" during patrol breaks or in their vehicles. They were both tools of inflicting fear and bearers of systemic pressure. Their atrocities against the townspeople were, to some extent, a twisted venting of their own situations — at least at this moment, they were the ones wielding violence. However, their late-night exhaustion and snoring in their dreams also exposed their pitiful essence as "consumables." Orders demanded they display the Empire's iron fist, while their bodies craved the most basic rest.

### Story Reflections

This chapter depicts the chain reaction triggered by the intrusion event, like a stone thrown into stagnant water; ripples spread from the resistance stronghold to the Imperial base, and then to the various towns. We see reactions at different levels: the fox beastkin leader's cautious lurking and planning; the **white socks** officer's fury and self-preserving massacre; the bottom-level **Kik Soldiers'** confused march to death and mechanical execution.

The scene of the large-scale execution of patrol soldiers displays the Empire's cold logic to the extreme. When a specific responsible party cannot be found, the Empire exterminates an entire group to compensate for the mistake, quell superior anger, and warn those who come after. According to estimates, the **black socks soldiers** responsible for last night's patrol numbered about a company (30-50 men); they didn't even understand why they were dying before being collectively **milk-executed** in the execution room. Their **semen** could not be recovered due to the order, flowing wasted and becoming squandered strategic resources. **j56005** alone in the **recycling room** worked from dawn until sunset before finishing processing the mountain of corpses; his exhaustion and numbness are the epitome of the Empire's efficient "processing" of life.

And **j56005's** exhausted labor in the **recycling room** and that moment of "study" form a meaningful contrast. On one side is the system's large-scale, high-efficiency destruction and recycling of life; on the other side is a tiny individual who, in a mountain of corpses and a sea of blood, begins to develop an instinctual curiosity about "difference" and "cause." This curiosity is the most primitive, faint resistance to the individuality the Empire attempts to erase.

The undeserved disaster suffered by the town residents is a microcosm of civilian life under Imperial rule. Their homes are violently broken into, their property destroyed, their dignity trampled — only because the Empire needs to display its power, needs to find a non-existent "traitor," or simply needs to vent the superior's anger.

When the weapons of the **watchtower** are fully deployed, aiming at the town they were supposed to "protect," the essence of Imperial rule is laid bare: that is not protection, but absolute control threatened by destruction. And the seeds of rebellion are precisely under this absolute control and violence, struggling to find cracks — such as that secret about **milking execution** and **semen**. The question at the fox beastkin's nose may be more destructive than any captured weapon.
