A shockwave abruptly appeared in the hero's secret base. After the equipment detected it, an alarm sounded. The defense system activated. Those energy beams were all offset by the Demon King's protective shield. Subsequently, the Demon King lightly stamped his foot, and electric light burst throughout the facility's interior. All equipment was paralyzed. Only the emergency lights in the corners emitted a dim glow...

By this time, the hero had been tortured to the point of being unable to move. He knew the Demon King had come! Their last encounter had ended in his defeat! Now I have returned! The civilization of the last era has been buried! Do you still remember what I said? That without me, they are destined to be destroyed! You can't protect these primitives now either."

Electric sparks erupted from the hero's body again, igniting his black-and-white fur. The smell of charring greatly pleased the Demon King. The only thing lacking was that this beastman hero was a tough bone!

He could still endure without making a single sound! The Demon King pouted unhappily. His tail slapped a crack into the ground, expressing his displeasure. His hand clenched into a fist, smashing the hero heavily onto the ground from mid-air!

A layer of dark golden patterns appeared on the black-and-white fur! This was a sealing method specially researched over the years for this day!

Then he threw out a sphere. That sphere rapidly expanded in mid-air, wrapping the hero inside. It then quickly returned to its original size, leaving the base just like that.

Another spatial fluctuation occurred. His Majesty the Emperor, the Demon King, appeared in his own room. Sitting on the throne, the energy of the entire base began converging on His Majesty the Emperor! The two spatial jumps had consumed quite a bit of his energy!
