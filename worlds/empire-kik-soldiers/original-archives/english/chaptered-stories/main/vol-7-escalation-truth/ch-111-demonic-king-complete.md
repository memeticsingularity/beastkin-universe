# Chapter 111 Demonic King Complete

The Demon King's power shone like the sun. The entire room also began to distort. As if entering another space. The hero's eyes were instantly blinded. When he opened his eyes again, the Demon King had already taken the box.

Like crushing a potato chip, he broke the seal and took out the key! It was a crown. The Demon King closed his eyes, took a deep breath, and said: "You really have bad taste! The fifth key actually has such a design!"

In the space, two arms grew out from behind the hero. His four arms pressed together to form the shape of a flower. A blue light ball condensed. This was the strongest attack he could currently launch!

Several runes appeared around him like starlight. That light ball instantly expanded several times! The Demon King didn't pay attention to these. Instead, he focused on absorbing the fifth key.

The crown began to decompose into light particles and was absorbed. The metal ring behind him changed. From one concentric circle, it became three concentric circles! Rotating in different directions. The armor on his body also changed...

Only the skirt armor on both sides, the chest armor on his chest, the ankle guards on his calves, and a pair of black ankle guards exposing his foot claws remained! A metal ring appeared on the domineering tiger head. Flowing electric light covered his entire body.

Black-red electric light formed a strange and terrifying armor. This key not only restored the Demon King's power. Some memories also poured into his mind. He just maintained the posture with his arms spread open and froze.

The hero's hope was destroyed. Seeing the Demon King frozen in place, he decided to seize this opportunity! With a loud shout, the energy ball shrank. The energy was compressed and turned into a shuttle-like shape. Aiming at the Demon King's crotch, he fired it out!

The attack arrived in the blink of an eye, dragging a constantly changing trajectory to the Demon King's front. The original protective shield shattered. It didn't play any role at all. Then, a violent explosion shockwave sent the hero flying. It turned out this space was very small. Probably a spherical space with a diameter of about ten meters.

The shockwave kept echoing in the space. The hero was also covered in wounds. He had returned to his normal state. And the Demon King remained motionless, maintaining his previous posture. The hero decided to burn his life and launch another attack!

Flames burned on his body. Blood seeped out from his body. Every drop of blood was extremely scorching hot, representing the hero's determination! Those blood also burst out with brilliant light points. At this time, the hero only had one belief: to completely eliminate the Demon King! This move was also a technique he had researched after deep consideration since waking up this time! It could carry out atomic-level destruction of the target!

At this time, the Empire's general attack also began. In all bases, overwhelming combat vehicles and fighter jets poured out. They bombed the remaining territory of the Wollraf Empire!

The entire continent burned. His Majesty the Emperor's order was saturation bombing! Then wait for orders. Only knowing that explosions continued and flames illuminated the night sky. Houses and city walls collapsed. Casualties were countless. In the face of absolute power, the king was still directing the remaining army to evacuate. A bomb fell beside him. Blue flames swallowed him and everything around him like a tornado...

The strange space disappeared. The hero's brilliant blood transformed into a giant sword! The tip of the sword pointed directly at the Demon King's heart. At this time, the Demon King opened his eyes! He knew the whereabouts of the last key. The three concentric rings behind him flew out from behind. They rapidly grew larger and formed a sphere, enveloping the hero and himself.

The rings began to rotate and turned into a golden sphere. The hero's life-risking sure-kill technique was also forcibly interrupted. That giant sword was decomposed by a suddenly appearing vortex. All the power returned to the hero's body!

The hero panicked. His originally dwindling lifespan suddenly came back! He didn't understand what the Demon King had done. Why did he do this? What was his purpose! Now, he could no longer eliminate the Demon King.

Several screens appeared in front of him. On them, cities were engulfed in flames. Monumental blue flame tornadoes raged. Wherever they went, there was only ruins.

"You bastard! You... I'm going to kill you!" The hero roared angrily! Blood was about to drip from his eyes. This planet had only developed today's civilization with great difficulty! He couldn't remember how the previous civilization had perished.

He only remembered that he had to prevent the Demon King from destroying this planet again. Now, his wish had not been realized either! And the Demon King showed a smile. Coming to his front, he said: "It seems you still haven't remembered? After I was sealed, they never would have thought!"

"You! What are you talking about!"

"I'm talking about the truth. Do you know why I'm not in a hurry to kill you and not in a hurry to find the last key?" The Demon King said, stroking the hero's face.

"As expected of my beloved general!" The Demon King's voice was like thunder. The hero couldn't accept it! He thought the Demon King was certain of victory and had gone crazy! Absolutely impossible! He was the hero! He was a hero! Absolutely impossible to be the Demon King's beloved general!

The hero watched as the lightning on the Demon King's body transformed into a set of dark red clothes. And that armor also grew, gradually enveloping the Demon King's entire body. Even his tail was wrapped inside. The Demon King continued to change.

"It seems you still haven't remembered! You need some means!" The Demon King said, choking the hero's neck. A stream of information projected from his eyes. The hero cried out in pain!

Time seemed to return to 300,000 years ago! That was above a pile of ruins. The Demon King had a sword inserted into his heart. Lying on the ground, barely alive. Around him was a large group of the Demon King's subordinates. They wore black tight jumpsuits and black boots. There were armor modules on their chests and crotches. It's just that the crotch modules had fallen off. There was a large white patch on the crotch of each corpse. Under the masks, they all had expressions of fear.

The Demon King looked at the sword in the hero's chest, obstructing his vision. His power was being drained by the sword. It didn't stop until midnight. A huge amount of power gathered on the sword. The hero realized that this couldn't eliminate the Demon King. So he sacrificed his own sword. That sword split into six parts. The Demon King's power was also dispersed among them.

Only the sword tip was still in the Demon King's chest. Six objects floated beside the hero. The hero used his own blood to seal them. Then, with a wave of his hand, they flew away in different directions.

Behind the hero was a city. The buildings reached into the clouds. It was a city capable of accommodating a million people. There were still 6 such cities on the planet. When the Demon King was born, he led his subordinates to destroy two of them.

The scholars and warriors of the other four cities did their best to cultivate a hero to kill the Demon King. After several months of battle, all the Demon King's subordinates were eliminated. Finally, in this wasteland, the Demon King was pierced through the chest by the hero's sword!

The Demon King's power was sealed separately in the 6 parts transformed from the hero's sword. At this time, the Demon King's body was dissipating. Only a golden tiger head remained.

"This way, you still can't kill me! Did they think this would succeed? Or do you think you can succeed?" The Demon King, who only had his head left, mocked the hero.

This hero, who was chosen by all beastmen, grabbed the Demon King's head and looked at him levelly, saying: "It has already succeeded! We will seal and banish you from this planet. The civilization here will enter a new stage. Go to the distant starry sky to find new territory!"

"Don't dream! Without my leadership, this civilization will destroy itself within a thousand years! You will also be killed! Because without the Demon King to set you off! You! Will! Be! Betrayed!" The Demon King opened his mouth and mocked, as if it were a prophecy!

After seeing this memory, the hero shook his head vigorously. All of this was the Demon King's memory. He didn't remember how he fell asleep back then. But he was suspicious. Being in the Demon King's spherical space, he could tamper with everything. So the hero didn't believe this memory...

"You really have a stubborn temper! There's no need to lie to you in your current state! Do you know why absorbing those miscellaneous soldiers' semen can restore your power? Hahahaha!" The Demon King said, pouring another memory into him.

In a transparent cultivation pod, the hero was still a cub at this time. A golden tiger beastman wearing glasses was recording the data of this cub. This golden tiger beastman had a serious face. He looked like a scholar.

**Chapter 111 END**
