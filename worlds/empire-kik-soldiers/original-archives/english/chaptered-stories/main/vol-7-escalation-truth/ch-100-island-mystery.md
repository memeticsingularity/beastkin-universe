# Chapter 100 Island Mystery

His Majesty the Emperor's interest was piqued again. He walked to the hero's side, raised his foot, and doodled randomly on his body with his fingers. This ticklish sensation made the hero's head spin. It's just that with his power sealed, he had no time to research a way to break it.

Now his mind was full of that craving for primitive instinct. His legs twitched, but he just wouldn't open his eyes. His Majesty the Emperor suddenly straightened up, and an energy whip appeared in his hand. He whipped the hero fiercely!

No whip marks were left on the hero's body, but the pain was no less! After dozens of lashes, the hero's physical stats had dropped below those of a Black Socks soldier!

At this time, Hu Meng had arrived at the main base. A Blue Socks soldier was already waiting on the landing pad. Hu Meng only knew it was His Majesty the Emperor's order, so he followed that Blue Socks soldier toward the starship.

He already knew His Majesty's order! The Empire was going to launch an attack to completely devour the Wolflav Empire! On the way, Hu Meng especially wanted to bring a team to fight for the Empire. This was a great opportunity to earn merit. Thinking this, he arrived outside His Majesty the Emperor's room. Hu Meng quickly gathered his thoughts and ordered the dinosaur guard to keep watch outside the door!

As the door opened, Hu Meng also quickly adjusted his state. The door only opened halfway when His Majesty the Emperor's voice came: "Come in!"

Hu Meng walked in sideways, and the half-open door closed. There was a familiar smell in the room. Out of the corner of his eye, he saw a beastman lying on the ground. Hu Meng knelt there, waiting for His Majesty's orders...

"You're here! It seems you didn't see my speech. I captured the hero and gave him the greatest humiliation!" His Majesty the Emperor said, sitting on the throne with his back to Hu Meng.

Hu Meng still knelt there, not daring to make a sound, not even daring to look to the side. Hu Meng also knew what the smell in the room was—it was the smell of semen! His Majesty said he had captured the hero and given him punishment and humiliation! That definitely meant the hero had been dealt with!

At this time, His Majesty laughed and said, his majestic voice carrying over: "Hahahaha! As expected of my little tiger, it's exactly what you think! At the Hive Base, you were injured because of this. Now you can take your revenge!"

Hu Meng didn't expect His Majesty the Emperor to still remember his injury. The hero was indeed not easy to deal with! In that encounter that day, he had no way to stop his attack. He was even hit in a vital spot and injured...

Now was the time for revenge, so Hu Meng stood up and slowly approached the hero. After getting close, he saw a perfect fat-over-muscle physique, the standard black-and-white fur color of a bear beastman!

His body was covered with charred wound marks, but there was no bleeding. He lay there, barely alive. Hu Meng couldn't believe this was the hero. He completely lacked the aura of that day and looked worse off than a civilian!

"In this room, I'll give you one chance to ravage him as you please! Then you'll be given a new mission!" His Majesty the Emperor's voice came again. At the same time, the throne's seat turned around to watch how Hu Meng would act!

His Majesty the Emperor was very much looking forward to how the little black tiger would act. If he assaulted the hero, it would only show how foolish he was! The hero's current impotent rage didn't mean he wouldn't retaliate. If he chose to ask for instructions from himself, then his little life could be saved.

The hero was not very broad-minded. If Hu Meng laid a hand on him, he would likely be jerked off to death by the hero in the cruelest way in the future! Hu Meng thought for a moment, then knelt down to His Majesty the Emperor and said: "Your Majesty, this is your prey! This lowly one dares not!"

Hu Meng knew His Majesty hadn't issued the order yet; this was the result of his brainstorming. His Majesty the Emperor was very satisfied. He walked to Hu Meng's side and stroked his head, saying: "Very good. From now on, the work of guarding and interrogating the hero is entrusted to you! Two teams of Blue Socks soldiers will obey your dispatch. The location is in a building near the main tower of the main base! It's a prison, just not yet operational. The hero is its first prisoner!"

After Hu Meng accepted the order, His Majesty the Emperor commanded him to lie on the ground. He reached into his clothes and stroked his stomach. He could still feel his abs; he had actually gained quite a bit of weight recently! He should be even more delicious now!

Hu Meng made whimpering sounds from His Majesty's stroking. Finally, he patted his big head and left. The order just given had already taken effect. Blue Socks soldiers were immediately waiting outside!

Hu Meng only got up and straightened his clothes after seeing His Majesty disappear. He personally hoisted the hero off the ground and onto his shoulder, walking out of His Majesty's room. Only then did he hand him over to the Blue Socks soldiers under his command. Those soldiers used special collar shackles to lock the hero. A reaction occurred between the shackles, and a pure black jumpsuit instantly covered his entire body.

This outfit was specially prepared for the hero by His Majesty the Emperor! It could prevent him from accumulating energy. The shackles on his limbs could give him the most stimulating torture! That jumpsuit also had an auto-repair function!

It's just that the hero woke up when Hu Meng hoisted him. A very faint aura! That was the Demon King's aura. It seemed this tiger beastman had already drunk the Demon King's blood! No wonder he felt different when he first saw him! His chance to escape should also lie with him!

So he just needed to provoke him, make him lose his reason, and he could seize the opportunity! Anyway, these Kik soldiers weren't smart; they were just like the Demon King's subordinates of the past!

Hu Meng walked on one side, while the two teams of Blue Socks soldiers escorted this special-class prisoner to the deepest part of the prison. Even the cage was specially made. Hu Meng received the order to sit outside the cage and watch this prisoner!

The two teams of Blue Socks soldiers were on standby at any time. Outside the gate, a large number of Green Socks and Black Socks soldiers were on standby! Hu Meng just sat on the chair watching, while his Blue Socks subordinates massaged his legs and licked his socks. Hu Meng was so comfortable he was about to shoot!

His Majesty the King had already arrived at the starship in orbit. Those Kik soldiers were quite clever. They quickly formed up to receive the inspection. His Majesty the Emperor didn't even look at them; after all, they were just tools! Hu Meng and White Wolf were very precious tools and toys!

He went straight to the hangar on the starship and boarded a shuttle, flying straight toward that small island on the planet. Passing through the atmosphere, adjusting attitude and direction, skimming over the ocean, he arrived near the small island.

Lush vegetation covered it; it looked like a perfect place for seclusion! The island had fresh water and no large beasts; it was simply perfect! The nearby whirlpool could prevent many ships from coming! After landing on the beach, His Majesty the Emperor floated five centimeters above the ground. He floated toward the center of the island...
