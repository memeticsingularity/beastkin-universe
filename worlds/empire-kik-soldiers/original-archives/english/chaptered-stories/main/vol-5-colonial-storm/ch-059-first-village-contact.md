# Chapter 59 First Village Contact

After warming up, he pointed to five Green Socks soldiers and five Black Socks soldiers. He had them pick up their weapons and spar with him. After all, he hadn't experienced real combat many times himself.

Finally, he threw the spear away and said, "Get up now! Do you know why I picked you guys!"

The ten Kik soldiers stood at attention and shook their heads. Hu Meng walked up to them and explained, "Because your morning training results were all at the bottom! If your results are still not up to standard in three days, you will be dealt with!"

The ten subordinates' pupils shook, and they quickly replied, "Sir, please give us another chance! We... we promise to meet the requirements!"

"Then I'll be watching your results! Because if you don't meet the standard, you'll become food for the beast! If you perform well, you won't have to! Understood?" Hu Meng explained.

Although the Black Socks soldiers would eventually become food for the beast, giving them a little hope wasn't a bad idea. As for the Green Socks soldiers, don't think you're safe! The entire empire is His Majesty's plaything.

A row of cultivation chambers rose from the center of the training room. All soldiers entered the chambers in an orderly manner. After the doors closed, the lights in the cultivation chambers went out. At the same time, the same situation was happening in various areas of the starship! Unexpectedly, the starship started so suddenly, completely outside of today's plan!

The snow leopard deputy hurriedly had Hu Meng enter the cultivation chamber as well. Hu Meng ordered him to go in first. Then he entered an empty cultivation chamber. The lights of the entire starship went out one by one, and the huge engine nozzles emitted light...

The huge starship began to adjust its direction and slowly sailed out of the transfer space station in space. This space station was composed of more than a dozen bases distributed across the continent. Bases that hadn't flown up would remain in their original areas to maintain the empire's rule!

As the starship slowly flew out of the port, the 830-meter-long starship stopped. The other five 600-meter-long starships also flew to their predetermined positions. Looking at the star system's sun with its back to it, it started!

Distortion appeared in front of the fleet, and a huge spatial vortex emerged. Objects around it all twisted. The bows of all the starships also began to stretch and twist, slowly being sucked into the vortex. As the huge nozzles fully lit up, they then turned into a beam of light and disappeared into the vortex.

In a peaceful star system, next to a vibrant planet, space twisted for a moment. A starship appeared abruptly, followed closely by five slightly smaller starships.

The main ship was pitch black, whether it was the cockpit, the command room, or other places. Only rows and rows of cultivation chambers flickered with faint fluorescent lights.

Emergency indicator lights and consoles everywhere emitted regular glows, indicating that all systems were operating normally. The fleet of ships stopped in orbit, while the beastmen laboring on the planet looked up at the sudden black shadows.

Originally, they were all working, hunting, and children were playing. Suddenly, a huge vibration blew away the clouds in the sky. It also made those beastmen's heads buzz for a long time. After recovering, several black dots appeared in the sky, and then the black dots grew larger and larger.

They landed at the foot of a distant mountain. Their pure black appearance gave off a mysterious and fearful feeling. Even the bravest warrior in the village didn't dare to approach!

The armor plates on the starship retracted, sunlight shone in, and the red emergency lights stopped flashing. All the cultivation chambers lit up, and the doors opened one by one. The Kik soldiers who had taken refuge woke up and walked out.

Some physically weaker Black Socks soldiers directly knelt on the floor, retching! Hu Meng was just a little dizzy. He twisted his neck and moved all his joints.
