# Chapter 60 Local Soldier Breeding

Those Kik soldiers immediately formed a square formation. Hu Meng and the deputy stood to the side, awaiting His Majesty the Emperor's orders. Before long, a huge projection of His Majesty appeared, his majestic appearance intimidating all the Kik soldiers on the ship. In the projection, there were also more than 20 highest-level White Socks soldiers!

His Majesty's projection said excitedly, "Loyal subjects of the Empire! Now is the time to show your worth! After sunset, Hu Meng will lead the vanguard! First, take the nearby village as a starting point. The Kik soldiers on the other starships are responsible for building the base! The mountain next to it is a natural alloy material!"

After the call ended, all the Kik soldiers on the starships got moving. The bottom hatch of the starship opened, and countless engineering vehicles and machinery slowly drove to the open space at the foot of the mountain.

They moved like worker ants, their tracks rolling over the fragrant land, leaving gullies. Rumbling sounds resounded throughout the foot of the mountain, and huge machinery flew to the open space to begin construction!

Hu Meng led his team onto the battle vehicle. At this moment, he was in a vehicle with a humanoid upper body and tracked lower body propulsion. After removing his mask, he wore a black metal ring on his head. The ring controlled and connected to the machinery of the entire unit, commanding his entire body through brainwaves while sitting in the seat with his eyes closed, motionless.

His brain processed a massive amount of information. Without the assistance of the main ship's system, his brain would have exploded! The snow leopard deputy guarded outside the cockpit. In front of him was a machine gun that would be responsible for eliminating all enemies to protect the officer's safety!

Other Kik soldiers mobilized according to their assigned tasks. Nearby animals were directly startled away, and birds collectively took flight and fled far away. Battle vehicles drove toward the village in groups. The people working in the fields saw all this and were so frightened they sat on the ground, unable to get up for a long time.

What happened outside the village quickly reached the elder village chief! The village chief had all the women and children hide in the cave behind the village! Don't come out until the signal is given!

Then he led all the adult male beastmen to stand outside the village. They carried weapons and came to the village entrance in groups. At this time, Hu Meng's vanguard had already arrived at the village entrance.

Hu Meng ordered the vehicle to drive to a stop 100 meters ahead! Three squads disembarked from the battle vehicles and awaited orders! At the same time, he removed the metal ring from his head, picked up his mask, put it on, pushed the door open, and walked out of the cockpit.

"You are responsible for operating the weapons for deterrence!"

After leaving these words, Hu Meng left. He got off the battle vehicle, and the three squads behind him also followed. Hu Meng, holding a Gatling gun, shouted through the thought-wave communicator: "You primitive beastmen! I am the Empire's vanguard! I come with His Majesty's grace to occupy your planet! Guide you toward prosperity!"

A Black Socks soldier threw out a similar device. A young fox beastman picked it up and handed it to the elder. The elder followed suit and put it on his ear. Hu Meng helplessly repeated himself. At the same time, he glared at that Black Socks soldier. At this moment, a squad leader walked out, covered that Black Socks soldier's mouth, and dragged him away.

"Our village lives in peace and doesn't compete with the world. What exactly are you here to do! We don't need your help! Your machines have already destroyed many things!" The village chief replied neither humbly nor arrogantly.

Hu Meng looked at that fox beastman elder. He still didn't understand what consequences his answer would bring! At this moment, the chip received His Majesty's order. It required Hu Meng not to harm these villagers! After all, they still needed them to spread the Empire's glory!

Helplessly, Hu Meng could only say a lot of things, while pointing to the troops behind him! Let him know what the consequences of non-compliance were! At the same time, he also told his subordinates not to open fire without orders!

Actually, it was just having the deputy fire a shot at the open space in the distance. After a 10-meter crater appeared, the elder became honest. Then he invited Hu Meng into his house. During this time, a team of Black Socks soldiers followed Hu Meng, standing guard outside the village chief's house.

The village chief entertained Hu Meng with dissatisfaction in his eyes. He sat cross-legged on a cushion. His weapon was placed on the ground. He took out a projection device to display the Empire's technology. And those magnificent base buildings!

The elder also explained the situation of his own village. He received this Lord Hu Meng's guarantee. He would not harm his villagers, provided that they obeyed the Empire's rule!

All this information was also transmitted to His Majesty through the chip. His Majesty the Emperor was still very satisfied! After all, if the first village was conquered without force, it would become the Empire's strength! Outside the regular Kik soldiers, they would form a localized team!

At the same time, His Majesty also noticed the furnishings in the village chief's room. Those things were technological creations from the previous era. Fortunately, they hadn't discovered the true purpose of these items. So he had Hu Meng confiscate them for research!

After receiving the latest order, Hu Meng ordered the squad outside the door to bring the prepared tools. As some rewards, he gave them to them to help them learn the Empire's language and writing. Teach them some life skills and make some weapons to help them hunt and farm better.

Hu Meng didn't understand why His Majesty wanted to do this. He only knew to obey orders. Actually, His Majesty really liked this kind of game. Conquering a planet gave him a great sense of accomplishment.

It wasn't until dark that Hu Meng had his subordinates leave with the confiscated items. The village chief was relieved. Taking advantage of the night, he told the villagers about the Empire's intentions. Those villagers' eyes lit up with joy, some with suspicion, and some with anger. It was normal for opinions to be divided.

Hu Meng gave them three days to consider. This was also His Majesty's intention. On the battle vehicle, Hu Meng looked at the Black Socks soldier who had made a mistake. He was a leopard beastman, and what had happened earlier was because he had been a beat slow.

He had already been punished by the squad leader. Hu Meng looked at him, tied up, and said nothing. The Black Socks soldier said, "Sir! I know I was wrong! Please don't jerk me off, sir!"

"Rest assured! I won't jerk you off for now! If there's a next time, I'll strip your military socks off!" After Hu Meng finished speaking, he kicked him out.

Before long, they returned to the vicinity of the starship. The place had changed! The flat ground was like a mirror, and preliminary facilities had already been assembled. After returning to the base, Hu Meng began to report back. His Majesty's evaluation of him was very high! Because he didn't open fire right from the start!

**Chapter 60 END**
