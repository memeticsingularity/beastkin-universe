# Chapter 64 Aerial Bombardment

Hu Meng waited for the sun to rise. All his subordinates were ready to go. His Majesty suddenly changed his mind, watching several fighter jets ascend and fly into the distance. Kik soldiers in black tight-fitting jumpsuits climbed into the cockpits. After connecting the sensors, they took off vertically...

They didn't know why His Majesty the Emperor changed the plan. A major event had occurred a few days ago in the border city of the Wolflav Empire. A pitch-black icosahedron flew straight into the courtyard of the city lord.

It emitted a shockwave that knocked out all the servants and soldiers in the courtyard! A projection of a golden tiger beastman appeared in the courtyard. A golden ring shone behind him. He was bare-chested, looking down at all living beings, and spoke in the continent's common language: "You lowly servants! The Empire bestows upon you evolution and rebirth! You have three days to consider whether to submit or be destroyed!"

After that, the black regular icosahedron disengaged from its suspended state. The city lord was a tall crocodile beastman. His brass armor clattered as he moved. He ran swiftly to the courtyard, followed by guards carrying weapons. They struck that black object with their weapons. After a series of clanging sounds, not a single scratch was left.

The city lord immediately sent someone to send a message to the royal capital. A golden bird ascended and sliced through the sky toward the royal capital. That afternoon, His Majesty the King sent a command via a golden messenger! Don't pay any attention to that so-called Empire! If war breaks out, the entire army shall fight!

A wolf beastman minister wearing glasses picked up the letter on the ground and examined it carefully. Then he loudly dissuaded His Majesty the King: "Your Majesty! The description in the letter of that huge shadow! Doesn't Your Majesty have any impression! Think of the murals in the temple. Perhaps we should prepare for the worst! It's not too late to awaken that hero now!"

His Majesty the King stopped, turned around, and shouted: "Prime Minister Gulaf! That is an ancient legend! Besides, whether there is a hero in the temple is still a question. I have already ordered City Lord Kaka to prepare for battle. Nearby cities are also sending support there! There is no need to worry."

After receiving the order, City Lord Kaka dragged his big tail to the courtyard. He picked up his long-handled battle axe and heavily chopped at the object. He said loudly: "Now, on behalf of His Majesty the King, I declare war on you, invader! Come if you have the guts! Foolish enemy!"

After speaking, he struck again. The sphere received a response and the shockwave was emitted again! City Lord Kaka blocked it. This was the reason His Majesty the Emperor changed the plan!

That crocodile beastman's axe-swinging strength was immense. Calculations showed that even a Blue Socks soldier's armor couldn't withstand it! The strange power he carried could interfere with the power of energy weapons to a certain extent! If they fought him head-on, Hu Meng would suffer heavy losses!

The beast's abilities would come in handy. But after a bombing, how much viable force would remain to fight the Empire to the end! At the same time, key detection equipment was also under development. Obtaining the key was the most important thing!

The key could allow all his memories to awaken! Since he woke up, he had gradually created the current Empire. It seemed there was a reason why he was guided to this planet!

Thinking of this, he watched as the fighter jets had already launched their attack! Looking at the soldiers on the city walls wearing armor. Their physical strength was indeed impressive. The long spear weapons in their hands emitted energy that hit the fighter jets. A pale yellow shield neutralized it, while the fighter jet counterattacked, opening its bomb bay. Two missiles flew toward the city wall. A huge explosion accompanied by a mushroom cloud blew a large hole in the city wall!

The soldiers on the city wall fell down and were buried by debris, getting injured. The enemy's morale was in chaos. The fighter jet flew over the city wall and dropped bombs inside the city for bombing!

The sounds of explosions, screams, and cries drowned out the entire city. All kinds of suffering enveloped this city. After the thick smoke, there were cries everywhere. Blood, dirt, and rubble mixed into eerie colors.

The surviving beastmen searched everywhere for their loved ones. Looking at the collapsed and burning houses, they let out angry, sad cries and roars! The black giant bird in the sky was the god of death in their eyes! It was a demon!

The soldiers in the city were also rescuing their people under City Lord Kaka's command. Hu Meng also received the order for the troops to go all out to reach the designated location.

After a round of bombing, there were ruins everywhere. It was hard to tell if loved ones were alive or dead. There was also the smell of charred corpses. The city lord was extremely angry! He didn't expect them to be so cruel!

So he gathered all soldiers who could still move and brought out giant crossbows to respond. The Empire's fighter jets had completed the bombing, received the order, and began to return. Hu Meng's troops were also arriving soon. The originally three-day journey was just a figure of speech. The village chief also hadn't been to that city in a long time...

Moreover, he had no idea what kind of weapon an Empire battle vehicle was! At full speed, it only took a day to arrive. Hu Meng received the latest intelligence while in the battle vehicle. The enemy's strength could easily kill Kik soldiers in close combat!

All combat must avoid close-quarters fighting. Hu Meng was shocked! This was unbelievable! The bodies of Kik soldiers were enhanced. He didn't expect the native beastmen here to be so terrifying!

Seeing the returning fighter jets, he was relieved. The battle vehicle used artillery to blast away the obstacles ahead. This also laid a good foundation for road construction in the future. It wasn't until late at night that they saw the distant city.

The city walls were damaged there, and the thick smoke had dissipated. City Lord Kaka, in his mansion, was burning with anger. He watched his city turn into this state. In just one encounter, he was left completely helpless! He had great skills but no way to use them!

A scout ran over and said: "City Lord! Several huge battle vehicles have assembled outside the city! It looks like the enemy's second wave of troops!"

City Lord Kaka looked at that black sphere, wishing he could bite it to pieces! Then he quickly walked toward the outside of the city, passing through the heart-wrenching city to the outside. The huge battle vehicle on the opposite side shot out lights that illuminated that open space.

Hu Meng got off the battle vehicle, put on a translator, and said: "Foolish and ignorant enemies! Today is His Majesty's mercy that has left you time to repent! Submit or be completely destroyed!"

Hu Meng was just saying this. After all, the latest order still required them to take out the old components! Because His Majesty needed those items!

"Damned invaders! As a city lord, I will never surrender! You cruel fellows!" City Lord Kaka said as he swung his axe. That visible stream of air left a ditch in the ground.

In the blink of an eye, it was in front of Hu Meng. With a ding, Hu Meng grabbed a Black Socks soldier and threw him over. That Black Socks soldier didn't have time to react before being torn apart by the air blast.

**Chapter 64 END**
