# Chapter 3: Black Panther · The Proud One in the Shadows

## Scene-1: The Patience of a Hunter

Mu Mo's body relaxed on the cold ground, breathing becoming even. The wooden badge was clutched tightly to his chest, its warm texture transmitting through the uniform fabric. Memetic Singularity squatted beside him, reaching out to gently straighten the German Shepherd beastkin's somewhat messy uniform collar, the movement as meticulous as caring for a sleeping child. After doing this, he stood up.

The warehouse air seemed to settle into some intangible tension because of Xiong Zai and Mu Mo's consecutive "incidents." Memetic Singularity melted into the night like a drop of water, moving along the shadows cast by the shelves. His steps were extremely light, the rubber edge of his military boots touching the ground first, then slowly pressing the entire sole down, avoiding any friction sound that might be captured by sharp hearing. Occasionally, he would stop, back against a cold metal shelf pillar, listening sideways.

Distant rhythmic patrol footsteps could be heard—the wolf brothers on their cross patrol. Further away, faint sounds of metal doors opening and closing, possibly the wild boar Zhu Gang moving near the west side door. Airflow in the high ventilation ducts emitted continuous, low hissing sounds. All these sounds formed a three-dimensional web of sound in the empty warehouse.

Memetic Singularity needed to find the next node in this web.

According to information provided by Xiong Zai and Mu Mo, there was still a lone "high observer" in the warehouse—a black panther beastkin. He didn't cluster with others, liked staying in shadows and on top of shelves. Such a role was the hardest to deal with, because he had the best vision and highest alertness, while also being agile; once a mistake was made it might alert the enemy and cause the entire plan to collapse.

Memetic Singularity didn't act rashly. He chose a relatively high shelf in the central warehouse area, using the metal ladder welded to the side of the shelf, climbing upward silently. Each ladder rung was first gently tested with his palm, confirming no looseness or rust that might make noise, before shifting his weight up. During climbing, his body pressed tightly against the ladder, minimizing silhouette exposure.

Reaching the top of the shelf, the view suddenly opened up. Here was about six meters from the ground, stacked with some lightweight but large cardboard boxes, providing good cover. Memetic Singularity found a gap between two huge cardboard boxes, squeezed in sideways, and squatted down. From this position, he could observe a relatively large area below, especially the intersection of several main passages, and the metal beams near the roof further away—those were the areas the black panther was most likely to move in.

He adjusted his breathing, let his heartbeat steady, then began patiently waiting. Eyes slowly sweeping below. Light formed alternating stripes of brightness and darkness between shelves, dust slowly floating in the beams. Time passed second by second, each second stretched by extreme focus.

Below, two gray figures swept past one after another—the wolf brothers conducting another cross patrol. The distance between them was maintained well, able to respond to each other while covering a wider range. Memetic Singularity memorized their rhythm.

After about ten minutes, the wolf brothers patrolling the east and south sides briefly converged at an open area in the center of the warehouse. They spoke a few sentences in low voices, voices kept very low, Memetic Singularity could only capture a few fuzzy syllables. Then, one of the wolves (probably the younger brother Wolf Jie) walked toward the west side door direction where the wild boar Zhu Gang was guarding, possibly for routine communication or shift-change signal. While the older brother Wolf Xun continued his east patrol route, figure gradually disappearing behind a row of tall shelves.

Right at this alternating gap, in the warehouse southeast corner where huge wooden packaging crates were stacked, from the steel beam shadows near the roof, came an extremely faint, stretch-like "click" sound, so light it was almost swallowed by environmental noise.

Memetic Singularity's gaze instantly locked onto that direction.

On the steel beam, a slender, vigorous black figure was facing away from him, crouching on the beam. Even in the dim light, that black fur almost melted into the shadows, only the smooth muscle contours slightly reflecting environmental light, and that naturally drooping, tip-slightly-curled tail, indicating his presence. He would occasionally turn his head, amber eyes sweeping below in the dim light, ears keenly capturing all abnormal sounds.

This was the black panther Mo Ying.

Memetic Singularity didn't act immediately. He was observing the other's posture, breathing rhythm, areas where attention was focused. Mo Ying looked relaxed, but that relaxation was the loose state unique to predators, ready to explode at any moment. His tail swayed gently, the tip occasionally unconsciously drawing small circles, showing he was currently in a relatively calm monitoring state, not high alert.

Opportunity needed to be created, and also needed a little bit of "just right" push.

Memetic Singularity closed his eyes, concentrating his will. He didn't need supernatural power, only needed a little tilt in probability, a reasonable, natural "accident."

Will flowing.

Below, Wolf Jie had just left the central area, walking toward the west side door. Mo Ying's gaze seemed to follow Wolf Jie's figure for a moment, then turned back to his own responsible area.

Right at this moment—

Directly below Mo Ying, at the edge of the area where huge wooden packaging crates were stacked, a hard cardboard box full of some lightweight plastic parts, because of long-term stacking and possible moisture damage, the bottom edge
**just so happened to** begin softening, tearing. Right at the instant when Mo Ying's gaze shifted away, attention slightly dispersed—

"Crash—!"

The cardboard box bottom completely ruptured, dozens of plastic parts pouring down, like a small waterfall, crashing onto the ground below and nearby wooden crates, emitting dense and crisp "ding ding dang dang" impact sounds and rolling sounds, unusually harsh in the silent warehouse!

The instant the sound rang out, the black figure on the steel beam instantly tensed. Mo Ying's ears suddenly turned toward the sound source, amber pupils contracting into vertical lines in the dim light, the entire body entering a ready-to-pounce posture from relaxed crouching. His head lowered, gaze sharp as knives, staring dead at the scattered parts and that broken cardboard box below.

A few meters away, Wolf Jie who had just walked some distance was also startled by this sudden sound, suddenly turning around, hand on weapon, vigilantly looking toward the sound direction. "What's going on?" he said in a low voice, voice carrying a trace of being disturbed unpleasantly.

Mo Ying didn't respond, his full attention focused on judging the nature of this "accident." Natural damage? Or man-made interference? His nose twitched slightly, trying to capture abnormalities in the dust and plastic smells.

Below, Wolf Jie seeing Mo Ying had no reaction, shouted again: "Mo Ying? What's the situation on your side?"

Mo Ying only then shook his head very slightly, indicating he was also observing. His tail stopped swaying, tightly pressed against the steel beam, body pressed lower, like a fully drawn bow.

Wolf Jie seeing he didn't give warning, relaxed slightly, muttering: "Damn, scared me." But he still walked a few steps toward the scattered parts direction, preparing to check. "You keep watching, I'll see what's up with this box."

This was exactly the brief separation window Memetic Singularity needed. Wolf Jie's attention was attracted by the ground abnormality, while the high Mo Ying, though vigilant, had part of his attention drawn by this "accident" and his companion's action below.

Memetic Singularity moved.

## Scene-2: The Proud Observer

Mo Ying didn't like noise.

Especially this kind of sudden, meaningless noise. It broke the oppressive yet regular silence of the warehouse, like a stone thrown into a calm pond. His ears flattened backward slightly, expressing inner displeasure. Amber eyes quickly swept across the scattered parts below, the broken cardboard box edge, and surrounding shadows that might hide danger.

**Natural aging? Moisture damage?**
He judged. The tear at the cardboard box edge looked irregular, not like being cut by a sharp tool. The trajectory of parts scattering also followed free fall. Might just be coincidence. But at this juncture, any coincidence was worth suspecting—Xiong Zai was missing, Mu Mo also lost contact, though the captain hadn't officially reported yet, but Mo Ying's own observation had already detected abnormalities. Those two guys, one silly one quiet, but neither was the type to leave their post without permission.

His gaze followed Wolf Jie who was checking below. This gray wolf younger brother was always a bit rash, checking movements big and open, not meticulous enough. Mo Ying's heart passed over a trace of faint disdain.
*Idiot. If there really is an ambush, you squatting like that with your back to open ground is the best target.*

But he didn't voice a reminder. This wasn't his duty. His duty was to monitor high places and shadow areas, provide warning and potential rapid support, not guide ground patrols on how to act. Moreover, he didn't want to have unnecessary communication with these "colleagues." They were too noisy, too stupid, or too... uncomfortable. Like that hyena Gou Sheng, every time being swept by those slender eyes, Mo Ying had a feeling of being dirtied.

He preferred solitude. Up high, in shadows, everything was clear and controllable. He could see others' actions, while others found it hard to notice him. This made him feel safe, and also conformed to his proud nature. Beastshield this job to him was just a place to play to his strengths and exchange for survival resources. He didn't care about the specific content of missions, only cared whether they could be completed cleanly and efficiently, then return to his own corner. Those "cleaning" missions, he executed efficiently and coldly, like dealing with a pile of lifeless obstacles. There was nothing wrong with this, the strong prey on the weak, natural law. He was just better at playing the "strong" side.

Below, Wolf Jie had roughly finished checking, stood up, patted the dust off his hands. "Seems like the box just aged, nothing special." He shouted upward, "Mo Ying, you keep watching, I'll go back to patrol route."

Mo Ying didn't respond, only nodded very slightly, though he knew Wolf Jie probably couldn't see. His gaze turned back to farther areas, ears turning, capturing other sounds in the warehouse. Ventilation duct hiss, distant faint footsteps (possibly Wolf Xun or others), and... an extremely faint, almost nonexistent... fabric rubbing sound?

His ears instantly turned toward the approximate direction the sound came from—the shelf area to his right rear. The sound was too light, light enough to make him suspect if it was an illusion. Maybe a rat? Or just shelf slight deformation caused by temperature change?

He maintained absolute stillness of his body, only ears and eyes slowly moving, searching for any abnormality. **Too quiet.**
He suddenly realized. After the noise just now, the warehouse fell into an even deeper silence, even the distant patrol footsteps seemed to become fuzzy. This silence made the hairs on the back of his neck slightly stand up.

Then, he heard—not a sound, but a feeling. An extremely faint change in air flow, coming from behind him, carrying a tiny temperature difference.

**Behind!**

Beast instinct sent out a sharp alarm at this moment! Mo Ying's waist twisted with astonishing flexibility and force, the entire person sliding half a foot to the left like a boneless black shadow, while that muscular, smooth, short-black-fur-covered slender arm carried wind-breaking sound, sweeping backhand toward the direction he sensed the threat!

Fast! Ruthless! Accurate! The black panther's reaction speed was displayed to the fullest at this moment!

His arm swept through empty air.

The anticipated collision didn't happen. The attacker was more cunning than he anticipated, or faster. This thought had just risen in Mo Ying's mind when his crotch transmitted an unimaginable, soul-shaking pain!

**Wha—?!**

He completely didn't see how the attack arrived. Only knew that at the instant he twisted to dodge, attention fully focused on blocking and counterattacking the upper body attack, his lower body—that most vulnerable, most private part—was hit by something hard (knee? elbow?) with terrible force and precision, striking up hard! Through the tight military-green shorts fabric, that soft organ was directly hit, squeezed, as if about to shatter!

"Ugh—!"

A suppressed-to-the-extreme, mixed-with-extreme-pain-and-fury muffled groan burst from deep in his throat. That fierce backhand sweep instantly lost all force, softly brushing past the attacker's neck side. His originally vigorous-as-cheetah body suddenly went rigid, all muscle strength disappearing like a receding tide, replaced by a devastating paralysis and pain spreading from his crotch throughout his body. He could no longer maintain balance on the steel beam, body uncontrollably sliding downward in paralysis.

During the falling process, he felt himself being caught by a pair of arms, both falling together from the beam more than two meters high. Back hitting a pile of relatively soft abandoned burlap bags below, emitting a dull "thud," most of the impact absorbed by the bags. But he almost couldn't feel the falling impact, all senses occupied by that continuous, sharp pain from his crotch. That hand causing all this still held his vital point firmly and steadily, not relaxing in the slightest.

He was pressed into the burlap bags, back against rough burlap, facing dim light and the attacker's fuzzy outline. A palm quickly covered his mouth—lips very thin, touch slightly cool. The other hand, that hand bringing hellish pain, used just-right force, maintaining suppression and control of his crotch.

Mo Ying lay under the attacker, body somewhat soft from the sudden blow and fall, but not completely losing resistance consciousness. He could feel his whole body tense muscles trembling slightly, that was the manifestation of pain and trying to gather strength. Chest heaving violently, the black tight uniform clearly outlining the lines of his chest and abdominal muscles—not particularly burly, but each piece refined and solid, full of predator explosive power. His slender legs clad in white socks, weakly bending and stretching, trying to find a power point, but the fatal threat transmitted from his crotch made all his strength attempts seem futile.

His face was very close. Short black fur covered his cheeks, amber eyes even in pain and shock still sharp as knives, staring dead at the attacker pressing on him—a monkey beastkin. Inside there was none of Xiong Zai's terror or Mu Mo's despair, more was cold anger, humiliation, and a beast-like fierceness of being outmaneuvered. His ears pressed flat backward, throat emitting low, threat-filled "woo-woo" sounds, but because his mouth was covered, only becoming dull rumbling.

**Ambush! Despicable! Actually using this kind of despicable means!** Fury burned in his chest, but stronger than fury was that physiological, almost collapsing sharp pain, and deeper layer, the humiliation of his own vulnerability being so easily grasped.
*Careless! Actually caught by this kind of lowly move... must break free! Kill him!*

He tried to gather waist and abdominal strength, wanting to knee-strike the other, or use his still-free left hand to counterattack. But every time he tried to gather strength, that hand below would apply a little pressure as warning, that sharp pain immediately interrupting his strength gathering, making him involuntarily spasm. This was a vicious cycle: pain prevented exertion, unable to exert meant unable to break free, unable to break free meant pain continued.

**Move! Damn it, move!**
He roared inwardly, canine teeth grinding with clicking sounds, black nose flaring constantly from anger and pain, exhaling hot breath. He could smell the very faint, clean scent on the other, mixed with his own cold sweat brought by pain and tension, and the moldy smell and dust smell of the burlap bags. Humiliation gnawed at his pride like a poisonous snake. He, Mo Ying, known for agility and alertness, actually in his own monitoring high place, was caught like a chick by someone using this method!

"Quiet." The monkey beastkin pressing on him lowered his voice, tone calm, even carrying a trace of recognition for his reaction speed, "You're very keen, but the current situation, struggling will only bring more pain. I don't want to kill you, only want to talk with you. Cooperate a bit, good for everyone."

Mo Ying's amber eyes stared fiercely at the other, breathing hurried, but the threat sounds in his throat gradually lowered, body tense resistance force also slightly relaxed a bit. He wasn't giving up resistance, but evaluating, waiting for opportunity. This was a clever and proud hunter.

"Good." The other slowly released the hand covering his mouth, but right hand and body suppression didn't decrease in the slightest. "I know you're the high observer. Like to act alone?"

Mo Ying's pupils slightly contracted, but didn't speak, only coldly looking at the other. The other knew his duty? Asked from Xiong Zai or Mu Mo? Those two wastes indeed fell.

"I don't care about Beastshield's missions. I'm only interested in people." The other continued speaking, voice steady, like stating facts, "For example, you. You seem not very sociable? Wolf brothers like to act together, wild boar guards the door, dog clan those two patrol together. Only you, like to stay up high, stay in shadows."

Mo Ying's mouth corner extremely slightly curled, seeming to be disdain, or like default. He still remained silent. **Sociable? Boring. Those guys... noisy, stupid. Up high sees clearly, also quiet.**

"Like quiet?" The other followed his expression to ask, "Don't like their way of doing things, or simply don't like them?"

A very light cold snort came from his throat, finally opening his mouth, voice low and hoarse, carrying the magnetism unique to felines, but cold as iron: "Is there a difference?" His gaze swept across the other's face, then moved away, looking at the gap between burlap bags beside them, "All are... puppets acting according to orders. Noisy puppets and quiet puppets, essentially no different."

These words revealed a sense of alienation and faint mockery. He seemed to lack identification toward companions, even toward Beastshield this system.

"Including your captain? He's also a puppet?"

Mentioning Tiger Captain, Mo Ying's gaze slightly moved, silent for a few seconds, then coldly said: "He's stronger than others. At least, he knows what he wants to do, and has the ability to do it. But... that's all." The evaluation was very brief, but compared to the pure disdain for others, toward Wang Xiaolin there seemed to be a bit more objective recognition of a "strong person," but only to this point. He didn't feel he had loyalty or respect toward Wang Xiaolin, more like recognition of an "effective tool."

"Then, what about yourself?" The other looked into his eyes, "Why do you stay here? Also for 'full stomach'? Or is there some other... pursuit?"

Mo Ying's breathing seemed to stop for an instant. He again turned his gaze toward the other, this time, the meaning of weighing more heavy, as if weighing the other's questioning intent and weight. In those amber eyes of his, beneath the cold, there seemed to be some deeper thing flowing.

"...Pursuit?" He repeated this word, tone somewhat strange, as if feeling this word very unfamiliar, or like chewing its meaning. "Here, what pursuit can there be? Complete missions, survive, not be treated as useless garbage and disposed of. That's all."

But beneath this cold shell, there was a trace of extremely faint, nearly forgotten or buried by himself fluctuation—desire for "free action"? Yearning for "not being bound"? Or, simply some kind of weariness toward the current "observer" identity? He didn't want to admit, didn't even want to think deeply.

He didn't mean to elaborate, the other didn't press further. Everyone has corners unwilling to easily show others.

Time was limited. Distant, Wolf Xun's patrol footsteps seemed to be approaching this area again.

The monkey beastkin pressing on him also seemed to detect this. The other's posture adjusted slightly, making their faces closer. Mo Ying could more clearly smell that clean, slightly wild scent on the other, see the tense skin beneath black fur, and those eyes that remained sharp undiminished even in a controlled state.

"Mo Ying," the other opened his mouth, voice lower and more serious than before—the other knew his name! "If there was a place, where you didn't need to do those 'cleaning' missions, didn't need to stay in shadows monitoring your own kind, also didn't need to obey those orders you find 'noisy' or 'stupid'... a place where you could act and live according to your own will, even pursue that possibly nonexistent 'pursuit' you spoke of... would you consider it?"

His gaze first showed obvious fluctuation, no longer pure cold or weighing, but mixed with surprise and doubt, incomprehension, and a trace of being touched by the words, even he himself hadn't clearly realized the stirring.

"What place?" His voice was still hoarse, but with a bit less coldness, a bit more inquiry.

"A home." The other slowly said, while left hand took something out from his bosom, raising it before his eyes. That was a warm wooden badge, emitting soft luster in the dim light, simple yet full of presence. "A place composed of family members who mutually recognize and guard each other. No numbers, no ranks, only choice and responsibility."

Mo Ying's gaze fell on the badge, not moving away for a long time. Those sharp eyes of his, at this moment, seemed to be examining the promise and possibility represented behind the badge.

"Choice?" He repeated, tone carrying deep doubt, but beneath the doubt, was deeper touch. "I can choose not to go? Can choose to leave?"

"Before you become family, you have the freedom to choose to accept or not accept the invitation. After you become family, what you have is the responsibility to jointly build and guard the home, and the freedom to leave at any time—though I believe, by then you probably won't want to leave." The other's answer was clear and candid.

He fell silent. Distant, Wolf Xun's footsteps were getting closer, already at the neighboring shelf area.

He could feel his body's tension, heartbeat acceleration, and inner intense struggle. Pride made him unwilling to easily accept what seemed like "charity" way out, rationality told him the danger and bad situation of the current state, while that deeply hidden, vague desire for some different life was stirring.

**—Absurd... how could there be such a place? —Is he deceiving me? For what? —But... if really could... no longer need to listen to those orders... no longer need to watch those boring killings... —Danger... he's the enemy... —But... he could have killed me just now... or treated me like the others... he didn't...**

Footsteps closer, almost right outside the corner.

The other looked at him, no longer speaking, only holding the badge, quietly waiting. This was his moment of choice.

Finally, at the instant when Wolf Xun's footsteps were about to appear outside the wooden crate stack where they were hiding, Mo Ying suddenly closed his eyes, and when he opened them again, the struggle at the bottom of his eyes transformed into a desperate determination. He nodded very slightly, almost imperceptibly.

At the same time, he extended that hand wearing black fingerless gloves, fingers slender and forceful, fingertip lightly touching the wooden badge in the other's palm.

A stream of warm current instantly established connection through the badge. Contract, concluded.

The other immediately released all suppression, putting the badge into his quickly withdrawn hand, while saying in a low voice: "Pretend unconscious, don't move."

Mo Ying's reaction was astonishingly fast. Almost at the instant the words fell, he had already adjusted his posture, making himself go limp on the burlap bags, closed his eyes, breathing becoming weak and even, as if truly unconscious, only that hand holding the badge, tightly pressed against his side, feeling the warm touch of the wood and the strange connection contained within.

The other also quickly rolled to behind a pile of burlap bags nearby, holding breath and hiding.

Wolf Xun's gray figure appeared at the edge of the wooden crate stack. He twitched his nose, sharp wolf eyes sweeping across the surroundings, gaze soon landing on the "unconscious" Mo Ying.

"...Mo Ying?" Wolf Xun called in a low voice, vigilantly approaching two steps, hand on the weapon at his waist. He carefully looked at Mo Ying's state, then sniffed the air, brows furrowing. "Hey! What's wrong with you?" He squatted down, tentatively pushing Mo Ying's shoulder.

Mo Ying had no reaction, as if having lost consciousness.

Wolf Xun's expression became grave, he immediately stood up, speaking in a low, rapid voice into the earpiece: "Captain, this is G-3 Wolf Xun. Southeast corner cargo stack discovered Mo Ying collapsed, situation unclear, seems to have been attacked. Requesting instructions... no enemy traces found nearby."

He reported while even more vigilantly looking around, hand never leaving his weapon.

Mo Ying lay on the burlap bags, motionless, but fingers holding the badge slightly tightened. **Home...**
This unfamiliar vocabulary rippled in his heart. The warm connection transmitted from his palm dispelled some bodily pain and inner cold. He didn't know what lay ahead, but at least, this new path was one he himself chose to touch the badge. For someone accustomed to everything being arranged, monitored, this itself was an unprecedented experience.

And that self-proclaimed Memetic Singularity monkey beastkin, like a silent ghost, had already again melted into shadows, going to conduct the next one-on-one conversation that might change another soul's fate.

The warehouse's silence, because of Mo Ying's "unconsciousness" and Wolf Xun's alarm, was completely broken. A storm was brewing.

### Those Still Living

**[Living Being -> Living-G-5-Black Panther Beastkin-Mo Ying]**
His name is Mo Ying, designation G-5. This name perhaps originates from his shadow-like movement style and pitch-black fur color. This black panther beastkin has a slender, vigorous figure, smooth muscle lines sliding like mercury beneath the black tight uniform, full of the explosive power and grace unique to felines. His chest muscles aren't particularly thick, but solid and lean; waist slender and forceful, connecting to powerful hips and long, straight legs wrapped in white knee-high socks. His movements are light and silent, even moving on rough concrete floor or cold steel beams, almost making no sound. Short black fur smooth, with a dark luster under weak light, amber vertical pupils like two cold flames in the dim light. He carries a clean, slightly wild scent, mixed with extremely faint sweat smell and some cool sensation similar to a night forest.

Mo Ying's inner world is as deep and lonely as the shadows he inhabits. He is proud, alert, accustomed to solitude, mostly holding alienation or even disdain toward Beastshield companions. He is not without feeling, only suppressing all emotions beneath a cold exterior. He efficiently executes "cleaning" missions, viewing them as necessary "work" and manifestation of the "law of the jungle," but deep inside perhaps feels a trace of barely detectable weariness toward repetitive killing. He respects the strong (like Tiger Captain), but not out of loyalty, rather recognition of "efficiency" and "capability." What does he desire? Perhaps he himself isn't very clear—possibly pure freedom of action, possibly escaping the binding of being ordered, or possibly just a "corner belonging to himself" where he can quietly stay, undisturbed by stupid noise. He is extremely unfamiliar with concepts like "warmth," "family," even instinctively rejects, viewing them as symbols of weakness and burden.

When the attack descended in a way he didn't anticipate, Mo Ying first felt extreme shock and fury. The humiliation of having his physiological weakness grasped far exceeded fear of death. He struggled, tried to resist with strength and technique, only to discover himself powerless before the most primitive physiological structure. This sense of loss of control was devastatingly destructive to his pride. During the subsequent interrogation, the other precisely touched upon his disdain for companions and the system, and that deeply hidden, faint tremor toward "different possibilities." When the concept of "home" was proposed, especially when "choice" and "freedom" were emphasized, Mo Ying's solid heart defense appeared cracks. The other didn't force, didn't promise illusory goodness, only gave a "can leave" option. This respect for independent will unexpectedly moved him.

At the final moment, under the pressure of companion footsteps approaching, Mo Ying made a desperate choice. The instant fingertip touched the badge, the warm connection established brought not only confirmation of contract, but also a strange sense of liberation—he was no longer the monitoring tool G-5 under Beastshield's cold system, but "Mo Ying" acting for a "possibility" he himself chose. Though the road ahead was unknown, though pride still faintly ached, that warm wooden badge held in hand, like a stone thrown into a deep pool, rippled the first circle of warmth in his lonely, cold heart lake. From now on, shadows might no longer be merely hiding places, but could also become quiet barriers guarding the home.

### Story Thoughts

Mo Ying's story is a precise "surgery" targeting a proud soul. Memetic Singularity didn't try to melt him with warmth, but used extreme pressure (physical pain, loss of control, dignity frustrated) to force him to reveal flaws, then used "free choice" and "respect for independence" as keys to gently knock on his tightly closed heart door.

This process was full of confrontation and tension. Every silence of Mo Ying, every cold gaze, every mocking answer with ridicule, was a manifestation of his inner fortress. However, under absolute physiological disadvantage and the other's core-pointing questions, the internal contradictions of this fortress (weariness toward the status quo, faint desire for "different") were exposed. Finally, what was given to him was not recruitment, but an equal "invitation" and "right to choose." For a soul accustomed to everything being arranged, protecting itself with coldness and alienation, this had unbelievable attraction—it meant being treated as a complete, decision-making "person," not a tool.

When Mo Ying finally touched the badge, choosing to pretend unconscious and cooperate, what we see is not a "tamed" hunter, but an individual who made an autonomous choice. His pride didn't disappear, but was guided in a new direction: from maintaining personal isolation, to guarding a collective he chose to join. That desire for quiet and independence might find a healthier, warmer expression in the future home—for example, becoming the home's most alert night guardian, enjoying a quiet understood and needed by others while they are noisy.

This story makes me deeply feel that true acceptance and understanding sometimes don't require forcibly changing the other's personality, but seeing and respecting the real needs in his core (even if he himself hasn't clearly realized), and providing a framework where this need can be realized in a more positive way. Mo Ying's "cold," might become the future home's most reliable "quiet" and "vigilance." And this is precisely where connection based on choice is most moving.
