## Synopsis / 故事简介

Last night at the alley entrance, R-302 (gray wolf beastkin, sniper) was the sniper ordered to execute the deserter Leishan. He witnessed the entire process, including Singularity breaking the collar, the tiger beastkin stripping and offering his socks, and ultimately being accepted and taken away by Singularity. But he didn't fire—perhaps because Singularity's presence made him feel unpredictable risk, or because something inside him was moved. Now, he is being punished for failing his mission, his life hanging by a thread. How will Singularity operate?

昨晚在巷口，R-302（灰狼兽人，狙击手）正是那个奉命处决叛逃者雷山的狙击手。他目睹了全过程，包括奇点掰断项圈、虎兽人脱衣献袜、以及最终被奇点接纳带走。但他没有开枪，可能是因为奇点的存在让他感到无法预测的风险，或者是因为他内心受到了触动。现在，他因为没有完成任务而正在遭受处罚，危在旦夕。奇点将如何操作？
