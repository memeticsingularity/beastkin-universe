## Synopsis / 故事简介

In the northern district of White Capital, Beastshield's seventh cold storage facility. The air at minus fifteen degrees is filled with the smell of frozen meat and the pungent odor of refrigerant. Bounty hunter Chen Mo tracks a fleeing Beastshield supply manager here, but encounters a black-uniformed bear combat soldier—Shandun. Unlike other combat soldiers, Shandun is trying to let the manager go. His chip is not intelligence, not weapons, but something more precious than life—what he chooses to give up is his own future as a male beastkin.

白之都北区，兽盾第七冷藏库。零下十五度的空气里弥漫着冷冻肉类的腥味和制冷剂的刺鼻气味。赏金猎人陈墨追踪一个逃跑的兽盾物资管理员至此，却遇见了一个黑制服的熊兽人武斗兵——山敦。与其他武斗兵不同，山敦正试图放走那个管理员。他的筹码不是情报，不是武器，而是比性命更珍贵的东西——他选择放弃的，是自己作为雄性兽人的未来。
