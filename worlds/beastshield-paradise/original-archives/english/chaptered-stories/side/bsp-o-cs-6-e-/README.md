## Synopsis / 故事简介

The shadows of Beastshield Company headquarters are long enough to swallow all untimely warmth. For the black tiger combat soldier designated E-719, "Fengya," the only light in his life comes from that powerful, dazzling golden lion colleague "Yaoyang," whom he regards as his only close friend and secret crush. Until a routine night patrol brings him to the depths of an abandoned warehouse, to the other side of his "light"—a cold, sharp, and questioning gun barrel.

兽盾公司总部的阴影，长到能吞噬掉所有不合时宜的温情。对于编号E-719的黑虎武斗兵"锋牙"来说，他此生唯一的光，来自那位强大、耀眼、被他视为唯一挚友与暗恋对象的金狮同事"耀阳"。直到一次寻常的夜间巡逻，将他带至废弃仓库的深处，带到他"光"的另一面——冰冷、锋利、且充满质问的枪口前。
