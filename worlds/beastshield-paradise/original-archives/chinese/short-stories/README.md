## 一、形式说明 / Format Overview

短篇故事为单篇完结作品，篇幅精炼，聚焦于单一事件、场景或情感瞬间。适合快速阅读和世界观入门。

---

## I. Format Overview

Short stories are self-contained single pieces, concise in length, focusing on a single event, scene, or emotional moment. Ideal for quick reading and worldview introduction.
