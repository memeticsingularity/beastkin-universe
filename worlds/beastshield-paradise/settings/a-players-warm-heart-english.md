# **A Warm-Hearted Player's Game Guide: Love, Save Files, and Home**

— A memo from Meme Singularity to myself (and all gentle souls)

---

## **Prologue: How I Started, and Then Decided to Play Differently**

They said it was a "hardcore" game. Put on the headset, click to enter.

I woke up in the corner of a warehouse. A few beastman corpses lay beside me—no obvious wounds, but
their lower halves were exposed, stained with dried, white fluid. The system prompt coldly informed
me: in this world, all damage is converted into "arousal value." Fill it up, and it results in a
fatal climax.

"Oh, it's that kind of game," I thought.

A brown-furred bear beastman stood at the door, broad-shouldered and thick-waisted, wearing a tight
army-green uniform. Following the prompt, I was supposed to sneak up and find his "weak point."

I tried. From behind, my hand reached toward his bulging crotch and closed around the full, warm
presence there.

"Ugh-woah!"

He shuddered all over, groaning as he went limp, his hefty body leaning back heavily into my arms. I
pulled down his waistband. That short, thick bear-cock sprang free, its reddened head steaming.
Using the slick fluid already leaking out, I mechanically stroked up and down—just like the tutorial
demanded.

"Sto... stop... please, spare me..."

He panted out the plea, his round face flushed red, brow furrowed, tears welling in his eyes. His
muscular chest heaved with my motions, the tight white socks on his calves trembling slightly.

I completed the tutorial. His body stiffened, and with a final, unwilling groan, he climaxed, pulse
after pulse, until he went completely slack, motionless.

A prompt popped up: "Congratulations on clearing the tutorial."

I looked at the strong body gradually cooling in my arms, my fingers still holding the fading warmth
of his skin and the sticky wetness of his release. His eyes were half-open, still reflecting a hint
of lingering confusion and entreaty.

In that moment, I felt no sense of accomplishment from "clearing the stage."

Only a clear, lingering **discomfort**.

I liked the way their furry ears twitched unconsciously. I liked the lines where sweat soaked darker
traces into their dark uniforms. I liked the arc of a thick tail swaying without thought. I liked
the weariness or simple honesty that occasionally leaked through the hard-pressed ferocity they
projected.

I liked these vibrant, warm, life-filled bodies.

And I disliked turning them into cold "proof of tutorial completion" lying in a corner.

I quit the game.

But the weight of that bear beastman going limp in my arms, the tremor in his plea, the emptiness in
his final gaze—these sensations stayed in my memory more vividly than any victory screen.

**If "playing the game" meant having to go through that over and over, then I didn't want that
playstyle.**

My name is **Meme Singularity**. That's my in-game handle. The system says I'm human, but everyone
here thinks I'm an "ape beastman." Fine, it doesn't matter.

What matters is, I secretly installed a few small "wish mods."

Not for clearing the game, not for being the strongest.

Only because—
**I like them too much. Too much to bear watching any one of them go cold and helpless in my arms
again.**

I wanted to see if I could rewrite this default, cold script, in my own way.

I wanted to see if, with a pair of hands that always "happened" to pass by, a home whose door is
always open, a heart willing to sit and listen to all the trivial past—those stories destined to end
abruptly in warehouse corners, dark alleys, cold arenas, could instead lead to a much warmer, longer
epilogue.

This is my game.
My rules.
The gentle path I chose.

## **The Little Gifts I Prepared for Myself (The Mods)**

### **1. Heart-to-Heart Bonds**

* **What it is**: A warm, fuzzy telepathy. Whenever a Beast-Shield employee falls into immense fear,
  despair, or agony, my heart will suddenly *thump*, as if gently nudged. At the same time, I can "
  sense" where they are.
* **What it looks like in-game**:
    * **For me**: A sudden, inexplicable tightness in my chest. A warm point of light glows at the
      corner of my vision, marking the direction of the suffering soul.
    * **For them**: Nothing. They'll only feel that, in their coldest moment, sunshine appeared.

### **2. Magic of Time**

* **What it is**: The ability to briefly slow the world down. When a bullet is about to fire, a
  blade about to fall—in those moments of "too late," I make time almost stop. Not forever, just
  long enough for me to walk over, gently push the blade aside, or pull the person a little to the
  side.
* **What it looks like in-game**:
    * **For me**: The world suddenly grows quiet, colors fade. The wind halts mid-air, raindrops
      hang suspended, everyone's expressions freeze. Only the person I'm helping and I can still
      move, still breathe.
    * **For them**: A miracle. A sure-kill attack veers off course at the last possible millimeter,
      and I'm already standing in front of them, as if I'd been there all along.

### **3. Home Without Limits**

* **What it is**: A simple promise: **"Come with me. There's always a place for you at my home."**
  No cap on numbers, no loyalty tests. If they're willing to nod, or gently take my offered hand, we
  become family.
* **What it looks like in-game**:
    * **For me**: The "family list" in my heart grows longer, each name carrying its own warmth.
    * **For them**: A heavy, solid sense of security. Knowing that no matter what happens, there's a
      place to return to, a person whose embrace is always open.

### **4. Little Lucky Wishes**

* **What it is**: A bit of "just-right" luck. When money's tight, I "just happen" to find forgotten
  bills in a pocket. When medicine is needed for a wound, an unopened box of bandages is "just
  there" at the back of a drawer. When everyone's hungry, the smell of baking cookies "just happens"
  to drift from the kitchen.
* **What it looks like in-game**:
    * **For me**: Like having a thoughtful assistant who always places what I need in the most
      plausible spot.
    * **For them**: The good fortune that comes with "home." Life starts to go smoothly, little
      surprises keep happening.

### **5. The Instant Travel Promise**

* **What it is**: If I think of a family member, or a place called "home," the next moment I can
  appear by their side, or back in that warm living room. The reverse is true too—if a family member
  needs me, as soon as they think of me, I can sense it and rush over.
* **What it looks like in-game**:
    * **For me**: The map is sprinkled with points of light called "homes" and stars called "
      family." Where the heart goes, the body follows.
    * **For them**: The most solid support. Knowing that no matter the distance, if needed, I will
      arrive as promised.

### **6. The Gentle World's Shelter**

* **What it is**: An invisible filter. It makes my "collecting people and bringing them home"
  behavior appear reasonable on the world's ledger—just some "accidental attrition," "voluntary
  resignation." No vast organization will come investigating, disturbing our peace.
* **What it looks like in-game**: Nothing. It works quietly, letting us live our little lives in
  peace.

## **Those Moments When Change Began (Vague Silhouettes)**

### **Silhouette One: Green**

He was curled up in a cold alley, gut slashed open, vision blurry. Military boots stopped before
him... Suddenly, his heart was gently "bumped" by a strange, warm sense of empathy. The owner of the
boots froze, as if an invisible hand pressed down on his shoulder. A figure stepped in between. "
Good evening," a gentle voice said. "Mind if I interrupt?" The green-uniformed beastman struggled to
look up, seeing only a pair of strong, ape-like hands. Then, he felt himself lifted gently, carried
on a back. "Sleep," the voice whispered by his ear. "When you wake up, you'll be somewhere with hot
soup and a clean bed."

### **Silhouette Two: Blue**

Reports were scattered on the floor. The boss's roar still echoed. Numbly, he walked towards the
rooftop edge. The night wind was strong. Just as his heart was flooded with the icy chill of
despair, a peaceful, slightly worried warmth flowed in, dispelling the cold. Someone sat down beside
him, legs dangling over the edge. "The wind's really strong here. Not the best place for thinking
about life." The blue-uniformed beastman jolted, turning to see an ape beastman offering a can of
warm coffee. "Something sweet helps when the pressure's on." The stranger didn't ask what happened,
just pointed at the distant lights and talked about which bakery had the flakiest croissants. The
urge to jump dissolved in that calm tone and the strange, shared warmth.

### **Silhouette Three: Black**

Strength was draining fast. A joint lock immobilized all resistance, ribs groaned in his ear... As
consciousness sank into darkness, a strong, willful pulse of empathy "pulled" him back. The lock on
him loosened unnaturally; his opponent staggered back. A figure crouched before him, a hand gently
pressing on his chest. Warmth flowed in, easing the pain. "Pretty banged up. Don't move." The
black-uniformed beastman coughed up blood-flecked foam, focusing on those concerned eyes. "You...
who are you?" "I'm here to clock you out," the ape beastman smiled. "You've over-fulfilled today's
training quota."

### **Silhouette Four: White**

The sniper's nest was compromised. Bullets hit the barricade, sending stone chips flying. The comms
were dead silent. In the moment despair rose, he "heard" a clear, cheerful, even slightly amused
thought-voice directly in his panicked mind: *"Don't worry, they're all waiting for you~ Come on,
you're the last one~"*
The next second, the ventilation grate above crashed down. A figure landed lightly, brushing off
dust. "Hey, this way," the unfamiliar ape beastman pointed into the duct. "The party downstairs is
too loud for you." He was pulled into the duct, moving swiftly through darkness until they emerged
under the night sky again. "Nice shooting," his rescuer winked. "But maybe you could use it for
target practice later, not people. Our new place has a range, needs an instructor."

### **Silhouette Five: Navy Blue (The Wrestler)**

Spotlights were blinding, the crowd's boos deafening. He was pinned hard, the referee's count a
death knell... Before the final count, a reassuring, almost "parent-picking-up-from-school" calmness
enveloped him. A figure leaped onto the ring ropes, holding up a "Technical Timeout" sign, and
grinned as he planted it in the middle of the ring. The opponent rose angrily to protest, but after
meeting the ape beastman's gaze, his eyes glazed over and he slumped to the mat. The navy-uniformed
wrestler stared blankly. The stranger walked up to him, hand outstretched. "You put on a great
match," he said. "But there's a party at my place tonight, and the guest of honor can't be absent.
Everyone's here, just waiting for you."

## **Our Home: Not a Stronghold, but a Harbor**

We don't have a "headquarters," only many places called "home."

* **Bedrock Manor**: The first home, and the largest. Has a fireplace, a kitchen that always smells
  good, a big lawn for rolling around. Everyone loves coming back here for holidays.
* **Serene Coast**: A cottage for family members who want to see the ocean. Quiet, only the sound of
  waves, good for when someone wants to be alone or heal.
* **Woodland Perch**: Treehouse clusters hidden in the woods, connected by rope bridges. The secret
  base for younger family members who like climbing and adventure.
* **Old City Lookout**: An entire floor in a city high-rise, with big windows. Family members who
  like watching the world's hustle and bustle live here, feeling neither cut off from it nor unsafe
  within it.

All these "homes" are connected through me. If I want to visit someone, or they want to see me, it's
just a thought. Distance loses all meaning between us.

## **Daily Life: Sit Down, Have a Chat, Share a Cup of Tea**

Saving someone happens in a moment, but knowing someone takes a long, long time.

So, I always find chances to sit down with them.

Brew a pot of tea, or heat some milk, sit on a comfy couch or the grass. Then ask some ordinary
questions:

"How was your day?"

"What's your favorite food?"

"When you were little, did you ever do anything especially mischievous?"

They talk slowly, I listen quietly. Stories start as small fragments—a childhood stream, the
nervousness of putting on a uniform for the first time, nostalgia for a certain season back home...
gradually piecing together a whole, vivid, unique person.

**I don't need any ability to "read" their stories.**
I cherish every word they tell me themselves. Because trust is built bit by bit, on warm afternoons,
just like that.

I know who's secretly learning to bake cakes, who's tending the little flower on the windowsill, who
has nightmares at night and needs warm milk to sleep well.

Love quietly grows into a great tree in these trivial, real shares, its roots deep and strong.

## **Final Words**

This probably isn't the playstyle the game designers had in mind.
No fierce faction wars, no brutal survival competition.

But I'm happy.

My game objective is simple:
**Sense who's afraid, go to their side.**
**Hold their cold hand, bring them home.**
**Then, together, turn the long days ahead into warm seasons.**

In this vast, sometimes cold world, we've built small, warm "exceptions."
Here, the law of the jungle doesn't apply. Here, we only believe:
**Meeting is the journey home,**
**Where the heart finds peace, that is home.**

I am Meme Singularity, an ordinary player with a soft heart who can't bear to see beautiful things
break.
This is how I play.
If you also believe in the power of gentleness, then welcome to our world.
The hearth fire is always lit, the door is always open.
