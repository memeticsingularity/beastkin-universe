# Chapter 3 Night Faces, Whispers, and an Unopened Gift

Qi dian walked down the hallway, his footsteps so light they were almost inaudible. The walls were a warm off-white, presenting a near-illusory tranquility under the soft lighting. He recalled Yan Sui's appearance just now—that tiger beastkin lying on the bed, light-golden fur scattered across the pillow, breathing gradually steadying, gaze transitioning from pain and sorrow to a fragile, near-vulnerable relaxation.

He walked into the kitchen.

It was an open-concept design, connected to the living room. The cabinets were light wood-colored; the countertops were white marble-patterned, clean and tidy. Qi dian opened the refrigerator; cool air rushed out, carrying a fresh chill. Inside were various ingredients—fresh vegetables, eggs, meat, and some seasonings he had prepared earlier.

He took out two eggs, a bunch of greens, several bundles of thin noodles, and a small piece of lard.

Clear broth noodles. He thought—the simplest yet most stomach-warming thing. Yan Sui's stomach couldn't handle anything too greasy right now, but a bowl of steaming hot noodles with the aroma of lard should make him comfortable.

Qi dian took out a ceramic pot from the cupboard, filled it with water, and placed it on the stove. Blue flames "poofed" to life, licking at the bottom of the pot. He quietly watched the water gradually warm, tiny bubbles rising from the bottom, slowly growing larger, rolling.

While waiting for the water to boil, he began to prepare the ingredients. He broke apart the greens one by one, rinsing them under running water. The water flowed over the emerald leaves, washing away the earthy scent, leaving behind a fresh plant aroma. He set the washed greens aside to drain, then began to crack the eggs.

The eggshell tapped lightly against the bowl's edge, making a crisp "crack" sound. Transparent egg white wrapped around a golden yolk, sliding into the bowl. He cracked a second one; the two yolks lay quietly in the bowl like two tiny suns.

The water boiled.

The boiling water surface rolled with white foam, making "gurgle gurgle" sounds. Steam rose, carrying moist vapor, permeating the kitchen air. Qi dian put the noodles into the pot; the thin noodles softened the moment they touched the hot water, stretching and rolling in the water.

He picked up chopsticks, gently stirring the noodles to prevent them from sticking. The noodles danced in the boiling water, gradually changing from stiff to soft, their color shifting from the grayish-white of raw noodles to the milky-white of cooked noodles.

Just then, he sensed something.

It was the feeling of being watched. Not malicious, but... careful, carrying curiosity and some indescribable emotion.

Qi dian didn't immediately turn around. He continued stirring the noodles with his chopsticks, but the corner of his eye glanced toward the kitchen doorway.

Someone was standing in the doorway.

No, a beastkin.

Yan Sui.

He was wearing those off-white pajamas, barefoot, quietly standing in the shadow of the doorframe. Short, light-golden fur gleamed with a soft luster under the hallway light; two tiger ears tilted slightly forward, as if carefully listening to the sounds in the kitchen. His tail hung behind him, the tip swaying gently, betraying some unease or hesitation in its owner's heart.

Qi dian's heart stirred slightly.

He didn't immediately speak, pretending not to have noticed, continuing with his task. He fished out the noodles, placed them into the already-prepared bowl, then scooped up a ladle of the hot noodle-cooking broth, pouring it over the noodles. Clear broth soaked the milky-white noodles, rising in a puff of white steam.

Then he picked up an egg, tapped it lightly against the edge of the pot, letting the white and yolk slide into the boiling water. The poached egg slowly solidified in the water, the white turning from transparent to snowy white, the yolk still maintaining its golden color.

Throughout the whole process, he could feel that gaze continuously falling on him.

Finally, when the poached egg was cooked to medium, Qi dian turned around and looked toward the doorway.

"Yan Sui?" His voice was very light, as if afraid of startling something.

The tiger beastkin in the doorway stiffened slightly; his ears instantly flattened backward, and his tail stiffened for an instant. He was like a child caught with a secret, a flash of panic and shame in his eyes.

"I..." Yan Sui's voice was very light, carrying a just-awakened hoarseness, "I... didn't mean to spy..."

Qi dian smiled; that smile was very gentle, without any hint of blame.

"It's okay," he said, "why aren't you sleeping? Did I wake you?"

Yan Sui shook his head, slowly walking out from the shadow of the doorframe, into the kitchen. His steps were very light, bare feet on the floor, almost soundless. Short, light-golden fur looked especially soft under the light, those hairs trembling slightly in the air as he walked.

"I... can't sleep," he said, voice low, "being alone... I'm a little scared."

When he said this, his gaze was somewhat evasive, as if unaccustomed to exposing his vulnerability. That bandaged hand unconsciously gripped the hem of his pajamas, knuckles whitening slightly from the force.

Qi dian's chest tightened.

He looked at the tiger beastkin before him—that tall, sturdy body, yet at this moment appearing so lonely, so much in need of protection. He recalled everything Yan Sui had experienced in the Beastshield world, recalled the way his eyes rimmed red when speaking of his father in the hot spring, recalled his words "every single day."

"Then..." Qi dian's voice grew even softer, "would you like to come in and sit for a while? The noodles will be ready soon."

Yan Sui's eyes brightened slightly, as if having received some permission. He nodded, slowly walking into the kitchen.

Qi dian brought a chair from the dining room, placing it in a corner of the kitchen, away from the stove and hot pots.

"Sit here," he said, "be careful not to get burned."

Yan Sui obediently sat down. That chair seemed a bit small for him; his broad shoulders and sturdy body made the chair look somewhat cramped. His thigh muscles tensed into lines under the off-white pajamas; bare feet on the floor, toes slightly curled.

"Is there... anything I can help with?" Yan Sui asked, voice carrying a desire to do something.

Qi dian shook his head, turning back to watch the poached egg in the pot.

"No need, just sit," he said, "your injuries haven't healed yet; rest well."

"But..."

"Really, no need," Qi dian's voice was very firm, yet very gentle, "just chat with me. Cooking alone is quite boring."

Yan Sui quieted down. He sat in the chair, hands on his knees, gaze following Qi dian's movements. His eyes looked especially bright under the kitchen light—that was a color between amber and dark brown, carrying the vertical pupils characteristic of beastkin.

Qi dian fished out the cooked poached egg, placing it on the noodles. Then he put the greens into the pot, quickly blanching them in the noodle-cooking water. The emerald greens rolled a few times in the hot water, their color becoming even more vivid. He fished them out, neatly arranging them beside the noodles.

Finally, he used a spoon to scoop a small piece of lard, placing it on the noodles. The moment the scalding hot broth was poured over it, the lard immediately melted, emitting a rich aroma. That was the mellow fragrance characteristic of animal fat, mixed with wheat aroma and the freshness of greens, permeating the kitchen air.

Yan Sui's nose twitched.

He took a deep breath; a flash of surprise and... nostalgia passed through those amber eyes.

"So fragrant..." he said softly, voice carrying a near-trembling emotion.

Qi dian carried two bowls of noodles, turning toward the dining room.

"Come," he said, "let's eat."

He placed the two bowls on the dining table, then pulled out a chair, gesturing for Yan Sui to come over.

The dining table was covered with a light-colored tablecloth, with simple tableware arranged on it. Light spilled from overhead, casting a warm halo on the tabletop. Outside the window was pitch black—Myriad Phenomena Courtyard's time differed from the outside world, but the feeling of night was real.

Yan Sui slowly stood up, walking to the dining table. He looked at the bowl of noodles before him—steaming hot, milky-white noodles soaking in clear broth, with a golden poached egg lying on top, emerald greens beside it, and strands of melted lard gleaming on the broth's surface.

He sat down, both hands cupping the bowl's edge.

The bowl was hot; the temperature transmitted through the ceramic to his palms, warm and real. He lowered his head, taking a deep breath, letting that aroma fill his nasal cavity.

"At Beastshield..." Yan Sui suddenly spoke, voice very light, like talking to himself, "I rarely got to eat things like this."

Qi dian picked up his chopsticks, looking at him.

"What did you usually eat?" he asked, tone very natural, without any interrogatory meaning.

Yan Sui's fingers rubbed against the bowl's edge; his gaze became somewhat distant.

"Nutrient paste," he said, "mostly nutrient paste. Various flavors, packed in tubes, squeeze it out and you can eat it."

His voice was very flat, but Qi dian could sense what lay behind that flatness.

"Sometimes..." Yan Sui continued, "there would be synthetic meat. Not real meat, synthesized from protein, the texture was very strange. And some compressed biscuits, terribly dry, one bite required drinking a lot of water to swallow."

He paused, lowering his head to look at the noodles in his bowl.

"I have... not eaten real food in a very long time."

Qi dian didn't speak, only listened quietly.

"Dad used to cook for me," Yan Sui's voice grew even lighter, carrying a sense of nostalgia, "real meals. With real rice, real vegetables, real meat."

His fingers unconsciously rubbed the chopsticks, fingertips sliding back and forth over the wooden surface.

"His braised pork was the best," Yan Sui said, a faint smile appearing at the corner of his mouth, that smile very light but very real, "fat but not greasy, melting in the mouth. He would cut the meat into small pieces, slowly stew it with soy sauce and sugar, stew until the sauce thickened, coating the meat pieces, glistening..."

His voice gradually trailed off, his gaze becoming somewhat distant.

"Every time I went home, he would make it for me. He said... Suisui has worked hard, needs to eat more meat to replenish."

Qi dian felt his eyes growing somewhat hot.

He lowered his head, using chopsticks to pick up a mouthful of noodles, sending them into his mouth. The noodles were soft, carrying wheat fragrance and the mellowness of lard; the hot soup slid down his throat, warming his stomach and his heart.

"He..." Yan Sui's voice choked somewhat, "he could also make dumplings. During New Year, we made them together. He rolled the wrappers, I filled them. I wrapped them very ugly, always letting the filling show, they turned into soup when cooked... but he never minded, always said they were delicious every time."

A drop of tears slid from the corner of Yan Sui's eye, dropping into the bowl, vanishing into the broth.

"I want..." his voice broke, "I want to eat his braised pork one more time... want to make dumplings with him one more time..."

Qi dian put down his chopsticks.

He looked at Yan Sui, looked at the tears and longing in those amber eyes, and felt a soft emotion welling up in his heart. He reached out, across the table, gently covering the back of Yan Sui's hand. That hand was large, with distinct knuckles, thick paw pads on the palm, currently trembling slightly from emotional agitation.

"Your father..." Qi dian asked softly, "is he alright now?"

Yan Sui's body stiffened slightly. He lowered his head, looking at the noodles in his bowl, silent for a long time. Then, he gently shook his head.

"He's gone." Yan Sui's voice was very light, like coming from very far away, "Two years ago... that night, after he settled me down, he went out again. He said he was going to find an old friend to discuss something... I fell asleep, thinking he would come back. But..."

His voice trembled slightly; his fingers unconsciously tightened, gripping Qi dian's hand.

"The next morning, my superior came to tell me... that he was already gone. When they found him... he had already..."

Yan Sui couldn't continue. His shoulders trembled slightly, tears dropping one by one onto the tabletop.

Qi dian's chest tightened. He gripped Yan Sui's hand, voice very light, carrying sincere condolences: "My condolences."

Yan Sui raised his head, looking at Qi dian. Those amber eyes were full of sorrow, but also a strange calm—that of having accepted reality, yet still mourning.

"Did he suffer?" Qi dian asked, voice very gentle.

Yan Sui shook his head.

"I don't know," he said, "they didn't tell me the details. I only know he never came back."

Qi dian was silent for a moment. He looked at Yan Sui, looked at the sorrow and expectation in those amber eyes, and felt a complex emotion welling up in his heart. He wanted to tell Yan Sui, wanted to let Yan Sui know that everything was still possible. But now was not the time.

"You'll be fine," Qi dian said softly, voice very gentle, "you'll live well. Your father... must also want you to live well."

Yan Sui raised his head, looking at him. A flash of confusion passed through those amber eyes, but more than that was a touched feeling of being understood. He gently nodded, fingers still tightly gripping Qi dian's hand.

"Mm," he said, voice very light, "I will."

They sat quietly at the dining table for a while, holding hands, neither speaking. The night outside the window was deep, but the room was warm, the light soft, two bowls of steaming hot noodles placed before them.

Then, Qi dian gently loosened his hand, picking up his chopsticks.

"Eat quickly," he said, "the noodles are getting cold."

Yan Sui nodded, wiping the tears from the corners of his eyes, also picking up his chopsticks. His movements were somewhat clumsy; that bandaged hand wasn't very flexible, but he still tried hard to pick up a mouthful of noodles, sending them into his mouth.

The moment the first mouthful of noodles entered his mouth, Yan Sui's eyes widened slightly.

That was a taste... he had almost forgotten. Wheat fragrance, fat fragrance, the deliciousness of hot broth, mixed together, permeating his mouth. The noodles were soft but not mushy, carrying a bit of chewiness. The broth was very light but endlessly aftertasted, carrying the mellowness of lard and the freshness of greens.

He chewed slowly, feeling the texture of the food in his mouth, feeling that warmth spreading from his mouth all the way to his stomach, then to his whole body.

"Delicious..." he said softly, voice carrying a near-reverent emotion.

Qi dian smiled, also lowering his head to begin eating.

They ate quietly, occasionally making light sounds of chopsticks touching bowl edges, or faint sounds of drinking soup. The night outside the window grew deeper and deeper, but the room was warm, the light soft, two figures casting long shadows under the light.

Yan Sui ate very slowly, chewing every mouthful for a long time, as if savoring, as if cherishing. He saved the poached egg for last, using chopsticks to gently break the yolk; golden-yellow egg liquid flowed out, mixing into the broth. He lowered his head, drinking the last mouthful of soup, then put down his chopsticks, letting out a long breath.

"Full?" Qi dian asked.

Yan Sui nodded, a satisfied expression appearing on his face.

"Very full," he said, "and also... very warm."

Qi dian stood up, beginning to clear the dishes.

"Let me help," Yan Sui also stood up, reaching out to take Qi dian's bowl.

"No need," Qi dian gently blocked his hand, "you sit, I'll do it."

"But..."

"Your hand is injured," Qi dian's tone was very gentle but also very insistent, "it's not good to touch water."

Yan Sui lowered his head to look at his bandaged hand, then silently withdrew his hand.

Qi dian carried the dishes into the kitchen, simply washing them. The sound of water was especially clear in the quiet night. Yan Sui stood in the kitchen doorway, watching his busy figure from behind, tail swaying gently behind him.

After washing the dishes, Qi dian dried his hands, turning to look at Yan Sui.

"Would you like... to take a walk?" he asked, "to digest, then sleep again."

Yan Sui nodded.

They walked out of the suite, coming to the outside of Myriad Phenomena Courtyard. The night air was very fresh, carrying the fragrance of grass and trees. There were no stars in the sky, but a soft light shone from somewhere unknown, illuminating the surroundings.

Various plants were planted in the courtyard, gently swaying in the night breeze. In the distance was a small patch of bamboo; bamboo leaves made "rustling" sounds in the wind. The air was permeated with the scent of flowers and earth, and an indescribable tranquility belonging to the night.

They walked slowly along a small path, footsteps very light, almost inaudible. Yan Sui walked beside Qi dian; he was more than half a head taller than Qi dian, but in the night, his figure seemed less imposing, instead carrying a dependent closeness.

"Here..." Yan Sui said softly, "is so quiet."

"Mm," Qi dian said, "Myriad Phenomena Courtyard is an independent space, without the noise of the outside world."

"Independent space..." Yan Sui repeated this word, as if digesting its meaning, "then... what about the people outside? The people from Beastshield... they can't find this place?"

"They can't find it," Qi dian's voice was very calm, "it's very safe here. You can rest assured."

Yan Sui was silent for a moment, then gently nodded.

They continued walking, crossing a patch of grass; grass blades made faint "rustling" sounds underfoot. Night wind blew past, bringing a chill; Yan Sui subconsciously shrank his shoulders.

"Cold?" Qi dian asked.

"A little..." Yan Sui answered honestly.

Qi dian stopped, turning to look at him. In the night, Yan Sui's outline was outlined by soft light, short light-golden fur trembling slightly in the wind. His eyes looked especially bright in the darkness, like two amber-colored stars.

"Then let's go back," Qi dian said, "you should rest."

Yan Sui didn't move.

He stood there, looking at Qi dian, tail swaying uneasily behind him. His fingers twisted together, as if hesitating about something.

"Qi dian..." he finally spoke, voice very light, "tonight... can you sleep with me?"

When he said this, his ears flattened backward, a flush of shame appearing on his face. He lowered his head, not daring to look into Qi dian's eyes.

"I... by myself..." his voice grew smaller and smaller, "I'll have nightmares."

Qi dian's chest tightened.

He looked at the tall tiger beastkin before him, looked at his lowered head, flattened ears, uneasily swaying tail, and suddenly realized—no matter how strong his body, no matter how many hardships he had experienced, deep inside, he was still a child in need of protection, in need of companionship.

"Okay," Qi dian's voice was very gentle, "I'll stay with you."

Yan Sui raised his head, gratitude shining in his eyes.

They returned to the bedroom. Yan Sui lay on the bed, looking at Qi dian, tail swaying gently behind him.

"You... you can sleep up here," Yan Sui said, voice carrying a hint of hesitation but also expectation, "the bed... is big enough."

Qi dian hesitated.

"I'm afraid I'll press on you," he said, "you still have injuries."

"It won't," Yan Sui shifted to the side, leaving a large space, "you... you can sleep on this side."

Qi dian looked at the expectation in those amber eyes, and his heart softened. He nodded.

"Then I'll get another pillow."

He took out a spare pillow from the cabinet, placing it on the other side of the bed. Then, he carefully lay down on the bed, maintaining a small distance from Yan Sui.

Yan Sui lay on his side, facing Qi dian, tail extending from under the blanket, gently sweeping across the bedsheet. His breathing was very light, but Qi dian could feel that warm breath brushing against his face.

"Sleep," Qi dian said softly, "I'm here with you."

Yan Sui closed his eyes. His breathing gradually became steady, his body relaxing. With Qi dian's companionship, he finally sank into sleep.

The room was very quiet, only Yan Sui's even breathing. Qi dian lay quietly, listening to that breathing, feeling the body temperature of the tiger beastkin beside him—that temperature was somewhat higher than a human's, like a warm stove, radiating heat in the darkness. He could smell the faint fragrance on Yan Sui's body—that was the sulfur scent of the hot spring mixed with a beastkin-specific aroma similar to sun-dried fur.

Qi dian carefully turned over, facing Yan Sui. In the dim light, he could see the tiger beastkin's outline—broad shoulders slowly rising and falling with breath, short light-golden fur spreading on the pillow like a soft flower. His lips were slightly parted, revealing a bit of tongue tip—that was the expression beastkin made when relaxed.

Qi dian reached out, gently placing his hand on Yan Sui's shoulder. He could feel the thick muscle's texture in his palm, could feel the body temperature transmitted through the fabric. That was a... real, living feeling.

Qi dian's consciousness also gradually became hazy.

Just as he was about to fall asleep, he sensed something unusual beside him.

Yan Sui's body was trembling. It was a subtle but increasingly violent trembling, transmitted from the mattress to Qi dian's body. Qi dian immediately opened his eyes, turning his head.

Yan Sui on the bed was dreaming. His body was curled into a ball, both hands tightly gripping the blanket, knuckles whitening from the force. His brows were tightly furrowed, fine beads of sweat seeping from his forehead, short light-golden fur soaked with sweat and sticking to his skin. His lips were trembling slightly, emitting a suppressed, pained moan.

"No..." Yan Sui's voice squeezed from his throat, carrying a tearing pain, "don't... Dad..."

His body trembled even more violently, that trembling spreading from his limbs to his whole body, like a leaf swaying in a gale.

"Don't... I beg you..." his voice grew louder, carrying a sobbing tone, "don't kill him... don't..."

Qi dian immediately sat up from the bed, leaning close to Yan Sui. He reached out, gently grasping Yan Sui's uninjured hand. That hand was burning hot, palm full of sweat, fingers tightly twisting the blanket.

"Yan Sui," his voice was very light but very firm, "Yan Sui, wake up."

Yan Sui didn't wake. He was immersed in the nightmare, unable to extricate himself. His hand tightly gripped Qi dian, force so strong it seemed like it would crush Qi dian's fingers.

"Dad!" he suddenly shouted, voice carrying a heart-rending despair, "Dad—!"

His body suddenly bounced up from the bed, eyes snapping open—but those amber eyes had no focus at all, only endless fear and pain.

"Yan Sui!" Qi dian cupped Yan Sui's face with both hands, forcing him to look at himself, "Yan Sui, it's me, it's Qi dian! You're safe, you're here, you're very safe!"

Yan Sui's eyes slowly gained focus. He looked at Qi dian, looked into those black eyes, looked at that familiar face, and the fear in his eyes slowly receded.

"Qi... dian..." his voice was hoarse and broken, like coming from very far away.

"It's me," Qi dian's voice was very gentle, "you had a nightmare. It's okay, it was just a dream."

Yan Sui's body was still trembling, but he slowly relaxed. He lowered his head, burying his face into Qi dian's shoulder, letting out a suppressed whimper.

"I... I dreamed..." his voice was muffled, carrying a heavy nasal tone, "I dreamed they captured Dad again... I dreamed they... they in front of me..."

He couldn't continue, his body trembling violently, tears soaking Qi dian's shoulder.

Qi dian reached out, gently patting Yan Sui's back. His hand slid from Yan Sui's shoulder to his back, gently stroking over the thick back muscles, again and again, rhythm steady and gentle.

"It's okay," he said over and over, "it's okay, you're very safe, your father is also very safe. It was just a dream."

Yan Sui cried on Qi dian's shoulder for a long time. His tears were scalding hot, penetrating through the fabric, burning Qi dian's skin. His body trembled in Qi dian's embrace, like an injured small animal that had finally found a harbor to rely on.

Gradually, his trembling subsided, his breathing also steadied somewhat. But he didn't leave Qi dian's embrace; instead, he hugged him even tighter.

Qi dian reached out, hugging Yan Sui back even tighter. He could feel that tiger beastkin's body temperature, could feel the rise and fall of his chest, could feel the beating of his heart—that beating was fast, but slowly becoming steady.

They held each other quietly in the darkness, neither speaking. The room was very quiet, only each other's breathing interweaving in the darkness, gradually synchronizing.

After a long time, Yan Sui gently loosened his hand, withdrawing from Qi dian's embrace, lying back on the bed. His eyes were red, tear stains still hanging at the corners of his eyes, but his gaze was much clearer.

He lay on his side, looking at Qi dian, tail extending from under the blanket, gently swaying on the bedsheet.

"Thank you," he said softly, "thank you... for staying with me."

Qi dian looked at him, a soft emotion welling up in his heart. He reached out, gently grasping Yan Sui's hand.

"Yan Sui," he said softly, "I want to give you a gift."

Yan Sui's eyes widened slightly, looking at Qi dian.

"A gift?" he softly repeated, then quickly shook his head, "no need, no need, how could I... you've already done so much for me, I..."

His voice carried unease and refusal, fingers unconsciously twisting the corner of the blanket.

Qi dian tightened his grip on his hand, interrupting him.

"It's not anything valuable," he said, voice very light, carrying a gentle insistence, "it's just... something I want to give you."

Yan Sui looked at him, a flash of confusion in his eyes.

"But..." he was still hesitating.

"You'll know when the time comes," Qi dian said, not revealing more, "when the right moment arrives."

Yan Sui was silent. He didn't ask what the gift was, nor did he refuse again. He simply gently nodded, eyes carrying an obedient compliance.

"Okay," he said, voice very light, "then... I'll thank you in advance."

His tail moved slightly, extending from under the blanket, resting on Qi dian's wrist. That was a wordless closeness, a way of confirming the other's existence.

Qi dian looked at his appearance, a complex emotion welling up in his heart. He reached out, raising Yan Sui's hand to his own chest, pressing it against his own heart.

"Feel it?" he said, "my heartbeat. It's real, and I'm real."

Yan Sui felt the beating heart beneath his palm, felt that warm body temperature and real touch, his gaze becoming gentle.

"Mm," he said softly, "I feel it."

"Sleep," Qi dian said, lying on the bed, facing Yan Sui, "I'm here with you."

Yan Sui nodded, shifting toward Qi dian's direction, closing the distance between the two. He closed his eyes; that tail resting on Qi dian's wrist gently tightened, as if confirming Qi dian's existence.

The night outside the window grew deeper and deeper.

He lowered his head to look at Yan Sui's sleeping face, thinking silently in his heart:

When the right moment comes, I will give you that gift. A gift... that can make you whole again.

But for now, sleep. Tonight, you only need to sleep peacefully.

Yan Sui's breathing was steady and deep, clearly already sunk into dreamless sleep. His face looked especially peaceful in sleep, brows no longer furrowed, the corner of his mouth even carrying a faint smile. His tail curled slightly on Qi dian's wrist, like a gentle shackle, yet also like a wordless promise.

And Qi dian, quietly lying beside him, guarding him, until the first ray of dawn pierced through the night sky of Myriad Phenomena Courtyard.
