# Chapter 13: The Barren Spine Plains

**Windswept sands surged, the land parched and cracked, crops struggled to grow, and resources were
scarce and
impoverished. The **Barren Spine Plains** had always been this way. In this economically backward
region, inhabitants
maintained the custom of living in tribal settlements.**

**"Ahead is the **Sunset Tribe**, like other tribes, it was recently occupied by
the **Blackwater Tribe**."** The
speaker was a tall, burly **yak beastman** wearing animal-skin shoulder wraps and a loincloth.

**"Thank you for guiding us, **Governor Mo**. We of the **Beast Shield Corporation** have come at
the government's
command to assist you in quelling the unrest."** **Ya Que**, a director of **Beast Shield
Corporation**, stood beside
the yak beastman. Behind them, besides the few guards led by the yak beastman, dozens of
green-uniformed **Beast Shield
Soldiers** stood in two rows, orderly and disciplined.

**Mo Yunfeng** shook his head. **"The forces you've brought may not be enough."**

**"Don't worry. Each one of them is a warrior who can take on ten by himself. They won't disappoint
you, Governor Mo."**
**Ya Que** replied confidently.

**Mo Yunfeng** nodded. He believed that since they were sent by the government, they certainly
wouldn't be mediocre. He
began to describe the situation: **"The **Barren Spine Plains** originally had dozens of scattered
tribes, most
coexisting harmoniously without infringing on each other. But a year ago, the beastmen of
the **Blackwater Tribe**
suddenly rose to power and began swallowing up other tribes. The other tribes were no match for them
before their might.
Now, it's a situation where they stand alone and dominant."**

**Ya Que** pondered for a moment. **"Forgive my bluntness, but that shouldn't necessarily be a bad
thing. Unified tribes
are easier to manage and aid development. Perhaps this is a precursor to civilized progress."**

**"Tribes merging with each other is indeed very common, and the government often turns a blind eye.
But
this **Blackwater Tribe** is abnormal!"** **Mo Yunfeng** shook his head.

**"Oh? How are they abnormal?"**

**"The abnormality lies in the residents of the **Blackwater Tribe**! They've been reproducing like
crazy recently, and
their population has increased exponentially, more than tenfold what it was five years ago! This is
already exaggerated
to the point of being detached from reality!"** **Mo Yunfeng** slammed his three-meter-long *
*Zhanmadao** into the
ground, angrily stating: **"If it were just that, it would be one thing. But their temperament has
also become very
strange. Each one has intelligence as low as that of a ten-year-old child, completely lacking
self-control. When angry,
they kill; when sexually aroused, they grab someone to mate with, like uncivilized beasts! So we
call
them **Savage Beasts**. Later, they heard it often and even began calling themselves that. They
simply have no sense of
shame!"**

**Ya Que** felt something was off: **"Seeing is believing. Why don't we go in now and see what
these **Savage Beasts**
really look like."**

Entering the tribe, the scene was shocking. The newly conquered tribal brick houses lay collapsed
everywhere, the
original inhabitants' corpses scattered in all directions. The surviving residents weren't much
luckier. The **Savage
Beasts** kicked and punched them, and those with any looks or build were being ridden by **Savage
Beasts**, thrusting
wildly. A group of **Savage Beasts** with erect lower bodies stood around waiting in line, ready to
take over once the
current one finished. The resident being used already had a vacant look in his eyes, unsure if he
was numb or had
fainted.

Two **Savage Beasts** who had just finished noticed the **Beast Shield** party. They roughly stuffed
their
still-dripping hard members into their shorts, drew their long knives, and stepped forward, roaring
in their rough,
simple voices: **"Which tribe are you from? Drop your weapons and surrender. Savage Beasts don't
kill."**

The two **Savage Beasts** approached closer. **Ya Que** watched with interest from the sidelines. He
intended to witness
**Governor Mo's** skills firsthand.

The government-ruled **Beast Alliance Nation** was divided into five regions: central and the four
directions. The
government soldiers of each region were commanded by a governor. These five governors were the
strongest combat forces
dispatched by the government.

The two **Savage Beasts** drew nearer, continuously uttering threats. **Mo Yunfeng** didn't even
look at them. He simply
raised his over-hundred-kilogram **Zhanmadao** and gave it a light swing. The **Savage Beasts'**
threats abruptly
ceased. As the dust stirred up by the blade tip settled, the two **Savage Beasts'** bodies thudded
to the ground one
after another. Their heads, however, were neatly lined up on the horizontally held **Zhanmadao**,
mouths agape in
astonishment, as if they hadn't yet realized they were decapitated.

**Mo Yunfeng** moved the heads on his **Zhanmadao** in front of **Ya Que**. **"This is
a **Savage Beast**."**

**Ya Que** sighed inwardly at **Mo Yunfeng's** skill. He leaned in without reservation to observe
carefully.

The two **Savage Beasts** weren't completely dead yet. Though their mouths hung open, unable to make
a sound, their
eyeballs were still shifting left and right, seemingly trying to figure out what had happened. Then
their gaze slowly
rolled upward, gradually glazing over.

![Mo Yunfeng Holds Heads on Blade](../../../../images/main-story/ch-13-heads.png)

Apart from slight differences in build and skin tone, these **Savage Beasts** looked almost
identical, as if cast from
the same mold. They had bear-like head shapes, tiger-like claws, ox-like builds, wild boar-like
tusks, and so on -
features of various beast species. Their mostly useless tails had completely disappeared.
Considering the factor of the
**Savage Beasts'** explosive population growth, **Ya Que** guessed that **Savage Beasts** were a
species resulting from
extensive and indiscriminate crossbreeding among beastmen, optimized for the current survival
environment.

The surrounding **Savage Beasts**, noticing their kin had been killed, warily closed in, glaring
viciously at **Mo
Yunfeng's** party, growling and baring their teeth.

They pressed closer and tighter. **Mo Yunfeng** shook off the two heads, preparing to engage. This
time, however, **Ya
Que** stepped forward first: **"Let us handle these small fries. It's a good chance for Governor Mo
to witness the
strength of the **Beast Shield Soldiers**."**

**Ya Que** raised his hand. The **Beast Shield Soldiers**, who had long been restless and eager to
earn merit,
immediately charged into the crowd of **Savage Beasts**, slaughtering as they went. An exception was
when a dark-skinned
**water buffalo Beast Shield Soldier** also tried to step forward to fight, **Ya Que** called him
back: *
*"**Number Four**, why did you follow along? Didn't I say your task isn't combat? Just stay
obediently at the camp."**

**Number Four, the water buffalo beastman**, froze, hurriedly stepped back, stood at attention, and
saluted: **"Report,
Director Ya! I also want to go into battle to kill the enemy and achieve merit for Beast Shield!"**

**"Are my orders no longer effective?"** **Director Ya** twisted his fingers slightly, his tone icy,
his gaze as if
looking at an item about to be discarded.

**"No! I... I'm sorry! I... I'll return to camp immediately!"** The **water buffalo** panicked and
turned to leave.

**"Come receive your punishment after the battle ends."** After issuing this command, **Ya Que**
ignored him.

**"Yes..."** The **water buffalo beastman** ran back to camp dejectedly.

Although each **Savage Beast** was fierce and skilled in battle, facing the physically enhanced **
Beast Shield Soldiers
**, they could only take a beating. No matter if they used axes or knives, the **Savage Beasts**
could only cause
superficial, non-crippling wounds to the **Beast Shield Soldiers**, while a single strike from a **
Beast Shield Soldier
** could take half their life!

Under these unfair conditions, the battlefield became a one-sided slaughter of **Savage Beasts** by
the **Beast Shield
Soldiers**. Before long, dozens of **Savage Beasts** had been massacred. The remaining **Savage
Beasts** could only flee
in panic. Several were picked off by the **Beast Shield Soldiers** using firearms. In the end, only
a handful escaped
alive. Three **Savage Beasts** were captured alive and escorted before **Ya Que**, kneeling to await
judgment.

**"If you don't want to die, answer my questions honestly."** **Ya Que** got straight to the point.
He walked up to the
leftmost **Savage Beast** captive, lifted his chin, and asked: **"Who is the leader of
the **Blackwater Tribe**, and
where is he now?"**

The questioned **Savage Beast** answered tremblingly: **"Savage Beast... Savage Beast doesn't know.
Savage Beast is just
a miscella..."**

*Ssshhht!*

The **Savage Beast's** throat was slit!

The **Savage Beast**, still wearing a terrified expression, clutched his neck, coughed dryly a few
times, then fell
forward face-down on the ground. His body twitched twice and then lay still.

**Ya Que** approached the second captive. This **Savage Beast** had already been scared out of his
wits by the death of
the first one. His body shook like a sieve. **Ya Que** lifted his chin with a bloodied claw tip and
repeated the
previous question.

**"Savage Beast really doesn't know! Savage Beast swears! Savage Beast really doesn't know, don't
kill Savage Beast!"**
To save his life, this **Savage Beast** cried, hugged **Ya Que's** leg, and begged pitifully.

**"How repulsive."** **Ya Que** swung his elbow. The pleading **Savage Beast** was decapitated. His
head rolled twice on
the ground, his mouth still opening and closing in plea. By the time he realized what happened, he
was already devoid of
sensation.

**Ya Que** disgustedly kicked away the headless body of the **Savage Beast** and approached the
third captive, saying
coldly: **"I'm running out of patience."**

The last **Savage Beast**, not knowing how to save his life, could only lower his head, tremble, and
await death in
despair.

Just as **Ya Que** prepared to act, **Mo Yunfeng** suddenly spoke up: **"Perhaps they truly don't
know. Savage Beasts
have low intelligence; they don't know how to lie."**

**Ya Que** thought for a moment and changed the question: **"Who do you take orders from during
actions? Where is that
person?"**

Hearing this, the **Savage Beast's** eyes lit up. Without thinking, he hurriedly answered: **"Savage
Beast listens
to **Wu Sa**! **Wu Sa** is in the camp to the west!"**

**"**Wu Sa**? Is he also a **Savage Beast**?"**

**"Yes... and no..."**

**"Speak clearly. What do you mean by 'yes and no'?"** **Ya Que** pressed impatiently.

**"Savage Beast isn't clear either. Savage Beast has told everything he knows. Don't kill Savage
Beast! Wuwuwu."** The *
*Savage Beast** was so frightened by **Ya Que's** intimidation that he began sobbing. His lower body
lost control,
yellow liquid soaking his animal-skin shorts and dripping down.

**Ya Que** stepped back in disgust and waved his hand. **"Forget it. Get lost."**

Hearing this, the **Savage Beast** was granted a reprieve. He repeatedly kowtowed to **Ya Que**,
thanking him for
sparing his life.

**"Don't celebrate too soon. I only guaranteed that the Beast Shield people won't kill you. As for
how the tribal
survivors here deal with you, that's none of my business."** **Ya Que** snorted coldly.

Only then did the **Savage Beast** react. He was surrounded by the surviving villagers of the
conquered tribes. Not long
ago, he had raped several of them!

As soon as **Ya Que** finished speaking, the villagers closed in on the **Savage Beast**, tightening
the circle.

**"No... don't... ahhhhhh——!"** Amid the **Savage Beast's** desperate wails, the villagers vented
all their anger and
hatred upon him. The **Savage Beast** was beaten into a bloody, pulpy mess.
