**"Chief Fu, what is the meaning of this? Why have we stopped the investigation into the
pharmaceutical company?"** *
*Qin Feng** barged into the police chief's office and demanded without ceremony.

**"It's an order from the higher-ups."** **Chief Fu**, a water buffalo beastman, answered
helplessly.

**"Why?"**

**"Have you seen the recent news?"** **Chief Fu** asked in return.

**"Of course I have. It's almost all about those modified beastmen lately. That company
mass-produces these super
beastmen, packages them as bodyguards, and rents them out for profit. This is completely inhumane!

We should investigate
the details even more!"**

**"Then, do you know where these modified beastmen come from?"**

**Qin Feng** was taken aback. He hadn't considered this question.

**"Aren't they from their own employees?"**

**"Their employees are all researchers. Where would they get so many strong beastmen?"**

**"Then… are they…?"**

**"From what I've learned, a small portion of these modified beastmen come from unregistered
drifters. The rest are all
prisoners transferred from jail, and most of them are death row inmates!"** **Chief Fu** sighed.

**"What! How could they mobilize prisoners from jail? Has the government high command been
infiltrated?"**

**Chief Fu** shook his head.

**"I don't know either. But as you can see, it's no surprise this case has been called off."**

***

**Beast Shield Corporation's** future seemed bright, but managing these **Beast Shield Soldiers**
became a major
problem.

Most of them were reformed prisoners, forced to adopt aggressive personalities in the prison
environment where the
strong prey on the weak.

Although their income from working at **Beast Shield** was considerable, they received very little
of it, barely enough
for basic expenses. Most of their earnings were used to pay off their sentences. Combined with the
inflated ego from
their enhanced physiques, they began to look down on discipline, ignore rules and regulations, and
engaged in arbitrary
plundering and violence, causing many criminal incidents.

In this beastman society where physical strength dictated aesthetics, the public initially held
favorable, even
worshipful, feelings towards the muscular **Beast Shield Soldiers**. However, due to their reckless
behavior in daily
life, public favor plummeted, and people began to avoid them.

The **Government** could no longer sit idly by and dispatched a military officer to **Beast Shield
Corporation**. A
meeting was convened at **Beast Shield Headquarters** with all the modified beastmen.

**"This is government officer Lei Zhou. From now on, he will be responsible for your training."** *
*Chairman Ya**
offered a perfunctory introduction before stepping aside, handing the microphone to a blue **lion
beastman** in military
uniform standing beside him.

This **lion beastman** was as tall as the **Beast Shield Soldiers**, with solid, densely packed
muscles bulging all over
his body, almost devoid of any visible fat. He exuded an imposing aura of natural authority.

**Lei Zhou** pushed the microphone aside and got straight to the point:

**"Assemble on the training grounds at six o'clock tomorrow morning."**

His voice, like thunder, echoed through the hall, steady and clear, as if it were his normal
speaking volume. The noisy
hall fell silent instantly.

But the silence lasted less than ten seconds.

**"Pfft… hahahaha."**

**"Who cares about you? Idiot!"**

**"Go home and suckle milk, little kitty! Pspspsps～"**

**"Hahahaha……"**

The hall was immediately submerged in jeers and mockery.

**"Had enough! You trash really think you're some kind of premium breed now?"** **Chairman Ya** was
the first to lose
his temper and roar. The thought that he had to rely on these small-time thugs to develop his
business filled him with
inexplicable irritation.

The **Beast Shield Soldiers** stopped their mockery, all of them clenching their fists and glaring
at **Chairman Ya**.

At that moment, a modified **ox beastman** in a green uniform stepped forward. The other **Beast
Shield Soldiers**
automatically cleared a path for him. He was taller and broader than the other **Beast Shield
Soldiers**, and the floor
vibrated with his steps!

Seeing him step up, the others revealed expressions of watching a good show. It seemed this **ox
beastman** was likely
the small ringleader privately elected by the **Beast Shield Soldiers**.

The **ox beastman** walked up to **Chairman Ya** and glared angrily at this slender beastman who was
a head shorter than
him.

**"I hate rich pricks like you the most, acting all high and mighty once you have money. How about
you repeat what you
just said to my face?"** The **ox beastman** leaned down, placing a wide hand on **Chairman Ya's**
shoulder to apply
pressure.

**"Hmph!"** **Chairman Ya** was not intimidated but instead smiled coldly. **"Seems I've been too
lenient lately. Now
even trash like you dares to challenge me!"**

*Whoosh!*

A sharp sound of wind.

It was the **ox beastman's** massive palm swinging towards **Chairman Ya**!

This slap was delivered with full force. With **Chairman Ya's** slight build, a hit like this could
probably knock his
head clean off!

But,

The **ox beastman's** palm stopped right in front of **Chairman Ya's** face.

It wasn't because he held back.

It was because **Lei Zhou's** right claw had clamped onto his wrist!

The **ox beastman** tried to exert force to pull his wrist back, but it didn't budge, as if his
right hand was cast into
a wall!

**"Unnecessary."** **Chairman Ya** snorted coldly, slipping his already claw-formed right hand back
into his trouser
pocket.

**"If you killed him, it wouldn't help my subsequent management."**

**Lei Zhou** finished speaking and released the **ox beastman's** wrist.

The **ox beastman** rubbed his bruised wrist, intending to continue his threatening stance.
Unexpectedly, **Lei Zhou**
suddenly grabbed him by the throat!

*Crack!*

The **ox beastman's** world suddenly rotated 45 degrees, and his mind went blank.

**"It has to be by my hand."** **Lei Zhou's** voice remained flat and unemotional, as if he had just
snapped a branch
blocking the path.

![Lei Zhou Snaps Ox Neck](../../../../images/main-story/ch-2-necksnap.png)

**"Kkhh… khh… cough……"**

The **ox beastman** with a broken larynx made dry, phlegmy coughing sounds, his hands clawing at the
air a few times.

Feeling bodily damage, the modified sexual organs began to attempt repair functions. The **ox's
member** slowly engorged
and became erect. However, the fatal injury was too severe, overloading the repair process.
Eventually, the **ox
beastman's** lower body twitched, and his member helplessly began to ejaculate.

As the **ox member** twitched, thick streams of semen were released, trapped within the **ox
beastman's** green tights,
forming a large, milky pouch. As the **ox beastman** struggled in agony, the pouch swung left and
right at his crotch.
Occasionally, a drop of semen leaked from the pant leg, running down his thick thigh and being
absorbed into his white
socks.

After ejaculating all his semen, the **ox beastman** finally let his arms fall and breathed his last
breath.

The hall fell deathly silent.

**"Did he… just snap Ox Boss's neck bare-handed?"**

**"Impossible! Our enhanced bodies are bulletproof now!"**

**"Is that guy even a beastman…"**

The dead **ox beastman's** corpse uncontrollably began to fall to the ground, but **Lei Zhou**
didn't let go. Instead,
he dragged him by the neck to the center of the crowd.

The **Beast Shield Soldiers**, witnessing him kill, were intimidated by his strength and
automatically cleared a path
for him out of fear.

**"Listen up. You are just a bunch of prisoners, scum of society. I can torture and kill you at any
time, without
bearing any legal responsibility."** **Lei Zhou's** tone remained steady and calm, but this time, no
one dared to laugh
at him.

**"Assemble on the training grounds at six tomorrow morning. Those who are late will share his
fate."**

After speaking, **Lei Zhou** threw the **ox beastman's** corpse into the crowd. The
several-hundred-pound corpse was
tossed as easily as an empty sack by **Lei Zhou**, landing face-down heavily on the floor. The
semen-filled pouch burst
under the **ox beastman's** weight, splashing ox semen in all directions, exploding into a large,
milky-white floral
pattern on the floor, splattering onto the white socks and military boots of the surrounding **Beast
Shield Soldiers**.
