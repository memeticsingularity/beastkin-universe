> *Everyday Life and Farewell*  
> A story about warmth and sudden goodbyes. The tiger beastman **Xin** lives a simple, content life
> with his uncle **Oak**, who raised him. Yet, a routine escort mission changes everything. As the
> morning light of departure fills their small home, no one knows it is the moment the gears of fate
> begin to turn.

---

**Glowing Snail Blood**, emits fluorescence at night. It can be dried, ground into powder, and
stored. Used to treat **Spotless Disease**. Powdered **Glowing Snail Blood** does not glow, but can
regain its luminescent properties when exposed to water.

**Xin** closed the *Pharmacology Encyclopedia*, packed the prepared **Glowing Snail Blood** powder
into a medicine pouch, and tucked the pouch into a cloth bag at his waist.

As a **tiger beastman**, **Xin** was a head shorter than others his age. He once dreamed of
following in his uncle **Oak**'s footsteps, taking the exam to enter the **Alliance Fortress** and
becoming a guard of the **Beast Alliance City**. But his physical limitations forced him to abandon
that dream, channeling all his energy into another passion—becoming a **healer**.

"**Xin**! Your rabbit is chewing on my vegetable patch again! Next time I see it, I'll slaughter
it!" An angry shout came from the neighbor's house.

"Sorry, sorry!" **Xin** hurried into the yard, scooped up the escaped rabbit into his arms, and
repeatedly apologized to his neighbor.

In the evening, **Xin** served a pot of stewed meat and vegetables onto the table, then sat down
beside it, waiting for **Uncle Oak** to return home while reading the *Pharmacology Encyclopedia*.

**Xin** had never met his parents. **Oak** had raised him single-handedly. To support them, **Oak**
often worked multiple jobs, but he never complained about it in front of **Xin**.

**Oak** was extremely patient in caring for **Xin**. Young beastmen were energetic and loved to
claw, scratch, and bite the clothes and fur of adults. Impatient caregivers would often rip their
cubs off and toss them several meters away—a moment of peace was a moment of peace, right?

But **Oak** knew **Xin** was frail and couldn't withstand such rough handling. So no matter how much
the young **Xin** grabbed or bit him, **Oak** endured the harassment without complaint.

After **Xin** became capable of handling household chores, **Oak** applied for a guard position at
the **Alliance Fortress** and successfully passed the assessment. Their living conditions improved
considerably from then on. Neighbors, seeing that he had joined the **Alliance**, immediately tried
to set him up with partners, but **Oak** refused them all, using the excuse that he wasn't ready to
settle down. **Xin** knew in his heart that **Uncle Oak** simply couldn't let go of him.

"I'm home!" With the door opening, a burly **tiger beastman** entered the house. He had the exact
same fur color as **Xin**. He wore a blue short-sleeved shirt and shorts made of **Dragon Whisker
Cotton**, emblazoned with the city emblem of the **Beast Alliance City**, and durable leather *
*combat boots**—a standard **Alliance Guard** uniform.

This **Dragon Whisker Cotton** attire was said to be developed by the **Beast Alliance City**'s lord
by borrowing technology from the **Ape-Men Race**. Not only was it highly elastic to accommodate
various beastmen physiques, but it was also incredibly comfortable and allowed for easy movement.

**Xin** immediately closed his book, ladled a bowl of stew and handed it to the entering tiger,
complaining, "**Uncle Oak**, why are you so late today?"

"Sorry, sorry, got held up with some overtime work." **Oak** sat down, took a sip of the broth, and
couldn't stop praising **Xin**'s cooking.

Just then, **Xin**'s little white rabbit hopped over and rubbed against his leg. **Xin** knew it was
hungry too, so he fetched some leftover vegetable leaves from dinner to feed it.

"My goodness, a **tiger beastman** keeping a rabbit!" **Oak** said, massaging his temples in
exasperation. "You, you lack wildness. That's why your body is so frail."

**Xin** chose to ignore **Oak**'s teasing, but he accidentally noticed a fresh wound on **Oak**'s
forehead.

"Hey, how did you get hurt?" **Xin** put down the leaves and asked. The wound wasn't deep, but it
was bleeding.

"Scratched by a thorn bush. It's fine, it'll heal in no time."

"That won't do. What if it gets infected?" **Xin** pulled a bottle of self-ground **Bloodthread
Grass Extract** from his **herb pouch** and applied it evenly to **Oak**'s wound. **Oak**, though he
thought it was overkill, didn't want to dismiss **Xin**'s concern. He obediently lowered his head,
slurping his soup while letting **Xin** tend to him.

By the time **Xin** finished applying the medicine, **Oak** had also finished his dinner. He kicked
off his **combat boots** with flair, didn't bother removing his clothes or socks, and flopped
straight onto the bed. Then, spreading his arms open to **Xin**, he said with a grin, "Come on,
little **Xin**, time for bed."

"Why are you getting into bed without washing up? And it's still early." **Xin** complained.

"I need to sleep early. I have to leave at dawn tomorrow. This mission is to escort a young white
wolf to **Wolf Wind Valley** with the convoy."

"**Wolf Wind Valley**? That's the main city of the **wolf beastmen**, right? That's so far away."

"Yeah, I won't get to see my little **Xin** for several days." **Oak** put on an exaggerated
expression of distress.

**Xin** rolled his eyes at him, cleaned up the dishes, and also lay down on the bed. **Oak**
immediately wrapped **Xin** in his arms, using him as a body pillow, his legs draped over **Xin**.
The **Dragon Whisker Cotton** fabric of his clothes and his **white socks** rubbed against **Xin**'s
body, giving him a莫名奇妙的 (mòmíng qímiào - inexplicable) sense of comfort. **Oak**'s large tiger
head rested against **Xin**'s cheek, his warm breath making **Xin** feel somewhat unsettled.

"**Uncle Oak**... um... I'm not that little anymore. Shouldn't I have my own bed?" **Xin** asked
shyly.

The reason **Xin** asked was because in this world, beastmen had no distinction between male and
female. During procreation, they could act as either the seed-sower or the child-bearer. Therefore,
ambiguous relationships could exist between any two beastmen, and it was a matter to be taken
seriously. **Oak** had raised **Xin** since he was a cub and always saw **Xin** as his own child.
Sharing food and a bed had become a habit, so he hadn't given it much thought. But now that **Xin**
was grown, he had to face this issue.

"You're right, my **Xin** is all grown up now. Oh, don't you have your **Healer License Exam** in
three days? Once you pass, I'll personally build you a big wooden bed as a gift. How about that?"

"Deal!"

"Deal. Now sleep." **Oak** affectionately ruffled **Xin**'s head, holding him even tighter. He
closed his eyes and soon began to emit soft snores.

Not sleepy yet, **Xin** mischievously used his finger to gently scratch the inside of **Oak**'s
round ear. He only stopped when he saw **Oak**'s ear twitch backward from the tickling, satisfied.
This was a prank he had loved since childhood, often making **Oak** shake his head vigorously, much
to **Xin**'s amusement.

"**Uncle Oak**, when you're old, I'll definitely take good care of you." **Xin** stroked the
slightly graying fur on **Oak**'s tiger head, closed his eyes, and gradually drifted into sleep.

The next morning, when **Xin** woke up, he was alone in the vast bed. **Oak** had left at some
unknown hour.

For the next two days, **Xin** diligently memorized the various medical books **Oak** had bought
him, striving to consolidate all the knowledge he had acquired over the years, preparing
wholeheartedly for the **healer exam** three days later.

Under the setting sun, a dilapidated carriage jolted its way towards the interior of the **Fortress
**. The driver, hunched over, looked listless. The cargo on the cart was covered with a ragged
cloth, its shape uneven and unknown. It looked utterly desolate in the fading evening light.

**End of Chapter 01**

## Story Commentary and Reflection

> *The following commentary is based on this chapter's story content, written in casual emotional
language.*

### Their Final Stories

【**Oak** (Blue Uniform Alliance Guard)】
An ordinary tiger beastman who devoted his life to his "child." He probably dreamed of seeing **Xin
** pass his healer exam, then proudly boasting to the neighbors about his accomplished kid. That
morning, he must have gotten up quietly, looked at the sleeping **Xin**, and smiled with particular
tenderness. He set off with the faint mark of the **Bloodthread Grass Extract** **Xin** had
personally applied still on his forehead, perhaps even thinking about what kind of big bed to build
for **Xin** upon his return. He didn't know that the child who loved to tickle his ears and promised
to care for him would never get to wait for him to come home for dinner again. His full, ample tiger
breasts, those strong arms that always held **Xin**—in the end, who knows where they were scattered
in some corner of **Wolf Wind Valley**.

【**The Little White Rabbit**】
A rabbit happily munching on vegetables in a **tiger beastman**'s home. It probably thought the
world was safe because its owner was so gentle. It didn't know about the neighbor's anger, didn't
know its death would become the final straw that broke its owner's sanity, and certainly didn't know
it would be skinned and consumed raw by that very owner. Up to its death, it was likely just
thinking about the taste of the next leaf.

### The People Still Alive

【**Xin** (Healer Apprentice)】
In this chapter, he's still so soft. He minds that his uncle gets into bed unwashed, feels shy about
sharing a bed, and applies the **Bloodthread Grass Extract** with meticulous care. His heart is
filled with love and dependence on **Uncle Oak**, and small aspirations for the future—a bed of his
own, the identity of a **healer**. He hasn't yet tasted the metallic tang of raw flesh, hasn't
learned to speak with fangs and claws. At this moment, he is the last calm cloud before the storm,
the unrefined metal about to be cast into the furnace. Watching him mischievously tickle **Oak**'s
ear before sleep, thinking of the path ahead of him, one's heart feels heavy.

【**Neighbor Beastman**】
An irritable but perhaps not entirely bad character. He was just protective of his vegetable patch,
maybe also disapproving of a **tiger beastman** keeping a rabbit—something that went against the
grain. When he threw the rabbit's corpse into the yard, he probably just wanted to teach **Xin** a
lesson. He didn't know that his casual act became the spark that ignited the fires of hell.

### Story Reflection

Reading this chapter warms the heart, yet leaves it aching. The detail about using **Dragon Whisker
Cotton** clothes and white socks as a pillow is so poignant—that unadulterated intimacy between
family, carrying the scent of sweat and body warmth, feels so real it makes you want to cry. The
relationship between **Oak** and **Xin** is so beautiful: an imperfect uncle who loved with all his
might, a sensitive but grateful child. In this world of **only male beastmen** where strength reigns
supreme, their delicate emotional bond is as precious as fireflies in the dead of night.

And precisely because it's so beautiful, the impending shattering feels all the more cruel. When *
*Xin** says, "When you're old, I'll definitely take good care of you," I wish I could rush into the
story and shout at him: Stop him! Don't let him go! But that's how stories are—the deepest pain
always begins upon waking from the sweetest dream.

The world's setting is also fascinating. The **beastmen** have no gender distinction, yet they still
have family bonds, ambiguity, and so many complex emotions. **Oak** refusing partners for **Xin**'s
sake—this "paternal" (or caregiver's) love transcends biological roles, making it purer and more
moving. **Xin**'s shyness about sharing a bed also hints at the equally complex and subtle emotional
rules of this world.

Watching **Oak**'s back disappear into the morning light, watching **Xin**'s sleeping profile, I
know this warm dream is about to end.
