# United Beasts Alliance - Main Story

> This is the epic story of the tiger beastman **Xin**, who joins the **United Beasts Alliance
Fortress** to uncover the truth behind his uncle **Oak**'s murder and seek vengeance on the
> perpetrators. From a gentle apprentice healer to a new recruit who battles the elite guard **Zhan
Feng** and passes the trial through bloodshed, his path of transformation is filled with brutality,
> resolve, and challenges.  
> 📌 This story contains adult content (violence, death, combat injuries, world-specific depictions
> related to male beastmen). Please note the content rating before reading.

---

## ⚠️ Content Rating & Reading Suggestions

- **Overall Rating**: **R18**. The story deals with adult themes, including graphic violence, combat
  injuries, depictions of death, and world-specific descriptions of **male beastmen** (possessing
  both male and female characteristics) social relationships and emotions.
- **Reading Order**: **Strongly recommended to read in numerical chapter order** to fully experience
  the character's emotional journey and plot development.
- **Key Highlights**:
    - **Deep Emotional Portrayal**: Focuses on family bonds, loss, and the establishment of revenge
      motives.
    - **Brutal Rites of Passage**: The protagonist **Xin**'s transformation from gentle to resolute
      is realistic and shocking.
    - **Unique Combat Philosophy**: Emphasizes will, cunning, and unwavering determination beyond
      mere physical confrontation.
    - **Gradual World-Building**: The order of the **Beast Alliance City**, the power of the **Elite
      Alliance Guards**, the distant threat of **Wolf Wind Valley**—the background unfolds layer by
      layer.
- **Recommended Audience**: Suitable for readers aged 18 and above who can accept dark coming-of-age
  themes and are interested in character-driven stories.

## 🌍 World Overview

- **Core Setting**: The story takes place in a world composed of **male beastmen**, all of whom
  possess both male and female characteristics, resulting in complex social relationships and
  emotional bonds.
- **Major Factions**:
    - **United Beasts Alliance Fortress/City**: The main setting, a militarized organization with a
      strict hierarchy (e.g., ordinary blue-uniformed guards, elite black-uniformed **Elite Alliance
      Guards**).
    - **Wolf Wind Valley**: The main city of the **wolf beastmen**, with connections to the **Beast
      Alliance City** (e.g., escort missions), located far away.
- **Power System**: Hints emerge, such as the "aura" displayed by **Zhan Feng** that can intimidate
  opponents, suggesting extraordinary combat abilities.
- **Technology Level**: Possesses unique products like **Dragon Beard Cotton** (highly elastic and
  comfortable fabric), which incorporates technology from the **Ape-Men Race**.

## 📖 Story Synopsis

*United Beasts Alliance* tells the story of **Xin**, a tiger beastman who was once small, weak, and
dreamed of becoming a healer. His life is completely shattered when his uncle and guardian **Oak** (
a guard of the United Beasts Alliance) is brutally murdered during an escort mission. In pursuit of
truth and revenge, **Xin** undergoes a bloody ritual to leave his past behind, tempering both body
and mind. Three years later, he arrives at the Alliance Fortress to enlist, confronts the powerful
elite warrior **Zhan Feng**, and passes a trial in a crazed and resolute manner, successfully
entering the core of this strength-worshipping organization. His path of vengeance thus formally
begins, with powerful adversaries, hidden conspiracies, and a complex mentor-rival relationship with
**Zhan Feng** awaiting him ahead.
