> *Trial by Blood*  
> Three years can change many things. **Xin** transformed from the boy who ate rabbit raw into a
> relentless avenger seeking entry into the **Alliance Fortress**. At the **Beast Alliance City**'s
> guard recruitment, he must face a brutal trial against the **Elite Alliance Guard Zhan Feng**.
> This
> is not a fair contest—it's a demonstration of will, cunning, and savage instinct.

---

Three harsh winters had passed. The bones of lost loved ones had turned to dust. The arrival of
spring urged all things toward renewal, and the fangs of vengeance, like the first blades of grass,
began to sprout.

Today, the **Alliance Fortress** was bustling with activity. A long queue of **beastmen** stretched
below the city gate, nervously peering at the situation at the front.

At the head of the line stood a wooden table. Behind it sat a **wolf Alliance Guard**. He glanced at
the **boar beastman** at the front of the line, then bent his head to scribble on the **Guard
Application Form** on the table.

"Why do you want to join the **Alliance**?" the **wolf guard** asked without looking up.

"I want... to serve the **Beast Alliance City**, protect our home and city, dedicate my life to the
citizens of the **Beast Alliance City**!" The **boar beastman** raised a fist, forcing out a stiff,
high-sounding reason. But everyone knew the truth: for most, the appeal of being a **guard** was
simply the better pay and benefits.

"Alright. The interview results will be sent to your home within two days. Next."

The **boar beastman** stepped aside from the queue. The **beastman** behind him stepped forward.

"Name?"

"**Xin**."

The **wolf guard** looked up and sized up the **beastman** before him. This **tiger beastman** had
tightly packed muscles, no excess fat, and looked extremely fit—but was a head shorter than everyone
else.

The **wolf guard** sighed quietly and asked absently, "Why do you want to join the **Alliance**?"

Even as he spoke, he'd already started writing "Unqualified" in large letters on the information
sheet.

"I am here for revenge." **Xin** answered coolly, as if stating the most ordinary fact.

The **wolf guard** snapped his head up, staring at the **tiger beastman** in shock, his pen stopping
mid-stroke on the character for "Qualified."

"Three years ago, my uncle was one of you. He died under unclear circumstances during an **escort
mission**. I want to enter the **Alliance Fortress**, become an **Alliance Guard**, and uncover the
truth to avenge him." **Xin** stared at the **wolf guard**, enunciating each word clearly.

The **wolf guard** was at a loss. He turned to seek help from behind him.

Behind him, a massive **brown tiger beastman** sat with his arms crossed on a wooden chair, eyes
closed in repose. His uniform differed from the other **guards**. Though the cut and material were
the same, it was dyed a pure black, dark as midnight. Leaning against the chair was a **greatsword**
nearly as tall as **Xin** himself, looking incredibly imposing.

"**Commander Feng**?" The **wolf guard** called out tentatively.

The **brown tiger** slowly opened his eyes, rose from the chair, and walked over to **Xin** without
taking the **greatsword**.

A commotion rippled through the crowd behind.

"Look, isn't that **Zhan Feng**, one of the **Elite Alliance Guards**? He's overseeing the test!"
one **beastman** in the queue said with reverence.

"That little runt is probably scared stiff," another **beastman** gloated.

**Zhan Feng** looked down at **Xin** and uttered dismissively, "Kid, this isn't a place to play
revenge games. Go back where you came from."

"Yeah, get lost, kid, stop blocking the way," the crowd began to jeer.

"Eat some good stuff, grow taller, then come back, hahaha!" someone even mocked.

**Xin**, a full head shorter than the **brown tiger**, seemed like a kitten before **Zhan Feng**.
But he wasn't cowed by the imposing presence and ignored the taunts around him. He looked up,
meeting **Zhan Feng**'s gaze. "Does the **Alliance** choose soldiers based only on height? Seems to
me there's no shortage of pot-bellied good-for-nothings in **Beast Alliance City**." **Xin**'s eyes
swept over **Zhan Feng** provocatively, leaving no doubt who those "good-for-nothings" included.

Instantly, the jeering crowd fell silent. The **wolf guard** responsible for the forms stood up and
subtly took two steps back.

"Good, good." **Zhan Feng** nodded with closed eyes, pacing a few steps to the side. He wasn't
angered; instead, he smiled. "Seems you won't be convinced without a chance. Alright. If you can so
much as scratch me, I'll let you pass. How's that?"

"Deal!" That was exactly what **Xin** was waiting for.

At these words, the queuing **beastmen** forgot all about order. They instantly scattered, forming a
circle around the two. Those further back stood on tiptoe, craning their necks, afraid to miss the
spectacle.

**Zhan Feng** motioned for him to begin.

**Xin** crouched low and began circling **Zhan Feng**, his right hand drawing the **dagger** from
his waist, his eyes fixed intently on his opponent.

**Zhan Feng** remained completely relaxed, his pillar-like arms crossed over his chest. He even
yawned occasionally, his mockery palpable.

The moment **Zhan Feng** began his next yawn, **Xin** struck. He charged at **Zhan Feng**'s face
with his fastest speed.

"Oh? A frontal assault? Quite bold." **Zhan Feng** sneered, lowering his arms to prepare.

Just as he was about to enter **Zhan Feng**'s striking range, **Xin** suddenly stopped. From his
waist **herb pouch**, he grabbed a handful of **powder** and flung it at **Zhan Feng**'s face!

"What!" **Zhan Feng** hadn't expected this seemingly proud youth to resort to such a lowly,
underhanded trick. He had no choice but to leap back a step—he couldn't be sure if the **powder**
was toxic or not.

The moment **Zhan Feng** landed, **Xin**, who had stealthily circled to his side, finally made his
move. His right hand stabbed straight for **Zhan Feng**'s flank. The strike was lightning-fast and
came from a刁钻 (diāozuān - tricky) angle—an ordinary person would have had no way to avoid it.

But **Zhan Feng** was no ordinary person. His combat experience alone outweighed that of everyone
present combined. A dull "thwack" sounded as **Xin**'s wrist was firmly caught by **Zhan Feng**, who
had spun around.

Grasping **Xin**'s arm, **Zhan Feng** grinned, ready with a sarcastic remark. Suddenly, he sensed
something was wrong. **Xin**'s captured hand held no **dagger**! This strike too had been a feint!

Sure enough, almost the instant **Zhan Feng** caught his right wrist, **Xin** thrust with the *
*dagger** he had switched to his left hand during the powder distraction. This was his true killing
blow!

No matter how formidable **Zhan Feng** was, avoiding this was impossible!

Just as **Xin** was about to succeed, a strange **pressure** erupted from within **Zhan Feng**,
radiating outward. Struck head-on by this **pressure**, **Xin** was flooded with a peculiar
sensation. He felt like a piece of meat, a slab of fish lying on a chopping block awaiting the
butcher! And **Zhan Feng** was that butcher.

This momentary disruption made **Xin**'s movement hesitate for a split second. And that split second
was all **Zhan Feng** needed. **Xin**'s left wrist was also seized in a vice-like grip. **Zhan Feng
** twisted hard, and **Xin** cried out in pain, releasing his left hand. The **dagger** clattered to
the ground.

"How about it? Convinced?" **Zhan Feng** now controlled both of **Xin**'s wrists. **Xin** posed no
further threat, could only glare at him hatefully.

"Still not convinced? What other tricks do you have like this..."

*Crack*
*Crack*

The sound of grinding bone.

A bloom of blood blossomed on **Zhan Feng**'s chest!

"What!" **Zhan Feng** stared in disbelief at the scene before him.

In the moment **Zhan Feng**'s taunt hung in the air, **Xin** had deliberately dislocated his own
wrist joints and, ignoring the agony, launched himself into **Zhan Feng**'s chest, sinking his teeth
into the muscle!

**Zhan Feng** cried out in pain, instinctively releasing both hands and shoving **Xin** away with
tremendous force.

A *ripping* sound tore through the air. **Zhan Feng**'s black uniform was torn in two, exposing his
ample, well-defined **pectoral muscles** to the crowd.

**Xin** rolled several times on the ground before staggering to his feet. His hands hung limply at
his sides. Between his teeth, he still clutched a bloody strip of torn black fabric.

**Xin** spat out the blood-soaked cloth, coughed up a mouthful of bloody foam, and glared at **Zhan
Feng**. "Do you keep your word?"

The stunned crowd all turned their eyes to **Zhan Feng**, wondering if he would fly into a rage and
cleave **Xin** in two with his sword.

**Zhan Feng** reached up, wiped a finger across the wound on his chest, and looked at the blood on
his fingertip. To everyone's surprise, he broke into a fierce, excited grin. He pointed at **Xin**
and roared at the **wolf guard**, "Pass this kid! Skip all his remaining tests! And..." At that
moment, **Zhan Feng**'s smile grew even more ferocious. "Assign him to *my* unit!"

---

**End of Chapter 03**

> *When rules are pierced by fangs, the strong see the madness they desire.*

## Story Commentary and Reflection

> *The following commentary is based on this chapter's story content, written in casual emotional
language.*

### Their Final Stories

【None】
This chapter is a brutal trial, but not a slaughter. No lives end here. Only the former self, naive
illusions, and the so-called "rules" are utterly buried. The world of following procedures and
believing hard work brings reward died completely with the *crack* of **Xin**'s dislocating wrists.

---

### The People Still Alive

【**Xin** (The Avenging Applicant)】
Three years forged hatred into muscle and bone. The **Xin** standing before **Zhan Feng** was no
longer the boy trembling in a pool of blood, eating raw rabbit. His provocation was precise and
lethal, his tactics cunning and ruthless—the **powder** distraction, the feint to lure, the true
strike hidden in the other hand. But the most shocking act was the final bite. When he realized all
technical skill was useless against sheer power, he chose the most primitive, savage method: his
teeth. He chose to break his own wrists rather than fail to sink his fangs into the enemy’s flesh.
This wasn’t combat; it was a declaration: *For revenge, I can discard these hands, discard dignity,
become a beast*. Those hands, hanging useless from self-inflicted injury, held more power than any
raised blade. Watching him cough blood-flecked spittle, eyes burning like hot coals fixed on **Zhan
Feng**, one knows—**Oak**’s vengeance now has hope.

【**Zhan Feng** (Black Uniform Elite Alliance Guard)】
A powerful predator seeking “interesting prey.” Clad in the black uniform symbolizing elite power,
he sat like a mountain. He was bored by ordinary applicants, his yawns dripping with the arrogance
of the mighty. But when **Xin** spoke of “revenge” and challenged him directly, he awoke. The trial
condition he offered seemed like giving a chance, but was actually another form of arrogance. He
enjoyed the fight, enjoyed toying with his opponent, even enjoyed the momentary surprise of an
unexpected tactic. But he never imagined this “kid” would be this insane. When **Xin**’s teeth
pierced his chest muscle and tore his uniform, what he felt wasn’t rage, but excitement. The wound
on his chest and his exposed **pectorals** became medals of his recognition for **Xin**. That
ferocious grin and the order “assign him to *my* unit” showed he saw not trouble, but a raw,
dangerously potential piece of jade needing “polishing.” The days ahead for **Xin** will likely be
both hell and the best training ground.

【**Wolf Guard** (Blue Uniform Interviewer)】
An ordinary functionary caught between rules and the unexpected. He followed procedure, writing
“Unqualified,” asking rote questions. **Xin**’s “revenge” declaration froze his pen mid-stroke,
forcing him to seek his superior. Those two subtle steps backward were filled with an instinct to
avoid trouble and fear of a stronger presence. When he finally recorded the pass on **Zhan Feng**’s
order, he was probably thinking: *What a shift this turned out to be.*

【**Boar Beastman & the Spectators**】
They are the ordinary background noise of this world. The **boar beastman** spouted insincere
platitudes for better benefits. The spectators began as detached onlookers, gleefully mocking and
following the crowd. But when **Xin** performed that series of extraordinary acts, especially the
final bloody bite, they all fell silent, stunned. They are the measuring stick, showing how
“abnormal” **Xin**’s actions were; they are also the mirror, reflecting the complex attitude of
ordinary people in this strength-worshipping world toward the “exceptional”—the instantaneous shift
from mockery to awe.

### Story Reflection

This chapter is exhilarating yet spine-chilling. **Xin**’s growth is astounding. He hasn’t just
trained his body; he’s internalized his hatred into a terrifying combat intelligence and a ruthless,
ends-justify-the-means determination. He used his illusion of weakness (his height), his opponent’s
arrogance (**Zhan Feng**’s underestimation), and the environment (the **powder** creating
opportunity), turning a lopsided match into a contest of psychology and will.

The most awe-inspiring moment, of course, is the final bite. That wasn’t an animal’s tearing; it was
the ultimate maneuver after cold calculation. When all “human” tactics failed, he reverted to
“beast” instinct. This willingness to self-destruct for a goal is terrifying yet moving. He didn’t
just dislocate his wrists; he severed his last ties to concepts like “proper channels” and “fair
fights.”

**Zhan Feng**’s character is also firmly established. He’s not a simple antagonist or obstacle; he
has his own aesthetic and tastes. He appreciates savagery, madness, and those ruthless enough to do
anything for their goal. His torn uniform and bleeding chest are like an initiation ritual. Taking *
*Xin** into his unit is not out of pity, but like finding an interesting toy, or a deadly blade
worth sharpening. One can foresee that **Xin**’s future training under **Zhan Feng** will be
unimaginably brutal, but also the fastest path to strength.

The world’s power system also gets a glimpse. The **pressure** **Zhan Feng** unleashed at the end,
making one feel like “meat on a chopping block,” is clearly a supernatural ability. The enemies *
*Xin** will face likely wield not just muscle and steel, but such esoteric forces.

Watching **Xin** stand there, dislocated wrists dangling, bloody cloth strip between his teeth, it’s
like watching a blade just quenched, still smoking with the stench of blood and hot metal, being
handed to the one most skilled at wielding it. The road ahead will only be bloodier, darker. And *
*Xin**, licking his newly blooded fangs, is ready to chew through it all.
