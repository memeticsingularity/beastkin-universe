> *The Devouring of Innocence*  
> Dire news knocks on the door before dawn. When hope is handed over in a wooden box, the gentle
> healer's apprentice vanishes. A bloody ritual unfolds in the courtyard as **Xin** devours his own
> innocence in the most primitive way, swallowing the first bitter taste of revenge. From this
> moment
> on, his world holds only two things: survival, and vengeance.

---

On the day of the exam, **Xin** woke up very early. Excitement and nerves had prevented him from
getting enough sleep, but it didn't matter much to him. He hastily ate some leftovers from the night
before, picked up his **admittance card**, and prepared to leave.

Opening the door, he found two **beastmen** standing outside who hadn't been there moments before.
In his hurry, **Xin** almost bumped into their legs. Backlit by the sun, he couldn't make out their
features, only that they were as tall and broad as **Uncle Oak**.

"Who... who are you?" **Xin** asked nervously, his eyes slowly adjusting to the light as he looked
them up and down.

They wore the same blue **Alliance Guard uniform** as **Oak**. They stood tall and straight, and the
one in front held a **wooden box** in his hands, his face etched with sorrow.

**Xin** squinted, examining the **box** more closely. Suddenly, a dreadful thought struck him.

He had seen that kind of box before—at a neighbor's funeral. It was an **urn**!

"Sorry to disturb you so early," the **beastman** holding the box paused, then stammered, "Well, the
**escort team** that left three days ago was attacked by **bandits**. All the **guards** on the
mission were killed... um... since... you are **Oak**'s only family, so..." He held out the box.

Hearing this, **Xin** froze in place, unable to process the information for a moment. Pointing at
the box, he asked blankly, "So... you mean... these are **Uncle Oak**'s ashes inside?"

"No... that's not it," the other **Alliance Guard** waved his hand. "We would never cremate a *
*guard**'s body without the family's consent."

**Xin** patted his chest and exhaled, forcing a strained smile onto his face. "So this is just *
*Uncle Oak**'s personal belongings, right? You haven't found the body, have you? And you're just
assuming he's dead based on this stuff? And you came to deliver a death notice just like that?

That's way too careless!"

The two **guards** exchanged a glance, unsure how to respond.

"Alright then, let me see what **Uncle Oak** lost that has you all in such a tizzy..."

**Xin** took the box and flung it open. Upon seeing its contents, he was rendered utterly
speechless.

The object inside the **box** was neither personal effects nor ashes, but it shattered **Xin** more
completely than either could have.

**Oak**'s head lay quietly inside the box.

Beneath half-closed lids, the whites of the eyes had turned purplish. The pupils stared vacantly
ahead. The half-open mouth and the severed neck showed blackened bloodstains. **Xin** tried to
convince himself it was just a **tiger beastman** who looked like **Oak**, but the trace of *
*Bloodthread Grass Extract** beside a scar on the forehead mercilessly crushed his delusion.

In an instant, grief, rage, and terror flooded **Xin**'s mind, leaving him paralyzed, clutching the
**box**, utterly lost.

"Sorry, this is all we found. Also, this is the compensation the **Beast Alliance City** provides to
the family." The guard offered a pouch of coins, but **Xin** seemed not to hear him, standing
motionless with the box. The guard sighed, set down the money pouch, and hurried away.

"Gone..."
"Everything's gone..."
**Xin** muttered to himself...
"No more **Uncle Oak**..."
"No chance to repay him..."
"Gone..."
Trembling, **Xin** extended his index finger and gently stroked the inside of **Oak**'s drooping
ear, hoping it would twitch away as it always did. But this time, **Oak**'s ear remained still,
utterly unresponsive.

**Xin**'s tears finally spilled over, his expression twisting into something fierce.

"Who... who did this..."
"I will find out!"

"I'll tear them... limb from limb!"

**Xin** knew very well that to uncover the killer, joining the **Alliance Fortress** was essential.
But faced with his own frail body, a wave of despair washed over him.

"What can someone like me even do? I'm practically useless!" Intense self-loathing choked him.

Just as **Xin** was sinking into despondency, a white object thudded into the yard, followed by the
neighbor's angry shout: "I told you, if your rabbit comes chewing on my vegetables again, I'll
slaughter it!"

**Xin** turned his head dully. The sight of the little white rabbit's corpse, its head bloodied,
appeared in the yard. He remembered something **Oak** had once said: "You, you lack wildness!"

"Lack wildness... huh?"

**Xin** gently set down the **box**, pulled a **small knife** from the kitchen, and walked into the
yard like a sleepwalker, kneeling beside the rabbit's body.

"If I regain that wildness, maybe I can become strong." **Xin** murmured to himself. He slowly
raised the **knife**, then plunged it viciously into the rabbit's neck. With a downward slash, he
split the fur open! **Xin** tore off the rabbit's skin, ripped out a bloody piece of flesh with his
bare hands, and shoved it into his mouth, chewing frantically. An intense, metallic, gamy flavor
instantly filled his mouth and throat, triggering a gag reflex that forced the contents of his
stomach back up into his mouth. But **Xin** clamped his lips shut, gritted his teeth, and forced
himself not to vomit.

"If I can't even bear this pain, how can I possibly avenge **Uncle Oak**! What's the point of living
at all!" **Xin** pressed the **knife** blade against his own throat. He vowed that if he spit out
even the slightest morsel, he would slit his own windpipe right then and there! Driven by sheer
will, **Xin** managed to swallow the mouthful.

Then he swallowed the next piece...
And the next...

---

**End of Chapter 02**

> *Innocence devoured by bloody fangs. From now on, every breath tastes of rusty vengeance.*

## Story Commentary and Reflection

> *The following commentary is based on this chapter's story content, written in casual emotional
language.*

### Their Final Stories

【**Oak (Only the head remains)**】
All that finally returned to **Xin** was a head. That tiger head still bore the mark of *
*Bloodthread Grass Extract** **Xin** had lovingly applied—a tender, cruel irony. His half-open eyes
likely last saw the flash of the **bandits**' blades, or perhaps the sky over **Wolf Wind Valley**,
but certainly not **Xin**'s shattered face. He would never again wrap his strong arms around his
child, never warm that small bed with his full, ample tiger breasts. The little scratch on his
forehead had been so carefully tended, yet it couldn't stop the severing of his entire head. To his
last moment, he probably thought about returning to build **Xin** a big bed, never knowing he
himself would become the heaviest piece of timber to crush **Xin**'s entire world.

【**The Little White Rabbit (A second death)**】
It probably never imagined its death would hold such profound "significance." The first time, thrown
by the neighbor, it died as a "nuisance" being eliminated. The second time, skinned and eaten raw by
its owner, it died as a "sacrifice" offered up. Its soft fur and warm flesh became the rite of
passage for its master to bid farewell to the past and embrace savagery. It knew nothing, simply lay
there, its small corpse completing a brutal coming-of-age ritual.

### The People Still Alive

【**Xin (The Transformer)**】
In this chapter, **Xin** is like glass shattered by a hammer blow. We watch him transform from an
exam-bound apprentice, to an orphan receiving terrible news, to a madman clutching a head and
mumbling incoherently, and finally into a beast kneeling in a pool of blood, consuming raw rabbit
flesh. Each stage is suffocatingly painful. That gesture of stroking **Oak**'s ear grinds one's
heart against sandpaper. When he raises the knife not toward an enemy, but against his own throat,
forcing himself to swallow the raw meat on pain of death, the gentle **healer** apprentice dies in
that moment. What survives is a monster whose heart is now only a hollow pit filled with hatred.
Watching him gag and swallow, it's hard to tell whether he's swallowing rabbit flesh or his own
shredded soul.

【**Blue Uniform Alliance Guards (Two)**】
Two unlucky messengers of doom. They were likely just doing their duty, but they personally
delivered the box that could destroy a world. The sorrow and hesitation on their faces might have
held a grain of genuine sympathy, but in the face of such immense tragedy, any words are as pale as
paper. Their backs as they drop the money pouch and leave are like a microcosm of this indifferent
world—tragedy happens, you're notified, you're compensated, and then... you figure it out yourself.
They will never know the bloody transformation that took place behind that door after they left.

【**Neighbor Beastman (Second appearance)**】
This irritable neighbor, with a rabbit's corpse, delivers the "final lesson" to **Xin**. He probably
just meant to vent his anger, but inadvertently became the last straw that broke the camel's back
and provided the cruel textbook for "regaining wildness." He will never know that with one careless
toss, he didn't throw a dead rabbit, but a key that unlocked the gates of hell.

### Story Reflection

This chapter hurts. It's a slow, suffocating, excruciating pain of watching something beautiful be
ground to dust. The moment **Oak**'s head is revealed in the box, I almost stopped breathing
alongside **Xin**. That descent from hope ("just personal effects") to relief ("not ashes") to utter
devastation is written with cruel precision.

**Xin**'s transformation is the core of this chapter, and the most shocking part. He doesn't roar
and charge out for revenge. He first shatters, then despairs, and finally completes his
metamorphosis through an act of self-mutilation that resembles a religious rite. The act of eating
the rabbit raw is multi-layered symbolism: devouring "softness" (the rabbit representing his past
gentleness), practicing "wildness" (what Oak said he lacked), and a decisive "self-murder" (killing
his former self). The detail of holding the knife to his own throat to force himself to swallow is
particularly brutal—he's telling himself: either become strong, or die. There is no middle ground.

The world's cruel rules are laid bare here. The **Beast Alliance City**'s compensation is a cold
transaction, the neighbor's callousness is everyday cruelty, and the **bandits**' violence is a
distant, yet suddenly real, fatal threat. Trapped between these forces, **Xin** chooses to become
something more brutal than all the brutality around him, just to survive.

What hurts most are the tiny details: **Oak**'s ear no longer twitching, the **Bloodthread Grass
Extract** mark becoming the fatal identifier, **Xin**'s gagging physiological reaction as he
swallows... These details make the tragedy and transformation terrifyingly real. This isn't a热血 (
rèxuè -热血) hero's awakening. This is a soul being torn apart, reassembled, and stuffed full of
hatred and gore.

Watching **Xin** kneel in the blood, rabbit fur and blood-flecked spittle at the corner of his
mouth, I know the boy who tickled his uncle's ear and applied medicine so carefully is gone forever.
The road ahead is only darkness and blood, and he, licking his newly-sharpened fangs, is ready to
walk it.
